## Overview

The Features page lets you turn parts of your store on or off. Disabling a feature removes it from your admin panel and hides the corresponding pages and functionality from your storefront. This is useful if you run a service-based business with no products, a content-only site, or simply want to keep your admin tidy.

Go to **Settings → Features** to manage your enabled features.

---

## How It Works

![The Settings → Features page, with each feature listed under its category and an on/off switch beside it](/images/feature-flags/features.png)

Features are grouped into categories (E-Commerce, Content, and Marketing). Each feature has a name, a short description, and a switch. Changes take effect immediately. There's no save button, and a brief notification confirms each change.

Some features **depend on other features**. If a feature it depends on is off, its switch is grayed out and shows a badge like **"Requires Products"**. Turn the other feature back on and the switch works again. The tables below show what each feature needs.

![With Products switched off, Orders, Shopping Cart & Checkout, Payments, Coupons & Discounts and Rewards Program are grayed out, each with a Requires badge](/images/feature-flags/requires-badge.png)

*Products is switched off here. The features that need Products are grayed out and say "Requires Products". Features further down the chain, such as Payments, Coupons & Discounts and Rewards Program, are grayed out too. Hover over any badge to see which feature to turn on first.*

The check **follows the whole chain**. A switch is grayed out when a feature it needs is switched off, or is itself grayed out because of something it needs. For example, switching Products off grays out Orders and Shopping Cart & Checkout. Payments needs both of those, so Payments is grayed out as well, even though you never switched Orders or the cart off yourself.

The badge names the features the switch **directly** needs, not the one at the bottom of the chain. With Products off, Payments reads **"Requires Orders, Shopping Cart & Checkout"**, and Coupons & Discounts reads **"Requires Shopping Cart & Checkout"**. Hover over it and the tip reads Enable "Orders" and "Shopping Cart & Checkout" first to use this feature. Turn those back on, and Payments works again.

Your own switch settings are remembered. If you had Payments on before you turned Products off, it goes back to on when Products comes back. You don't need to set it up again. The sections below spell out what each switch does.

<Callout type="info" title="Who can change features">
  Owners and managers can turn features on and off. Staff members can't open this page. One feature, **Products CSV export**, can only be switched by the store owner. See [Team and Roles](/docs/team-and-roles).
</Callout>

Disabling a feature removes it from your admin sidebar and hides its pages from your storefront. Links your template adds on its own for that feature are hidden too. Links you've added yourself in **Site Setup → Navigation** are hidden automatically on many templates. On others, remove them there yourself.

## Which Features You Can Toggle

These are the features that have a switch on your Features page. The **On by default** column shows how every new store starts.

### E-Commerce

| Feature | On by default? | Needs | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Products** | Yes | Nothing | Your product catalog and all product-related admin pages. Several other features depend on this one. |
| **Orders** | Yes | Products | Receive and manage customer orders, including fulfillment. See [Orders](/docs/orders). |
| **Shopping Cart & Checkout** | Yes | Products | Lets customers add items to a cart. Off, your shop works like a catalog with no Add to Cart button. |
| **Checkout** | Yes | Products, Shopping Cart & Checkout | Lets customers finish and complete their purchase. |
| **Payments** | Yes | Orders, Shopping Cart & Checkout | Lets customers pay for their purchase. See [Payments & Payouts](/docs/payments-and-payouts) and [Finances](/docs/finances). |
| **Inventory Management** | Yes | Nothing | Track stock levels, manage standalone inventory items, and get low-stock alerts. Works without Products, so it's handy for supplies and rentals you don't sell directly. See [Inventory Management](/docs/inventory-management). |
| **Rental Check-outs** | No | Inventory Management | Check rentable items out to events or clients and back in, with due dates, partial returns, and damaged or lost tracking. |
| **[Coupons & Discounts](/docs/discounts-and-coupons)** | No | Shopping Cart & Checkout | Create discount codes and promotional offers. See [Discounts and Coupons](/docs/discounts-and-coupons). |
| **Collections** | No | Products | Group products into collections and show them on your site. See [Collections](/docs/collections). |
| **[Wishlist](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock)** | Yes | Products | Lets shoppers save products to a wishlist, with heart icons on product cards and a wishlist page. See [Wishlist & Back-in-Stock Alerts](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock). |
| **[Back-in-Stock Notifications](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock)** | Yes | Products | Adds a "notify me" form to out-of-stock products and emails shoppers when the item is restocked. See [Wishlist & Back-in-Stock Alerts](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock). |

| **Product Subscriptions** | No | Products, Payments | Let customers subscribe to a product on a recurring schedule, billed through your connected Stripe account. See [Subscriptions](/docs/subscriptions). |
| **Donations** | No | Nothing | Accept one-time donations or tips through Stripe, Venmo, or Cash App. See [Donations & tips](/docs/donations). |

## What Happens When You Disable a Feature

In every case below, your data is kept. Turning the feature back on restores it.

### Disabling Products

This is the most impactful switch. Turning it off grays out every feature that needs Products, and then everything that needs *those*:

- **Directly:** Orders, Shopping Cart & Checkout, Checkout, Collections, Wishlist, Back-in-Stock Notifications, Product Reviews, and Product Subscriptions.
- **Through Orders and the cart:** Payments, Coupons & Discounts, and Rewards Program. Product Subscriptions also needs Payments.

None of these have anything to work on without products, so they go off together. Your own switch settings for them are kept and come back when you turn Products back on.

- Finances leaves your sidebar, because Payments is off. It stays only if **Donations** is on, since a donations-only store still needs it. The Tax Guide needs Payments, so it is unavailable either way.
- Discounts leaves your sidebar, because Coupons & Discounts is off.
- Your `/shop` storefront page returns a 404.
- Product, order, collection, and review pages disappear from your admin.
- Inventory Management stays available (it doesn't depend on Products), so you can still track standalone items and supplies.

Re-enabling Products restores everything. Your products and orders are all preserved.

### Disabling Shopping Cart & Checkout, or Checkout

- **Shopping Cart & Checkout** off: your site works like a catalog. Products, prices, and options still show, but every template hides the **Add to Cart** button, the quantity picker, and the cart icon, and the cart page and checkout show "page not found". Customers can still save products to their wishlist and sign up for back-in-stock emails. A "Cart is disabled" notice appears at the top of your [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard).
- **Checkout** off: customers can still fill a cart, but there's no way to check out. Use this if you want shoppers to browse and build a cart but not buy yet.
- Payments off also stops checkout from starting.

Turning **Shopping Cart & Checkout** off also grays out the features that need it: Checkout, Coupons & Discounts and Payments, and through Payments, Product Subscriptions. Discounts leaves your sidebar, and Finances does too unless Donations is on.

### Disabling Orders

- The Orders section disappears from your admin sidebar.
- Customers no longer see an order history in their account.
- Existing order data is preserved and accessible again when you re-enable it.

Turning Orders off also grays out Payments and Rewards Program (both need Orders), and Product Subscriptions too, since it needs Payments. Customers can't check out either. **Finances** is hidden because Payments is off, unless Donations is on. If you only want to pause buying, turn off Checkout instead.

### Disabling Inventory Management

- The Inventory section disappears from your admin sidebar.
- The Rental Check-outs tab is hidden (that feature depends on Inventory).
- Existing item and check-out data is preserved and accessible again when you re-enable it.
- Products linked to an item keep drawing from that item's stock when they sell. Orders don't check this switch.

### Disabling Rental Check-Outs

- The Check-outs tab in Inventory is hidden. If units are still checked out, the Items page shows a banner linking to them.
- New check-outs can't be created.
- Existing check-outs remain viewable and can still be checked in.

### Disabling Collections

- The `/collections` storefront pages return a 404.
- The Collections admin section is hidden.

### Disabling Galleries

- Your Galleries admin section is hidden.
- Galleries you've inserted into pages or blog posts show a "Gallery content is not available" placeholder instead of the images.

### Disabling Blog

- The `/blog` storefront pages return a 404.
- The Blog admin section is hidden.

### Disabling Testimonials

- The `/testimonials` storefront page is hidden.
- The Testimonials admin section is hidden.

### Disabling Customer-Facing Extras (Wishlist, Back-in-Stock, Product Reviews, Rewards)

- **Wishlist:** heart icons and the wishlist page disappear.
- **Back-in-Stock Notifications:** the "notify me" form no longer appears on out-of-stock products.
- **Product Reviews:** the review form and review display are hidden.
- **Rewards Program:** earning and redeeming pause, and customers keep their balances.

## Common Use Cases

**Service business with no products**
Turn off Products. This removes the shop, cart, orders, and payments from both your admin and storefront, so your site becomes a content- and contact-focused presence. Turn on **[Services](/docs/services)** or **[Events](/docs/events)** if they fit. Inventory stays available for tracking supplies and rental equipment. Turn it off too if you don't need it.

**Content or portfolio site**
Turn off Products, and turn on Blog and Galleries. Your storefront focuses on articles, images, and a contact form, with no shopping at all.

**Catalog only**
Keep Products on but turn off **Shopping Cart & Checkout**. Customers can browse your products and prices and get in touch, but there's no Add to Cart button or cart anywhere on your site.

**Soft launch**
Keep Products on but turn off **Checkout**. Your products are visible and customers can build a cart, but they can't buy yet. Turn Checkout back on when you're ready.

**Taking donations or invoicing clients**
Turn on **Donations** or **Invoices**. Neither needs Products, so you can use them on a site that doesn't sell anything. With Donations on, Finances stays in your sidebar even when Products and Payments are off.

They're on by default for every store (see [the table above](#managed-by-the-simplepress-team)). Everything else, including Product Reviews and Coupons & Discounts, you can switch yourself.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will disabling a feature delete my data?**
No. Your products, orders, blog posts, gallery images, testimonials, and so on are all kept when you disable a feature. Re-enabling it restores full access.

**Why is a feature's switch grayed out?**
It depends on a feature that's currently off, or on one that is grayed out itself. The badge next to the switch names the features it needs, and the hover tip says which to turn on first.

**I turned off Blog, but the "Blog" link is still in my storefront header. Why?**
On many templates, disabling a feature automatically hides its links from your storefront navigation, including links you added yourself. If the link stays, go to **Site Setup → Navigation** to remove it.

**Can I disable Customer Accounts?**
Not yourself. Customer Accounts is managed by the SimplePress team and is on by default for every store. [Contact us](/docs/expectations#getting-help-and-requesting-changes) if you need it changed.

**I'm a manager and can't switch Products CSV export. Why?**
It unlocks bulk data export, so only the store owner can turn it on or off. A Manager who tries sees "Only the store owner can turn Products CSV export on or off," and the switch stays off. Using the export is Owner-only too.

**A feature I need isn't on this page. What do I do?**
Some features are managed by the SimplePress team and don't have switches. [Contact us](/docs/expectations#getting-help-and-requesting-changes) and we can make the change for you.
