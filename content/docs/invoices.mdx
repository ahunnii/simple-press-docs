## Overview

Invoices let you bill a customer for anything outside your normal checkout: a custom job, a wholesale order, a service you quoted by hand. You build the invoice, send it, and your customer opens a secure page that shows what they owe and how to pay you: bank transfer, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, cash or check, or any other way you list. When the money arrives, you record the payment and SimplePress keeps the balance, status and history up to date.

There are two ways to invoice, and you can use either or both:

| | **Invoices** | **QuickBooks Invoicing** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Best for** | Billing anyone, for anything | Quote-based businesses that take a deposit up front and bill the rest after the job |
| **Who sends it** | SimplePress, from your store | QuickBooks Online, from your QuickBooks company |
| **How customers pay** | With the methods you list (bank transfer, PayPal, Venmo and so on). There's no online card payment on these invoices. | Through QuickBooks' own invoice email |
| **Who records payments** | You, by hand | QuickBooks; the status syncs back to SimplePress |

Both kinds appear together on your **Invoices** page. Most of this page covers SimplePress invoices. [QuickBooks invoicing](#quickbooks-invoicing) has its own section near the end.

## Turning invoices on

**Invoices** is off until you switch it on:

1. Go to **Settings → Features**.
2. Turn on **Invoices**. If you want to invoice through QuickBooks, turn on **QuickBooks Invoicing** too.

**Invoices** then appears in your admin sidebar. It shows up when either feature is on. Only Owners and Managers can use invoices (see [Team & Roles](/docs/team-and-roles)).

### If you turn Invoices off later

Nothing is deleted. Your invoices stay listed, with an **Invoices are turned off** banner at the top of the Invoices page. You can still:

- view every invoice;
- record payments, and delete a payment you recorded by mistake;
- cancel an invoice.

Creating, editing, sending, sending reminders and deleting drafts are paused until you turn Invoices back on.

## Set up your invoices

Before your first invoice, go to **Settings → Invoices** (`/admin/settings/invoices`). Everything here prefills new invoices, and you can change any of it on a single invoice while you build it. Click **Save changes** when you're done.

![Settings → Invoices, with the Numbering, Defaults, Reminders and Payment methods cards](/images/invoices/invoice-settings.png)

*Invoice settings. The "Next invoice" line under Numbering previews the number your next invoice will get.*

### Numbering

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Prefix** | Shown before every invoice number, like the `INV-` in `INV-0007`. | `INV-` |
| **Zero-padding** | How many digits the number shows. With 4, invoice 7 reads `0007`. Anything from 1 to 8. | 4 |
| **Starting number** | A floor for new invoices, not a counter. Your next invoice gets this number or one more than your highest existing number, whichever is bigger. | 1 |

Below the fields, **Next invoice:** shows exactly what your next number will look like (for example, **INV-0001**). Changing these never renumbers invoices you've already created.

### Defaults

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Default due terms** | When new invoices are due: **Due on receipt**, **Net 7**, **Net 15**, **Net 30** or **Net 60**. Starts at **Net 30**. |
| **Default tax rate** | A tax percentage to apply to new invoices. Starts at 0. |
| **Default notes** | Text shown to the customer on every new invoice, like a thank-you. |
| **Default terms** | Optional fine print, such as late fees or your refund policy. |

### Reminders

Customers never get automatic reminder emails. The only reminders they receive are the ones you send by hand from an invoice. These two switches are for emails to **you**, and both start on:

| Setting | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Email me when an invoice becomes past due** | An email shortly after one of your invoices passes its due date, listing what's past due. |
| **Weekly summary every Monday morning** | Your outstanding, overdue and collected totals for the past week, sent on Monday morning in your business's time zone. It covers SimplePress invoices only, not QuickBooks invoices. |

### Payment methods

Payment methods are how customers pay you: the account details and instructions that appear on the invoice. Add the ones you accept:

1. Under **Payment methods**, click **Add payment method**.
2. Pick a **Type** and fill in the details.
3. Click **Save**.

![The Add payment method dialog with the Type dropdown open, listing Bank transfer, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, Cash or check and Other](/images/invoices/payment-method-dialog.png)

*Pick a type, and the fields below change to match.*

Every type also has an optional **Label**. You see it in your list, and emails use it as the method's name. Emails never include the details themselves.

| Type | Fields | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bank transfer** | Account holder name, Bank name, Routing number, Account number, Account type (Checking or Savings), SWIFT / BIC, Additional instructions | Account numbers are masked in your admin list. On the customer's invoice page they're shown in full, with copy buttons. |
| **PayPal** | PayPal.Me username or link, and/or PayPal email | Fill in at least one. The **Pay with PayPal** button only appears when you give a PayPal.Me username. |
| **Venmo** | Venmo username | Filled in for you from your donation settings if you've set a Venmo handle there. |
| **Cash App** | $Cashtag | Filled in for you from your donation settings if you've set a Cashtag there. |
| **Zelle** | Zelle email or phone, Recipient name | The recipient name is the name customers see in their banking app. |
| **Cash or check** | Payable to, Mailing address, Additional instructions | |
| **Other** | Title (for example, "Wire transfer"), Instructions | For anything else you accept. |

You can save up to 10 payment methods. Use the arrows to move a method up or down, the pencil to edit it, and the bin to remove it. The order here is the order customers see on the invoice. There's no on/off switch; you choose which methods to offer on each invoice while you build it.

<Callout type="info" title="Your account details stay private">
  Payment details are stored encrypted. The full details only appear on the secure invoice page your customer opens, never in emails.
</Callout>

## Create an invoice

<video src="/videos/invoices/create-and-send-invoice.webm" poster="/videos/invoices/create-and-send-invoice-poster.png" controls muted playsInline className="rounded-lg border" />

*Creating an invoice: find the customer, add an item from the catalog, save the draft, mark it as sent and copy the link.*

You can start a new invoice from any of these places:

- **Invoices → New invoice** (`/admin/invoices/new`);
- a customer's page, with the **New invoice** button on their **Invoices** card (see [Customers](/docs/customers));
- the command palette (`Cmd + K` / `Ctrl + K`), by choosing **New invoice**.

The header shows the number the invoice will get, such as **INV-0007 (assigned on save)**. The number is only taken once you save.

![The New invoice page, with Customer and Line items on the left and a live preview of the invoice on the right](/images/invoices/invoice-builder.png)

*As you fill in the form, the preview on the right shows exactly what your customer will see.*

### Customer

Click **Find existing customer** and search by name or email, or type a **Customer name**, **Email** and (optionally) **Phone**. Click **Add billing address** if you want an address on the invoice (Street address, Apt/suite, City, State, ZIP).

SimplePress links the invoice to the customer with that email, or creates a new customer record if there isn't one yet. While the invoice is a draft, you can still change who it's for.

### Line items

A new invoice starts with one blank line. Fill in a **Description**, **Qty** and **Unit price**, and the **Amount** works itself out.

- **Add line** adds another blank line.
- **Add from catalog** lets you search your products, variants and services and add them with their name and price filled in.
- Use the arrows on a line to reorder it, and the bin to delete it.

Quantities can have up to three decimal places (handy for hours, like 1.5). If you only use catalog items, delete the blank starting line. Otherwise saving stops with "Description is required".

### Discount & tax

Under **Discount**, choose **No discount**, **Amount ($)** or **Percent (%)**, then enter the discount. Set the **Tax rate** as a percentage. Tax is worked out after the discount.

### Due date

Choose **Due on receipt**, **Net 7**, **Net 15**, **Net 30**, **Net 60** or **Custom date**. Preset terms are counted from the day you send the invoice, not the day you create it.

### Payment methods

Each payment method you saved in settings appears with a checkbox, and all of them start ticked. Untick any you don't want to offer on this invoice. You can untick every method, but then the invoice gives your customer no instructions for paying you, so we recommend leaving at least one ticked.

### Notes & terms

**Notes** and **Terms** start with your defaults from settings. Edit them for this invoice if you like.

### Saving

Click **Save draft**. Drafts are only visible to you, and you can keep editing them until you send.

On a wide screen, the live preview sits next to the form. On a narrower screen, switch between the **Edit** and **Preview** tabs at the top.

**Limits:** an invoice can have up to 100 lines, each line's unit price can be up to $1,000,000, and the whole invoice can total up to $10,000,000.

## Send it

Open the draft and click **Send**. The **Send INV-0001** dialog gives you two choices:

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Email to** your customer's email | SimplePress emails the invoice with a link to view it. Add a **Message (optional)** to show above the invoice summary. Click **Send invoice**. |
| **Mark as sent without emailing** | No email goes out. Use this when you'll share the link yourself, by text for example. Click **Mark as sent**. |

![The Send INV-0001 dialog, with "Email to" selected, a Message box, and the total and due date summary](/images/invoices/send-dialog.png)

*The dialog shows the total and the due date your terms work out to.*

Once it's sent, the invoice can't be edited. If something's wrong, cancel it and create a new one.

You can't send an invoice that totals $0, or one whose custom due date has already passed.

### Sharing the link yourself

On a sent invoice, click **Copy link** to copy the link to the customer's secure invoice page. Each copied link works for 365 days from when you copied it. If a customer opens an older, expired link, they see "This invoice link has expired" and are asked to contact you for a new one. Copy a fresh link and send it to them.

## What your customer sees

The link opens a page showing your logo and business details, who the invoice is for, the line items and totals, and the balance due.

![The customer's invoice page, with line items, totals, How to pay cards for bank transfer and Zelle, and a Payments received table](/images/invoices/customer-invoice-page.png)

*The secure invoice page on a computer. Each payment detail has a copy button.*

- A status pill reads **Awaiting payment**, **Partially paid**, **Overdue**, **Paid ✓** or **Cancelled**.
- **How to pay** lists the payment methods on the invoice, with copy buttons. Each one shows the invoice number as the **Reference**, so you can match payments up.
- **Payments received** lists the payments you've recorded.
- Once the invoice is paid, the payment instructions are replaced by "Paid in full — thank you." If you cancel it, the page says "This invoice was cancelled."
- **Print / Save as PDF** at the top lets your customer print it or save a copy.

Customers don't need an account to open the link. They can't pay by card on this page; they pay you with one of the methods you listed, and you record the payment.

Each invoice shows its number and status, "Issued … · Due …", the total, how much is due (or "Paid in full"), and a **View invoice →** link to the secure page. Drafts never appear here. QuickBooks invoices don't appear here either; QuickBooks emails those to your customer.

## Record payments

When a customer pays you, open the invoice and click **Record payment**.

![The Record a payment dialog, with Amount, Date, Method, Reference, Note, and an Email receipt to customer checkbox](/images/invoices/record-payment-dialog.png)

*The amount starts at the full balance due.*

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Amount** | Starts at the balance due. It can be less (a partial payment) but not more. |
| **Date** | The day you received the money. |
| **Method** | Bank transfer, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, Cash or check, Other, or Card. |
| **Reference (optional)** | A check number or transaction ID. |
| **Note (optional)** | Anything you want to remember about this payment. |
| **Email receipt to customer** | Emails the customer a receipt. Ticked for you if the invoice was emailed, unticked if you marked it as sent without emailing. |

Click **Record payment**. If a balance is left, the invoice becomes **Partially paid**. When the balance reaches zero, it becomes **Paid**. You can record as many payments as you need.

SimplePress doesn't move money, so there's nothing to refund. If you recorded a payment by mistake, click the delete icon next to it in the **Payments** card and confirm **Delete this payment?** The balance goes back up.

## The invoice page

Click any invoice to open it. The bar at the top shows only the actions that fit the invoice's status:

| Button | When you see it |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Drafts |
| **Send** | Drafts |
| **Delete draft** | Drafts. Drafts are deleted for good; they were never sent, so nothing needs cancelling. |
| **Record payment** | Sent and partially paid invoices with a balance left |
| **Send reminder** | Sent and partially paid invoices |
| **Cancel invoice** | Sent and partially paid invoices |
| **Print** | Any invoice except a draft |
| **Copy link** | Any invoice except a draft |

![An invoice's page, with the action bar, the invoice itself, and Payments, Details and Activity cards on the right](/images/invoices/invoice-detail.png)

*A partially paid invoice, with its payment, details and activity on the right.*

On the right:

- **Payments** lists each payment's date, method, amount, reference, note and whether a receipt went out, with a delete icon.
- **Details** shows how the invoice was sent (**Sent via**), when the customer **First viewed** and **Last viewed** it, and how many **Reminders sent**.
- **Activity** is the invoice's history: Created, Edited, Emailed to customer, Marked as sent, Email failed to send, Viewed by customer, Reminder sent, Payment recorded, Payment deleted, Receipt sent, Cancelled, and Overdue alert sent to you.

### Send a reminder

Click **Send reminder**, add a **Message (optional)**, and send. Your customer gets a reminder email with a link back to the invoice.

You can send one reminder every 24 hours per invoice. Until then the button is greyed out, and hovering over it shows "You can send another reminder after …".

### Cancel an invoice

Click **Cancel invoice**. In the **Cancel this invoice?** dialog:

- add a **Reason (shown to the customer)** if you like;
- leave **Email the customer that this invoice was cancelled** ticked to let them know, or untick it.

Click **Cancel invoice** to confirm, or **Keep invoice** to back out. Any payments you'd already recorded stay on the invoice. A cancelled invoice can't be reopened.

### Print

Click **Print** to open a printer-friendly version, then use its **Print** button (or **Back to Invoice** to return). To get a PDF, choose "Save as PDF" in your browser's print window. SimplePress doesn't create a PDF file itself.

## The Invoices list

**Invoices** in the sidebar (`/admin/invoices`) lists every invoice, newest first.

![The Invoices page, with Outstanding, Overdue and Paid last 30 days summary cards, status tabs, and a table of invoices](/images/invoices/invoices-list.png)

*The Invoices list. SimplePress and QuickBooks invoices sit side by side.*

At the top, three summary cards:

- **Outstanding**: what you're still owed, and how many invoices that is. Click it to see them.
- **Overdue**: what's past due. Click it to see those invoices.
- **Paid, last 30 days**: payments received in the last 30 days.

The tabs filter by status: **All**, **Draft**, **Outstanding**, **Overdue**, **Paid** and **Cancelled**. Outstanding covers sent and partially paid invoices; a sent invoice shows as **Outstanding** in the list.

Use the search box to find an invoice by number, customer name or email. **Filters** lets you change the **Sort** (Newest first, Oldest first, Due soonest, Highest amount, Lowest amount), and, once you have QuickBooks invoices, the **Source** (All sources, SimplePress or QuickBooks).

The columns are **Invoice**, **Customer**, **Status**, **Due**, **Total** and **Balance**. The **⋮** menu on a row has **View**, plus **Edit** and **Delete draft** on drafts. To record a payment, send a reminder or cancel, open the invoice.

### Invoice statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Saved but not sent. Only you can see it, and you can still edit or delete it. |
| **Outstanding** | Sent and waiting for payment. |
| **Partially paid** | Some of the balance has been paid. |
| **Overdue** | Past its due date with a balance left. This is worked out from the due date in your business's time zone. |
| **Paid** | Paid in full. |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled by you. No more payments can be recorded. |

## QuickBooks invoicing

If you run a quote-based business, QuickBooks invoicing lets you collect a deposit when a customer accepts your quote, then bill the rest after the job, all from SimplePress. The invoices are created in your own QuickBooks Online company and emailed by QuickBooks. Customers pay through QuickBooks' invoice email, so money never passes through SimplePress.

### Connect QuickBooks

1. Turn on **QuickBooks Invoicing** in **Settings → Features**.
2. Go to **Settings → Integrations** and find the **QuickBooks Online** card. It only appears once the feature is on.
3. Click **Connect QuickBooks**, sign in with your Intuit account, and choose your company. You come back to SimplePress when it's done.

Only Owners and Managers can connect QuickBooks.

![The QuickBooks Online card in Settings → Integrations, showing Connected, the company name and ID, Open QuickBooks and Disconnect buttons, and Deposit defaults](/images/invoices/qbo-integrations-card.png)

*A connected QuickBooks company, with the deposit defaults below. (This screenshot is from a test company, which is why it says Sandbox.)*

The card shows one of these states:

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Not configured** | QuickBooks isn't available for your store yet. [Contact us](mailto:help@generativejustice.org) and we'll set it up. |
| **Not connected** | Click **Connect QuickBooks**. |
| **Needs reconnect** | QuickBooks no longer accepts the saved connection. Intuit connections expire after about 100 days, or when access is revoked in QuickBooks. Click **Reconnect**. You can't send invoices until you do. |
| **Connected** | Shows your **Company**, **Company ID**, and "Connected since … · Last synced …", with **Open QuickBooks** and **Disconnect** buttons. |

**Disconnecting** asks you to confirm. Your existing invoices stay in QuickBooks and in SimplePress; you just can't send new ones until you reconnect. Your deposit settings are kept. If you reconnect to a **different** QuickBooks company, invoices from the old company are marked "Belongs to a previous QuickBooks company" and stop syncing.

### Deposit defaults

Below the connection, **Deposit defaults** prefill the deposit invoice on each quote. You can always change the amount on a single invoice.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Deposit amount** | **Percent of quote** or **Fixed amount**. | Percent of quote |
| **Deposit percent** / **Deposit amount** | The percentage (1 to 100) or the dollar amount. | 25% |
| **Invoice due in (days)** | How many days customers get to pay a new QuickBooks invoice, from 0 to 90. | 7 days |

Click **Save**.

### Deposit and final invoices from a quote

This works with the **Quote Calculator** feature. When a lead comes in, open it from **Quotes** (`/admin/quotes`). On the lead's page:

1. In the **Final quote** card, enter the **Amount** you're charging and click **Save amount**. QuickBooks invoices are worked out from this number.
2. In the **QuickBooks invoices** card, click **Send deposit invoice**.

![A lead's Final quote card with a $2,500 amount, and the QuickBooks invoices card with Send deposit invoice and Send final invoice buttons](/images/invoices/qbo-lead-card-before.png)

*Before any invoices: the summary line shows the quote, deposits invoiced so far and what's left.*

The **New invoice** dialog opens with the deposit ready to go. Pick **25%**, **50%**, **75%** or **Custom** (each preset shows the dollar amount), and the dialog shows what's left after this deposit.

![The New invoice dialog for a deposit, with 25%, 50%, 75% and Custom presets, Kind set to Deposit, and the customer's details filled in](/images/invoices/qbo-deposit-dialog.png)

*A 25% deposit on a $2,500 quote. "Email the invoice from QuickBooks now" is unticked here, so the button reads Create invoice.*

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Kind** | **Deposit**, **Final** or **Custom**. Locked when you open the dialog from a lead. |
| **Amount** | The amount to bill. |
| **Customer name**, **Customer email**, **Phone (optional)** | Filled in from the lead. |
| **Due date** | Starts at your **Invoice due in (days)** default. |
| **Billing address (optional)** | Click to expand. |
| **Description (optional)** | The line on the invoice. Leave it empty to use "Deposit — (your business)", "Final balance — (your business)" or "Services — (your business)". |
| **Memo to customer (optional)** | A message shown on the QuickBooks invoice. |
| **Email the invoice from QuickBooks now** | Ticked by default, and the button reads **Send invoice**. Untick it to create the invoice in QuickBooks without emailing it yet; the button then reads **Create invoice**, and you can send it later. |

Need another deposit? The button becomes **Send another deposit**. Deposits can't add up to more than the final quote.

After the job, click **Send final invoice**. The dialog shows a breakdown: your **Final quote**, minus **Deposits invoiced**, equals **Remaining**, and the amount starts at what's remaining.

![The New invoice dialog for a final invoice, showing Final quote $2,500, minus $625 in deposits invoiced, equals $1,875 remaining](/images/invoices/qbo-final-dialog.png)

*The final invoice subtracts the deposit, even though the deposit hasn't been paid yet.*

<Callout type="info" title="Unpaid deposits are still subtracted">
  The final invoice subtracts every deposit you've invoiced, paid or not. An unpaid deposit is still collectible in QuickBooks, so don't add it back into the final. Deposits that failed or were voided aren't subtracted.
</Callout>

Each lead can have one final invoice. Once it exists, **Send final invoice** is greyed out with the note "Final invoice already sent".

![The QuickBooks invoices card after sending, listing a Final and a Deposit invoice, each with amount, status, date, balance and an Open in QuickBooks link](/images/invoices/qbo-lead-card-after.png)

*After invoicing: each invoice shows its kind, amount, status, date and balance, with an Open in QuickBooks link.*

Click **Refresh statuses** to check QuickBooks for payments right away. If **Send deposit invoice** or **Send final invoice** is greyed out, the hint underneath tells you why: "Set a final quote or estimate first", "Set a final quote first" or "Final invoice already sent".

### Invoices that aren't tied to a quote

You can also send a QuickBooks invoice from the **Invoices** page:

- Click the arrow next to **New invoice** and choose **Issue via QuickBooks**. If QuickBooks Invoicing is your only invoicing feature, **New invoice** opens the QuickBooks dialog directly.
- Or open the command palette and choose **New QuickBooks invoice**.

![The New invoice split button with its menu open, showing Issue via QuickBooks](/images/invoices/qbo-issue-menu.png)

*Issue via QuickBooks sits under the arrow next to New invoice.*

### QuickBooks invoices in the list

QuickBooks invoices appear in the same **Invoices** list as your SimplePress ones, and count toward the summary cards. Use **Filters → Source** to show only one kind.

![The Invoices list with the Filters menu open on Source (All sources, SimplePress, QuickBooks), and QuickBooks invoices marked with a QuickBooks badge](/images/invoices/invoices-list-quickbooks.png)

*QuickBooks invoices carry a QuickBooks badge and their kind (Deposit or Final).*

Each QuickBooks invoice has a **QuickBooks** badge, its kind underneath, and the invoice number QuickBooks gave it. If it came from a quote, the lead's name appears under the customer. The row's **⋮** menu offers:

| Action | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Send** | The invoice was created but not emailed yet. |
| **Resend** | The invoice is sent or overdue. |
| **Refresh status** | Any invoice that exists in QuickBooks. |
| **Retry** | The invoice failed or got stuck (Error or Pending). |
| **Open in QuickBooks** | Any invoice that exists in QuickBooks. |

**Sync now** at the top of the page checks QuickBooks for every invoice at once.

### Statuses and syncing

| Status | What it means | Which tab |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Waiting to be created in QuickBooks. | All only |
| **Created** | In QuickBooks but not emailed yet. | Outstanding |
| **Sent** | Emailed by QuickBooks. | Outstanding |
| **Overdue** | Past its due date with a balance left. | Overdue |
| **Paid** | Paid in QuickBooks. | Paid |
| **Voided** | Voided in QuickBooks. | Cancelled |
| **Error** | QuickBooks couldn't create or update it. The reason is shown in red on the row. | All only |

SimplePress checks QuickBooks for changes automatically, roughly every 15 to 30 minutes. You can check right away with **Sync now**, **Refresh status** on a row, or **Refresh statuses** on a lead. The status, balance, due date and paid date come back from QuickBooks. The amount, memo and customer don't.

To change or void a QuickBooks invoice, do it in QuickBooks. As the note at the bottom of the list says, the status syncs back within about 30 minutes, or straight away with **Sync now**.

The weekly summary email covers SimplePress invoices only, not QuickBooks invoices.

### What happens in QuickBooks

- **Customers:** SimplePress looks for a QuickBooks customer with the same email and uses them, or creates a new customer.
- **Products and services:** deposits are billed to a "Deposit" product/service; final and custom invoices use "Services". SimplePress creates these in QuickBooks the first time it needs them.
- **Card and bank payments:** if your QuickBooks plan includes QuickBooks Payments, your customers can pay the invoice by card or bank transfer (ACH) from QuickBooks' email.

### Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Connect QuickBooks in Settings → Integrations first" | You haven't connected a QuickBooks company yet. See [Connect QuickBooks](#connect-quickbooks). |
| "QuickBooks needs to be reconnected" | The connection expired or was revoked. Go to **Settings → Integrations** and click **Reconnect**. |
| "QuickBooks invoicing is turned off…" | Turn **QuickBooks Invoicing** back on in **Settings → Features**. Your QuickBooks invoices are kept while it's off, but they don't sync. |
| "This QuickBooks connection was made in sandbox mode, but this deployment is configured for production…" (or the other way round) | Your store is connected to the wrong kind of QuickBooks company. Reconnect in **Settings → Integrations**. |
| "Only open invoices (created, sent, or overdue) can be re-sent" | The invoice is already paid or voided, so there's nothing to email. |
| A red error on an invoice's row | QuickBooks turned the invoice down and says why (for example, a missing or invalid detail). Fix the problem, then choose **Retry** from the row's **⋮** menu. |
| "Not found in QuickBooks" | The invoice was deleted in QuickBooks. |

## Common questions

**Can customers pay a SimplePress invoice by card?**
No. Customers pay you directly with the methods you list on the invoice, and you record the payment. If you want card payments on invoices, [QuickBooks invoicing](#quickbooks-invoicing) with QuickBooks Payments can do that.

**Can I edit an invoice after sending it?**
No. Only drafts can be edited. Cancel the sent invoice (you can tell the customer why) and create a new one.

**I recorded a payment by mistake. How do I undo it?**
Open the invoice and delete the payment from the **Payments** card. There's nothing to refund, because SimplePress never handled the money.

**Can I delete a sent invoice?**
No. Only drafts can be deleted. Cancel a sent invoice instead; it stays in your list for your records.

**Do customers get automatic reminders?**
No. Customers only get the reminders you send with **Send reminder**. You can get automatic past-due alerts and a weekly summary for yourself in **Settings → Invoices**.

**My customer says their link doesn't work.**
Links last 365 days from when they were made. Open the invoice, click **Copy link** to make a fresh one, and send it to them.
