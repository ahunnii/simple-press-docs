## Overview

Subscriptions let a customer sign up once and receive a product on a repeating schedule — for example a bag of coffee every month, or a box of soap every two weeks. They are charged automatically each time, and every successful charge shows up in your **Orders** list as a normal order for you to fulfill.

You choose which products can be subscribed to, how often they repeat, and whether subscribers get a discount for committing. Customers manage their own subscription (skip a delivery, pause, update their card, or cancel) from a link in their emails, so you don't have to field those requests.

> **Requires Stripe.** Subscriptions are billed through your connected Stripe account, so your store must be connected and able to accept payments before shoppers can subscribe. See [Payments & Payouts](/docs/payments-and-payouts) and [Stripe](/docs/stripe).

---

## Turning Subscriptions On

1. Go to **Settings → Features**.
2. Switch on **Product Subscriptions**.
3. A **Subscriptions** item now appears in your admin sidebar, and a new **Subscriptions** card appears when you edit a product (**Products →** edit a product).

Turning the feature on does not change any of your products by itself. Nothing is offered as a subscription until you switch it on for a specific product.

**Product Subscriptions** is off by default, and you can turn it on or off yourself. It needs **Products** and **Payments** (Stripe) to be on as well.

See [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags) for how the Features page works.

## Setting Up a Product for Subscription

1. Go to **Products** and open the product you want to offer (see [Products](/docs/products)).
2. Find the **Subscriptions** card.
3. Switch on **Offer as a subscription**. This adds a Subscribe option alongside Add to Cart on the product page.
4. Under **Cadence**, tick every repeat schedule you want to offer. You must choose at least one.
5. Optionally enter a **Subscribe & save** percentage. Subscribers pay this much less than the regular price.
6. Save the product.

### Available Cadences

| Option            | How often the customer is charged |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Every week**    | Once a week                       |
| **Every 2 weeks** | Every other week                  |
| **Every month**   | Once a month                      |
| **Every 2 months**| Every other month                 |
| **Every 3 months**| Once a quarter                    |

These five schedules are the only ones available. If you tick more than one, the customer picks their preferred schedule on the product page.

### Subscribe & Save

The discount is a whole-number percentage from 0 to 90. It is applied to the product's price on every delivery for as long as the customer stays subscribed, and the customer sees how much they are saving on the product page and at sign-up. Leave it at 0 to charge the regular price.

### Products With Variants

You switch subscriptions on for the whole product, not for individual variants. When a product has variants (size, flavor, and so on), the customer chooses a variant first, and the subscription is billed at that variant's price.

### When the Subscribe Option Is Hidden

The Subscribe option does not appear on a product page when:

- the product is marked as coming soon,
- the product or the chosen variant is out of stock,
- Subscriptions is turned off for your store,
- no repeat schedule is ticked on the product,
- the items in a delivery would cost less than $0.50 after the Subscribe & save discount (the lowest amount Stripe can charge), or
- the product has [Product options](/docs/product-options). A product customers build or personalize is bought through the cart instead.

If your Stripe account isn't connected yet, the panel still shows on the product page. When a customer clicks it, they see "Subscriptions aren't available right now". Connect Stripe before you start offering subscriptions (see [Stripe](/docs/stripe)).

## What Your Customers See

### On the Product Page

Below the Add to Cart button, a panel headed **Subscribe & save 5%** (the number is your discount percentage) shows the price "per delivery + shipping", a line like "You save $1.50 vs. the one-time price", and — if you offered more than one cadence — a **Delivery frequency** choice. It also says "Shipping is quoted for your address at the next step. Cancel anytime." The **Subscribe** button takes them to the next step.

![The Subscribe & save panel on a product page, showing the price per delivery plus shipping, the savings, and a Subscribe button](/images/subscriptions/product-subscribe-panel.png)

*The Subscribe panel on a computer.*

![The same Subscribe panel on a phone, with the text wrapping to fit the narrow screen](/images/subscriptions/product-subscribe-panel-mobile.png)

*The same panel on a phone.*

How this panel looks matches your template. Your customer does not need an account to subscribe.

### The Subscribe Page

The page is headed **Subscribe**. Before paying, the customer fills in **Email**, **Full name**, and **Phone** (all required), then chooses **Ship to address** or **In-store pickup** (pickup only appears if you have in-store pickup turned on in your settings). For shipping they enter an address, and your normal shipping rates are applied to quote the cost of each delivery.

A **Subscription summary** card shows the price per unit, the Subscribe & save discount, shipping per delivery, the per-delivery total, and the delivery frequency. If you use automatic tax, it shows "Tax: Calculated at checkout" and the total reads "Per-delivery total (before tax)". It states clearly that the "First charge today, then $X every … until you cancel." If you have a Terms of Service or Refund Policy page, a notice links to them.

The customer clicks **Continue to secure checkout** and lands on a secure Stripe page to enter their card. When it succeeds they see a "You're subscribed!" page and receive a confirmation email.

![The Subscribe page with contact details, a shipping address form, and a Subscription summary card next to a Continue to secure checkout button](/images/subscriptions/subscribe-page.png)

*The Subscribe page before the customer fills it in.*

If the product or variant sells out before the customer gets here, they see a "Currently out of stock" page with a **Back to product** button instead.

> **Shipping address and price are locked.** The address, the shipping rate quoted at sign-up, and the product price are frozen for the life of that subscription. If you change the product's price later, or the customer moves, existing subscriptions are not affected. A customer who moves must cancel and subscribe again with the new address.

### Managing a Subscription

Every email about a subscription includes a **manage** link. It opens a page where the customer can see:

- the product, quantity, and how often they are billed,
- the next delivery date and their per-delivery total,
- their delivery address or pickup choice,
- their five most recent orders, under **Recent orders**, and whether each has shipped, and
- an **Actions** section with buttons to manage the subscription.

![The Manage your subscription page, with the subscription details, delivery address, recent orders, and the Skip next delivery, Pause, Update payment method and Cancel subscription buttons](/images/subscriptions/manage-subscription.png)

*The page customers see from the manage link in their emails.*

![The same Manage your subscription page on a phone](/images/subscriptions/manage-subscription-mobile.png)

*The manage page on a phone.*

| Button                    | What it does                                                                              |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Skip next delivery**    | Skips one delivery with no charge. The customer can **Undo skip** until it takes effect.  |
| **Pause**                 | Stops billing and deliveries until the customer (or you) resumes.                         |
| **Resume**                | Starts a paused subscription again.                                                       |
| **Update payment method** | Sends the customer to a secure Stripe page to change their card.                          |
| **Cancel subscription**   | Ends the subscription immediately. The customer confirms in a dialog (**Keep subscription** or **Yes, cancel**). |
| **Subscribe again**       | Shown on a cancelled subscription. Takes the customer back to the product to sign up again. |

Manage links stay valid for about six months. If a link has expired, or the customer has lost the email, they can go to your store's `/subscriptions/manage` page, enter their email address, and click **Email me my subscription links**. They see "Check your email", and an email arrives with a fresh secure link for each of their subscriptions.

If you have turned Subscriptions off, the manage page says "Some options are temporarily unavailable — you can still update your card or cancel."

### In the Customer's Account

Customers who have an account on your store can also see all their subscriptions under **Subscriptions** in their account area (`/account/subscriptions`; see [For Your Customers](/docs/for-your-customers)). Each row shows the product, "Qty · cadence · next date", "$X / delivery", and a **Manage →** link. If they have none, they see "You don't have any subscriptions yet" with a **Browse products** button, and a link, "Subscribed without an account? Look up your subscription by email →".

## Managing Subscriptions in Your Admin

Go to **Subscriptions** in your admin sidebar. If you turn the feature off, this sidebar item disappears. The page is still reachable by its address and shows "Product subscriptions are turned off".

### The Subscriptions List

![The Subscriptions admin page with four summary tiles, a search box, and a table of subscriptions](/images/subscriptions/subscriptions-list.png)

*The Subscriptions list.*

Until a customer subscribes, the page says "Subscriptions appear here once a customer subscribes. First, enable subscriptions on a product (Products → edit → Subscriptions)." with a **Go to products** button.

At the top, four tiles summarize your whole store (they don't change when you filter the table):

- **Active** — subscriptions that are billing normally
- **Paused** — subscriptions on hold
- **Past due** — subscriptions whose last payment failed
- **Est. monthly recurring** — an estimate of the monthly money from active subscriptions, with weekly and quarterly schedules converted to a monthly equivalent. Treat it as a guide rather than an exact forecast, because it excludes tax and does not account for skipped deliveries.

Below the tiles you can search ("Search by customer or product…") by customer name, email, or product, and filter by status (the filter starts at **Any status**). The table shows each customer, the product and variant, how often they are billed, the quantity, the price per delivery, a date, and the status.

The date column changes meaning with the status:

| Status        | Date shown                                                      |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Active**    | Next billing date (or "one skipped" if the customer skipped one) |
| **Past due**  | The date the payment failed                                     |
| **Paused**    | When it resumes, or "Indefinitely"                              |
| **Cancelled** | The date it was cancelled                                       |

### Statuses

| Status         | Meaning                                                                                                   |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Active**     | Billing normally.                                                                                         |
| **Past due**   | A payment attempt failed. Stripe keeps retrying and the customer has been asked to update their card.     |
| **Paused**     | Billing and deliveries are on hold.                                                                       |
| **Cancelled**  | Ended. Nothing more will be charged.                                                                      |
| **Incomplete** | The customer started signing up but hasn't finished paying yet. Nothing has been charged.                 |

Abandoned sign-ups that never reach the payment step are removed automatically after about a day, so they don't clutter your list.

### The Subscription Detail Page

Click a customer's email (or **View**) to open a subscription. The **Subscription** card shows:

- **Product**, **Variant**, **Quantity**, and **Cadence**
- **List price**, **Subscribe & save**, and **Price per unit**
- **Shipping per delivery** and **Per-delivery total**
- **Delivery method**
- **Created**, **Next billing**, **Delivery skipped** (if the customer skipped one), and **Current period**
- **Pause resumes at**, on a paused subscription. A pause with no end date reads "Indefinitely — the owner must resume it manually".
- **Cancelled at** and **Cancel reason**, on a cancelled subscription
- A **Stripe subscription** link to the subscription in your Stripe dashboard

A **Customer** panel links to the customer's profile, and a **Locked shipping address** card (or a pickup card) shows their delivery choice.

An **Orders** table lists every order this subscription has produced, each linking to the full order.

![A subscription's detail page with the Pause and Cancel buttons, the Subscription card, the Customer and Locked shipping address panels, and the Orders table](/images/subscriptions/subscription-detail.png)

*A subscription's detail page.*

### Your Actions

At the top right of the detail page:

- **Pause** — puts the subscription on hold with no end date. The customer is emailed. The customer, or you, can resume it at any time. You can pause an active or past-due subscription.
- **Resume** — restarts a paused subscription. If a customer skipped a delivery, this button reads **Undo skip** and puts that delivery back on.
- **Cancel** — ends the subscription immediately. A confirmation asks you to be sure. **No refund is issued**, any delivery that was already paid for still ships, the customer is emailed, and it cannot be undone.

A cancelled subscription shows who or what ended it:

| Cancel reason on the detail page                                | What happened                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cancelled by customer                                           | The customer cancelled from their manage page.                         |
| Cancelled by store                                              | You cancelled it from the admin.                                       |
| Ended by Stripe (payment failed or removed in Stripe)           | Stripe ended it, either after payments kept failing or because it was removed in Stripe. |
| Checkout was never completed                                    | The customer didn't finish signing up.                                 |

### Sync Now

The **Sync now** button at the top of the list asks Stripe for the latest state of your subscriptions and updates the list. You rarely need it — SimplePress updates automatically as changes happen and also double-checks every few hours — but it's useful if something looks out of date.

> **Make changes here, not in Stripe.** The detail page links to the subscription in your Stripe dashboard so you can look at payments. Don't change the quantity, price, or address there: those edits are not copied back to SimplePress and will cause renewal orders that don't match. Use Pause and Cancel in SimplePress instead.

## Renewals and Orders

Every time a subscription is successfully charged — the first payment and each renewal — SimplePress creates an order automatically. You never need to open Stripe to find out that a delivery is due.

- The order appears in your [Orders](/docs/orders) list with a **Subscription** badge. Open it and click the badge to jump back to the subscription. See [Orders](/docs/orders) for fulfillment, shipping, and refunds.
- It starts as **paid** and **unfulfilled**, ready for you to ship or hand over.
- You receive a new order email titled "New subscription order #N — your store name", and the customer receives an order confirmation with their manage link.
- Stock is reduced for each renewal order just like a normal sale, and you get your usual low-stock alert if it applies. See [Inventory Management](/docs/inventory-management).
- You can refund a subscription order like any other order. Refunding an order does not cancel the subscription — cancel it separately if the customer wants to stop.

The order records the price the customer actually paid, including shipping and, if you use automatic tax, the tax Stripe collected.

### Shipping, Tax, and Discount Codes

- **Shipping** is calculated once, when the customer signs up, from their address and your shipping settings, and the same amount is charged with every delivery. If shipping is free for that order, nothing is added. In-store pickup subscriptions are never charged shipping. See [Shipping](/docs/shipping).
- **Tax** is added to each charge only if you have automatic tax turned on. See [Tax](/docs/tax).
- **Discount codes** cannot be used on a subscription. The Subscribe & save percentage is the only discount available. See [Discounts & Coupons](/docs/discounts-and-coupons).

## Failed Payments

When a renewal charge fails (for example the card has expired):

1. The subscription's status changes to **Past due**.
2. The customer is emailed asking them to update their payment method, with a link to their manage page. You are also emailed so you know a renewal is failing.
3. Stripe retries the charge. Each failed attempt sends the customer a new email.
4. If a retry succeeds, the subscription returns to **Active** and the order is created as normal.
5. If every retry fails and Stripe gives up, the subscription is cancelled and the reason reads "Ended by Stripe".

The customer can fix a card problem at any time using **Update payment method** on their manage page. This stays available even if you have turned the Subscriptions feature off.

## Emails

These emails are sent automatically:

| Email                                  | Goes to                       | When                                                  |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Subscription confirmed                 | Customer                      | The customer finishes signing up                      |
| New subscription                       | You                           | A customer signs up                                   |
| Order confirmation / new order         | Customer / You                | Every successful charge, including renewals           |
| Payment failed                         | Customer and You              | A renewal charge fails (once per attempt)             |
| Subscription paused, resumed, or delivery skipped | Customer           | The subscription is paused, resumed, or a delivery is skipped |
| Subscription cancelled                 | Customer and You              | The subscription ends for any reason                  |
| Manage links ("Manage your *store name* subscriptions") | Customer          | The customer asks for their links from the lookup page |

You get three alerts about subscriptions: a new subscription, a failed payment, and a cancellation.

You can preview these emails, and change the subject line of the four customer emails (Subscription Started, Payment Failed, Cancelled, and Updated), under **Notification Emails** in your admin. See [Email Preview](/docs/email-preview).

## Feature Flag

| Setting                    | Value                                                                        |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Name**                   | Product Subscriptions                                                        |
| **Default**                | Off                                                                          |
| **Where to toggle**        | Settings → Features (you can turn it on or off yourself)                     |
| **Requires**               | Products and Payments                                                        |

Because Subscriptions depends on Products and Payments, switching either of those off also switches Subscriptions off. See [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags).

### If You Turn It Off

Turning Subscriptions off stops *new* subscriptions but never strands the people who already have one:

- The **Subscriptions** item disappears from your admin sidebar. The page itself still works if you go to it directly, and shows "Product subscriptions are turned off".
- The Subscribe option and the Subscribe page disappear, and the Subscriptions card disappears from the product form (your per-product settings are kept for when you turn it back on).
- Existing subscriptions **keep billing** at Stripe, and every renewal **still creates an order**.
- On the Subscriptions page, **Pause**, **Resume**, and **Sync now** are disabled. **Cancel** still works.
- Customers can still **cancel** and **update their card** from their manage link, but **Skip**, **Pause**, and **Resume** are hidden.

To stop billing a subscriber, cancel their subscription — turning the feature off is not enough.

## What You Can and Cannot Do

**You can:**

- Offer any product as a subscription, with one or several of five repeat schedules
- Give subscribers a Subscribe & save discount of up to 90%
- Let customers subscribe as guests, with shipping or in-store pickup
- See every subscription, filter by status, and search by customer or product
- Pause, resume, or cancel any subscription from the admin
- Fulfill renewals as ordinary orders and refund them like any other order
- Let customers skip, pause, update their card, or cancel on their own

**You cannot:**

- Create a subscription on a customer's behalf — customers sign up themselves from the product page
- Offer a custom schedule beyond the five listed (for example every 10 days or every 6 months)
- Set different subscription prices or schedules for individual variants
- Change a subscriber's product, quantity, price, or delivery address — they would need to cancel and subscribe again
- Use discount codes on subscriptions
- Offer a subscription on a product that has [product options](/docs/product-options)
- Skip a delivery on a customer's behalf (the customer does this themselves), or set a pause with an end date from the admin
- Get an automatic refund when you cancel — cancelling never refunds money already collected, so refund the order separately if needed
- Take subscriptions without a connected Stripe account
