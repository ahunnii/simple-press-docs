## Overview

The blog lets you publish articles, news, announcements, or any long-form writing on your storefront. Each post gets its own page at `/blog/[slug]`, and all published posts appear on a listing page at `/blog`. You can write a post now and **schedule** it to go live later (within about 15 minutes of the time you pick).

Owners and Managers can manage the blog. Staff can't open it.

<Callout type="info" title="Blog is off by default">
  Turn it on in **Settings → Features** (see [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags)). Until then it won't appear in your admin or on your storefront.
</Callout>

---

## The Blog Posts List

In the admin sidebar, go to **Content → Blog**. The page is titled **Blog Posts**, and each row shows the post's cover image, title, excerpt, and `/slug`, plus its **Published** date and **Status**.

![The Blog Posts list with search, Filters, and a Published date column](/images/blog/blog-list.png)

### Finding and sorting

- **Search** matches the title, the slug, and the excerpt. Click **Filters** to open the **Status** and **Sort** options.
- **Status** filters between **All posts**, **Published**, **Scheduled**, and **Drafts**.
- **Sort** offers **Recently published**, **Oldest published**, **Recently updated**, **Least recently updated**, **Title A–Z**, and **Title Z–A**.

### Row actions

Click a post's title to open it, or use the three-dot menu at the end of the row for **Edit**, **View on storefront** (published posts only, opens in a new tab), and **Delete**.

### Doing several at once

Tick the checkboxes on the left. A bar appears with:

- **Publish** and **Unpublish**
- **Delete**: only the store **Owner** sees this button. Managers don't.

Deleting asks you to confirm and is permanent.

## Creating a Blog Post

Click **Create Blog Post**. The editor has a toolbar across the top and two tabs: **Content** and **SEO**.

The toolbar has:

- A status label: **Unsaved Changes** or **Saved**
- The **Published** switch. **New posts start Published**, so switch it off before you save if you're not ready, or if you want to schedule it.
- A **More Options** menu: **View on storefront** (published posts only), **Reset** (throws away unsaved changes), and **Delete** (saved posts only)
- **Save & create another** (new posts only)
- **Save changes**

### Content Tab

The **Post Content** card has:

- **What is the post title?** _(required)_: the headline on the listing and post pages.
- **Publish date**: the date used to order your posts. "Leave blank to use the date the post was created."
- **Schedule publish**: a date and time. Only appears while **Published** is off (see [Scheduling a Post](#scheduling-a-post)).
- **Provide a short description of the post**: the excerpt. Shown on listing cards and used as the fallback meta description.
- **Upload an image that represents this post**: the post's cover image, shown on the listing card and at the top of the post. Choose **Upload from device**, or **Choose from library** if the [Media Library](/docs/media-library) is turned on.
- **Write out your post content**: the full post in the [text editor](/docs/text-editor).

Under the title you'll see the post's **Storefront URL** with an **Edit in SEO** link. The slug itself is on the SEO tab.

![The post editor Content tab with the post title, Storefront URL, Publish date, short description, cover image, and the text editor toolbar](/images/blog/post-content-tab.png)

*Schedule publish appears once the Published switch is turned off.*

### Rich-text tools

Posts get the full [text editor](/docs/text-editor): formatting, pictures, links and buttons, rows and columns, collapsible sections, tables, and video. The **Insert elements** menu can also add a **Gallery** (see [Galleries](/docs/galleries)), **Embed** (see [Embeds](/docs/embeds)), **Quote calculator**, or **Form** (see [Forms](/docs/forms)), but each only shows up when that feature is switched on in **Settings → Features**.

Blog posts don't have a **Page width** setting. That's only for custom pages (see [Pages & Policies](/docs/pages-and-policies#content-tab)).

### SEO Tab

- **Meta Tags**:
  - **URL Slug** _(required)_: the post's address, `yourstore.com/blog/[slug]`.
  - **Meta Title**: shown in browser tabs and search results. Falls back to the post title.
  - **Meta Description**: the search snippet. Falls back to the excerpt.
- **Open Graph Image**: the picture used when the post is shared on social media.
- **Search Result Preview** and **Social Media Preview**: how the post may look in Google and when shared.

![The post editor SEO tab with URL Slug, Meta Title, Meta Description, Open Graph Image and the two preview cards](/images/blog/post-seo-tab.png)

### How the URL slug works

- **Before the post is published or scheduled**, the slug follows the title as you type.
- **Once it's been published**, changing the title doesn't change the URL. The Content tab shows "Title changed — the URL hasn't." with an **Update URL** button.
- **Setting a schedule locks the slug**, because the post will go live on its own without you reopening the editor. The form says "the URL is locked from here on, so it won't follow further title edits."
- If you change the slug of a saved post, the SEO tab warns "Heads up — this will change the post's URL."

<Callout type="warn" title="Changing a URL breaks old links">
  SimplePress does **not** create a redirect. Anyone with the old link will land on a "not found" page, and navigation menu items won't follow the rename. Update them under **Site Setup → Navigation**.
</Callout>

Slugs must be unique across your **blog posts, pages, and policies**.

## Publishing, Drafts, and Scheduling

### The Published switch

- **Off (Draft)**: the post is hidden from the blog and from direct links. Owners and Managers can still preview it.
- **On (Published)**: the post is live.

### Scheduling a Post

1. Switch **Published** off.
2. Pick a date and time in **Schedule publish**.
3. Click **Save changes**.

The post shows as **Scheduled** in the list (use the **Scheduled** status filter to find them), and SimplePress publishes it automatically after that time. To cancel, clear the date.

Two things to know about the timing:

- **The time you pick is in the time zone of the computer or phone you're scheduling from.** It is not the **Time Zone** in your [General Settings](/docs/general-settings#time-zone). The note under the field ("Scheduled for ...") shows the time in your device's time zone.
- **It can go live up to about 15 minutes late.** SimplePress checks for scheduled posts about every 15 minutes, so a post set for 9:00 AM appears at the first check after 9:00.

### Publish date and ordering

Posts are shown **newest first by Publish date**. There's no drag-to-reorder. To move a post up or down, change its **Publish date**; backdating a post moves it further down the list.

## Editing in the Site Editor

You can also edit a post on your storefront preview in the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor): change its **Title**, **Excerpt**, and **Content** as drafts, and use **Open post settings** to jump back to the full editor for the URL, SEO, and cover image. The Site Editor also lets you reword the blog's headings and labels on templates that offer them.

## How the Blog Looks on the Storefront

The design adapts to your active [template](/docs/templates). On the **Default** template:

- **`/blog`** has a heading (**Blog** unless you change it) with a small **Journal** label above it, an intro line ("News, tips, and updates from our team. Use the search box to find a topic."), and a **Search posts...** box. The newest post is shown large, followed by a **More posts** grid, each with a "Read article →" link.
- **A post page** shows the hero image, the title, and the full content. At the bottom, up to three other posts appear under **Continue reading** and **More articles**.

## What You Can and Can't Do

**You can:**

- Create, edit, publish, unpublish, schedule, and delete posts
- Backdate or change a post's Publish date
- Change a post's URL slug (there's no redirect)
- Set SEO details per post
- Insert galleries and other elements into post content
- Edit post text in the Site Editor

**You can't:**

- Use a slug that another post, page, or policy already has
- Reorder posts by hand; they follow the Publish date

---

## Feature Flag

Blog is **off by default**. Go to **Settings → Features** and switch on **Blog**. Switching it off removes the blog from your admin and makes `/blog` show "not found"; your posts are kept and come back if you switch it on again.
