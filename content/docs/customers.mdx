## Overview

**Customers** is your address book for everyone who has bought from, or interacted with, your store. For each person you can see their orders, how much they've spent, and whether they've chosen to receive marketing emails. You can also keep private notes about them, adjust their rewards points, start an invoice, and anonymize their record when they ask to be forgotten.

You don't add customers by hand, and you can't edit their name, email, phone or addresses. Customers manage those details themselves from their own account on your storefront.

> **Who can do what:** Owners and Managers can edit notes, adjust points and anonymize customers. Staff can view the list and each customer's orders, but they can't see notes and they don't get the **Adjust points** button. See [Team and Roles](/docs/team-and-roles).

## The Customer List

Open **Customers** in your admin sidebar (`/admin/customers`).

![The Customers page with two summary cards, a search box, a Filters button and a table of customers with their orders, total spent, marketing status and join date](/images/customers/customers-list.png)
*The Customers list. The two cards at the top always show your whole store, even while you search or filter.*

### Summary cards

- **Total Customers**: everyone in your customer list.
- **All customers accepting marketing**: how many have opted in to marketing emails.

### The table

The list shows 25 customers per page.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** | Their name, with their email address underneath. A **Deletion requested** or **Anonymized** badge appears here when it applies. |
| **Orders** | How many orders they've placed. |
| **Total Spent** | Their lifetime spend across those orders. |
| **Marketing** | A **Subscribed** badge if they've opted in to marketing emails, otherwise "—". |
| **Joined** | The date their customer record was created. |
| **View** | Opens their detail page. |

### Search and filters

Type a name or email into **Search customers…** and press **Search**. Use **Filters** to narrow or reorder the list.

![The Filters popover open on the Customers page, showing Marketing, Privacy and Sort dropdowns](/images/customers/customers-filters.png)
*The Filters popover: Marketing, Privacy and Sort.*

| Filter | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Marketing** | All customers, Accepts marketing, Doesn't accept |
| **Privacy** | All customers, Deletion requested, Anonymized |
| **Sort** | Newest, Oldest, Name A–Z, Name Z–A, Most orders, Highest spend |

If nothing matches, you'll see "No customers match your filters" with a **Clear filters** button. A brand-new store shows "No customers yet".

## A Customer's Detail Page

Click **View** on any row. The top of the page shows the customer's name and a "Joined" date. The left side holds their order history, and cards down the right side hold everything else.

![A customer's detail page with the Customer and Summary cards, plus Rewards, Invoices, Notes and Privacy cards down the right](/images/customers/customer-detail.png)
*A customer's detail page. This customer hasn't ordered yet, so Order History is empty.*

### Order History

This reuses the same table you see on the Orders page: order number, items, total and status. Click an order to open it. If the customer hasn't ordered, you'll see "No orders yet. This customer hasn't placed any orders."

![A customer detail page with two orders listed under Order History and an Opted in marketing badge](/images/customers/customer-detail-orders.png)
*A customer with order history and marketing turned on.*

### The cards on the right

| Card | What it shows or lets you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** | Name, Email, Phone, and a **Marketing** badge reading **Opted in** or **Opted out**. |
| **Summary** | **Total Orders** and **Total Spent**. |
| **Rewards** | Their points balance and a history of points earned and spent. Owners and Managers get an **Adjust points** button. |
| **Invoices** | Their recent invoices, with a **New invoice** button. |
| **Notes** | Private notes about the customer. |
| **Privacy** | Deletion requests and the **Anonymize customer** button. |

### Rewards

The **Rewards** card shows how many points the customer has and what they earned or redeemed. To correct a balance, click **Adjust points**, enter the number of **Points** and a **Reason**, and save. Use a negative number to deduct points. You might do this to fix a mistake or to thank someone with a bonus.

The Rewards program itself is switched on in **Features**. See [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags).

### Invoices

The **Invoices** card lists the customer's latest invoices with their number, status, balance and due date. An **Overdue** badge appears on any that are past due. Click **New invoice** to start an invoice for this customer, or **View all invoices** to see everything on your Invoices page. If you've turned Invoices off and the customer has none, the card is hidden. See [Invoices](/docs/invoices).

### Notes

Use **Notes** to remember things about a customer: a preferred contact method, a special arrangement, how they like to be greeted. Click **Edit**, type under "Add notes about this customer...", then choose **Save**. A "Notes saved" message confirms it. **Cancel** discards your changes.

Notes are private. Customers never see them, and Staff can't see them either.

## Where Customers Come From

Customer records are created for you. A person is added to your list when they:

- place an order;
- have a manual order or an invoice created for them;
- leave a review or a testimonial;
- join your rewards program;
- save an address; or
- subscribe to something on your store.

> **Creating an account doesn't add someone to the list.** A shopper who signs up but never does any of the things above won't appear in Customers yet.

When a shopper creates an account or verifies their email, SimplePress looks for an existing customer record with the same email and links it to their account. If they ordered as a guest earlier, those orders are already attached to them, so there's nothing for you to do.

Customer Accounts is always on and managed by SimplePress. You don't need to switch it on.

## What Customers See on Your Storefront

Your customers have their own account area on your site (`/account`) where they sign in to manage their own details.

![A storefront account page called Preferences, showing the account menu, a Marketing Emails switch and a Data and Privacy card](/images/customers/account-preferences.png)
*A customer's Preferences page: the Marketing Emails switch and the Data & Privacy card.*

The account menu has these pages:

| Page | What the customer does there |
| --- | --- |
| **Orders** | Review past orders and their status. |
| **Address Book** | Add, edit, remove and choose default addresses. |
| **Subscriptions** | Manage subscriptions, if you offer them. |
| **Invoices** | View and pay invoices, if you use them. |
| **Rewards** | See their points, if you run a rewards program. |
| **Settings** | Update their name, email and password. |
| **Security** | Manage sign-in options. |
| **Preferences** | Choose marketing emails, download their data, or ask for deletion. |

Some of these only appear when the matching feature is on for your store.

On a phone, the account menu becomes a row of tabs across the top.

## Marketing Emails

Marketing is always the customer's choice. You can see each customer's status, but you can't change it for them.

- Customers **opt in** by turning on the **Marketing Emails** switch on their **Preferences** page. It reads **Subscribed** when on and **Unsubscribed** when off.
- It's **off by default**. Checking out doesn't sign anyone up.
- The switch only works once the customer has a customer record. A brand-new account with no record yet sees "Place your first order to enable email preferences."
- Clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of one of your emails turns it off. So does anonymizing the customer.

Customers who are opted in show a **Subscribed** badge on the list, and they're the ones who receive broadcasts from **Email Marketing**. That feature is off by default; turn it on in **Features**. See [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags).

## Privacy Requests

Shoppers can handle two privacy tasks themselves, under **Data & Privacy** on their Preferences page:

- **Download my data** gives them a copy of their personal data.
- **Request account deletion** flags their record for you.

When a customer asks to be deleted, a **Deletion Requested** alert appears on their detail page, and a **Deletion requested** badge shows next to their name on the list. You can find all of them with **Filters → Privacy → Deletion requested**.

### Anonymize a customer

To act on a request, open the customer and use the **Privacy** card.

1. Click **Anonymize customer**.
2. In the "Anonymize this customer?" box, read the warning and click **Anonymize**.

Anonymizing scrubs the customer's name, email, phone and saved addresses and removes their personal details from their reviews and testimonials. Their orders stay in your records, anonymized, so your sales and tax history remain complete. Afterwards the card says "Anonymized on" followed by the date.

> **Anonymizing can't be undone.** There's no way to bring back the customer's details once you confirm, so check that you've picked the right person.

There is no **Delete** button. Anonymizing is how you remove someone's personal information while keeping your order history intact.

## Exporting Your Customers

There's no export button on the Customers page, and you can't tag customers. Your customer information is included in **Settings → Data & Export**, which downloads your whole site as one ZIP file. Customers appear there as spreadsheets. See [Data & Export](/docs/data-and-export).

## What You Can't Do

- **Edit names, emails, phone numbers or addresses.** Customers update these themselves.
- **Change someone's marketing choice.** Only the customer can.
- **Delete a customer.** Use **Anonymize customer** instead.
- **Merge duplicates.** If the same person used two email addresses, they stay as two customers.
- **Create a customer by hand.** Start an order or an invoice instead and the record is created for you.
