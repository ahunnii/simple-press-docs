Before we get started, you need to make sure you have the following:

- **A referral from someone at the Center for Generative Justice / Artisanal Futures** (e.g. your friend, family member, or colleague).
- **A domain address** (e.g. `coolbusiness.com`). Your site cannot be finalized until your domain is connected to your storefront, and you'll need it connected before you start selling.
- **A Stripe account** ([stripe.com](https://stripe.com/)). Stripe is how customers pay by card when they check out on your shop. Wallets like Cash App Pay, Apple Pay, and Google Pay also come through Stripe, so a Stripe account is still required for those. Some other ways to get paid don't need Stripe: [invoices](/docs/invoices) can be paid by bank transfer, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, cash, or check, and [donations](/docs/donations) can take Venmo and Cash App even without Stripe connected.
- **A fulfillment method.** SimplePress doesn't buy shipping labels for you. You will need to get shipping labels on your own and add the tracking information to each order so your customers are notified. We recommend [PirateShip](https://pirateship.com/) for discounted UPS and USPS labels, with no extra fees—just the cost of the label. If you have a physical location, you can also offer in-store pickup at checkout (see [Shipping](/docs/shipping)).
- **Two email addresses:**
  - One public email address for customer contact (this will be publicly available).
  - One for order and site notifications (can be the same as above, if preferred).
- **Site materials:** Your logo, images, copy/text for your site, and an idea of the pages you want on your storefront.

## Meeting

To begin, we set up a meeting with you to discuss your needs and goals for your business.

We offer two different options that we would discuss: SimplePress or WordPress hosting. SimplePress is minimal. Minimal features, minimal customization, minimal business. WordPress is more for those who want to manage every little detail of their site.

For the sake of simplicity, we will focus on SimplePress in this guide.

## Sandbox Site

Once we talk about everything we need to, then we get started setting up your sandbox site. This would have temporary credentials to access the site, Stripe that you can play around with payments, and overall just get a sense for what SimplePress is all about.

If you have a website already, we like to try and get all your content up onto the sandbox site so you can do a comparison.

Usually we build you your own custom template: send us your current site, logo, photos, colors, and examples of sites you like, and we recreate or design your site as a template only your store can use. While that's in the works, you can start with one of our ready-made templates. See [Templates](/docs/templates) for more.

Once we have the sandbox site set up for you, then we send you your temporary credentials to access the site. We then move on to our 'testing phase'.

## Testing Phase

Because we are a super small team, we want to make sure you are comfortable with the platform before we move on to the final step. So once you get your sandbox site, we are giving you **one week** to play around with it.

<Callout type="info" title="Super Important">

Please know that this free to use platform is still in development. We at the Center for Generative Justice / Artisanal Futures operate with a very small development team (pretty much just me :D). By using SimplePress, you acknowledge our terms and conditions. Your feedback shapes the platform and can benefit other business owners using SP. We will do our absolute best to resolve any issues in a timely manner. Any feature requests, cosmetic adjustments, and low risk issues will be triaged based on our current work load with all of our clients and ongoing projects. Please contact us at [help@generativejustice.org](mailto:help@generativejustice.org) for any questions. I do my best to respond within 24 hours, but if I don’t, please send a follow up email. Thanks for your understanding and for your assistance in making SimplePress the greatest resource for small business owners!

</Callout>

During this time, please verify that the 'must have' features for your business are present and working as expected. Verify that your website looks good and that you are comfortable with the platform.

We want to try and resolve any issues or questions you have during this time.

When you log in, you'll land on the **Setup Guide**: a short checklist (connect Stripe, connect your domain, add a product, and make the storefront yours) to finish before you can start selling. The [Launch checklist](/docs/launch-checklist) walks through each step, plus a few final checks before you go live.

![The Setup Guide page, showing Setup Progress and each step marked done or still to do](/images/launch-checklist/welcome.png)

*The Setup Guide shows what's done and what's left.*

## Next Steps

Once we have verified that the 'must have' features are working as expected, and you are comfortable with the platform, then we move on to the final step. We get your domain and Stripe connected to your site.

Once both are ready, we do a final check-in meeting to go over the final details and get you set up with your site.

After that, you are ready to start selling!
