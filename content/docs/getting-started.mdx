Before we get started, you need to make sure you have the following:

- **A referral from someone at the Center for Generative Justice / Artisanal Futures** (e.g. your friend, family member, or colleague).
- **A domain address** (e.g. `coolbusiness.com`). Your site cannot be finalized until your domain is connected to your storefront, and you'll need it connected before you start selling.
- **A Stripe account** ([stripe.com](https://stripe.com/)). Stripe is how customers pay by card when they check out on your shop. Wallets like Cash App Pay, Apple Pay, and Google Pay also come through Stripe, so a Stripe account is still required for those. Some other ways to get paid don't need Stripe: [invoices](/docs/invoices) can be paid by bank transfer, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, cash, or check, and [donations](/docs/donations) can take Venmo and Cash App even without Stripe connected.
- **A fulfillment method.** SimplePress doesn't buy shipping labels for you. You will need to get shipping labels on your own and add the tracking information to each order so your customers are notified. We recommend [PirateShip](https://pirateship.com/) for discounted UPS and USPS labels, with no extra fees—just the cost of the label. If you have a physical location, you can also offer in-store pickup at checkout (see [Shipping](/docs/shipping)).
- **Two email addresses:**
  - One public email address for customer contact (this will be publicly available).
  - One for order and site notifications (can be the same as above, if preferred).
- **Site materials:** Your logo, images, copy/text for your site, and an idea of the pages you want on your storefront.

## Meeting

To begin, we set up a meeting with you to discuss your needs and goals for your business.

We offer two different options that we would discuss: SimplePress or WordPress hosting. SimplePress is minimal. Minimal features, minimal customization, minimal business. WordPress is more for those who want to manage every little detail of their site.

For the sake of simplicity, we will focus on SimplePress in this guide.

## Sandbox Site

Once we talk about everything we need to, then we get started setting up your sandbox site. This is where you can play around with payments in Stripe and get a sense for what SimplePress is all about.

If you have a website already, we like to try and get all your content up onto the sandbox site so you can do a comparison.

Usually we build you your own custom template: send us your current site, logo, photos, colors, and examples of sites you like, and we recreate or design your site as a template only your store can use. While that's in the works, you can start with one of our ready-made templates. See [Templates](/docs/templates) for more.

## Getting Into Your Store

Once your sandbox site is ready, we invite you to it as its **Owner**. You'll get an email from your store's name with the subject "You've been invited to join" followed by your store's name. It has an **Accept Invitation** button.

![The invitation email, headed "You've been invited to join Your Store", saying you've been invited as an Owner on SimplePress, with an Accept Invitation button](/images/getting-started/invite-email.png)

1. Click **Accept Invitation**. It opens on your store's own web address.
2. Click **Create Account** if you're new to SimplePress, or **Sign In** if you already have an account. **Use the same email address the invitation was sent to.**
3. If you're creating an account, tick the box to agree to SimplePress's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Then check your email and click the verification link. It expires after an hour.
4. Back on the invitation page, click **Accept Invitation**. You're taken to your store's admin.
5. The first time you open the admin, you'll see a screen titled **One thing before you continue**. As the Owner, tick the box to agree to the Seller & Merchant Agreement and Acceptable Use Policy, then click **Agree and continue**. You only see this once. See [Your Agreement with SimplePress](/docs/your-agreement).

The invitation lasts **14 days** and works once. If it says "This invite has expired", email [help@generativejustice.org](mailto:help@generativejustice.org) and we'll send you a new one. More detail, including what to do if you're signed in with the wrong email, is in [Team & Roles](/docs/team-and-roles#what-the-invited-person-does).

### If your site came from Artisanal Futures

If your site was set up through Artisanal Futures, you may get a link to a **Claim Your Site** page instead of an invitation.

1. Open the link. It shows your store's name and the email address the site is reserved for. That email can't be changed.
2. If you're new, fill in your name and a password. If you already have a SimplePress account with that email, enter your password.
3. Tick the box to agree to SimplePress's terms, then click **Create account & claim** or **Sign in & claim**.
4. If your email isn't verified yet, check your inbox and click the verification link. Then click **I've verified my email — continue**.

If you're already signed in with the right email, you just tick the box and click **Claim my site**.

Claim links last **14 days** and work once. If you see:

- **Link expired**: the link ran out before it was used. Contact Artisanal Futures support for a fresh one.
- **Already claimed**: the site already has an owner. If that was you, click **Sign in to your site**.
- **Invalid or expired link**: the link was mistyped, or the site is no longer available. Contact Artisanal Futures support to have it sent again.

![The Invalid or expired link card on the Claim Your Site page, asking you to contact Artisanal Futures support to have your claim link re-sent](/images/getting-started/claim-invalid.png)

Once you're in, we move on to our 'testing phase'.

## Testing Phase

Because we are a super small team, we want to make sure you are comfortable with the platform before we move on to the final step. So once you get your sandbox site, we are giving you **one week** to play around with it.

<Callout type="info" title="Super Important">

Please know that this free to use platform is still in development. We at the Center for Generative Justice / Artisanal Futures operate with a very small development team (pretty much just me :D). By using SimplePress, you agree to our terms. See [Your Agreement with SimplePress](/docs/your-agreement) for a plain-language summary. Your feedback shapes the platform and can benefit other business owners using SP. We will do our absolute best to resolve any issues in a timely manner. Any feature requests, cosmetic adjustments, and low risk issues will be triaged based on our current work load with all of our clients and ongoing projects. Please contact us at [help@generativejustice.org](mailto:help@generativejustice.org) for any questions. I do my best to respond within 24 hours, but if I don’t, please send a follow up email. Thanks for your understanding and for your assistance in making SimplePress the greatest resource for small business owners!

</Callout>

During this time, please verify that the 'must have' features for your business are present and working as expected. Verify that your website looks good and that you are comfortable with the platform.

We want to try and resolve any issues or questions you have during this time.

When you log in, you'll land on the **Setup Guide**: a short checklist (connect Stripe, connect your domain, add a product, and make the storefront yours) to finish before you can start selling. The [Launch checklist](/docs/launch-checklist) walks through each step, plus a few final checks before you go live.

![The Setup Guide page, showing Setup Progress and each step marked done or still to do](/images/launch-checklist/welcome.png)

*The Setup Guide shows what's done and what's left.*

## Next Steps

Once we have verified that the 'must have' features are working as expected, and you are comfortable with the platform, then we move on to the final step. We get your domain and Stripe connected to your site.

Once both are ready, we do a final check-in meeting to go over the final details and get you set up with your site.

After that, you are ready to start selling!
