## Overview

New to SimplePress? This page explains the words you'll run into in your admin and in these guides. Each term links to the page that covers it in more detail.

---

## A

### Abandoned checkout email

An automatic email sent to a shopper who gave you their email address during checkout but didn't finish buying. It's off until you switch it on, and you can edit its wording. See [Email Marketing](/docs/email-marketing#abandoned-checkout-emails).

### Admin

The private control panel where you run your store: products, orders, customers, settings, and more. Your customers never see it. It's the screens with the sidebar on the left, and the [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard) is its home page.

---

## B

### Back-in-stock alert

A "notify me" form on a sold-out product. A shopper enters their email, and SimplePress sends them one email when the item can be bought again. See [Wishlist & Back-in-Stock Alerts](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock).

### Banner / Popup

Two ways to get a message in front of every visitor. The **banner** is a thin bar across the top of your site. The **popup** is a window that appears over your homepage. Switch them on under **Settings → Features**, then set them up and show or hide them on one page. See [Banner and Popup](/docs/banner-and-popup).

### Broadcast (email marketing)

A one-off email you write once and send to every customer who opted in to marketing, such as an announcement or a newsletter. See [Email Marketing](/docs/email-marketing).

---

## C

### Collection

A group of products with its own page on your storefront, such as "Summer Drop" or "Gifts under $25". A product can belong to more than one collection. See [Collections](/docs/collections).

### Command palette

The search box at the top of every admin page (or `Cmd + K` / `Ctrl + K`). Use it to jump to any page, start a common task, or look up an order, customer, or product. See [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard#finding-things-fast).

### Coupon / discount

A code your customers enter at checkout to get money off their order. See [Discounts and Coupons](/docs/discounts-and-coupons).

### Custom domain

Your own web address, like `myshop.com`, connected to your store. Your subdomain keeps working after you add one. See [Domain](/docs/domain).

### Custom template

A storefront design built specially for one business, rather than shared with others. You'll only see the templates assigned to your store. See [Templates](/docs/templates).

### Customer account

A login that lets a shopper see their past orders, save addresses, and manage subscriptions. Shoppers can also check out as guests without one. See [What Your Customers See](/docs/for-your-customers).

---

## D

### Dispute (chargeback)

When a customer asks their bank to reverse a card payment. Stripe holds the money while you respond with evidence. See [Disputes](/docs/payments-and-payouts#disputes).

### Donation

A one-time gift or tip a shopper sends through your store's donate page, by card through Stripe or through Venmo or Cash App. You can call it Donate, Tip or Support. See [Donations & tips](/docs/donations).

### Draft vs. published

In the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor#saving-and-publishing), your changes save automatically as a private **draft** that only you and your team can see. They reach your customers only when you click **Publish**, and then your site is **published**. **Discard** throws the draft away and goes back to what's live.

### Dropdown

A menu item with sub-items. Visitors hover over it or click it to see a list of links underneath. You can add one level of sub-items to any item in your menu. See [Navigation](/docs/navigation#dropdowns-sub-items).

---

## E

### Embed

Outside content, such as a booking calendar or a map, shown inside a page, a blog post, or a service page. You paste a web address or an embed code, and it can show in the page or behind a button that opens it in a dialog. Needs the **Embeds** feature. See [Embeds](/docs/embeds).

### Event

A market, pop-up, class, or other dated happening you list on your site's Events page, with a flier, date, place, and an optional link. Needs the **Events** feature. See [Events](/docs/events).

---

## F

### FAQ

Your list of frequently asked questions and answers, written under **Site Setup → FAQ**. They appear on your FAQ page, and some templates can show a few of them in other sections. See [FAQ](/docs/faq).

### Feature switch

An on/off switch under **Settings → Features** that adds a part of the store, like Blog or Wishlist, or removes it from your admin and storefront. Turning one off doesn't delete anything. See [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags).

### Finances

The admin page that shows money in from your orders, what Stripe took in fees, your recent payouts, sales tax collected and an INFORM Act gauge. See [Finances](/docs/finances).

### Footer quick links

The column of links in your storefront footer. Set them under **Site Setup → Navigation**, either to mirror your main menu's top-level links or as your own list of up to 12 links. See [Navigation](/docs/navigation#footer-quick-links).

### Form

A custom form you build, such as a catering inquiry or a sign-up sheet, and add to a page. Answers arrive as **entries** in your admin and by email. Needs the **Forms** feature. See [Forms](/docs/forms).

---

## I

### INFORM Act

A US law about verifying high-volume online sellers. Finances shows an early-warning gauge. See [INFORM Act compliance](/docs/finances#inform-act-compliance).

### Inventory item

Something you keep track of in stock that isn't necessarily a product you sell, such as supplies or rental equipment. Products can draw from an item's stock. See [Inventory Management](/docs/inventory-management).

### Invoice

A bill you build and send to a customer outside the normal checkout, for services, wholesale, or a custom arrangement. See [Invoices](/docs/invoices).

---

## M

### Maintenance mode / Coming soon

A setting that shows visitors a maintenance or coming-soon screen instead of your storefront while you get ready to launch. Find it under **Settings → Maintenance Mode**. See [Launch Checklist](/docs/launch-checklist).

### Media Library

Every photo and video uploaded to your store, in one place, so you can find, download, reuse, and clean them up. See [Media Library](/docs/media-library).

---

## N

### Navigation / menu

The links in your header: the menu across the top of your site, and the tap-open menu on phones. You build it, along with your footer quick links, under **Site Setup → Navigation**. See [Navigation](/docs/navigation).

---

## O

### Order / fulfillment

An **order** is created when a customer completes checkout (or when you add one by hand). **Fulfillment** is getting it to them: shipping it (with or without tracking), or marking it ready for pickup. See [Orders](/docs/orders).

---

## P

### Payout

Money Stripe sends from your Stripe balance to your bank account. Payments from customers sit as pending, then become available, then are paid out. See [Payments and Payouts](/docs/payments-and-payouts).

### Points / Rewards program

The loyalty program in which customers earn points on orders, birthdays, and social follows, then trade the points for a discount code. See [Rewards Program](/docs/rewards).

### Product

Something you sell, with a name, price, photos, and description. See [Products](/docs/products).

---

## Q

### Quote calculator

A step-by-step price questionnaire you place on a page of your site. Visitors answer a few questions and leave their contact details, and each one becomes a request in your Quotes inbox. See [Quote Calculators](/docs/quote-calculators).

---

## R

### Reward code

A single-use discount code a customer gets when they trade points for a reward. They enter it at checkout like any other code. See [Rewards Program](/docs/rewards).

---

## S

### Search readiness score

The score on the **SEO & Meta** page's **Score** tab, also shown as a strip on your Dashboard, that counts what's done and what's left for search engines and shared links. See [SEO & Search Readiness](/docs/seo).

### Service

A service page on your storefront, such as Facial Treatments, that lists what you offer. SimplePress doesn't schedule appointments or take payment for services, so customers book through an outside booking tool you link or embed. See [Services](/docs/services).

### Service item (specific service)

One option inside a service, such as a 60-minute facial, with its own price and duration. A service can hold several of them. See [Services](/docs/services).

### Setup Guide

A checklist page, linked from your admin sidebar, that walks you through getting your store ready: connecting payments, a custom domain, adding products, and customizing your storefront. It also has links to this help guide and to contact support. See [Launch Checklist](/docs/launch-checklist).

### Site Editor

The full-screen editor where you change your storefront's words, pictures, and sections while watching a live preview. See [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor).

### Site Setup

The admin area under **Content** where you set up things you configure once and rarely change. Its cards are **Brand & Appearance** (template, logo, favicon, social links), **Policies**, **Navigation**, **SEO & Meta**, **FAQ** (see [FAQ](/docs/faq)), and **Banner & Popup**. See [Brand & Appearance](/docs/storefront-and-branding).

### Storefront

The public website your customers see and shop on, as opposed to the admin. See [Storefront and Branding](/docs/storefront-and-branding).

### Stripe / Stripe Connect

Stripe is the payment company that processes your customers' card payments. **Connecting** your Stripe account to SimplePress is what lets customers pay you, and your money goes to your Stripe account, then to your bank. See [Stripe](/docs/stripe).

### Subdomain

The free web address every store gets at signup, such as `yourstore.simplepress.co`. It's permanent and always works, even after you add a custom domain. See [Domain](/docs/domain).

### Subscription

A product customers receive and pay for automatically on a repeating schedule, like every month. Each charge shows up as a normal order. See [Subscriptions](/docs/subscriptions).

---

## T

### Team member / role

Someone you've invited to your admin. Each person is an **Owner**, **Manager**, or **Staff**, which decides what they can see and do. See [Team and Roles](/docs/team-and-roles).

### Template

The overall design and layout of your storefront. Switching templates doesn't delete your products, pages, or settings. See [Templates](/docs/templates).

### Template fields

The named content slots inside your template, such as a homepage headline or an About photo. You edit them in the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor), not in a separate screen. [Template Fields](/docs/template-fields) explains what each field type accepts.

---

## V

### Variant

One version of a product, such as a size or color, each with its own price and stock if you want. See [Products](/docs/products).

### Videos

YouTube videos shown on your site's Videos page, added one by one or pulled in automatically from your channels and playlists. Needs the **Videos** feature. See [Videos](/docs/videos).

---

## W

### Wishlist

A heart icon on your products that lets shoppers save items for later. Saved products appear on a Wishlist page for each shopper. See [Wishlist & Back-in-Stock Alerts](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock).
