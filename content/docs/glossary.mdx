## Overview

New to SimplePress? This page explains the words you'll run into in your admin and in these guides. Each term links to the page that covers it in more detail.

---

## A

### Abandoned checkout email

An automatic email sent to a shopper who gave you their email address during checkout but didn't finish buying. It's off until you switch it on, and you can edit its wording. It carries an **Unsubscribe** link and your business mailing address, so it only sends once you've added a full address in **Settings → General**, and it isn't sent to anyone who has unsubscribed. See [Email Marketing](/docs/email-marketing#abandoned-checkout-emails).

### Admin

The private control panel where you run your store: products, orders, customers, settings, and more. Your customers never see it. It's the screens with the sidebar on the left, and the [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard) is its home page.

### Agreement (Seller & Merchant Agreement)

The terms every Owner accepts once, on the **One thing before you continue** screen, alongside the Acceptable Use Policy. It sets out what you're responsible for (taxes, refunds, your products and content) and what SimplePress is. See [Your Agreement with SimplePress](/docs/your-agreement).

### Audience (email marketing)

The group of people a campaign goes to. It always starts from customers who opted in to marketing emails, and you can narrow it to **All subscribers**, **Customers who've ordered**, **Subscribers who haven't ordered**, people who **bought specific products**, people who **haven't ordered in** a set number of days, or people who **signed up in the last** few days. The audience is worked out when the email goes out. See [Choosing who gets it](/docs/email-marketing#choosing-who-gets-it).

---

## B

### Back-in-stock alert

A "notify me" form on a sold-out product. A shopper enters their email, and SimplePress sends them one email when the item can be bought again. See [Wishlist & Back-in-Stock Alerts](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock).

### Banner / Popup

Two ways to get a message in front of every visitor. The **banner** is a thin bar across the top of your site. The **popup** is a window that appears over your homepage. Switch them on under **Settings → Features**, then set them up and show or hide them on one page. See [Banner and Popup](/docs/banner-and-popup).

### Business hours

The days and times you're open, shown on your contact page on most templates and shared with search engines if you choose. Set them under **Settings → Business Hours**. See [Business Hours](/docs/business-hours).

---

## C

### Campaign (email marketing)

A one-off email you write once and send to customers who opted in to marketing, such as an announcement or a newsletter. You can send it to everyone on your list or to a group, now or on a schedule. Every campaign you send is kept in a history with the results for each person. See [Email Marketing](/docs/email-marketing).

### Closer prices

Lower local delivery fees for customers who live nearest to you. You set up to three rings inside your delivery radius, such as "$3.00 within 10 miles", and anyone farther out pays the main delivery fee. See [Shipping](/docs/shipping#local-delivery).

### Collapsible section

A block of text with a title that visitors click to open and close, handy for FAQs and long details. You add one from the **Insert elements** menu in the text editor. On your storefront it always starts closed. See [Text Editor](/docs/text-editor#collapsible-sections).

### Collection

A group of products with its own page on your storefront, such as "Summer Drop" or "Gifts under $25". A product can belong to more than one collection. See [Collections](/docs/collections).

### Color swatches

The exact color dot shown for each color of a product, such as "Hunter Green" or "Rose Gold". You set them in the variant section of the product editor. Leave them on **Auto** and SimplePress matches common color names for you. Only templates that show color dots for variants use them. See [Products](/docs/products#color-swatches).

### Command palette

The search box at the top of every admin page (or `Cmd + K` / `Ctrl + K`). Use it to jump to any page, start a common task, or look up an order, customer, or product. See [Finding Your Way Around the Admin](/docs/admin-basics#searching-and-jumping-around).

### Consent history

The log of when a customer joined or left your marketing emails, how it happened (checkout, a sign-up form, their account, an email link, or you), and when their emails were stopped because of a bounce or spam report. You see the latest ten entries under **Recent activity** on the customer's **Email marketing** card. It starts when the feature does, so people who subscribed earlier start empty, and it isn't included in your exports. See [Customers](/docs/customers) and [Email Marketing](/docs/email-marketing).

### Coupon / discount

A code your customers enter at checkout to get money off their order. A use of a limited code is held as soon as the shopper goes to pay, and given back if they don't finish. See [Discounts and Coupons](/docs/discounts-and-coupons).

### Custom domain

Your own web address, like `myshop.com`, connected to your store. Once it's active, visitors to your old subdomain are sent on to it. See [Domain](/docs/domain).

### Custom template

A storefront design built specially for one business, rather than shared with others. You'll only see the templates assigned to your store. See [Templates](/docs/templates).

### Customer account

A login that lets a shopper see their past orders, save addresses, and manage subscriptions. Shoppers can also check out as guests without one. See [What Your Customers See](/docs/for-your-customers).

---

## D

### Delayed payment method

A way to pay that settles days later instead of right away, such as a bank debit. SimplePress supports these. The shopper sees a processing page, and the order appears once the money clears. See [Payments & Payouts](/docs/payments-and-payouts#bank-debits-and-other-delayed-payment-methods).

### Dispute (chargeback)

When a customer asks their bank to reverse a card payment. Stripe holds the money while you respond with evidence. See [Disputes](/docs/payments-and-payouts#disputes).

### Do-not-email list

SimplePress's memory of addresses that must not get your marketing emails or abandoned checkout reminders, such as people who unsubscribed or whose address bounced. It's kept even if you anonymize the customer or they sign up again with a new record, so a stray email can't go out to them. See [Email Marketing](/docs/email-marketing) and [Customers](/docs/customers).

### Donation

A one-time gift or tip a shopper sends through your store's donate page, by card through Stripe or through Venmo or Cash App. You can call it Donate, Tip or Support. See [Donations & tips](/docs/donations).

### Double opt-in

A newsletter sign-up that needs a second step. When someone fills in a sign-up form on your site, SimplePress emails them a link, and they're only subscribed after they press **Confirm subscription**. Checking the box at checkout or turning on the switch in their account subscribes them straight away. See [Email Marketing](/docs/email-marketing).

### Draft vs. published

In the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor#saving-and-publishing), your changes save automatically as a private **draft** that only you and your team can see. They reach your customers only when you click **Publish**, and then your site is **published**. **Discard** throws the draft away and goes back to what's live.

### Dropdown

A menu item with sub-items. Visitors hover over it or click it to see a list of links underneath. You can add one level of sub-items to any item in your menu. See [Navigation](/docs/navigation#dropdowns-sub-items).

---

## E

### Email style (email marketing)

The look of your marketing emails: accent color, page background, font, header, social links and footer note. Each template has its own default look, and you can change it under **Email Marketing → Email style**. It only affects campaigns, not receipts or other order emails. See [Your email style](/docs/email-marketing#your-email-style).

### Embed

Outside content, such as a booking calendar or a map, shown inside a page, a blog post, or a service page. You paste a web address or an embed code, and it can show in the page or behind a button that opens it in a dialog. Needs the **Embeds** feature. See [Embeds](/docs/embeds).

### Event

A market, pop-up, class, or other dated happening you list on your site's Events page, with a flier, date, place, and an optional link. Needs the **Events** feature. See [Events](/docs/events).

---

## F

### FAQ

Your list of frequently asked questions and answers, written under **Site Setup → FAQ**. They appear on your FAQ page, and some templates can show a few of them in other sections. See [FAQ](/docs/faq).

### Feature switch

An on/off switch under **Settings → Features** that adds a part of the store, like Blog or Wishlist, or removes it from your admin and storefront. Turning one off doesn't delete anything. See [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags).

### Finances

The admin page that shows money in from your orders, what Stripe took in fees, your recent payouts, sales tax collected and an INFORM Act gauge. See [Finances](/docs/finances).

### Footer quick links

The column of links in your storefront footer. Set them under **Site Setup → Navigation**, either to mirror your main menu's top-level links or as your own list of up to 12 links. See [Navigation](/docs/navigation#footer-quick-links).

### Form

A custom form you build, such as a catering inquiry or a sign-up sheet, and add to a page. Answers arrive as **entries** in your admin and by email. Needs the **Forms** feature. See [Forms](/docs/forms).

### Form 1099-K

A US tax form that reports the card payments you received in a year. It comes from Stripe, not SimplePress. See [Weekly, Monthly & Yearly Checklist](/docs/owner-routines).

---

## I

### INFORM Act

A US law about verifying high-volume online sellers. Finances shows an early-warning gauge. See [INFORM Act compliance](/docs/finances#inform-act-compliance).

### Inventory item

Something you keep track of in stock that isn't necessarily a product you sell, such as supplies or rental equipment. Products can draw from an item's stock. See [Inventory Management](/docs/inventory-management).

### Invoice

A bill you build and send to a customer outside the normal checkout, for services, wholesale, or a custom arrangement. See [Invoices](/docs/invoices).

---

## L

### Local delivery

Delivery you do yourself to customers who live within a set number of miles of your ZIP code. Shoppers choose it at checkout next to shipping and pickup when their address is inside your radius. You turn it on under **Settings → Shipping**. See [Shipping](/docs/shipping#local-delivery).

---

## M

### Maintenance mode / Coming soon

A setting that shows visitors a maintenance or coming-soon screen instead of your storefront while you get ready to launch. Find it under **Settings → Maintenance Mode**. See [Maintenance Mode & Coming Soon](/docs/maintenance-mode).

### Media Library

Every photo, video, and PDF uploaded to your store, in one place, so you can find, download, reuse, and clean them up. See [Media Library](/docs/media-library).

---

## N

### Navigation / menu

The links in your header: the menu across the top of your site, and the tap-open menu on phones. You build it, along with your footer quick links, under **Site Setup → Navigation**. See [Navigation](/docs/navigation).

---

## O

### Order / fulfillment

An **order** is created when a customer completes checkout (or when you add one by hand). **Fulfillment** is getting it to them: shipping it (with or without tracking), or marking it ready for pickup. See [Orders](/docs/orders). To buy a label, see [Shipping labels with Pirate Ship](/docs/shipping-labels).

### Owner reply

A public response from your store under a customer's product review, written by an Owner or Manager. It shows under the review on your product page as a response from your store name. The reviewer isn't emailed. See [Reviews](/docs/reviews#replying-to-a-review).

---

## P

### Page width

A setting on each custom page: **Standard** keeps the page in your template's usual reading column, and **Wide** uses the full content width. Blog posts and policies don't have it. See [Pages & Policies](/docs/pages-and-policies#content-tab).

### Payout

Money Stripe sends from your Stripe balance to your bank account. Payments from customers sit as pending, then become available, then are paid out. See [Payments and Payouts](/docs/payments-and-payouts).

### Platform admin

A member of the SimplePress team. Platform admins can open any store's admin to set it up, help with support, or fix problems. See [Who at SimplePress can see your store](/docs/leaving-or-closing#who-at-simplepress-can-see-your-store).

### Points / Rewards program

The loyalty program in which customers earn points on orders, birthdays, and social follows, then trade the points for a discount code. See [Rewards Program](/docs/rewards).

### Product

Something you sell, with a name, price, photos, and description. See [Products](/docs/products).

### Product options

Choices a customer makes on a product before adding it to the cart: pick a color or charms from a list (which can add to the price), or type some text such as an engraving. Not the same as a [variant](#variant). Needs the **Product options & personalization** feature. See [Product Options](/docs/product-options).

---

## Q

### Quote calculator

A step-by-step price questionnaire you place on a page of your site. Visitors answer a few questions and leave their contact details, and each one becomes a request in your Quotes inbox. See [Quote Calculators](/docs/quote-calculators).

---

## R

### reCAPTCHA

An invisible check on sign-in, sign-up, password reset, and some forms that tells people apart from spam bots. Ad blockers and strict privacy settings can sometimes block it. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).

### Reward code

A single-use discount code a customer gets when they trade points for a reward. They enter it at checkout like any other code. See [Rewards Program](/docs/rewards).

### Row (columns)

A block in the text editor that puts your text, pictures, or buttons side by side in two, three, or four columns. On phones, the columns stack into one. See [Text Editor](/docs/text-editor#rows-and-columns).

---

## S

### Scheduled campaign

An email campaign you've set to go out later, 10 minutes to 90 days from now, in your store's time zone. It sends within about 15 minutes of the time you chose and is locked until it sends. To change it, click **Unschedule to edit**, which moves it back to your drafts. See [Scheduling a campaign](/docs/email-marketing#scheduling-a-campaign).

### Search readiness score

The score on the **SEO & Meta** page's **Score** tab, also shown as a strip on your Dashboard, that counts what's done and what's left for search engines and shared links. See [SEO & Search Readiness](/docs/seo).

### Service

A service page on your storefront, such as Facial Treatments, that lists what you offer. SimplePress doesn't schedule appointments or take payment for services, so customers book through an outside booking tool you link or embed. See [Services](/docs/services).

### Service item (specific service)

One option inside a service, such as a 60-minute facial, with its own price and duration. A service can hold several of them. See [Services](/docs/services).

### Setup Guide

A checklist page, linked from your admin sidebar, that walks you through getting your store ready. The steps depend on your store's kind of business (selling products, services, informational, or donations) and each one is marked Required or Optional. Typical steps are a custom domain, Stripe, a first product or service, sales tax, shipping or pickup, contact info, policies, and customizing your storefront. It also has links to this help guide and to contact support. See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started#your-setup-guide) and the [Launch Checklist](/docs/launch-checklist).

### Shipping label

The postage label you stick on a package. SimplePress doesn't sell labels; you buy them from a carrier or Pirate Ship and add the tracking number to the order. See [Shipping labels with Pirate Ship](/docs/shipping-labels).

### Site Editor

The full-screen editor where you change your storefront's words, pictures, and sections while watching a live preview. See [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor).

### Site Setup

The admin area under **Content** where you set up things you configure once and rarely change. Its cards are **Brand & Appearance** (template, logo, favicon, social links), **Policies**, **Navigation**, **SEO & Meta**, **FAQ** (see [FAQ](/docs/faq)), and **Banner & Popup**. See [Brand & Appearance](/docs/storefront-and-branding).

### Space (text editor)

An empty gap you add between blocks from the **Insert elements** menu in the text editor, sized Small, Medium, or Large. It shows as a striped box while you edit and as plain space on your site. See [Text Editor](/docs/text-editor#adding-space).

### Storefront

The public website your customers see and shop on, as opposed to the admin. See [Storefront and Branding](/docs/storefront-and-branding).

### Stripe / Stripe Connect

Stripe is the payment company that processes your customers' card payments. **Connecting** your Stripe account to SimplePress is what lets customers pay you, and your money goes to your Stripe account, then to your bank. See [Stripe](/docs/stripe).

### Style panel

The **Style** panel in the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor#style), under **Design**. It holds the settings that affect your whole site: color palettes and font pairings (on templates that offer them) and the **Text width on wide pages** choice.

### Subdomain

The free web address every store gets at signup, such as `yourstore.simplepress.co`. You can build and test on it, but you need your own custom domain before you start selling. Once a custom domain is active, visitors to the subdomain are sent on to it. See [Domain](/docs/domain).

### Subscription

A product customers receive and pay for automatically on a repeating schedule, like every month. Each charge shows up as a normal order. See [Subscriptions](/docs/subscriptions).

### Suppressed (email marketing)

A customer you no longer email because their address bounced, they marked one of your emails as spam, or the email provider blocked the address. It happens automatically, and they show an orange **Suppressed** badge in the Customers list and on their **Email marketing** card. Order emails like receipts still go out. See [Bounces and Spam Reports](/docs/email-marketing#bounces-and-spam-reports).

### Suspended / closed store

A store SimplePress has paused (suspended) or shut down (closed). Its storefront and admin stop working for everyone except the SimplePress team. Your Stripe account and domain stay yours. See [If Your Store Is Paused or Closed](/docs/leaving-or-closing).

---

## T

### Team member / role

Someone you've invited to your admin. Each person is an **Owner**, **Manager**, or **Staff**, which decides what they can see and do. See [Team and Roles](/docs/team-and-roles).

### Template

The overall design and layout of your storefront. Switching templates doesn't delete your products, pages, or settings. See [Templates](/docs/templates).

### Template fields

The named content slots inside your template, such as a homepage headline or an About photo. You edit them in the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor), not in a separate screen. [Template Fields](/docs/template-fields) explains what each field type accepts.

### Text editor

The formatted-writing box used for page content, blog posts, and other longer text (also called rich text). It handles headings, pictures, links and buttons, rows and columns, and more. See [Text Editor](/docs/text-editor).

---

## V

### Variant

One version of a product, such as a size or color, each with its own price and stock if you want. Not the same as [product options](#product-options), which are choices a customer makes on top of a product. See [Products](/docs/products).

### Videos

YouTube videos shown on your site's Videos page, added one by one or pulled in automatically from your channels and playlists. Needs the **Videos** feature. See [Videos](/docs/videos).

---

## W

### Wishlist

A heart icon on your products that lets shoppers save items for later. Saved products appear on a Wishlist page for each shopper. See [Wishlist & Back-in-Stock Alerts](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock).
