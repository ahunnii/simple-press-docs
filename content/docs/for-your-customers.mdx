## Overview

Your customers will email you questions like "How do I track my order?", "How do I cancel my subscription?" or "Where did my wishlist go?" This page walks through your storefront as a shopper sees it: browsing, checkout, their account, and every email they can get from you. Use it to answer those questions and to learn what your store does for customers.

<Callout type="info" title="Your template sets the look, not the steps">
Each [template](/docs/templates) styles these pages its own way, so buttons and layouts on your store may look a little different from what's described here. The steps and features are the same on every template.
</Callout>

Some parts only show up when a feature is turned on under **Settings → Features**:

| Feature (as labelled in Settings → Features) | On by default? | What shoppers get                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Shopping Cart & Checkout**                 | Yes            | Add to Cart, the cart, and checkout                                               |
| **[Coupons & Discounts](/docs/discounts-and-coupons)**                      | No             | A discount code box at checkout                                                   |
| **[Wishlist](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock)**                                 | Yes            | Heart icons on products and a Wishlist page                                       |
| **[Back-in-Stock Notifications](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock)**              | Yes            | A "Notify me" form on sold-out products                                           |
| **Customer Accounts**                        | Yes            | Sign-up, sign-in, and the account area (only the platform team can turn this off) |
| **Product Subscriptions**                    | No             | A Subscribe option on products you set up for it                                  |
| **[Rewards Program](/docs/rewards)**                          | No             | A Rewards tab where shoppers earn and redeem points                               |
| **Invoices**                                 | No             | An Invoices tab in the account area                                               |
| **Donations**                                | No             | A Donate page                                                                     |
| **[Product Reviews](/docs/reviews)**                          | No             | Star ratings and reviews on product pages (writing one needs an account)          |

See [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags) for how to turn these on and off.

## Browsing and the Cart

Shoppers browse your shop, collections, and product pages, choose a variant (size, color, and so on) when there is one, and click **Add to Cart**. Some templates open a slide-out cart right away. All templates also have a full cart page at `/cart`.

In the cart, shoppers can:

- change quantities with the plus and minus buttons (they can't add more than you have in stock),
- remove items, and
- see an estimated total before going to checkout.

Some things to know about the cart:

- **It's saved in the shopper's browser, not their account.** If they leave and come back on the same phone or computer, the cart is still there. If they switch devices or clear their browser data, the cart starts out empty. This applies to signed-in shoppers too.
- **It keeps itself up to date.** If you change a price, the cart shows the new price. If an item sells out or you unpublish it, it's removed from the cart and the shopper sees a short message.
- **No buying when it's turned off.** If **Shopping Cart & Checkout** is off, your site works like a catalog. Your products and prices still show, but the Add to Cart button and the cart icon are hidden, and the cart page and checkout are turned off, so nobody can buy.

## Checkout

<video src="/videos/for-your-customers/checkout.webm" poster="/images/for-your-customers/checkout.png" controls muted playsInline className="rounded-lg border" />

A shopper going from product page to checkout.

Shoppers don't need an account to check out. Guest checkout is always available.

### Step 1: Your checkout page

The checkout page asks for:

- **Contact information:** email, full name, and phone number (all required).
- **Delivery:** if you offer in-store pickup, they choose **Ship to address** or **In-store pickup**. Pickup shows your pickup location and any instructions you've written, and there's no shipping charge.
- **Shipping address:** if they're shipping. Signed-in shoppers with saved addresses can pick one instead of typing it out.
- **Discount code:** only when **Coupons & Discounts** is on. They type a code and click **Apply**. If the code works, they see how much they saved. If it doesn't, they see a message explaining why (for example, it expired or the order is below the minimum). Only one code can be used per order. Rewards codes go in this same box. See [Discounts and Coupons](/docs/discounts-and-coupons).

The order summary shows the subtotal, any discount, shipping, and an **Estimated total**. Tax and the final total are worked out on the next step. A short notice under the button links to SimplePress's Terms of Service, and to your Terms of Service or Refund Policy page if you've published one.

![The checkout page with contact details, shipping address, discount code box, and order summary](/images/for-your-customers/checkout.png)

### Step 2: Secure payment with Stripe

When they click **Continue to Payment**, they go to a secure Stripe payment page with your store's name on it. There they enter their card, or use any other payment method you've turned on in your Stripe account. Tax is added there if you use automatic tax (see [Tax](/docs/tax)).

- **Items are held for 30 minutes.** Once a shopper reaches the payment page, the items they're buying are reserved so nobody else can buy the last one first. If they don't pay within 30 minutes, the hold ends and the items go back on sale.
- **If they click back,** they return to your checkout page with their cart unchanged.
- **If you haven't connected Stripe,** shoppers see a message that checkout isn't available yet instead of the checkout form. See [Stripe](/docs/stripe).

## After They Order

Once payment goes through, shoppers land on an **Order Confirmed!** page. It thanks them, lists what happens next, and shows the email address the confirmation was sent to. Their cart is cleared.

- You can edit the heading and the "What happens next?" list in the site editor.
- On many templates the page also has a **View my orders** button (for signed-in shoppers) or a **Create an account** button (for guests).

![The Order Confirmed page with the next-steps list](/images/for-your-customers/order-confirmed.png)

A few moments later they get the **Order Confirmation** email with their items, totals, and shipping address. The email has a **View Order Status** button they can use at any time, no account needed. From there:

- When you add a shipment with tracking, they get an **Order Shipped** email with the carrier, tracking number, and a tracking link.
- If you mark the order fulfilled without tracking, they get an **Order Fulfilled** email.
- For pickup orders, they get a **Ready for Pickup** email when you mark the order ready.

See [Orders](/docs/orders) for how to do each of these on your side.

## Customer Accounts

### Signing Up

Shoppers create an account from the sign-in or account link in your site header, from `/sign-up`, or from the **Create an account** button after checkout (on templates that have one). They enter:

- their name,
- email address,
- a password (at least 8 characters), and
- a checkbox agreeing to SimplePress's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

They then get a **Verify your email** message. They can't sign in until they click the link in it. The link works for one hour. They can resend it from the waiting screen, or by trying to sign in, which takes them back to that screen. Once they click the link, they're signed in.

<Callout type="info" title="One account, every SimplePress store">
A shopper's account is a SimplePress account, not one just for your store. If they already shop at another SimplePress store, they can sign in with the same email and password. That's also why the password reset email says "Reset your SimplePress password."
</Callout>

### Signing In and Forgotten Passwords

Shoppers sign in with their email and password. There's no Google sign-in and no "magic link" sign-in. If they've forgotten their password, they click **Forgot password**, enter their email, and get a reset link that works for one hour.

### Past Orders Join Their Account Automatically

When a shopper creates an account, any orders they placed as a guest **with the same email address** show up in their account straight away. Orders placed with a different email address won't show up.

### The Account Area

Once signed in, shoppers have an account area with these tabs. Tabs tied to a feature only appear when that feature is on.

| Tab              | Shown when                       | What the shopper can do                                                                                                          |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Orders**       | Orders is on                     | See every order with its number, date, status, and total. Open one to see items, totals, tracking links, and the shipping address |
| **Subscriptions**| Product Subscriptions is on      | See their subscriptions and the next delivery date, and open the manage page for each one                                       |
| **Invoices**     | Invoices is on                   | See invoices you've sent them, with status and balance due, and open each one                                                    |
| **Settings**     | Always                           | Update their name and profile picture                                                                                           |
| **Security**     | Always                           | Change their password and see where they're signed in                                                                           |
| **Address Book** | Always (some templates hide it while Checkout is off) | Add, edit, and delete shipping addresses, and choose a default to use at checkout                                               |
| **Preferences**  | Always                           | Turn your marketing emails on or off, download their data, or ask for their account to be deleted                               |
| **Rewards**      | Rewards Program is on            | See their points balance, ways to earn, rewards they can redeem, and their codes                                                |

![The account area with the tab list and the Orders tab showing a few past orders](/images/for-your-customers/account-orders.png)

A few details shoppers sometimes ask about:

- **No "reorder" button.** To buy the same thing again, they add it to the cart from your shop.
- **Order statuses** shown to shoppers are Open, Completed, Cancelled, and Refunded.
- **Marketing emails:** the only place a shopper opts in to your marketing emails is the **Preferences** tab, and only after they've placed at least one order. They can opt out at any time there, or with the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. You can see each customer's choice but can't change it. See [Customers](/docs/customers).
- **Deleting their data:** if a shopper clicks **Request account deletion** on the Preferences tab, the request shows up on that customer's page in your admin under **Privacy**. You finish the request by anonymizing the customer there. Their past orders stay in your records, anonymized, for tax and legal reasons.

## Looking Up an Order Without an Account

Guests can check on an order without signing in:

1. They click **View Order Status** in their confirmation email, or go to `/order-status` on your store.
2. On `/order-status`, they enter the email address and order number from their confirmation email.
3. They get an email with a secure link to the order.

The order status page shows the order, payment, and fulfillment status, the items, the shipping address, and any tracking links. Each link works for 90 days. If an old link has expired, the page offers to send a new one.

![The Check your order status form](/images/for-your-customers/order-status.png)

> **Tip:** Most templates don't link to `/order-status` in the menu. If guests often ask how to find their order, add an "Order status" link to your footer or navigation.

## Wishlist

When **[Wishlist](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock)** is on, a heart icon appears on your products. Tapping it saves the product, and tapping it again removes it. Saved items are listed on the `/wishlist` page, where shoppers can open a product or remove it.

- **The wishlist is saved in the shopper's browser on that device only.** It isn't tied to their account, so it won't appear on their phone if they saved items on their laptop, and clearing their browser data wipes it.
- You can't see your shoppers' wishlists.

![The Wishlist page with a few saved products](/images/for-your-customers/wishlist.png)

## Back-in-Stock Alerts

When **[Back-in-Stock Notifications](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock)** is on, a sold-out product shows a short "get notified when it's back" message with an email box and a **Notify me** button. If the product has variants, the alert is for the variant they picked. No account is needed.

When the item can be bought again, the shopper gets one **Back in stock** email with a link to the product. It's sent once, so if the item sells out again before they buy, they'll need to sign up again. These emails go out automatically on a regular schedule after you restock, not the moment you save. If you turn the feature off, waiting requests are kept and the emails resume if you turn it back on.

## Subscriptions

When **Product Subscriptions** is on and you've set up a product for it, a **Subscribe** option appears on that product's page. Shoppers choose how often they want it, fill in their details on a Subscribe page, and pay on a secure Stripe page. They don't need an account.

After that:

- They get a **Subscription confirmed** email, then an **Order Confirmation** email for every charge, including each renewal.
- **Every subscription email links to their subscription page**, usually with a **Manage your subscription** button (the payment-failed email says **Update payment method**). That page lets them skip the next delivery, pause, resume, update their card, or cancel. Cancelling takes effect right away.
- **If they've lost the email,** they can go to `/subscriptions/manage`, enter their email, and get fresh manage links. Manage links work for about six months.
- **Signed-in shoppers** also see their subscriptions on the **Subscriptions** tab in their account.

Even if you later turn Product Subscriptions off, customers can always cancel and update their card.

See [Subscriptions](/docs/subscriptions) for the full flow.

## Rewards

When the **[Rewards Program](/docs/rewards)** is on, shoppers have a **Rewards** tab in their account. Depending on how you've set up the program, they can:

- **Join** the program (with a sign-up bonus, if you offer one).
- **Earn points** on paid orders, get a first-order bonus, get birthday points (they enter their birthday on the Rewards tab), and earn points for following you on social media.
- **Redeem points** for a reward. This creates a single-use discount code that's shown on the page and emailed to them. They paste it into the discount code box at checkout.

Some things to know:

- Shoppers need an account and must join before they can redeem.
- Redeeming only works while **Coupons & Discounts** is on. Otherwise they see "Redemption is temporarily unavailable."
- If an order is refunded, the points it earned are taken back.
- If you turn the program off, balances are kept. The Rewards tab disappears from the account menu until you turn it back on.

## Invoices

When you send an invoice by email, your customer gets a secure link to view it. They don't need to sign in. The invoice page shows the line items, totals, how much is paid and still owed, and how to pay with each method you accept, with copy buttons for account details. Signed-in customers also see their invoices on the **Invoices** tab. See [Invoices](/docs/invoices) for what each invoice email contains.

---

## Donations

When **Donations** is on, your store has a Donate page. Visitors can give a one-time amount by card through Stripe, or use your Venmo or Cash App handles if you've added them. Depending on your settings, the page may be called a donation, a tip, or support. After paying by card, they come back to the page and see a thank-you message.

---

## Getting in Touch

Your **Contact** page has a form that emails you directly. When you hit Reply, your answer goes straight to the shopper's email address (see [Contact Form](/docs/contact-form)). If you've added FAQs, your FAQ page lists them, which is a good place to answer the questions on this page before shoppers have to ask.

## Emails Your Customers Receive

Every customer email shows your logo (or your business name, if you haven't added a logo). It comes from "*Your Business* via SimplePress", and replies go to the **Owner Email** in your General settings (invoice, form, and quote emails use your **Support Email** instead). The two sign-in emails, **Verify your email** and **Reset your password**, are the exception: they have no reply address, so replies don't reach you.

You can preview most of these emails, and change the subject line and opening paragraph of many of them, under **Notification Emails** in your admin. See [Email Preview](/docs/email-preview).

## Common Customer Questions

Short answers you can copy, paste, and adjust.

**"How do I track my order?"**
Open your order confirmation email and click **View Order Status**. Once it ships, you'll also get a shipping email with a tracking link. If you can't find the email, go to our website's `/order-status` page, enter your email and order number, and we'll send you a fresh link. If you have an account, it's also under **Orders** in your account.

**"I made an account, but my old orders aren't there."**
Orders show up in your account when they were placed with the same email address you signed up with. If you used a different email at checkout, let us know the order number and we'll help.

**"I never got my verification email."**
Check your spam folder for an email titled "Verify your email." The link works for one hour. To get a new one, try signing in with your email and password. You'll be taken to a screen with a button to resend it.

**"How do I reset my password?"**
On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password** and enter your email. The reset email says "Reset your SimplePress password." That's expected, because your account works at every store that runs on SimplePress.

**"How do I cancel (or pause, or skip) my subscription?"**
Open any email we've sent about your subscription and click the link to your subscription (usually **Manage your subscription**). You can skip, pause, update your card, or cancel there. Lost the email? Go to our website's `/subscriptions/manage` page and enter your email for a new link.

**"Where did my cart (or wishlist) go?"**
Your cart and wishlist are saved in the web browser you used. If you switched phones or computers, or cleared your browser data, they start out empty, so you'll need to add your items again.

**"Can I use two discount codes?"**
Only one code can be used per order, and that includes rewards codes.

**"How do I use my reward points?"**
Sign in, open **Rewards** in your account, and redeem a reward. You'll get a one-time code on the page and by email. Paste it into the discount code box at checkout.

**"Can you tell me when this is back in stock?"**
On the product page, enter your email in the **Notify me** box. We'll email you once when it's available again.

**"How do I stop your marketing emails?"**
Click **Unsubscribe** at the bottom of any marketing email, or sign in and turn off marketing emails on the **Preferences** tab in your account.

**"Please delete my account and data."**
Sign in, go to **Preferences**, and click **Request account deletion**, or just ask us. Your personal details are removed, but past orders are kept, anonymized, for tax records.
