## Overview

**Settings → Shipping** is where you decide what you charge customers for delivery, whether they can pick up in person or have you deliver nearby, which countries you sell to, and what you tell shoppers about delivery times and returns.

Owners and Managers can open it. Staff can't. See [Team & roles](/docs/team-and-roles).

If your store sells products, **Set up shipping or pickup** is a step on your [Setup Guide](/docs/launch-checklist#the-steps). It ticks off once you change shipping from the default free shipping, turn on in-store pickup, or turn on local delivery. If free shipping is what you want, review this page and click **Mark as done** on the step.

The page has these cards, top to bottom:

1. **Shipping**: your shipping charges and the in-store pickup switch.
2. **Countries you sell to**.
3. **Local delivery**: deliver orders yourself to customers who live near you.
4. **Delivery times**.
5. **Returns**.

If you pick **Zone + weight rates**, three extra cards appear for setting up your zones.

For how to pack, ship and track orders once they come in, see [Orders](/docs/orders). To buy a discounted label and bring the tracking number back, see [Shipping labels with Pirate Ship](/docs/shipping-labels).

## Choose how you charge for shipping

Under **Shipping charges**, pick one of four options.

![The Shipping card with the four shipping options and the In-store pickup switch](/images/shipping/shipping-modes.png)

| Option | What customers pay |
| ------ | ------------------ |
| **Free shipping (all orders)** | Nothing. Shipping shows as Free. |
| **Flat rate shipping** | The same fee on every order. |
| **Flat rate — free when order subtotal reaches a threshold** | A flat fee, but free once the order is big enough. |
| **Zone + weight rates** | A price based on where the order is going and how much it weighs. |

Shipping rates are always typed in dollars, like `5.99`.

### Free shipping

Every order ships free. Use it when shipping is built into your prices.

### Flat rate

Type your fee in **Flat shipping rate (USD)**, for example `5.99`. Every order pays it, whatever its size or weight.

### Flat rate with a free shipping threshold

Type both amounts: **Flat shipping rate (USD)** and **Free shipping when subtotal reaches (USD)**.

![Flat rate with a threshold, showing both money fields](/images/shipping/flat-rate-threshold.png)

**Example:** flat rate `6.00` and threshold `50.00`. A $45 order pays $6 shipping. A $52 order ships free.

The threshold is checked against the **subtotal before any discounts**. A customer who has a $52 cart and uses a $10 coupon still qualifies for free shipping, even though they now owe $42 in products.

If a flat rate or threshold box is empty or isn't a valid amount, the box turns red with a message under it: "Enter a valid flat rate amount" or "Enter a valid free shipping threshold". A toast reading "Fix the highlighted shipping fields" appears, and nothing is saved until you fix the box.

### Zone + weight rates

This mode prices each order by two things: how far it's going, and how heavy it is. It's best when your orders vary a lot in size. It covers **US states** only.

When you select it, three cards appear:

**Origin & Starter Rates**

![Origin state, the Auto-generate starter rates button, and the Weight tiers](/images/shipping/zone-weight-editor.png)

1. Pick your **Origin state**: the state you ship from.
2. Click **Auto-generate starter rates** to fill in a starting set of zones and prices based on that state. You can edit everything afterwards, so treat it as a first draft.
3. Under **Weight tiers**, edit the weight bands. Each tier has a **Label**, a **Min (lb)** and a **Max (lb)**. The last tier has no maximum. Click **Add tier** for more.

**Zone rate matrix**

Each row is a **zone**: a group of states that share prices. Give it a **Zone name**, set a rate for each weight tier, and tick the **States in this zone**. A state can only be in one zone. Click **Add zone** to add another. If the same state is in two zones, SimplePress tells you which one.

**Fallback & defaults**

![The Fallback and defaults card with the fallback rate, optional free shipping threshold and default item weight](/images/shipping/fallback-defaults.png)

| Field | What it does |
| ----- | ------------ |
| **Fallback shipping rate (USD)** | Charged when the destination isn't in any of your zones, such as a US territory or anywhere outside the US. |
| **Free shipping threshold (USD) — optional** | If the subtotal reaches this amount, shipping is free. Leave it blank to turn it off. |
| **Default item weight (lb)** | The weight used for any product that doesn't have a weight of its own. Pick a cautious number so heavy orders aren't undercharged. |

Set each product's real weight in the product's **Package weight** box. See [Products](/docs/products#shipping-weight).

To save, you need at least one weight tier, at least one zone, a valid origin state and a valid fallback rate.

Zone prices come from the address the shopper types on your checkout page. So on Stripe's payment page, the address is locked and they can't edit it there. To change it, they click back to your checkout and start again. With free or flat-rate shipping, they can edit the address on Stripe's page. See [What Your Customers See](/docs/for-your-customers#step-2-secure-payment-with-stripe).

### Free shipping coupons

A discount code of the **Free shipping** type waives the shipping charge in every mode. See [Discounts and Coupons](/docs/discounts-and-coupons).

## In-store pickup

Turn on **In-store pickup** ("Let customers choose pickup at checkout (no shipping charge).") if customers can collect their orders from you. Pickup orders pay no shipping.

When it's on, two boxes appear:

![The pickup fields: location, and pickup hours and instructions](/images/shipping/pickup-fields.png)

| Field | What to enter |
| ----- | ------------- |
| **Pickup location** | Where customers collect. Leave it blank to use your business address from [General Settings](/docs/general-settings#business-address). |
| **Pickup hours & instructions** | When and how, for example "Mon–Fri 10am–6pm. Ring the bell at the side entrance." **Required.** |

If you leave the instructions blank, SimplePress asks for them with a message under the box: "Add pickup hours/instructions so customers know when to collect their order". The box is selected for you, a toast reading "Fix the highlighted shipping fields" appears, and nothing is saved until you add them. This works the same in every shipping mode, including **Zone + weight rates**.

![The inline error under the pickup instructions box](/images/shipping/pickup-required-error.png)

### What customers see

- **At checkout.** A **Delivery** card appears with two buttons: **Ship to address** and **In-store pickup**. It only shows when pickup is turned on.

  ![The Delivery card at checkout with Ship to address and In-store pickup](/images/shipping/checkout-delivery-card.png)

- **When they choose pickup.** Checkout shows your **Pickup location** and your instructions, and the order summary reads "In-store pickup (free)".

  ![Checkout with In-store pickup chosen, showing the pickup location, instructions, and the summary](/images/shipping/checkout-pickup.png)

- **In their confirmation email**, in a **Pickup Details** section.
- **In the Ready for Pickup email.** When their order is ready, open it in your admin and click **Mark Ready for Pickup**. The customer is emailed straight away, with your pickup location and instructions. See [Orders](/docs/orders#local-pickup-orders).

Pickup times are separate from [Business Hours](/docs/business-hours).

## Countries you sell to

Choose which countries show up as shipping destinations at checkout.

![The Countries you sell to card with United States on, and Canada and Mexico unavailable](/images/shipping/countries.png)

**United States** is always on. Canada and Mexico are greyed out, because international shipping isn't supported yet. If your store already had an international country turned on, a warning says so.

## Local delivery

Use **Local delivery** when you drop orders off yourself to customers who live near you. Shoppers choose **Local delivery** at checkout when their address is inside your delivery radius. You deliver the order, then mark it delivered in your admin.

Local delivery is a card on **Settings → Shipping**, below the shipping charges and above **Delivery times**. It isn't a feature flag, so there's nothing to switch on in your feature list. Owners and Managers can edit it.

![The Local delivery card with the radius, fee, closer prices and instructions](/images/shipping/local-delivery-card.png)

### Turn it on

Switch on **Offer local delivery** ("Customers inside your delivery radius can pick this instead of shipping."). The other fields only appear once it's on.

It works alongside every shipping mode (free, flat rate, threshold and zone + weight), and alongside in-store pickup. Pickup and local delivery are separate options, and you can have both on at once.

### The fields

| Field | What it means |
| ----- | ------------- |
| **Deliver from ZIP** | The ZIP code you measure distance from. Leave it blank to use the ZIP of your business address from [General Settings](/docs/general-settings#business-address). After you save, it shows "Measuring from [City], [ST]" so you can check it found the right place. |
| **Delivery radius (miles)** | How far you'll deliver, from 1 to 100 miles. |
| **Delivery fee** | What a customer pays for delivery. Type `0` for free delivery. If you add closer prices, this is the price for the farthest ring. The most you can enter is $10,000. |
| **Minimum order (optional)** | The smallest order you'll deliver. Leave it blank for no minimum. |
| **Closer prices (optional)** | Charge less (or nothing) for customers nearer to you. See below. |
| **Delivery days & instructions** | Something like "I deliver Tuesday and Thursday evenings. I'll text you when I'm on my way." Up to 1000 characters. |

The instructions are shown to customers at checkout and in their confirmation email.

### Closer prices

By default everyone inside your radius pays the **Delivery fee**. Click **Add a closer price** to charge less for customers who live nearer. Each row reads "Within [N] miles" and a price. You can add up to three, and each distance must be smaller than your radius and used only once.

**Example:** delivery radius `15`, **Delivery fee** `5.00`, and two closer prices: within 5 miles is `0`, within 10 miles is `3.00`. The card shows the summary as you type: "Free within 5 mi · $3.00 within 10 mi · $5.00 up to 15 mi".

| Where the customer lives | They pay |
| ------------------------ | -------- |
| Within 5 miles | Free |
| Between 5 and 10 miles | $3.00 |
| Between 10 and 15 miles | $5.00 (the **Delivery fee**) |
| More than 15 miles away | Local delivery isn't offered |

At checkout the customer pays the nearest closer price that covers them. If none does, they pay the main **Delivery fee**. A customer exactly at your radius counts as inside it.

### How distance is worked out

- It's the straight-line distance between the center of your ZIP code and the center of the customer's ZIP code. Your address stays private.
- Because it uses ZIP centers, the distance is approximate near the edge of your radius. A customer just inside or just outside might land on the other side of the line.
- Shoppers never see the distance or your ZIP code. They only see whether you deliver to them, and the price.
- It's for US addresses only.

### Minimum order, discounts and free shipping

- The **Minimum order** is checked against the subtotal **before discounts**. A $30 cart with a $10 coupon still meets a $25 minimum.
- Your store's free shipping threshold (the **Flat rate** option with a threshold, or the zone + weight threshold) doesn't apply to local delivery. The customer still pays your **Delivery fee**.
- A discount code of the **Free shipping** type does cover the delivery fee. See [Discounts and Coupons](/docs/discounts-and-coupons).

### Subscriptions

Subscription products can't use local delivery. They still ship or are picked up.

### Saving

Use the page's **Save changes** button in the toolbar. You'll see "Saving local delivery..." and then "Local delivery settings updated successfully". If a field has a problem, a toast reads "Fix the highlighted local delivery fields". If saving fails, it reads "Failed to update local delivery". A half-finished shipping charges form doesn't stop you saving only this card.

### Messages you might see

| Message | What to do |
| ------- | ---------- |
| "Enter a 5-digit ZIP code" | Fix **Deliver from ZIP**. |
| "We couldn't find that ZIP code." | The ZIP isn't recognized. Check it for typos. |
| "Enter the ZIP code you deliver from (your business address has no ZIP yet)" | Type a ZIP in **Deliver from ZIP**, or add a ZIP to your business address in General Settings. |
| "Enter how far you deliver, from 1 to 100 miles" | Fill in **Delivery radius (miles)** while local delivery is on. |
| "Enter a whole number from 1 to 100 miles" | Use a whole number, with no decimals. |
| "Enter a valid delivery fee (e.g. 5.00, or 0)" | Type a dollar amount, or `0` for free. |
| "Enter a valid minimum order amount" | Type a dollar amount, or leave it blank. |
| "You can add up to 3 closer prices." | Remove a row before adding another. |
| "Enter your delivery radius first." | Type the radius, then add closer prices. |
| "Must be less than your [N]-mile radius." | A closer price's distance has to be smaller than your radius. |
| "Each distance can only be used once." | Two rows have the same distance. Change one. |
| "Enter a price (e.g. 3.00, or 0 for free)" | Fill in the price on that row. |
| "That price is too high" | Lower it. The most is $10,000. |
| "Keep this under 1000 characters" | Shorten **Delivery days & instructions**. |

### What customers see

- **At checkout.** The **Delivery** card adds a **Local delivery** button next to **Ship to address** (and **In-store pickup**, if it's on). See [What Your Customers See](/docs/for-your-customers#step-1-your-checkout-page).

  ![Checkout with Local delivery chosen, showing the ZIP check and delivery details](/images/shipping/checkout-local-delivery.png)

- **In the confirmation email**, with their delivery address and your instructions.
- **When you deliver it**, you mark the order delivered. See [Orders](/docs/orders#local-delivery-orders).

### Other places it shows up

- Your **Shipping policy** template gains a **Local Delivery** section describing your radius, prices, minimum and instructions. See [Pages and Policies](/docs/pages-and-policies).
- **Download your site** includes a **Local delivery** block in your site details. See [Data and Export](/docs/data-and-export).

## Delivery times and returns

These two cards are always on the page. They don't change what you charge. They give shoppers (and Google's product listings) a clearer picture of what to expect.

![The Delivery times card and the Returns card with Accept returns chosen](/images/shipping/delivery-returns.png)

**Delivery times** has two parts, each with a minimum and maximum number of days:

- **Handling time**: how long you take to get an order out the door.
- **Transit time**: how long it's in the mail.

**Returns** starts with **Return policy**: **Not set**, **No returns** or **Accept returns**. If you accept returns, you also fill in:

- **Return window (days)**
- **Who pays return shipping**: **Free returns**, **Customer pays** or **Flat return fee** (with a **Return fee (USD)**)
- **How customers return items**: **By mail**, **In store** or **Either**

These details help your products show shipping and returns information in Google results. They also link to your Returns & Refunds policy. See [Pages and Policies](/docs/pages-and-policies) and [SEO](/docs/seo#google-shipping-and-returns-details).

The **Save changes** button at the top saves everything on the page in one go. If a delivery or returns field has a problem, a message says "Fix the highlighted delivery and returns fields".

## Saving

Use **Save changes** in the toolbar. The badge shows **Saved** or **Unsaved Changes**. There's no Reset button on this page.

When it works, you'll see "Shipping settings updated successfully", and "Delivery and returns settings updated successfully" if you changed those cards.

## What customers see in the cart and at checkout

The **cart** can't know the price yet, because it doesn't know where the order is going. It shows shipping as **Calculated at checkout**, and the total as **Estimated total**.

![The cart summary with Shipping showing Calculated at checkout, and Estimated total](/images/shipping/storefront-cart-shipping.png)

At checkout, shipping shows as Free, a dollar amount, or **In-store pickup (free)**. In Zone + weight mode, it reads **Calculated at checkout** until the customer enters their state, then briefly **Calculating…** while the price loads. A note on the page says "Tax and final total are confirmed on Stripe Checkout." Tax is worked out on Stripe's page, so the total can still change a little there.

## What you can and can't do

| You can | You can't |
| ------- | --------- |
| Offer free, flat-rate, threshold or zone + weight shipping. | Set a different shipping rate per product or variant. |
| Price by US state and by weight with zones. | Use live carrier rates (such as a real-time USPS or UPS quote). |
| Waive shipping with a Free shipping coupon. | Price shipping by country: only US destinations are supported. |
| Offer in-store pickup with its own location and instructions. | Turn pickup on without instructions. |
| Deliver locally within up to 100 miles, with up to three cheaper rings closer to you. | Show shoppers the distance, or use local delivery for subscriptions. |
| Show delivery times and a returns policy. | Ship outside the United States, or offer local delivery outside it. |
