## Overview

Your SimplePress account is your personal login. It is separate from your store's settings. It holds your name, your photo, your password, and the list of devices you're signed in on.

You use **one SimplePress login for every store** you belong to. If you run two stores, or help on a friend's team, you sign in with the same email and password. A new name, photo or password applies everywhere at once.

> **Where to find it:** In the admin, click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar. A menu opens with your name and email, then **Settings** and **Sign Out**. Choose **Settings**. Every signed-in person can do this, whether they are an Owner, Manager or Staff.

![The menu at the bottom of the admin sidebar, showing a name and email, Settings and Sign Out](/images/your-account-and-security/account-menu.png)

**Settings** doesn't open an admin page. It takes you to your account pages on your storefront, at `/account/settings` and `/account/security`. They are drawn in your store's template, so they match your storefront's look. A custom template may style them a little differently from the screenshots here.

On the left of those pages is the account menu. It lists Orders, Address Book, Subscriptions, Invoices, Rewards, **Settings**, **Security** and Preferences. Some items only appear when the matching feature is on. Orders and Address Book are for shopping as a customer. This page covers **Settings** and **Security**.

## Your profile

Go to your name menu → **Settings**. You'll see a **User profile** card.

![The User profile card with an avatar, a Name field and a Save changes button](/images/your-account-and-security/profile-settings.png)

| Field | What it does |
| ----- | ------------ |
| **Avatar** | Click **Change avatar**, then **Upload avatar** or **Delete avatar**. Any image type works. SimplePress shrinks it to a small square (256 pixels), so a plain, centered photo looks best. Without a photo you see your initials. |
| **Name** | The name shown next to your account. Change it, then click **Save changes**. |

You'll see "Profile updated successfully", "Avatar changed successfully" or "Avatar deleted successfully" after each change.

Your name and photo show in the admin sidebar and in the **Members** table on your [Team](/docs/team-and-roles) page. Your customers don't see your owner name. They see your store's name and branding.

### Your email address

Your email is how you sign in, and it's where password and verification emails go. **You can't change it yourself.** There's no email field on the Settings page.

If you need a different sign-in email, ask the SimplePress team at **help@generativejustice.org**.

## Changing your password

Go to your name menu → **Settings**, then click **Security** in the account menu.

![The Change password card with Current password and New password fields](/images/your-account-and-security/change-password.png)

1. Type your **Current password**.
2. Type your **New password**.
3. Click **Update password**.

You'll see "Password changed successfully". Changing your password **signs you out of your other devices**. The device you're using stays signed in.

If your account doesn't have a password yet, the card is called **Set password** instead. It says "You don't have a password yet. Request a reset link to set one up." Click **Send reset link** and follow the email.

### Forgot your password?

You don't need to be signed in for this.

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Type your **Email** and click **Send reset link**.
3. The page says "Check your email". Open the email and click the link.
4. On the **Reset Password** page, type your new password in **Password** and click **Reset Password**. There's no second box to confirm it, so type carefully (the eye icon shows what you typed).
5. You'll see "Password reset successfully" and go back to the sign-in page.

![The Forgot Password card with an Email field and a Send reset link button](/images/your-account-and-security/forgot-password.png)

The link in the email **expires after 1 hour**. If it has expired, start again from **Forgot password?**.

Resetting a forgotten password does **not** sign out your other devices. If you think someone else may have your password, sign in with the new one, then check **Active sessions** below and remove any device you don't know.

## Active sessions

Still on the **Security** page, the **Active sessions** card lists every device where you're signed in. Sessions from every store you've signed in to appear in the same list.

![The Active sessions card with the current session and one other device](/images/your-account-and-security/active-sessions.png)

Each row shows:

- an icon for the device type
- the browser and system, like "Chrome, macOS"
- a **Current session** badge on the device you're using now
- on every other row, how long ago it was last used, like "10 Hours Ago"

SimplePress doesn't show IP addresses or locations.

| Button | Where | What it does |
| ------ | ----- | ------------ |
| **Sign Out** | On your current session | Signs you out of this device. |
| **Revoke** | On any other session | Signs that device out. You'll see "Session revoked successfully". |

A revoked device can stay signed in for **a few minutes** (up to about 5) before it is fully signed out. If you lost a device, also change your password right away.

**How long you stay signed in.** A sign-in lasts about **7 days**. Each time you use SimplePress, the clock is topped up, so you stay signed in on a device you use regularly. After about a week without using it, that device signs you out and you sign in again. There's no "remember me" box, and it can't be made longer or shorter.

## If sign-in says it couldn't verify your browser

Sign-up, sign-in and **Forgot password?** are protected by Google reCAPTCHA. It runs quietly in the background, so there's no box to tick. Ad blockers, privacy extensions, some strict privacy browsers, and some VPNs or work networks can block it. If that happens, you'll see:

- "We couldn't verify your browser. Wait a moment and try again — if it keeps happening, a browser extension or network filter may be blocking reCAPTCHA."
- "We couldn't verify this request. Please try again, or contact support if the problem continues."

To fix it, allow your SimplePress site in your ad blocker or privacy extension (or turn it off for that site), switch off your VPN, or try another browser or your phone's mobile data. If you still can't get in, email help@generativejustice.org.

If you try too many times in a row, you'll see "Too many attempts. Wait a minute and try again." Wait a minute, then try once more.

## Verifying your email

You have to verify your email before you can sign in. When you create an account, SimplePress emails you a link. Until you click it, signing in sends you to a **Verify Email** page that says "Check your email for a verification link."

![The Verify Email card, with a Check your email message and a Not seeing it help list](/images/your-account-and-security/verify-email.png)

- The link **expires after 1 hour**. Clicking it verifies you and signs you in.
- Didn't get it? Click **Resend**. You can ask again after a short wait (the button counts down, like "Resend in 45s"). If you opened the page without being signed in, you may not see the button. Try signing in again to get it back.
- Check your spam or junk folder, and make sure you typed the right address.

This applies to everyone, including people you invite to your team. See [Team & Roles](/docs/team-and-roles).

## Customer accounts vs. your owner account

Your owner login is the same kind of account your shoppers create. A customer signs up on your storefront and uses the same `/account` pages to see their orders, addresses and preferences. What you can do with a role comes from your place on the [Team](/docs/team-and-roles) page.

As an Owner or Manager, you can open any customer in **Customers** to add private notes or to **anonymize** them (**Anonymize this customer?**) when they ask to be forgotten. Customers can't see your notes. See [Customers](/docs/customers).

## If you think someone else got into your account

Act on it right away. Work through these in order:

1. **Change your password.** Go to your name menu → **Settings** → **Security**, and use **Change password**. This signs out every other device. If you can't sign in, use **Forgot password?** instead, then come back to this list.
2. **Sign out other devices.** On the same Security page, look at [Active sessions](#active-sessions) and click **Revoke** on any device you don't recognize. A revoked device can stay signed in for a few minutes.
3. **Check your Team.** If you're an Owner, go to **Settings → Team** and look at **Members** and **Pending Invitations**. Remove anyone you don't recognize. Managers can see the list but can't change it, so ask an Owner. See [Team & Roles](/docs/team-and-roles#removing-a-team-member).
4. **Check Stripe.** Sign in to your Stripe Dashboard, look at the list of team members in your Stripe settings, and remove anyone you don't know. Check that your bank account under **Business → Bank accounts and currencies** is still yours. Change your Stripe password if you use the same one. SimplePress can't see or change any of this. See [Account Setup](/docs/stripe/account-setup#step-1--add-your-bank-account).
5. **Change your email password too,** if the same password was ever used there. Password reset emails go to that inbox.
6. **Email help@generativejustice.org** and tell us what you saw, so we can help.

## Staying safe

- Use a strong password you don't use anywhere else.
- Check **Active sessions** once in a while and click **Revoke** on any device you don't recognize.
- Don't share your login. Invite people to your team instead, so each person has their own account and role. See [Team & Roles](/docs/team-and-roles).

## What you can and can't do

| Action | Available? |
| ------ | ---------- |
| Change your name and photo | Yes |
| Change your password | Yes |
| Reset a forgotten password by email | Yes |
| See your signed-in devices and sign one out | Yes |
| Change your sign-in email | No. Ask the SimplePress team at help@generativejustice.org |
| Sign in with Discord, Google or another service | No. Email and password only |
| Turn on two-factor sign-in | No |
| Delete your account yourself | No. Ask the SimplePress team |
| Use different logins for different stores | No. One login covers all your stores |
