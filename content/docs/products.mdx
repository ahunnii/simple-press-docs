## Overview

Products are the core of your store. Each product has a name, a price, photos, and a page on your storefront. You can add variants for sizes and colors, track stock, schedule a launch, and choose which collections it belongs to.

<Callout type="info">
Products is on by default and can be switched off in **Settings → Features**. Turning it off also turns off everything that depends on it: **Orders**, **Cart**, **Collections**, **Checkout**, **Wishlist**, **Back-in-stock** alerts, **Product Reviews**, and **Product Subscriptions**. **Inventory** does not depend on Products and keeps working. See [Feature flags](/docs/feature-flags).
</Callout>

## The Products list

Go to **Products** in your admin sidebar (under **Catalog**) to see your whole catalog.

![The Products page with the search box, Filters button, Export products (CSV) and Add Product buttons, and a table of products with photos, prices, variant counts, and Published or Draft badges](/images/products/products-list.png)

Each row shows:

- **Product** — the photo, the name, and the URL slug
- **Price**
- **Variants** — "No variants" or "N variants"
- **Status** — a **Published** or **Draft** badge

### Finding and sorting

- **Search products…** matches a product's name, its URL slug, its SKU, and the SKU of any of its variants.
- **Status** and **Sort** are in the **Filters** menu beside the search box.
- **Status** filters between **All products**, **Published**, and **Drafts**.
- **Sort** offers **Newest**, **Oldest**, **Name A–Z**, **Name Z–A**, **Price low–high**, and **Price high–low**.

Long catalogs are split into pages. If your filters match nothing, you'll see "No products match your filters" with a **Clear filters** button. A brand-new store shows "No products yet" with an **Add Your First Product** button.

### Row actions

Click a product's name to open it, or use the three-dot menu at the end of the row:

- **Edit**
- **View on storefront** (only for published products)
- **Duplicate**
- **Delete**

### Doing several at once

Tick the checkboxes on the left to select products. Your selection stays when you move to another page. If your filters match more products than fit on the page, you'll get an option to select all of the matching products at once.

A bar appears with:

- **Publish** and **Unpublish** — a message appears afterwards with an **Undo** button.
- **Delete** — only the store **Owner** sees this button.

## Creating or editing a product

Click **Add Product** to create a product, or click a product's name to edit it. The toolbar across the top has:

- **Back**, and the title ("New Product" or the product's name)
- A status badge: **Draft** (new and untouched), **Unsaved Changes**, or **Saved**
- The **Published** switch
- A **More Options** menu: **View on storefront** (published products only), **Reset**, **Duplicate** (existing products only), and **Delete** (existing products only)
- **Save changes** — and, on a new product, **Save & create another**, which saves and gives you a blank form for the next one

You can also press **Cmd+Enter** (Mac) or **Ctrl+Enter** (Windows) to save.

The form has up to five tabs: **Basics**, **Collections** (only when [Collections](/docs/collections) is on), **Options** (only when [Product options](/docs/product-options) is on), **SEO**, and **Product page**. If a field on a tab has a problem when you save, that tab shows a red dot.

## The Basics tab

![The top of the Basics tab, showing the Basic Information card and the Pricing card with a cost per item entered, the margin, the profit bar, and suggested prices](/images/products/product-basics.png)

### Basic Information

- **Name** — required. This is what customers see on the storefront.
- **Description** — a plain-text description of the product.
- **Storefront URL** — shown under the name, like `/shop/your-product`. It has an **Edit in SEO** link, because the slug itself lives on the **SEO** tab (see [The SEO tab](#the-seo-tab)).

The URL slug follows the name as you type. It stops following once the product has been published, or once you edit the slug yourself. After that, if you rename the product, the form shows "Name changed — the URL hasn't." with an **Update URL** button. Click it if you want the address to match the new name. Otherwise the address stays put.

### Schedule publish

While a product is unpublished, a **Schedule publish** field appears. Pick a date and time, and SimplePress publishes the product for you after that time. Leave it empty to publish by hand with the **Published** switch.

Two things to know about the timing:

- **The time you pick is in the time zone of the computer or phone you're scheduling from.** It is not the **Time Zone** in your [General Settings](/docs/general-settings#time-zone). If you schedule a 9:00 AM launch from a laptop set to Pacific time, it goes live at 9:00 AM Pacific, even if your store is set to Eastern. The note under the field ("Scheduled for ...") shows the time in your device's time zone.
- **It can go live up to about 15 minutes late.** SimplePress checks for scheduled products about every 15 minutes, so a product set for 9:00 AM appears on your storefront at the first check after 9:00.

### Pricing

- **Price** — the selling price.
- **Compare at price** — optional. The "original" price, shown crossed out next to the real one to signal a sale. It must be **greater than** the price. If it isn't, you'll see "Compare-at price must be greater than price".
- **Cost per item** — optional, and private. What the product costs you to make or buy.

When you fill in a cost, a profit helper appears. It shows your margin, and a bar that splits the price into cost, estimated card fee, and profit. The card fee is an estimate based on Stripe's standard 2.9% + 30¢. Under it, **Suggested prices by margin** shows buttons. Click one to set the price to hit that margin.

If the product has variants, the card says pricing is managed per variant, and the price fields move down to the **Product Variants** section. **Cost per item** stays here.

### Subscriptions

This card only appears when **Product Subscriptions** is switched on in **Settings → Features** (it's off by default). Turn on **Offer as a subscription**, choose the **Cadence** options customers can pick, and set a **Subscribe & save** discount from 0 to 90 percent. See [Subscriptions](/docs/subscriptions). A product that has [Product options](/docs/product-options) can't be sold as a subscription.

### Shipping weight

Enter the **Package weight** in pounds or kilograms. Your shipping setup uses it to work out rates. If your store's shipping mode doesn't use weight, the card says so and offers a **Set a weight anyway** link. See [Shipping](/docs/shipping).

### Inventory

For a product without variants:

- **SKU** and **Barcode (UPC/EAN/GTIN)** — optional codes. With variants, each variant gets its own.
- **Track Inventory** — turn this on to count stock. It's off by default, and an untracked product always shows as in stock.
- **Allow Backorders** — only shows when Track Inventory is on. Customers can still buy when the count is zero.
- **Inventory Quantity** — only shows when Track Inventory is on and the product has no variants. With variants, you set stock on each variant.
- **Low Inventory Threshold** — only shows when Track Inventory is on. You get an email when stock drops to this number. Leave it blank for no alert.
- **Inventory item** — pick a stock item from your [Inventory](/docs/inventory-management) to draw stock from, or leave it on "None — use individual inventory". Rental items can't be linked. When you link one, set **Units consumed per purchase**. The product's own stock controls are hidden while it's linked. This box only shows when Inventory Management is on (or the product is already linked).

Stock counts, adjustments, and the Inventory pages themselves are covered in [Inventory management](/docs/inventory-management).

## Product images

The **Product Images** card is on the **Basics** tab. It shows how many photos you've used, like "3 of 10 images". A product can have up to 10.

![The Product Images card with the Add images menu open, showing Upload from device and Choose from library](/images/products/images-add-menu.png)

### Adding photos

Click **Select images** (or **Add images** once you already have some). What happens next depends on whether the **Media Library** is on:

- **Media Library on** — a menu opens with **Upload from device** and **Choose from library**.
- **Media Library off** — your computer's file picker opens straight away.

You can also drag photos from your computer and drop them anywhere on the card. You can add several at a time.

Media Library is on by default. It can be switched on or off in **Settings → Features**. See [Media Library](/docs/media-library).

### Choosing from the library

**Choose from library** opens the **Choose images** window. It lists everything in your Media Library.

![The Choose images window with a search box, a grid of library pictures with two selected, and the "2 selected · 6 left" footer with Cancel and Add 2 images](/images/products/choose-images-dialog.png)

- Use **Search by filename…** to find a picture.
- Click pictures to select them. Pictures already on this product are tagged **Added**.
- The footer shows "N selected · M left", where M is how many more photos the product has room for.
- Click **Add N images** to add them, or **Cancel** to back out.

If you try to pick more than will fit, you'll see "You can add N more images", or "You can't add any more images" when the product is full. Pictures from the library are already hosted, so they're added right away. There's nothing to upload.

### Photos from your device

Photos from your computer or phone are prepared first (you'll see "Preparing photos…"):

- **HEIC** photos, like the ones iPhones take, are converted automatically.
- Large photos are shrunk to a maximum of 2,560 pixels and compressed.
- After that, each photo must be **under 5 MB**. Most phone photos fit.

These photos are shown as previews only. The upload happens when you click **Save changes**, and the card reminds you: "Pending images upload when you save."

### Limits and skipped files

- At 10 photos, you'll see "This product already has 10 images — remove one to add more".
- If you add more than will fit, the extra ones are skipped: "Added X of Y images — products can have at most 10 (Z skipped)".
- A file that isn't a picture is skipped: `Skipped "<file>": not an image`.
- A photo still over the size limit after processing is skipped: `Skipped "<file>": must be less than 5MB`.
- A library picture that's already on the product is skipped: "Skipped images that are already in the gallery".
- If you drop files while photos are still being prepared, you'll see "Still preparing photos — try again in a moment".

### Order, alt text, and removing

- **Reorder** by dragging a photo by its handle. You can also reorder with the keyboard.
- The **first photo is the primary image**. It's the one on your product card and the shop grid. The card marks it "Primary image".
- **Alt Text** — describe each photo ("Describe this image..."). It helps people using screen readers, and helps search engines. See [Accessibility and Your Site](/docs/accessibility) for tips on writing it.
- Click the red **X** on a photo to remove it from the product. The change is saved when you click **Save changes**.

## Variants

Use variants for a product that comes in different sizes, colors, or other options. On the **Basics** tab, the **Product Variants** card starts with an **Add Variants** button. Once a product has variants, the button reads **Edit Options**.

<video src="/videos/products/create-product-with-variants.webm" poster="/videos/products/create-product-with-variants-poster.png" controls muted playsInline className="rounded-lg border" />

*A short clip: adding a new product named Docs Demo Tee with a price of $28, adding a Size option with S, M, and L values, generating the variants, and saving.*

### Variant Options

1. Click **Add Variants**. The **Variant Options** panel opens.
2. The panel opens with one option already started, named `Size`. Keep that name, change it (like `Color`), or click **Add Option** for another one.
3. Type its values in the **Values** box, separated by commas, like `Small, Medium, Large`.
4. Repeat for any other option.
5. Click **Generate Variants**. SimplePress makes one variant for every combination.

A product can have up to 100 variant combinations. If regenerating would remove variants you've already filled in, you'll be asked "Remove existing variants?" and need to confirm with **Yes, regenerate**.

### Variant fields

Each variant row has:

- **Name** — built from the option values, like "Small / Black". You can't edit it. Change the option values instead.
- **Price** — leave it blank to use the product's price. A variant price of $0 isn't allowed. You'll see "A variant price can't be $0…".
- **Compare At** — the crossed-out original price for this variant.
- **Stock** — this variant's quantity. It matters when **Track Inventory** is on.
- **SKU** and **Barcode** (UPC/EAN/GTIN) — optional codes.
- **Image** — pick one of the product's own gallery photos for the variant (**Pick a gallery image**, or **None**). Upload the photos in **Product Images** first.

### Bulk edit

Tick the checkboxes on several variants. A **Bulk edit:** bar appears with:

- **Set Price** — one price for every selected variant
- **Set Qty** — one stock count for every selected variant (only when **Track Inventory** is on)
- **Delete selected**

![The Product Variants table with several rows ticked and the Bulk edit bar showing Set Price, Set Qty, and Delete selected](/images/products/variants-bulk-edit.png)

## The Options tab

Use **Options** when customers should build or personalize a product: pick a chain color, choose up to three charms, or type an engraving. Each option can add to the price, and a choice can even use another product's stock. Variants are the versions you stock and sell (a size or a color). Options are the choices a customer makes on top of that.

This tab only appears when **Product options & personalization** is on in **Settings → Features**. On a new product, save it first. Changes on this tab save right away, without pressing **Save** on the product.

![The Options tab for a Charm Necklace, listing the Chain, Charms and Engraving options with Edit buttons and the Create a new option button](/images/product-options/options-tab.png)

The full guide, including choices, a free charm, engraving, tracking stock and what shoppers see, is on the [Product Options](/docs/product-options) page.

## The SEO tab

The SEO tab controls how the product shows up in Google and when someone shares its link.

![The SEO tab with Meta Tags, Open Graph Image, and the Search Result Preview and Social Media Preview](/images/products/product-seo-tab.png)

### Meta Tags

- **URL Slug** — the end of the product's address. The slug `blue-mug` gives you `/shop/blue-mug`. Each slug must be unique in your store.
- **Meta Title** — the title in search results and browser tabs. The counter counts your business name too, so aim for 60 characters or fewer in total. Leave it blank to use the product name.
- **Meta Description** — the summary in search results. The counter goes up to 160 characters. Leave it blank to use the product description.

### Open Graph Image

The picture used when the product is shared on social media. If you don't add one, the first product photo is used.

### Previews

**Search Result Preview** and **Social Media Preview** show roughly how the product will look in Google and when it's shared.

<Callout type="warn">
Changing the URL of a product that's already published breaks links to the old address, including bookmarks and search results. SimplePress does not set up a redirect, so visitors to the old link see a "not found" page. The form warns you before you save. If the product has been live and shared, think twice.
</Callout>

## The Product page tab

This tab holds the extra content that goes on the product's page. What shows on the storefront depends on your template.

![The Product page tab with the Additional information editor, Feature highlights list, and the Presentation switches](/images/products/product-page-tab.png)

### Additional information

The [text editor](/docs/text-editor) for longer details, like care instructions, sizing, or materials. It has headings, formatting, alignment, links and buttons, pictures, rows and columns, collapsible sections, and tables. Galleries, forms, and embeds are there too when those features are on. There's no video. The Default template shows this under a **Details** section on the product page.

The product **Description** field above is plain text, so it doesn't have any of this.

### Feature highlights

A list of short selling points, each with an icon and a label, like a leaf and "100% Natural". Click **Add feature** to add one. Many templates show these as small badges on the product page.

### Presentation

- **Product tagline** — a short line that goes with the product name. Some templates show it on the product page. It's also added to the product name in the cart, like "Tote Bag - The everyday essential".
- **Coming soon** — on the product page, the add-to-cart area is replaced with a "Coming Soon" notice. The product also moves to the end of your shop list, and checkout refuses it even if someone has the direct link.
- **Featured** — marks the product as featured. Templates that have a featured-products area use it.

## Publishing, scheduling, duplicating, and deleting

### Publishing

The **Published** switch in the toolbar decides whether the product is on your storefront. **New products start with Published switched on**, so switch it off before saving if you're not ready.

An unpublished product:

- doesn't appear on the storefront
- shows a "not found" page, even to someone with the direct link
- is refused at checkout

You can also publish and unpublish several products at once from the list. To go live at about a set time, use [Schedule publish](#schedule-publish).

### Duplicating

**Duplicate** (in the row menu, or **More Options** on the product) makes a copy named "Name (Copy)". The copy is saved as a **draft** and its SKUs are cleared, so nothing goes live until you publish it. If the product has [options](/docs/product-options), the copy uses the same ones. They're shared with the original, not copied, so editing an option later changes it on both.

### Resetting

**More Options → Reset** throws away your unsaved changes and puts the form back to how it was when you opened it. On a new product, it clears the form.

### Deleting

Delete from the row menu or from **More Options → Delete**. A confirmation appears first, and it can't be undone.

**Deleting a product removes:** its variants, photos, collection memberships, reviews, and back-in-stock requests.

**Deleting a product keeps:** your past orders, and your inventory history. Orders still show what the customer bought.

If the product is used as a choice in another product's [options](/docs/product-options#deleting-a-product-thats-used-as-a-choice), deleting it deletes that choice too. The delete dialog warns you first: "This product is used as an option on N product(s). Deleting it removes that option."

## Exporting products (CSV)

<Callout type="info">
Export is only available to the store **Owner**. It's also off by default. To turn it on, go to **Settings → Features** and switch on **Products CSV export**.
</Callout>

With the switch on, an **Export products (CSV)** button appears at the top of the Products list.

![The Export products page with a search box, a Published only checkbox, a list of products with checkboxes, and the Export button](/images/products/export-products.png)

1. Click **Export products (CSV)**.
2. Use the search box to find products. Tick **Published only** to hide drafts.
3. Tick the products you want. The export button appears once you've ticked at least one.
4. Click **Export N Product(s)**.

The file is a WooCommerce-format CSV, so other stores can read it. A product with variants is written as one row for the product plus one row for each variant.

- **Prices** — a product or variant on sale is written with its compare-at price as the WooCommerce "Regular price" and its price as the "Sale price". Products that aren't on sale only have a regular price.
- **Weight** — written in grams, converted from the pounds or kilograms you entered.

If the SimplePress team imports the file back into a store (see below), the sale prices and weights come back too. Weights come back in kilograms, so a product saved as 2 lb returns as about 0.91 kg.

## Importing from another store

Moving from WooCommerce? The SimplePress team runs the import for you. Email **help@generativejustice.org** and attach your WooCommerce product export (CSV). We'll bring your catalog over, so there is no import tool for you to learn.

Here's what carries over:

- **Prices** — if a product has a sale price, the sale price becomes the selling price and the regular price becomes the compare-at price. Otherwise the regular price is the selling price.
- **Variants** — variable products come over with their options and variants.
- **Weight** — the WooCommerce weight in grams is saved in kilograms.
- **Stock** — quantities come over for products that track stock.
- **Photos** — each photo is copied into your SimplePress storage. A photo that can't be downloaded is skipped.
- **Categories** — each category becomes a [collection](/docs/collections).

**Tags and the short description are not kept.** Check your products after the import, then add photos or fix details as needed.

After an import, an **Import history** button appears at the top of your Products list. It shows each import that was run for your store and how it went. You can view it, but you can't run imports yourself.

## How products look on your storefront

Products live at `/shop` (the whole catalog) and `/shop/your-product` (one product). The exact look depends on your template. Here's how the **Default** template behaves. Other templates may style these things differently.

![The Default template's shop grid showing product cards with photos, names, prices, Sold Out badges, and a crossed-out compare-at price](/images/products/storefront-shop.png)

**On the shop grid**, a product card shows the main photo, the name, and the price. If variants have different prices, the price is followed by a "+". A compare-at price appears crossed out, with a **Save N%** badge. A product that's out of stock gets a **Sold Out** badge. There's a heart on each card so shoppers can save products to a wishlist (when Wishlist is on).

![The Default template's product page with photo gallery, price, add-to-cart button, and the Details section](/images/products/storefront-product.png)

**On the product page**, you'll see the photos, price, any variant choices, and an add-to-cart button. Below that, the **Details** section holds your Additional information, and your Feature highlights show as badges.

- **Products with options** show the option pickers (swatches, picture tiles, text boxes) above the add-to-cart button, and the price updates as the shopper chooses. See [What shoppers see](/docs/product-options#what-shoppers-see).
- **Coming soon** products show an amber "Coming Soon" notice instead of add-to-cart, and sort to the end of the shop.
- **Out of stock** products (Track Inventory on, zero left, backorders off) show a disabled **Out of Stock** button and a form to be emailed when it's back, if Back-in-stock is on.
- With **Allow Backorders** on, a product with no stock left shows "Backordered — ships when available" and can still be bought.
- **Unpublished** products show a "not found" page.

Unpublished and coming soon products are also refused at checkout, even if a customer has the link or a stale cart.

**Searching and sorting.** On the Default template, the shop page has a **Search products…** box, a **Collection** list, an **In stock** checkbox, and a **Sort** menu (**Featured**, **A → Z**, **Z → A**, **Price, Low to High**, **Price, High to Low**, **Newest**), with 12 products per page. Search matches only a product's **name** and **description**, so a SKU, collection name or variant option won't find it. If you want products to be found by a word, put it in the name or description. There is no search across the rest of your site. Other templates may lay these controls out differently. See [What Your Customers See](/docs/for-your-customers#searching-filtering-and-sorting-the-shop).

## Who can manage products

Owners and Managers can create, edit, duplicate, publish, and delete products. Only Owners can delete several at once, and only Owners can export. Staff can't open Products. See [Team and Roles](/docs/team-and-roles) for the full list.

## Can and can't

**You can:**

- Add products with up to 10 photos each
- Pick photos from your Media Library, or upload from your device
- Offer sizes, colors, and other options as variants, up to 100 combinations
- Let customers personalize a product with [product options](/docs/product-options): picked choices that add to the price, and typed text like an engraving
- Keep a product as a draft, or schedule it to go live later
- Duplicate a product as a starting point
- Group products into collections, and set SEO titles and descriptions for each
- Track stock, allow backorders, and get low-stock emails
- Export your catalog as a WooCommerce-format CSV (Owner only, once the switch is on)
- Have the SimplePress team import a WooCommerce catalog

**You cannot:**

- Use a slug that another product in your store already uses
- Set a negative stock quantity
- Change an order's items or prices after checkout. The product details are saved at the time of purchase
- Check out a "Coming soon" or unpublished product, even with stock on hand
- Set an automatic redirect from an old product URL to a new one
- Add custom fields beyond what the product form offers (product options cover choices and typed text, not free-form fields)
- Sell a product that has options as a subscription
- Access or edit another store's products
- Customize email templates that mention product data
