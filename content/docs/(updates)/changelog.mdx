This page highlights **documentation** changes and **product** milestones we surface for store owners. Timelines and scope can shift; treat this as a human-readable summary, not a legal commitment.

For release-level detail on the SimplePress codebase, you can also watch [GitHub releases](https://github.com/ahunnii/simple-press/releases).

## 2026-10-12

- **Product:** The Setup Guide is now tailored to your kind of business. The SimplePress team picks it when we set up your store: sells products, services, informational, or donations / nonprofit. Each step is marked **Required** or **Optional**, steps for features you don't use are hidden, and there are new steps for sales tax, shipping or pickup, your first service, donations, contact info and store policies. Until every Required step is done, going to your admin opens the Setup Guide instead of the Dashboard, and the Dashboard card and sidebar "Almost there!" box follow the same list. Stores set up earlier keep their original five steps. See [Your Setup Guide](/docs/getting-started#your-setup-guide).
- **Docs:** [Getting started](/docs/getting-started#your-setup-guide) and the [Launch checklist](/docs/launch-checklist#the-steps) describe the new Setup Guide. [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard), [Glossary](/docs/glossary), [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting), [Tax](/docs/tax), [Shipping](/docs/shipping) and a few other pages were updated to match.
- **Product:** The Dashboard is now customizable. Click **Edit dashboard** to add, remove, resize and rearrange cards, up to 24, and give each card its own time range (Today through All time) and an optional comparison to the previous period. There are new cards for donations, invoices, subscriptions, customers, quote requests, form submissions and visitors, and you can add the same card more than once. Each Owner and Manager has their own layout for each store. Revenue on the Dashboard now matches the Finances page and leaves out cancelled orders, and "Last N days" now counts N calendar days including today in your store's time zone. See [Customizing Your Dashboard](/docs/dashboard#customizing-your-dashboard).
- **Docs:** [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard) rewritten around the customizable layout, with a full list of cards. [Inventory Management](/docs/inventory-management#low-stock-alerts), [Analytics](/docs/analytics), [Finances](/docs/finances), [General Settings](/docs/general-settings) and [Weekly, Monthly & Yearly Checklist](/docs/owner-routines) were updated to match.
- **Product:** Invoices now have an **Activity** tab: one log of everything that happened to all your invoices (created, sent, viewed, reminded, paid, cancelled), with who did it, filters for invoice or customer, event and dates, and a CSV export. Each invoice's Activity card has a **View all activity →** link to it. See [Invoice activity](/docs/invoices#invoice-activity).
- **Docs:** [Invoices](/docs/invoices#invoice-activity) has a new Invoice Activity section, and [Weekly, Monthly & Yearly Checklist](/docs/owner-routines) now includes tallying invoice payments each month.

## 2026-10-11

- **Product:** Discount code uses are now counted when the shopper goes to the payment page, not when the order is placed. The use is held while the checkout is open (up to 30 minutes) or a bank payment is clearing (up to 14 days), and is given back automatically if the checkout times out or the payment fails. A **Usage Limit** can no longer be passed by several shoppers checking out at once, and **Limit uses per customer** counts that customer's open checkouts too. The **Usage** column shows held uses, like "1 / 1 (1 pending)", and a code that is only full because of pending checkouts stays active. Reward codes in use show **Pending** instead of **Used** on the customer's Rewards page and loyalty card until the order is final. If a shopper goes back from the payment page and continues again, their earlier checkout is closed and its held use moves to the new one, so they can still use a single-use or once-per-customer code. That closed checkout doesn't get an abandoned checkout email (a checkout that wasn't holding any stock or code isn't closed, and can still get one).
- **Docs:** [Discounts & Coupons](/docs/discounts-and-coupons#how-uses-are-counted) has a new How Uses Are Counted section, and [Rewards Program](/docs/rewards) and [Payments & Payouts](/docs/payments-and-payouts#bank-debits-and-other-delayed-payment-methods) mention held and pending uses.
- **Product:** SimplePress is now open source under the AGPL-3.0 license. The Terms of Service and Seller & Merchant Agreement were updated to say so, and SimplePress's policy pages now list help@generativejustice.org as the contact address. See [Your Agreement with SimplePress](/docs/your-agreement).
- **Docs:** New pages: [Finding Your Way Around the Admin](/docs/admin-basics), [Your Agreement with SimplePress](/docs/your-agreement) (a plain-language summary of the terms and who is responsible for what), [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting), [Weekly, Monthly & Yearly Checklist](/docs/owner-routines), [Added as Staff or a Manager](/docs/for-staff), and [If Your Store Is Paused or Closed](/docs/leaving-or-closing).
- **Docs:** [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started) now describes how you actually get into your store: an emailed invitation, or a claim link if your site came from Artisanal Futures.
- **Docs:** [What Your Customers See](/docs/for-your-customers) now covers shop search, filters and sorting, what shoppers can change on Stripe's payment page, checkout messages, why an order-lookup email might not arrive, sign-in checks blocked by ad blockers, and that checkout has no order-notes box.
- **Product:** Checkout now supports bank debits (ACH Direct Debit) and other delayed payment methods. The shopper sees an "Order received" page and a "Your order is processing" email, the items are held for up to 14 days, and the order appears in your Orders list once the money clears. If the payment fails, nothing is created, the items are released, and the shopper and you are emailed. There are two new emails you can reword in **Admin → Emails**: **Bank Payment Processing** and **Bank Payment Failed**. See [Bank debits and other delayed payment methods](/docs/payments-and-payouts#bank-debits-and-other-delayed-payment-methods).
- **Docs:** [Payments & Payouts](/docs/payments-and-payouts#bank-debits-and-other-delayed-payment-methods), [Stripe account setup](/docs/stripe/account-setup), [What Your Customers See](/docs/for-your-customers) and [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) now explain bank payments instead of asking you to keep them off.
- **Docs:** Scheduled products and blog posts go live in the time zone of the device you scheduled from, up to about 15 minutes after the time you pick. Added a checklist for if you think someone else got into your account to [Your Account & Security](/docs/your-account-and-security).
- **Docs:** Corrections: Galleries is under **Marketing** in the sidebar, SEO & Meta is under **Site Setup**, the setup box also waits for a custom domain, and every Owner sees the terms screen once.
- **Docs:** Settings pages checked against the current app, with new screenshots: [General Settings](/docs/general-settings) (time zone, structured address, map pin), [Shipping](/docs/shipping) (zone + weight rates, in-store pickup, delivery times and returns), [Custom Domain](/docs/domain), [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags), [Integrations](/docs/integrations) (Umami and QuickBooks), [Team & Roles](/docs/team-and-roles) (with a short video of inviting a team member), [Data & Export](/docs/data-and-export), and [Your Account & Security](/docs/your-account-and-security).
- **Docs:** New pages: [Business Hours](/docs/business-hours), [Maintenance Mode & Coming Soon](/docs/maintenance-mode), and [Accessibility and Your Site](/docs/accessibility).
- **Docs:** [Notification Emails](/docs/email-preview) now has an [Emails You Receive](/docs/email-preview#emails-you-receive) section listing every alert sent to you and your team, who gets it, and whether it can be switched off.
- **Docs:** [Your Account & Security](/docs/your-account-and-security) no longer describes changing your sign-in email or Discord sign-in, which aren't available. Ask the SimplePress team if you need your sign-in email changed.
- **Product:** Settings and editing fixes. Shipping now shows clear messages under the field when pickup instructions, a flat rate or a free shipping threshold are missing. [Maintenance Mode](/docs/maintenance-mode) tells you before you save whether your store is about to go offline or come back online, and the Umami badge shows what's saved. Turning off [Products, Orders or Shopping Cart & Checkout](/docs/feature-flags) now also turns off the features that depend on them, such as Payments and Coupons & Discounts, and Finances stays visible for stores with [Donations](/docs/donations) on. Gallery and product photos no longer count a leftover file name as alt text, and clearing alt text or a caption now saves. Pickup locations, Notification Email subject lines, and team invitation wording are more consistent.
- **Docs:** The Settings pages were updated to match, including [Business Hours](/docs/business-hours), [Shipping](/docs/shipping), [Maintenance Mode](/docs/maintenance-mode), [Integrations](/docs/integrations), [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags), [Finances](/docs/finances), [Notification Emails](/docs/email-preview) and [Accessibility](/docs/accessibility).
- **Product:** [Finances](/docs/finances#picking-a-time-period) now lets you pick last year (**Calendar year 2025**) in the range selector, so last year's totals are still there after January 1. The Set aside year-to-date tile now names the year it covers. Staff no longer see the **Almost there!** setup box, which they can't open.
- **Docs:** [Weekly, Monthly & Yearly Checklist](/docs/owner-routines) no longer asks you to write down your totals in late December.

- **Docs:** Reorganized [Inventory Management](/docs/inventory-management) and corrected check-ins, units sold and staff access, with a short video of checking rental items out and back in.
- **Docs:** New [Services](/docs/services) page: service pages, specific services, price options, add-ons, and booking through your own booking tool.
- **Docs:** Updated [Collections](/docs/collections) and added real screenshots across the Catalog pages.
- **Product:** Product CSV exports now keep sale prices (the compare-at price goes in "Regular price", the selling price in "Sale price") and convert pound weights correctly. See [Exporting products](/docs/products#exporting-products-csv).
- **Product:** Products can only draw stock from **stock** items, not rental items. A stock item that products sell from can't be turned into a rental. See [Linking a product to an item](/docs/inventory-management#linking-a-product-to-an-item).
- **Product:** The "Products CSV import/export" switch is now called **Products CSV export**. If the SimplePress team has imported products for you, an **Import history** button on the Products list shows how each import went.
- **Docs:** Edit Your Site pages checked against the current app, with new screenshots: [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor), [Brand & Appearance](/docs/storefront-and-branding), a new [Navigation](/docs/navigation) page, [Template Fields](/docs/template-fields) (now a field-type reference), [Pages & Policies](/docs/pages-and-policies), [Blog](/docs/blog), [Galleries](/docs/galleries), [Media Library](/docs/media-library), [Banner & Popup](/docs/banner-and-popup), and [Contact Form](/docs/contact-form).
- **Product:** SVG files are now accepted for logos.
- **Product:** A banner or popup link with a blank label now shows "Learn more".
- **Product:** The rich-text **Insert elements** menu only offers Gallery, Embed, Quote calculator, and Form when those features are on. It doesn't appear at all in banner, popup, and policy text.
- **Product:** FAQ hints now point to **Site Setup → FAQ**.
- **Product:** Policies can now be hidden with a **Show on your site** switch or deleted with **Remove policy**. A blank policy is never shown or linked. See [Hiding or removing a policy](/docs/pages-and-policies).
- **Product:** Custom pages can no longer use a web address that belongs to a built-in page (such as /about, /contact or /faq), since those pages could never be reached.
- **Product:** Navigation **Quick Add** now includes **Events**, **Videos** and **FAQ**. On templates with their own footer list, the footer option reads **Use template's default links** and names the links. See [Navigation](/docs/navigation).
- **Product:** A gallery that's only saved in templates you aren't using can now be deleted. The Site Editor page list shows **FAQ** instead of "Faq", and an old logo file you've replaced can be deleted from the Media Library.
- **Product:** Rich-text editors no longer show "Unsaved Changes" when nothing was changed.
- **Docs:** New Edit Your Site pages: [FAQ](/docs/faq), [Forms](/docs/forms), [Events](/docs/events), [Videos](/docs/videos), and [Embeds](/docs/embeds), with screenshots and two short Forms videos.
- **Product:** YouTube links pasted as an [embed](/docs/embeds) now play on your site, and event fliers show on the Events page.
- **Product:** A YouTube handle on its own (like `@yourhandle`) now works when adding a channel in [Videos](/docs/videos).
- **Product:** An embed, form, gallery, or quote calculator box you insert but never fill in is left out when a page saves, and save buttons wait (**Uploading…**) while a picture in your text is still uploading.

- **Docs:** New Grow pages: [Rewards Program](/docs/rewards), [Email Marketing](/docs/email-marketing) (including [abandoned checkout emails](/docs/email-marketing#abandoned-checkout-emails)), [Quote Calculators](/docs/quote-calculators), [Wishlist & Back-in-Stock Alerts](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock), [SEO & Search Readiness](/docs/seo), and [Analytics](/docs/analytics).
- **Docs:** Rewrote [Discounts & Coupons](/docs/discounts-and-coupons), [Product Reviews](/docs/reviews), and [Testimonials](/docs/testimonials) for the current app. Owners can turn coupons and reviews on themselves, and reviews and testimonials have approval, filters and bulk actions.
- **Docs:** New [Finances](/docs/finances) page: every card on the admin Finances page, the Tax Guide and the INFORM gauge, with a short video tour.
- **Docs:** [Payments & Payouts](/docs/payments-and-payouts) rewritten: how a payment becomes a payout, fees (SimplePress takes none), payments paused, and a new **Disputes** section.
- **Docs:** [Tax](/docs/tax) rewritten: set up Stripe Tax before turning on the switch, what gets taxed, and updated nexus thresholds.
- **Docs:** [Stripe Setup](/docs/stripe) pages updated to Stripe's current Dashboard labels, the Developer role's real access, and payout timing.
- **Product:** On [Finances](/docs/finances), "Net added to your balance" is now **Net from sales**: what your sales earned after refunds, fees and disputes, with payouts left out. The year-to-date tax tile now takes refunds off, and a **Tax Guide** button sits at the top of the page.
- **Product:** The [INFORM Act gauge](/docs/finances#inform-act-compliance) now covers the last 12 months and needs both 200 sales and $5,000, as the FTC rule does. Cancelled orders no longer count, and the alert and bars both turn amber at 75%.
- **Product:** The in-app Tax Guide now lists the setup steps in the right order (Stripe Tax settings, then registrations, then the switch), with Florida's $100,000 threshold and Stripe's filing service. See [Tax](/docs/tax).
- **Product:** Connecting Stripe now shows a message when you come back from Stripe: connected, cancelled, or what went wrong. See [Integrations](/docs/integrations).
- **Product:** Orders can be filtered by **Disputed** payment, and disputed orders show a red badge. Their payment status can't be overridden while the dispute is open. Dispute and payments-paused emails now also go to every Owner and Manager. See [Disputes](/docs/payments-and-payouts#disputes).
