This page highlights **documentation** changes and **product** milestones we surface for store owners. Timelines and scope can shift; treat this as a human-readable summary, not a legal commitment.

For release-level detail on the SimplePress codebase, you can also watch [GitHub releases](https://github.com/ahunnii/simple-press/releases).

## 2026-10-26

- **Product:** Products can have up to **3 videos** alongside their photos. Open a product's **Photos & videos** card, click **Add**, and upload MP4, WebM or MOV files up to 50 MB each, up to 10 items in all. The first item must be a photo, since it's what shows on product cards and at checkout. Each video has a **Description** field that screen readers read out, and it plays muted on a loop in your shop. Shoppers can pause it, turn the sound on, or open it larger. You can also pick videos from your Media Library.
- **Product:** **Local delivery** lets you deliver orders yourself to customers who live near you. Turn it on under **Settings → Shipping**, then set the ZIP code you deliver from, your delivery radius (up to 100 miles), a delivery fee, optional **closer prices** for nearby customers, an optional minimum order, and the delivery days and instructions shoppers see at checkout and in their confirmation email. Customers inside your radius can choose it at checkout, alongside shipping and pickup. On the order, **Mark as Delivered** finishes it off, and you can email the customer.
- **Product:** You can bring your contact list over from your old website. The SimplePress team imports a file from Wix, Shopify, Square or a spreadsheet, and never adds those people to your email list. Each import gets one invite email, "Still want to hear from [your store]?", and only people who press **Yes, keep me on the list** join. Anyone who unsubscribed on the old site stays blocked. Owners and Managers can see past imports under **Import history** on the Customers page.
- **Docs:** [Products](/docs/products#add-a-video) covers videos, [Shipping](/docs/shipping#local-delivery) and [Orders](/docs/orders#local-delivery-orders) cover local delivery, [What Your Customers See](/docs/for-your-customers) covers the local delivery checkout, and [Email Marketing](/docs/email-marketing#bring-your-list-from-your-old-website) and [Customers](/docs/customers#imported-contacts) cover importing contacts.

## 2026-10-19

- **Product:** Owners and Managers can reply to product reviews. In **Reviews**, open a review's **…** menu and choose **Reply**. Your reply shows under that review on the product page as a response from your store. Each review gets one reply of up to 2,000 characters, which you can edit or remove. The reviewer isn't emailed.
- **Product:** Color options can show the exact color dot for each shade. If a product has an option named Color, Colors, Shade, Finish or similar, its variant section has **Color swatches**. Pick the color for each one, or leave it on **Auto** and SimplePress matches common color names. Only templates that show color dots for variants use them.
- **Docs:** [Reviews](/docs/reviews#replying-to-a-review) and [Products](/docs/products#color-swatches) cover these.

## 2026-10-22

- **Product:** Email Marketing has a new **Email style** page. Pick your accent color, page background, font (sans or serif), header (logo centered, logo left, or store name), logo size, whether to show your social links, and a short footer note, with a live preview you can switch between **Desktop** and **Mobile**. Every template starts with its own look, and **Reset to template default** brings it back. Only campaigns use it; receipts and other order emails look the same as before.
- **Product:** The campaign editor can show the whole email before you send it, including the header, footer, address and unsubscribe link, on desktop and mobile widths. Three new blocks are made for email: **Products** (pick up to four products; prices, photos and names are refreshed from your shop when the email sends, and hidden or deleted products are left out), **Space** (small, medium or large) and **Button** (in your accent color).
- **Product:** **New campaign** now opens a page of starting points: **Start from scratch**, **Sale**, **New arrival**, **Event** and **Newsletter**, with the parts to replace in [square brackets]. If any brackets are left in your subject, preview text or message, an amber note reminds you before you send, without blocking the send. You can also **Duplicate** any campaign from the list or from its page.
- **Product:** You can choose who gets each campaign: **All subscribers**, **Customers who've ordered**, **Subscribers who haven't ordered**, **Bought specific products**, **Haven't ordered in…** or **Signed up in the last…**, with a live count. Only people subscribed to marketing are ever included, and people who unsubscribed or bounced are always left out.
- **Product:** Campaigns can be scheduled. **Schedule…** sits next to **Send now** and lets you pick a date and time in your store's time zone, from 10 minutes to 90 days ahead. It goes out within about 15 minutes of that time to whoever matches the audience at that moment. A scheduled campaign is locked until it goes out; **Unschedule to edit** moves it back to your drafts so you can change it, and its page shows the full email preview. If something is wrong at send time, such as a missing business address or nobody matching the audience, the campaign is marked **Failed** with the reason. **Send now** and **Schedule…** are greyed out while a Products block has no products in it.
- **Product:** Your social links now include **Threads**, and **Brand & Appearance** has a **Google Business Profile** link for the listing that shows on Google Maps. If a social link looks like it goes to the wrong site, you'll see a warning, though you can still save it. Google is also told about your social profiles, your Google Business Profile link, and your short name for page titles when it differs from your business name.

## 2026-10-16

- **Product:** Site search is new, and it's off by default. Turn it on under **Settings → Features** with the **Site search** switch in the Content section. Once it's on, your header gets a search icon that opens a quick search box, and there's a full results page at `/search`. Visitors can search your products, services, pages and blog posts, events, FAQs and collections, and only what's published and switched on shows up.
- **Docs:** New page: [Site Search](/docs/site-search), covering the search box, the results page and what's searched.
- **Product:** The text editor has more ways to move and add things. **Undo** and **Redo** are at the start of the toolbar, handy on phones and tablets. **More formatting** has **Highlight**, **Superscript** and **Subscript**. Point at a paragraph, heading, list, picture, row or collapsible section and you'll see a **+** button and a grip in the left margin: drag the grip to move that piece up or down the page, including into or between columns, or open the grip's menu for **Move up**, **Move down**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**. On a computer, Option+↑ and Option+↓ move the piece your cursor is in. Type **/** on an empty line to open a quick menu of everything you can add. Columns and collapsible sections keep an empty line at the bottom, so you can keep typing after a picture. Text color is no longer in the toolbar for pages, products, blog posts and other website text; it's still in the email campaign editor. Text that was already colored keeps its place, but shows in your template's colors.
- **Docs:** [Text Editor](/docs/text-editor) has new sections on moving things around and the **/** quick insert menu, plus updated toolbar, shortcut and FAQ entries for these changes.
- **Product:** Blank lines you add in the text editor now show on your site. Each empty line is one line of space, and Shift+Enter at the end of a line keeps its new line. Blank lines at the very start or end are still left out. There's also a new **Space** block in the **Insert elements** menu for a set gap (Small, Medium, or Large). Click it in the editor to change its size or remove it.

- **Product:** Email Marketing now works with campaigns. Write one with a subject, optional preview text and a formatted message, save it as a draft, send yourself a test, then send it. Sending goes out in the background over the next few minutes, so you can leave the page, and you can cancel a campaign while it's still sending. Every campaign is kept in a history, with who sent it and what happened to each person's email (delivered, bounced, marked as spam, unsubscribed, failed or skipped). You need a business address in **Settings → General** before you can send, because the law requires a mailing address in marketing emails.
- **Product:** There are new ways for customers to opt in. Checkout has an unticked **Email me news and offers** box (shown only when Email Marketing is on), and the Elegant and Wealth templates have newsletter sign-up forms that email a confirmation first. Unsubscribe links also work in one click from mail apps, and the unsubscribe page shows your store's logo or name. Addresses that bounce or mark an email as spam are stopped automatically, and the **Audience** card shows how many. The opted-in list export and the "Download your site" email subscribers sheet now say how and when each person opted in.
- **Product:** Email Marketing is now more careful about consent. Clicking **Unsubscribe** in an email opens a confirm page, and nothing changes until the person presses **Unsubscribe**. Each customer has an **Email marketing** card showing whether they're **Subscribed**, **Unsubscribed**, **Not subscribed** or **Suppressed** (yellow, for bounces, spam reports and blocked addresses), plus a **Recent activity** history of when and how they joined or left. Owners and Managers can **Mark as unsubscribed** someone who asks by reply, phone or in person. Unsubscribed and suppressed addresses go on a do-not-email list that survives anonymizing a customer. Abandoned checkout emails now have an **Unsubscribe** link and your mailing address, aren't sent to people who unsubscribed, and are skipped until you add a full address in **Settings → General**. Newsletter forms and the checkout box show a short consent note with a link to your privacy policy, and the Privacy template adds lines about marketing emails when the feature is on. With Email Marketing turned off, your history and subscriber export stay available and any campaign that's mid-send pauses until you turn it back on. Fixes in the same pass: unsubscribing from an abandoned checkout email now works for people who never subscribed, links to your own pages in a campaign (like `/contact`) become full links, Wealth's sign-up form shows a neutral "coming soon" message while the feature is off, and Elegant's consent note links to your own privacy policy.

## 2026-10-11

- **Product:** Discount code uses are now counted when the shopper goes to the payment page, not when the order is placed. The use is held while the checkout is open (up to 30 minutes) or a bank payment is clearing (up to 14 days), and is given back automatically if the checkout times out or the payment fails. A **Usage Limit** can no longer be passed by several shoppers checking out at once, and **Limit uses per customer** counts that customer's open checkouts too. The **Usage** column shows held uses, like "1 / 1 (1 pending)", and a code that is only full because of pending checkouts stays active. Reward codes in use show **Pending** instead of **Used** on the customer's Rewards page and loyalty card until the order is final. If a shopper goes back from the payment page and continues again, their earlier checkout is closed and its held use moves to the new one, so they can still use a single-use or once-per-customer code. That closed checkout doesn't get an abandoned checkout email (a checkout that wasn't holding any stock or code isn't closed, and can still get one).
