## Overview

The Media Library shows every image and video uploaded to your store, whether it was added on a product, a gallery, a page, or here directly. Use it to find, download, and clean up files. You can also upload a batch of photos up front and pick them later wherever you need them.

---

## Browsing Your Media

Go to **Media Library** in the admin sidebar. Files appear as a grid of cards with a thumbnail, file name, size, upload date, and a usage badge.

- **Type:** Images, Videos, Logo, Favicon, Testimonials, Gallery, or Other
- **Usage:**
  - **In Use:** shown somewhere on your store.
  - **Inactive template only:** only used by a template you're no longer using.
  - **Unused:** not used anywhere.
- **Sort:** newest, oldest, name, or file size
- **Search:** by file name, or by where a file is used. For example, searching a product's name finds its photos.

Hover over a usage badge to see exactly where a file is used.

## Uploading Images

Owners and managers can click **Upload images** to add photos straight to the library.

- Drag and drop images into the upload area, or click to choose them
- Up to **50 images** at a time. If you pick more, the first 50 upload and you can add the rest afterwards.
- Up to **5MB** per image. Large phone photos are resized automatically and HEIC photos are converted. Any image still over 5MB is listed as skipped.
- Only images can be uploaded here. Other files are skipped with a message. Videos are still added where they're used, such as an event or a video page.
- Keep the upload window open until it finishes. A progress bar shows how far along it is. When everything uploads, the window closes and your new images appear at the top of the library.

## Using Library Images

Anywhere you can add an image in the admin, open the upload menu and choose **Choose from library**. That includes product images, collection images, galleries, pages, blog posts, [services](/docs/services) (cover images and service item images), SEO images, and template content fields. Product images and galleries let you select several at once.

The same image can be used in as many places as you like.

---

## How Files Are Kept

- **Images uploaded in the Media Library stay until you delete them here.** Removing one from a product or gallery, or deleting that product or gallery, never removes it from your library.
- **Images uploaded directly on a product or gallery** are cleaned up automatically once nothing uses them any more.

---

## Downloading and Deleting

- **Download** saves a copy of the file to your computer.
- **Delete** is only available for files that aren't in use. Remove the file from everywhere it's used first; the usage badge shows where. Deleting is permanent.
- **Your logo and favicon** are always protected. Replace them by uploading a new one in branding settings.
- **Bulk delete** (owners only): tick the cards, or use **Select all on page**, then click **Delete**. You can delete up to 25 files at a time. Any selected files that are in use are skipped and stay selected, so you can see which ones survived.

---

## Feature Flag

The Media Library is **on by default** and can be turned off in **Settings > Features**. Turning it off hides the Media Library page and the **Choose from library** options. Your files are not deleted.
