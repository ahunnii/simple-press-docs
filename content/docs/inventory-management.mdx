## Overview

SimplePress gives you two ways to track stock:

- **Per-product inventory.** Each product (or each variant) has its own stock count. Use this when a product's stock doesn't affect anything else.
- **Inventory items.** An item is a stock count that lives on its own on the **Inventory** page. You can leave it standalone (supplies, rentable equipment) or link products to it so several products draw from one shared count.

You can use either approach, or both, across different products in your catalog. A third piece, **Rental Check-outs**, sits on top of items and lets you lend rental items out for an event and check them back in.

---

## Per-Product Inventory

### Where to find it

Go to **Products** in the admin sidebar and open a product. On the **Basics** tab, the **Inventory** card is in the right-hand column, below the images. It starts with the **SKU** and **Barcode (UPC/EAN/GTIN)** fields. If the product has variants, those two fields move to each variant row instead, and the card says so.

![The Inventory card on a product's Basics tab with SKU, Barcode, the Inventory item box, Track Inventory switched on, and the Allow Backorders, Inventory Quantity and Low Inventory Threshold fields below](/images/inventory-management/product-inventory-card.png)

### The Inventory card fields

After **SKU** and **Barcode** (and, if Inventory Management is on and you have stock items, the **Inventory item** box; see [Linking a product to an item](#linking-a-product-to-an-item)), the card has, in this order:

1. **Track Inventory** ("Enable inventory tracking for this product"). Off by default.
   - **Off:** The product is always treated as in stock. Customers can always add it to their cart and check out, whatever quantity you have entered.
   - **On:** SimplePress enforces your stock. Customers can't check out something with no stock left (unless you also allow backorders).
2. **Allow Backorders** ("Allow customers to order when out of stock"). Only shown while tracking is on. See [Allow Backorders](#allow-backorders) below.
3. **Inventory Quantity.** The number of units you have on hand. This field is for products without variants; you can't enter a negative number.
4. **Low Inventory Threshold.** The stock level that triggers a low-stock email. It must be 1 or more; leave it blank for no low-stock email. See [Low-Stock Alerts](#low-stock-alerts).

Tracking is a product-level setting. There is no per-variant toggle, no per-variant backorder rule and no per-variant threshold. If your product line mixes tracked and untracked options, make them separate products.

If you link the product to an inventory item, these individual controls are replaced by the item's stock. See [Linking a product to an item](#linking-a-product-to-an-item).

### Variants

A product with variants has no single quantity. Each variant has its own **Stock** field in the **Product Variants** card, and these only show while **Track Inventory** is on.

To set several at once, tick the checkboxes next to the variants. A **Bulk edit:** bar appears with a **Qty** box and a **Set Qty** button (next to a price box and **Set Price**, and a **Delete selected** button). This is handy when you restock a whole product line.

![The Product Variants card with two variants ticked and the Bulk edit bar showing the Qty box and the Set Qty button](/images/inventory-management/variant-stock-bulk-edit.png)

### Allow Backorders

- **Off (default):** Once stock reaches zero, customers can't add the product to their cart and it shows as out of stock on the storefront.
- **On:** Customers can keep ordering when stock is zero or lower. This suits made-to-order products, pre-orders, or anything you're happy to fulfil after restocking. Stock counts down past zero, so a variant showing `−3` means three more units were ordered than you had.

### Stock badges on the storefront

- A simple product with backorders on shows a **Pre-order** badge only when its stock is exactly 0. Below zero there is no badge.
- **Coming Soon** and **Sale** badges take priority over **Out of Stock** and **Pre-order**.
- For a product with variants, **Out of Stock** appears only when *every* variant is at zero. A single sold-out variant is just unavailable in the variant picker.

## Inventory Items

### What an item is

An inventory item is a supply or piece of equipment you track on the **Inventory** page. Each item is one of two types:

- **Stock** (the default): consumable supplies. You can **Use** units (take them out) and **Restock** units (add them back).
- **Rental**: returnable things you lend out and get back, like a throne, chairs or tables. Rental items can be adjusted and restocked, but have no **Use** action. They can also be checked out; see [Rental Check-outs](#rental-check-outs).

### When to use an item

Use a **standalone item** when:

- You want to track supplies or equipment that aren't sold directly as products (rentables, supplies for internal use).
- You want a change history and a unit cost on record for budgeting or auditing.

Use **shared stock** (products linked to an item) when:

- You sell the same underlying stock in different sizes or configurations.
- Running out of that stock should mark several products out of stock at once.
- You want one place to manage and restock a shared resource.

Use **per-product inventory** when each product has its own stock that doesn't affect any other product.

**Example:** You stock fabric in 4-yard rolls and sell it as a 12-yard cut (3 rolls), a 24-yard cut (6 rolls) and a 48-yard cut (12 rolls). Create one item called "Fabric Rolls" with your roll count, then link all three products to it with units-consumed values of 3, 6 and 12. When a 12-yard cut sells, 3 rolls come off the item.

### The Items page

Go to **Inventory** in the admin sidebar. The page has up to three tabs: **Items**, **Check-outs** (only when Rental Check-outs is on) and **Activity**. It opens with the description "Track standalone stock and rentals, and link products to share an item's stock." Owners and Managers also see **Import**, **Export** and **New item** buttons. Until you've made one, the page shows "No inventory items yet" with a **Create item** button.

![The Inventory page on the Items tab with a mix of Stock and Rental items, showing the search box, Filters button, a low-stock count in orange, the Out / Total, Unit cost and Units sold columns, the ⋮ menu pinned at the end of each row, and the New item, Import and Export buttons](/images/inventory-management/items-list.png)

**Searching and filtering.** The **Search items…** box matches an item's name, description, SKU, category and location. The **Filters** popover has:

- **Stock:** All items, Low or out of stock, Out of stock.
- **Availability:** All items, **Unsellable (reserved out)** (items where every unit is held by storefront checkouts in progress).
- **Type:** All types, Stock, Rental.
- **Category:** All categories, or one of yours.
- **Sort:** Name A–Z (the default), Name Z–A, Newest, Oldest, Lowest stock, Highest stock, Most units sold, Fewest units sold, Most products, Fewest products, SKU A–Z, Category A–Z.

The list shows 25 items per page. The **Low or out of stock** filter uses only each item's own **Low-stock threshold**. An item with no threshold set counts as "low" only once it is out of stock. (The dashboard widget works a little differently; see [Low-Stock Alerts](#low-stock-alerts).)

**Columns.** Narrow screens show fewer columns; the rest appear as the window gets wider.

| Column | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Name | With the SKU and description underneath |
| Type | Stock or Rental |
| Category | |
| Location | |
| On hand | The count, an **Adjust** button, and "N reserved" when storefront checkouts are holding some |
| Out / Total | Rental items, only when Rental Check-outs is on: units currently checked out, and on hand plus out |
| Unit cost | Owner and Manager only |
| Units sold | Net units sold through orders (see [Units sold](#units-sold)) |
| Products | How many products draw from the item. Owner and Manager only |

At the end of each row, the **⋮** menu stays pinned to the right edge. On wide screens, where every column shows, scroll the table sideways to see the columns under it. The menu has **View details**, **Use** (stock items), **Restock**, **Check out** (rental items, when Rental Check-outs is on), **Edit**, and **Delete** (Owner and Manager).

> **Backorders:** Items don't support backorders in the admin. If an item reaches zero, products linked to it show as out of stock until you restock it.

### Creating and editing an item

Click **New item** (or **Edit** in the ⋮ menu). The dialog is titled **New item** or **Edit item**.

- **Type:** **Stock** ("Consumable supplies you use up") or **Rental** ("Items you lend out and get back"). If Rental Check-outs is off, Rental is greyed out with a note to turn it on under **Settings → Features**.
- **Name** (required), **SKU** (optional), **Category** (optional), **Storage location** (optional).
- **Unit cost / replacement value.** Shown only to Owners and Managers.
- **Description** (optional).
- **Starting quantity.** New items only. It appears in the history as "Initial count".
- **Low-stock threshold** (optional). Triggers the low-stock email; see [Low-Stock Alerts](#low-stock-alerts).

Click **Create** or **Save changes**. Two type changes are blocked:

- **Rental to stock** while units are still checked out. Check them in first.
- **Stock to rental** while products are linked to the item. Rental is greyed out with a note such as "1 product sells from this item. Unlink it to make it a rental." Unlink the products first (see [Linking a product to an item](#linking-a-product-to-an-item)).

### Use, Restock and Adjust

**Use** and **Restock** (⋮ menu) are titled "Use: [name]" and "Restock: [name]". Both ask for a **Quantity** and an optional **Note**, and show "On hand: N · Available: N". Available means on hand minus anything held by storefront checkouts in progress. **Use** can't take more than what's available ("Only N available"), so it never takes units a shopper is paying for.

**Adjust** (the button next to the count) is titled "Adjust: [name]". It shows "Current quantity: N" and sets an absolute count rather than adding or subtracting, which is what you want after a physical count. For a rental item, the number you set is the count on hand; units out on check-outs aren't included.

- **New quantity**
- **Reason:** Restock, Adjustment (the default), Correction, Damage / Loss, or Return. In the history, "Damage / Loss" shows as **Damaged**.
- **Note** (optional)

Click **Update** to save the new count.

**Stale-count protection.** When you open the dialog, SimplePress remembers the current count. If a sale, check-out or someone else's adjustment changes it before you save, the save is refused and a message tells you the count changed to N and to review and re-enter. The **New quantity** box is also overwritten with the fresh count, so you need to type your number again.

![The Adjust dialog with the current quantity, a new quantity entered, the Reason set to Restock, an optional note, and the Cancel and Update buttons](/images/inventory-management/adjust-dialog.png)

### Deleting an item

**Delete** asks you to confirm. Deleting an item removes it permanently, along with its movement history. Products linked to it are detached and immediately set to out of stock under individual tracking. You can't delete an item while units are checked out.

### The item detail page

Click an item's name (or **View details**) to open its page. The header has **Adjust**, **Use**, **Restock**, **Check out** and **Edit** buttons, as they apply to that item's type and your role. There is no Delete button here; use the ⋮ menu on the list.

![A rental item's detail page showing the header buttons, the Total, Out and Available cards, the Details card and the Currently out table](/images/inventory-management/item-detail.png)

**Cards across the top.**

- **Stock items:** On hand, Reserved (only shown if above 0), Units sold (net), and Returned.
- **Rental items:** Total, Out, Available and Reserved. There is no "On hand" card. **Total** is on hand plus out, **Out** is units on open check-outs, and **Available** is on hand minus reserved.

If every unit is reserved, a "Nothing available to sell" alert appears.

**Details card.** Type, SKU, Category, Storage location, Unit cost / replacement value (Owner and Manager only), and **Low stock alert at**.

**Currently out.** For rental items, a table of the check-outs the units are on.

**Linked products.** A table of the products drawing from the item: **Product**, **Units per purchase** and **Status**. Staff see these as text only.

**Movement history.** Every change to the item's quantity, 25 per page:

- Columns: Date, Reason, Change, Resulting qty, Reference (the order or check-out, if any), By, Note.
- Reasons you'll see: Sale, Oversell (not deducted), Return, Adjustment, Correction, Damaged, Restock, Used, Import, Checked out, Checked in, Lost, and Initial count.
- Damaged and Lost rows from a check-in show a change of 0 on this page, because the units already left the on-hand count at check-out. The Activity log and a check-out's own History show "—" for those rows.

### Units sold

**Units sold** is net: Sale rows minus order-linked Return rows (from refunds and cancellations that returned items to inventory). Manual movements never change it, so Use, Restock and Adjust don't distort your sales numbers. Oversell rows aren't counted as units; they only trigger a warning on the Units sold column ("N sale(s) could not be deducted — units sold may be understated").

### Linking a product to an item

A simple product (no variants) can draw its stock from a **stock** item. You'll only see the selector if Inventory Management is on and you have at least one stock item.

1. Open the product and find the **Inventory** card on the **Basics** tab.
2. In the **Inventory item** box, pick an item from **Item**. The default is "None — use individual inventory". Each option reads "[name] ([qty] units)".
3. Enter how many units the product uses each time it sells in **Units consumed per purchase**.
4. Save the product.

![The Inventory card with an item selected, the Units consumed per purchase field, and the note that individual inventory tracking is disabled](/images/inventory-management/product-item-link.png)

Once linked, the note "Individual inventory tracking is disabled while an inventory item is selected. Manage its stock from the Inventory page." replaces the product's own quantity controls.

- **Products with variants can't be linked.** The selector is hidden while a product has variants.
- **Rental items can't be linked.** They're lent out and come back, so a sale shouldn't subtract from them. Rental items don't appear in the picker, and the product won't save if you try to link one ("Rental items can't be linked to a product. Choose a stock item.").
- A product that was linked to a rental item before this rule still shows that link, so you can unlink it. Saving other changes on it works as usual.
- If Inventory Management is switched off, the selector only appears on a product that's already linked, so you can unlink it.

### How item stock shows on the storefront

The storefront works out how many of a linked product a customer can buy from the item's count and the product's units-consumed value:

```
Available to purchase = floor(item quantity ÷ units consumed per purchase)
```

With 10 rolls and a product that uses 6 rolls, the storefront shows 1 available. If that comes to zero, the product shows as out of stock.

> **Cart note:** If a customer has several linked products in their cart that together need more than the item has, the cart doesn't warn them, because each product is checked on its own there. At checkout, SimplePress adds up everything drawing on the same item, removes the products that can't be fulfilled, and tells the customer. Keeping item counts accurate avoids this.

## Rental Check-outs

Rental Check-outs lets you record rental items going out to a customer or event and coming back. It needs Inventory Management on, and it is off by default.

### Turning it on

Go to **Settings > Features** and switch on **Rental Check-outs**. This adds the **Check-outs** tab to the Inventory page, makes **Rental** selectable when creating an item, and adds **Check out** to rental items. See [Feature Switches](#feature-switches).

### Creating a check-out

Go to **Inventory > Check-outs** and click **New check-out**. You can also use **Check out** in a rental item's ⋮ menu to start with that item already added.

![The New check-out form with an event name, customer, due-back date and notes filled in, and two rental items added with quantities](/images/inventory-management/new-checkout.png)

1. **Event or client** (required). This is the check-out's label.
2. **Customer name** (optional).
3. **Check-out date.** It can't be in the future.
4. **Due back** (optional). It can't be before the check-out date.
5. **Notes** (optional).
6. Under **Items**, use **Add a rental item…** (search by name or SKU) and enter how many units of each to send out. Picking the same item again adds one more unit.
7. Click **Create check-out**.

Each item shows how many units are available. If you enter more than that, a warning reads "Only N available — the check-out will be rejected unless this is lowered." If any item can't be covered, the whole check-out is rejected.

Once created, the check-out takes those units off the item's **On hand** count, with a "Checked out" row in the history. The item's **Total** stays the same.

*Checking rental items out for an event, then checking them all back in.*

### The check-out list

**Inventory > Check-outs** has tabs with counts: **Open**, **Overdue**, **Closed** and **All**, plus a **Search by event or client…** box. It shows 25 check-outs per page, with these columns: Label, Customer, Checked out, Due back, Items / Units out, Outstanding, Status, and By.

![The Check-outs list on the All tab, with one Closed and one Open check-out and their outstanding counts](/images/inventory-management/checkouts-list.png)

A check-out's status is one of:

- **Open:** units are still out.
- **Overdue:** open and past its due date (in your business's time zone).
- **Closed:** nothing is outstanding any more. A check-out closes automatically once every unit has been returned, damaged or lost.

### The check-out page

Open a check-out to see:

- A **Details** card: Customer, Checked out, Due back, Closed, Created by, and Notes.
- A line table: Item, Out, Returned, Damaged, Lost, Outstanding.
- **History**, listing every movement tied to this check-out. That includes the original "Checked out" rows, followed by check-ins and write-offs. Damaged and Lost rows show "—" in the Change column.

The buttons are **Check in** (open check-outs only) and **Edit details**. **Edit details** ("Edit check-out details") lets you change the event or client, customer name, due date and notes at any time, even after the check-out is closed.

### Checking items in

Click **Check in**. The **Check in items** dialog shows each line with **Returned**, **Damaged** and **Lost** boxes, and an optional note.

![The Check in items dialog with the Returned box pre-filled with every outstanding unit and empty Damaged and Lost boxes](/images/inventory-management/check-in-dialog.png)

**Returned starts out filled in with every outstanding unit**, so a full return is one click on **Check in**. **Return all** simply puts the boxes back to that state. For a partial return, lower the numbers.

- Returned plus Damaged plus Lost can't exceed what's outstanding. Anything you leave outstanding stays out.
- **Returned** units go back onto the item's on-hand count with a "Checked in" row.
- **Damaged** and **Lost** units permanently drop out of the item's total and never return to available stock. If the item itself has since been deleted, only Damaged and Lost can be recorded for that line.

### When Rental Check-outs is off

- Existing check-outs stay viewable and can still be checked in.
- You can't create new check-outs. The **New check-out** page shows the "Rental Check-outs" feature-disabled page.
- The **Check-outs** tab is hidden. If units are still out, a notice titled "N units are still checked out" appears at the top of the Items tab, linking to "Check them in from the check-outs list".
- **Rental** is greyed out in the New item dialog, and items can still show as "out" on their detail page.

## CSV Import and Export

### Export

Go to **Inventory > Items** and click **Export** to download `inventory-YYYY-MM-DD.csv` with all your items.

**Importable columns:**

- SKU: unique identifier (optional)
- Name: item name
- Type: "stock" or "rental"
- Category: item category (optional)
- Location: storage location (optional)
- Quantity on hand: current count
- Low stock alert at: threshold for email alerts (optional)
- Unit cost: in dollars and cents (for example, 12.50)
- Description: item description (optional)

**Info-only columns (ignored on re-upload):**

- Checked out: units currently on check-outs (rental items only)
- Reserved: units held by storefront checkouts in progress
- Linked products: number of products drawing from this item

### Import

Click **Import** on the Items tab. Only `.csv` files are accepted.

1. **Choose a CSV file** (max 2 MB, max 2,000 data rows).
2. **Review the preview.** It shows badges for N new, N updated, N quantity changes, N unchanged and N errors (skipped), then:
   - A **Quantity changes** table (Item, Current, New) and a **Changes** table (Row, Item, Changes).
   - Rows that will be skipped because of errors, with the reason for each.
   - Warnings, for example setting a quantity below what's reserved, or updating a rental item that has units checked out.
   - **Ignored columns:** any column the import doesn't recognize.
3. **Confirm** by clicking **Import N items**. When it finishes you'll see "Import complete".

![The import preview showing the new, updated and quantity-change counts, the Quantity changes and Changes tables, and the ignored column](/images/inventory-management/import-preview.png)

The preview lists only the first 20 quantity changes, the first 50 other changes and the first 100 errors, even if there are more. Everything still imports when you confirm.

Problems with the file as a whole stop the import until you fix the file: the wrong format, no Name or SKU column, too many rows, an empty file, or a file with unbalanced quotes. Problems with individual rows don't. Those rows are skipped and the rest still import.

When you confirm, SimplePress commits row by row:

- Each new or changed quantity adds a history entry with the reason "Import" and the note "CSV import: [filename]".
- **Re-running the same file is safe.** Values are compared and only updated if different.
- If an item's quantity changed between the preview and your confirmation (for example, a sale came in), that row's quantity is skipped rather than overwritten, and you're told which items.
- A row that changes a stock item's type to rental is refused if products are linked to that item. The rest of the file still imports.

### Column aliases

Column headers are case-insensitive. These aliases are accepted:

- **SKU:** "SKU", "Item code", "Code"
- **Name:** "Name", "Item", "Item name"
- **Type:** "Type", "Item type"
- **Category:** "Category"
- **Location:** "Location", "Storage location"
- **Quantity:** "Quantity", "Qty", "On hand", "Quantity on hand"
- **Low stock alert at:** "Low stock alert at", "Low stock threshold", "Reorder point", "Low stock"
- **Unit cost:** "Unit cost", "Cost", "Replacement value"
- **Description:** "Description", "Notes"

The file needs at least a Name or a SKU column.

### Matching rules

Rows are matched to existing items in this order:

1. **SKU first.** If the row has a SKU and an existing item has the same SKU (case-insensitive, unique per business), the row updates that item.
2. **Then name.** If no SKU matches, an exact name match (case-insensitive) is used. If that item has no SKU yet, the row's SKU is filled in. If it already has a different SKU, the row is an error.
3. **New item.** If neither matches, a new item is created (a Name is required). Two new rows with the same name are an error.
4. **Ambiguity is an error.** If several items match on name, or the same SKU or item appears on more than one row of the file, the affected rows are errors.

### Blank cells

A blank cell means "leave that value unchanged". An import never clears a field, so it's safe to export, edit and re-import. Every importable column can be updated this way, not only Quantity. To clear a value (for example, to remove a category), edit the item in the admin instead. A rental item can't be switched to **stock** while units are checked out.

### Import limits

- **File:** `.csv`, up to 2 MB and 2,000 data rows
- **Name:** up to 191 characters
- **SKU:** up to 64 characters
- **Category** and **Location:** up to 100 characters each
- **Description:** up to 5,000 characters
- **Quantity:** whole numbers, 0 or more. Thousands separators are fine ("1,000")
- **Low stock alert at:** whole numbers, 1 or more
- **Unit cost:** up to $1,000,000

### Type synonyms

Case-insensitive:

- **Stock:** "stock", "consumable", "consumables", "supply", "supplies"
- **Rental:** "rental", "rentals", "rentable", "returnable"

### Cost format

- Whole dollars: `50` = $50.00
- Dollars and cents: `50.99` = $50.99
- With a dollar sign: `$50.99`
- With a thousands separator: `1,000` or `1,000.50` = $1,000.50

## How Stock Is Deducted

The same two steps apply to per-product inventory and to items.

1. **At checkout.** Before sending the customer to Stripe, SimplePress checks availability: what's on hand, minus what other shoppers are holding in checkouts still in progress. If a product is out of stock, or an item can't cover the whole order, the affected products are removed from the cart and the customer sees a message. For tracked products without backorders, and for item-linked products (which hold their units on the item), the stock is then held for **30 minutes** while they pay. If they abandon checkout, the hold is released.
2. **When payment is confirmed.** Stock is deducted inside a database transaction. For items, the total units needed across all linked products in the order are deducted together. If two customers try to buy the last unit at the same moment, only one deduction succeeds and the other becomes an [oversell](#oversells). A sale is never counted twice.

Stock is also deducted for:

- **Manual orders** created with the payment status **Paid**. A manual order saved as **Pending** takes no stock.
- **Pending manual orders switched to Paid.** On the order, open **More Options → Override Status → Payment Status** and choose **Paid**. That's the moment the sale happens, so stock is deducted (and the customer's order count and loyalty points are updated). No confirmation email is sent. Paid is one-way: you can't switch an order back to Pending, so cancel it instead.
- **Subscription renewal orders**, when each renewal is created.

---

## Oversells

In rare high-traffic moments, a customer can pay for stock that sold out between the cart check and payment confirmation. When that happens:

- The order is still created and confirmed (Stripe has already charged them).
- Stock is **not** adjusted. It stays where it was.
- The history records it with the reason "Oversell (not deducted)".

It's flagged in a few places: an **Oversold items need attention** banner on the **Dashboard** (last 30 days), an **Oversold items in this order** alert on the order, and a **Some sales could not be deducted** alert on the item's page.

You'll need to fulfil these orders by hand: restock and ship, or contact the customer to arrange a refund or substitution. Products with **Allow Backorders** on never oversell; they keep counting down below zero.

## Low-Stock Alerts

### Dashboard widget

The **Dashboard** has a **Low Stock Alerts** card showing what needs attention first.

![The Dashboard's Low Stock Alerts card listing products and inventory items that are running low, with a Manage Stock button](/images/dashboard/low-stock.png)

- A published product (or variant) with **Track Inventory** on appears when its stock is at or below its own **Low Inventory Threshold**, or **10** if you never set one. Drafts and products already below zero from backorders are left out.
- A **stock-type item** appears the same way: its own **Low-stock threshold**, or 10 if none is set. It's shown as "Inventory item" with "N left", and clicking it opens the item. Items appear whether or not their linked products are published. Rental items never appear, because "2 on hand" may just mean the rest are out with customers.
- Within each group, the lowest stock comes first. The card shows up to 5 products and up to 5 items.
- **Manage Stock** opens your products list.

The dashboard falls back to 10 when no threshold is set, but the Items page's **Low or out of stock** filter doesn't. See [The Items page](#the-items-page).

### Email notifications

Alerts go to your store's owner email. See [Emails You Receive](/docs/email-preview#emails-you-receive) for all of your alert emails in one place.

**Products.** Set a **Low Inventory Threshold** (1 or more) on a tracked product. When a sale brings stock down to or below it, you get an email with a link to the product. For a product with variants the threshold applies to each variant, and alerts are tracked for the product as a whole.

**Items.** Set a **Low-stock threshold** on the item. The same logic applies. Item emails link to the **Inventory** page list rather than the item itself. Threshold emails fire only for sales, not for manual adjustments, Use, or check-outs.

| Situation | Email subject |
| --- | --- |
| Product drops to or below its threshold (above zero) | "Low inventory alert: …" |
| Product reaches zero, backorders off | "Out of stock: …" |
| Product reaches zero, backorders on | "Out of stock (backorders on): …" |
| Item drops to or below its threshold | "Low base unit stock: …" |
| Item reaches zero | "Base unit out of stock: …" |

The two item subjects still use the older "base unit" wording; they're the same item you see on the Inventory page.

Each alert fires **once per down-cycle**:

- If stock goes straight from above the threshold to zero in one order, only the out-of-stock email is sent.
- After a restock, the alert resets so you'll hear about it again if stock falls back down. For manual changes (editing the quantity, or Adjust, Use and Restock on an item), the low-stock alert resets once stock is back above the threshold. For a refund or cancellation restock, alerts reset only if the item or product had hit zero and is now back above zero.
- A blank threshold means no low-stock email. The out-of-stock email still goes out when stock hits zero.

## Restocking After a Refund or Cancellation

When you cancel or refund an order, the dialog can include a **Return items to inventory** checkbox.

- **Checked:** Stock is restored for every line on the order, in full, even for a partial refund. For item-linked products, the matching units go back onto the item. These show as "Return" rows in the history and reduce the item's net units sold.
- **Unchecked:** Stock isn't restored. Use this if the customer is keeping the item, the product was damaged, or you don't want to re-list it.

The box starts **checked** when you cancel and **unchecked** when you refund.

It only appears when it makes sense, so you can't restock the same order twice:

- **Cancelling a Pending (never-paid) manual order** has no restock option. It never took any stock.
- **Cancelling an order that already has a refund** has no restock option. Use the refund's own box instead.
- **Refunding a cancelled order** never restocks. Cancelling already offered it.

See [Orders](/docs/orders) for full details on refunds and cancellations.

## Feature Switches

Both are in **Settings > Features**.

- **Inventory Management** is on by default. It's independent of Products, so you can manage items even with Products off, for supplies and rentables that aren't sold on your storefront. If you turn it off, the **Inventory** page is replaced by a "feature disabled" message.
- **Rental Check-outs** is off by default and requires Inventory Management. See [Rental Check-outs](#rental-check-outs) for what it adds and what happens when it's off.

## Who Can Do What

Owners, Managers and Staff can all work with inventory. Staff can also open **Orders** and **Customers**.

| Action | Owner / Manager | Staff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| View items, movement history and the Activity log | Yes | Yes |
| Adjust, Use and Restock items | Yes | Yes |
| Create check-outs, check items in, edit check-out details | Yes | Yes |
| Create, edit and delete items | Yes | No |
| Import and export items CSV | Yes | No |
| Export the Activity log to CSV | Yes | No |
| See unit cost | Yes | No |
| See the Products column and open linked products to edit | Yes | Text only |

Per-product inventory settings are part of editing a product. See [Team and Roles](/docs/team-and-roles) for the full role list.

## Can and Can't

**You can:**

- Track stock per product, or per variant, with an optional low-stock threshold for each
- Allow backorders on a product so customers can order past zero
- Set one quantity across several variants at once
- Create standalone stock or rental items with SKU, category, location and unit cost
- Link a simple product to an item so several products share one count
- Adjust, Use and Restock items, with a recorded reason, protected against stale counts
- Check rental items out with due dates, and check them back in with partial returns and damage or loss
- Review every movement in the Activity log and export it
- Import and export items as CSV
- Choose whether to return stock when you refund or cancel an order

**You can't:**

- Enter a negative quantity by hand
- Set backorder rules or thresholds per variant (both are product-level)
- Link a product that has variants to an item (remove the variants first)
- Use per-product tracking and an item on the same product at the same time
- See a history log for per-product stock changes in the admin (it's tracked internally but not shown)
- Delete an item, or change a rental item to stock, while units are checked out
- Create new check-outs while Rental Check-outs is off
- Get automatic emails or reminders for overdue check-outs
- Link a check-out to an invoice or an order
- Clear a field with a CSV import (blank cells leave values unchanged)
