## Overview

SimplePress sends a lot of emails for you: order confirmations, shipping notices, refund notices, invoices, alerts when stock runs low, and more. The **Notification Emails** page lets you see every one of them, and change the wording of many.

On this page you can:

- **Preview** every automatic email, with your logo and store name in place.
- **Change the subject line** of many customer emails.
- **Add an intro paragraph** to the order emails, the abandoned checkout email, and the quote request confirmation.
- **Turn on abandoned checkout emails**, which are off until you switch them on.

You can't send yourself a test email from here, and you can't rewrite the body of an email. The preview is on screen only. See [What You Can and Cannot Do](#what-you-can-and-cannot-do).

<Callout type="info" title="Who can use it">
  Owners and Managers can open Notification Emails and change the wording. Staff can't open it. See [Team and Roles](/docs/team-and-roles).
</Callout>

## Where to Find It

In your admin sidebar, open **Content → Notification Emails**. You can also type "notification" into the search box at the top of the admin to jump straight there.

The page is titled **Notification Emails**: "Preview your transactional emails and customize the subject line and intro text of customer-facing ones". "Transactional" just means automatic emails that go out when something happens, such as an order or a payment. They are not marketing emails, with one exception: the **Abandoned Checkout** reminder is listed here, but it is sent like a marketing email. It has an **Unsubscribe** link and your mailing address in its footer, and it isn't sent to anyone who unsubscribed. For newsletters, see [Email Marketing](/docs/email-marketing).

The page has two columns:

- **Templates** on the left: one button for each email.
- **Preview** on the right: shows the email you picked. If the email can be customized, a **Customize** card appears above the preview.

Until you click a button, the preview area says "Select a template to preview".

## How Previews Work

A preview is a sample. Nothing is sent when you click a button.

- **Your logo and store name** are real. If you haven't uploaded a logo, the email shows your store name as text instead. Add one under **Site Setup → Brand & Appearance**.
- **Customer names, addresses, products, amounts and codes** are made-up samples (for example "John Doe" or "Jane Smith"). Buttons and links in a preview are samples too.
- **Order numbers and items:** if your store has any orders, several previews use details from one of your real orders: **Order Confirmation**, **Order Confirmation (pickup)**, **Ready for Pickup**, **Order Fulfilled (no tracking)**, **Order Refunded**, **Order Cancelled** and the two **New Order** emails use the real order number. The **New Order** emails also use the real totals, and **Abandoned Checkout** shows real items. The first order the page finds is used, not necessarily the newest. When [Product options](/docs/product-options) is on, the **Order Confirmation** and **New Order (owner)** previews also add sample choices to the first item (see the note under the table below). **Order Shipped** always shows order number 1001, and other amounts (such as the refund amount on **Order Refunded**) are samples. If you have no orders yet, the page uses order number 1001 and a "Sample Product".
- **Customized wording shows up as you type.** If you change a subject or intro, the preview updates a moment later, before you save. A line above the preview, **Subject:**, shows the subject line customers will see. If you haven't written your own subject, that line shows the standard subject the real email uses. For example, the pickup confirmation reads "Order #1015 confirmed — pickup details inside" (the number comes from the order the preview uses, or is 1001 if you have no orders), **Subscription Updated** reads "Your subscription is paused" or "Your subscription is back on", and the subscription emails name the sample product, such as "Your Premium Coffee Pack subscription is confirmed". Invoice previews show the sample invoice number.

## Every Email on the Page

The page always lists all of these, whether or not the matching feature is turned on. For example, the invoice emails are listed even if you don't use [Invoices](/docs/invoices). An email only goes out when something actually happens, so unused features never email anyone.

The tables below group the emails by topic. On the page itself, the buttons sit in one long **Templates** list in a different order, so use the names to find each one.

"Customize" tells you what you can change on the page. See [Customizing an Email](#customizing-an-email).

### Orders and Shoppers

| Button | Who gets it | When it's sent | Customize |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Order Confirmation** | Customer | Payment goes through, including each subscription renewal. For orders you create by hand, only if you choose to send it. | Subject and intro |
| **Order Confirmation (pickup)** | Customer | Same email, for pickup orders. It adds the pickup location and instructions. | Subject and intro (shared with **Order Confirmation**) |
| **Ready for Pickup** | Customer | You click **Mark Ready for Pickup** on a pickup order. | Subject and intro |
| **Order Shipped** | Customer | You add a shipment with a tracking number. | Subject and intro |
| **Order Fulfilled (no tracking)** | Customer | An order becomes fully fulfilled without tracking numbers. | Subject and intro |
| **Order Refunded** | Customer | You refund an order and leave **Notify customer by email** ticked. | Subject and intro |
| **Order Cancelled** | Customer | You cancel an order and leave **Notify customer by email** ticked. | Subject and intro |
| **Bank Payment Processing** | Customer | A shopper pays by bank (such as ACH Direct Debit) and the money hasn't cleared yet. The order appears once it clears. See [Bank debits and other delayed payment methods](/docs/payments-and-payouts#bank-debits-and-other-delayed-payment-methods). | Subject and intro |
| **Bank Payment Failed** | Customer | A bank payment fails. No order is created, and the shopper is invited to order again. | Subject and intro |

| **Back in Stock** | Customer | A sold-out item that a shopper asked about can be bought again. | No |

The **Order Confirmation (pickup)** button is a second view of the same email, so changes you make to one apply to both.

**Choices on order emails.** When an order includes products with [options](/docs/product-options), **Order Confirmation** and your **New Order** alert list each item's choices under its name, as "Group: value +$X". The payment processing, payment failed and abandoned checkout emails show them in one short line. While **Product options & personalization** is on, the **Order Confirmation** and **New Order (owner)** previews add sample choices to the first item of the order the preview uses: Charms: Heart (included), Star +$12.00, and Engraving “Amy” +$5.00. If one of that order's items already has real choices, the preview leaves it alone. The product name and price in the preview are the real item's, so the choices may not suit it.

![The Order Confirmation preview with an item from a real order, SPF 30 Mineral Sunscreen, showing the sample choices Charms: Heart (included), Star +$12.00 and Engraving “Amy” +$5.00 under the product name](/images/email-preview/order-confirmation-options.png)

**Back in Stock** is the email a shopper gets after asking to be told when a sold-out item returns. You can preview it here, but you can't change its wording. See [Wishlist & Back-in-Stock Alerts](/docs/wishlist-and-back-in-stock).

For the shopper's side of these emails, and how to turn each off, see [Emails Your Customers Receive](/docs/for-your-customers#emails-your-customers-receive).

### Alerts for You

These are sent to you, not to customers. Customers never see them, and none of them can be customized. Most go to your **Owner Email** (set in your General settings). The **Contact Form** email goes to your **Support Email** instead. For who gets what, and which ones you can switch off, see [Emails You Receive](#emails-you-receive) below.

| Button | When it's sent |
| --- | --- |
| **New Order (owner)** | A new order is paid. |
| **New Order (subscription)** | A subscription order comes in, such as a renewal. It also shows the subscription schedule. |
| **Contact Form** | A visitor sends your contact form. It goes to your **Support Email**. Replying to it writes back to the visitor. |
| **Low Inventory Alert (owner)** | A product's stock drops to its low-inventory number (set on the product). |
| **Out of Stock Alert (owner)** | A product's stock reaches zero. |
| **Out of Stock — Backorders On (owner)** | A product reaches zero, but it still takes orders because backorders are on. |

The quote, invoice and subscription alerts for you are listed in their own sections below.

## Emails You Receive

Here is every automatic email that comes to **you** (or your team), in one place. Some have a button on this page so you can preview them. Others don't.

**Who gets what:**

- Most alerts go to your **Owner Email**, which you set in **Settings → General**. See [General Settings](/docs/general-settings#owner-email).
- **Payment disputes**, **payments paused** and **bank payment failed** also go to every Owner and Manager on your team, each in their own copy.
- **Staff** don't get any of these emails. See [Team and Roles](/docs/team-and-roles).
- Other alerts, such as new orders and low stock, go to the Owner Email address only, not to Managers.

| Email | When it's sent | Who gets it | Can you turn it off? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **New order** | A customer's checkout is paid, or a subscription renewal is paid. Orders you create by hand don't send it. | Owner Email | No |
| **Low stock** (product) | A sale brings a product to or below its **Low Inventory Threshold**. Sent once, until you restock. See [Inventory](/docs/inventory-management#low-stock-alerts). | Owner Email | Leave the threshold blank on the product |
| **Out of stock** (product) | A product's stock reaches 0. There is a separate version for products that still take backorders. | Owner Email | Only by not tracking inventory for that product |
| **Inventory item low** | An inventory item reaches its **Low-stock threshold (optional)**. | Owner Email | Leave the threshold blank on the item |
| **Inventory item out** | An inventory item reaches 0. | Owner Email | No |
| **Bank payment failed** | A shopper's bank payment fails. No order is created and the held items are released. You don't need to do anything. See [Bank debits and other delayed payment methods](/docs/payments-and-payouts#bank-debits-and-other-delayed-payment-methods). | Owner Email and every Owner and Manager | No |
| **Payment dispute** | A customer disputes a payment. No email is sent when the dispute closes. See [Disputes](/docs/payments-and-payouts#disputes). | Owner Email and every Owner and Manager | No |
| **Payments paused** | Stripe pauses charges on your account. Sent once. | Owner Email and every Owner and Manager | No |
| **New review** | A customer submits a review. Reviews you add yourself don't send it. See [Reviews](/docs/reviews). | Owner Email | Only by turning Reviews off |
| **Contact form message** | A visitor sends your contact form. | Support Email (Owner Email if no Support Email is set). Replies go to the visitor. | No |
| **Form entry** | A visitor submits one of your custom forms. See [Forms](/docs/forms). | The form's **Notify email**, then your Support Email, then your Owner Email. | You can send it somewhere else, but not turn it off. Entries are always saved in your admin. |
| **Quote request** | A visitor submits a quote calculator. See [Quote Calculators](/docs/quote-calculators). | Owner Email. Replies go to the visitor. | No |
| **Donation** | A card donation goes through. See [Donations](/docs/donations). | Owner Email. Replies go to the donor. | No |
| **Subscription started, payment failed, cancelled** | A customer subscribes, a renewal payment fails (again on each retry), or a subscription ends. Pausing or resuming doesn't email you. See [Subscriptions](/docs/subscriptions). | Owner Email. Replies go to the customer. | No |
| **Invoices past due** | A daily check finds invoices that went overdue in the last 7 days. You get one email per store. See [Invoices](/docs/invoices). | Owner Email | Yes: **Email me when an invoice becomes past due** in your invoice settings (on by default) |
| **Weekly invoice digest** | Monday morning in your store's time zone. Skipped if there is nothing to report. | Owner Email | Yes: **Weekly summary every Monday morning** in your invoice settings (on by default) |
| **Team invitation** | You invite someone to your team. The invitation is good for 14 days. | The person you invited. Replies go to your Owner Email. | No |
| **Campaign test** | You click **Send test to myself** on a campaign in Email Marketing. | You (the signed-in user) | It only happens when you ask |
| **Verify email and reset password** | You create an account or use **Forgot password**. | You | No |

<Callout type="info" title="Switching these off">
  Most of these have no off switch. The ones you can control are the two invoice emails, the stock alerts (by leaving the threshold blank), and the abandoned checkout email, which is for customers. See [Abandoned Checkout Emails](#abandoned-checkout-emails).
</Callout>

### Things that don't send an email

SimplePress does **not** email you when:

- a customer leaves a **testimonial**
- someone **accepts** your team invitation
- a **dispute closes** (check the order or Stripe)
- a subscription is **paused or resumed**
- you create an order by hand (a **manual order**)
- you add a review yourself

### Make sure these reach you

- **Keep your Owner Email current** in [General Settings](/docs/general-settings#owner-email). It's where most alerts land.
- **Set a Support Email** you check often in [General Settings](/docs/general-settings#support-email). Contact form messages go there.
- **Use a form's Notify email** if a different person should see that form's entries, such as the person who handles catering.
- **Check spam** the first time you get one of these, and mark it as "not spam" so later ones arrive.
- Owners and Managers on your team get dispute and payments-paused emails at their own sign-in addresses, so make sure those are right too.

## Customizing an Email

For the emails marked in the tables, you can change two things:

| Field | What it does | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subject** | The subject line of the real email. Leave it blank to use the standard subject. | 150 characters |
| **Intro text** | An optional paragraph shown at the top of the email, under the heading. | 1,000 characters |

Some emails only let you change the subject. For those, the **Intro text** box doesn't appear. Their message is written elsewhere (for example when you send an invoice), or it is fixed.

Both fields are **plain text**. Anything that looks like web code, such as `<b>`, won't save. There is no bold, no pictures and no buttons.

### Step by Step

1. Open **Content → Notification Emails**.
2. In the **Templates** list, click the email you want, for example **Order Confirmation**.
3. Find the **Customize "Order Confirmation"** card above the preview. Under the title is a short note on when this email is sent.
4. Type your own **Subject**. The standard subject is shown greyed out in the box as a guide, and it matches what customers get. Use these placeholders where they make sense:
   - `{orderNumber}` becomes the order number. Use it in order emails only.
   - `{businessName}` becomes your store name.
   - `{invoiceNumber}` becomes the invoice number. Use it in invoice emails only.
5. If the card has **Intro text**, type your paragraph. For example: "Thank you for supporting a small shop! We pack every order by hand."
6. Check the **Subject:** line and the email in the **Preview** card.
7. Click **Save changes**. A message confirms: "Email customizations saved".

You can edit several emails before saving. **Save changes** keeps all of your edits at once. While you have edits that aren't saved yet, **Unsaved changes** appears next to the buttons, and **Save changes** is greyed out when there is nothing new to save.

![The Customize "Order Confirmation" card with a custom Subject and Intro text typed in and not yet saved, and the Preview card showing the new subject line and the intro paragraph under the heading](/images/email-preview/email-preview-customize.png)

### What Customers See

Once you save, your wording is used in the real emails from then on. Emails already sent are not changed. If you resend an email from an order later, it uses your wording as it is at that moment.

- A custom **Subject** replaces the standard subject, including for the pickup version of the order confirmation.
- **Intro text** appears near the top of the email, under the heading. The rest of the email (items, totals, buttons, footer) stays the same.
- If you leave a field blank, the standard wording is used for that field.

A few things to know about subjects:

- **Placeholders only fill in where they apply.** `{orderNumber}` comes out blank in the abandoned checkout, subscription, quote (**Quote Request Received** and **Final Quote**) and invoice subjects, because there is no order number. `{businessName}` works in every customizable email.
- **Some subscription emails normally name the product.** **Subscription Started**, **Subscription Payment Failed** and **Subscription Cancelled** include the product's name in the standard subject, for example "Your Premium Coffee Pack subscription is confirmed". The preview shows a sample product here, and real emails use the customer's product. The subject you write can use `{businessName}` but can't include the product name. The standard subjects for **Subscription Updated** don't name the product at all.
- **Final Quote** has two standard subjects, depending on whether a price is included. If you write your own subject, it is used for both.

### Undoing a Change

1. Select the email.
2. Click **Reset to default**. This empties the **Subject** and **Intro text** boxes.
3. Click **Save changes** to make it official.

The **Reset to default** button is greyed out when both boxes are already empty. You can also just clear the boxes by hand and save.

## Abandoned Checkout Emails

**Abandoned Checkout** is the one email that also has an on/off switch. It is **off** until you turn it on.

1. Click **Abandoned Checkout** in the **Templates** list.
2. At the top of the **Customize "Abandoned Checkout"** card, switch **Send this email** on.

The switch takes effect right away. You'll see "Abandoned checkout emails are now sending" (or "Abandoned checkout emails are now off" when you switch it back). You don't need to click **Save changes** for the switch. That button is only for the subject and intro below it.

The switch controls whether the email goes out at all, not just how it looks. While it's off, shoppers who leave checkout never get a reminder, no matter what you've written in the subject and intro.

Under the switch is a note: "Reminders include an unsubscribe link and your mailing address, and are not sent to people who unsubscribed. Add a full mailing address in Business settings or reminders won't send."

If you haven't set a full mailing address, an orange **Business address needed** warning appears too: "Reminders won't send until you add a full mailing address: a street, city, and state or ZIP, or a PO box." Click **Go to business settings** to add it. See [Business address](/docs/general-settings#business-address).

![The Customize "Abandoned Checkout" card with the Send this email switch, the note about the unsubscribe link and mailing address, the Subject and Intro text fields and the Save changes and Reset to default buttons, above a preview of the email](/images/email-marketing/abandoned-settings-note.png)

![The orange Business address needed warning with a Go to business settings button](/images/email-marketing/abandoned-address-warning.png)

The preview shows the footer a real reminder carries: an **Unsubscribe** link, then your store name and mailing address. The address line only shows if you've set an address. The **Unsubscribe** link in the preview is a sample with no code in it, so clicking it opens a plain **Invalid link** page that says "This unsubscribe link is missing a token. Please use the link from your email." Real reminders have a working link.

For when it sends, who it reaches, and what it says, see [Abandoned Checkout Emails](/docs/email-marketing#abandoned-checkout-emails) in Email Marketing.

## What You Can and Cannot Do

**You can:**

- Preview every automatic email the page lists, with your own logo and store name
- Change the subject line of the emails marked **Subject only** or **Subject and intro**
- Add an intro paragraph to the order emails, the abandoned checkout email and the quote request confirmation
- Reset an email to its standard wording
- Turn abandoned checkout emails on or off

**You cannot:**

- Send a test email from this page. The preview is on screen only.
- Rewrite the body of any email, or change its layout, colors or buttons
- Add bold, pictures or links to your intro text
- Change the wording of the owner alerts, **Back in Stock**, the rewards emails, the sign-in emails, or the invoice cancelled, receipt and digest emails
- Turn off an email from this page. The only on/off switch here is **Send this email** on **Abandoned Checkout**. Other emails have their own controls where they start. See [Emails Your Customers Receive](/docs/for-your-customers#emails-your-customers-receive).
- Choose which address replies go to from this page

## Troubleshooting and FAQ

**The preview area says "Select a template to preview".**
Click one of the buttons in the **Templates** list. Nothing is shown until you pick one.

**I changed the wording, but a customer still got the old email.**
Check that you clicked **Save changes**. If **Unsaved changes** is showing, your edit is only on your screen. Also, only emails sent after you save use the new wording.

**Why doesn't the email I want have a Subject box?**
Only emails marked in the tables can be customized. Owner alerts, **Back in Stock**, the rewards emails, **Verify Email**, **Reset Password** and a few others are fixed.

**Why is there no Intro text box on Invoice Sent, Final Quote or the subscription emails?**
Those emails take their message from somewhere else (the invoice or quote you send), or have fixed text. Only the subject can be changed.

**My changes won't save.**
Subject and intro are plain text only. Remove anything that looks like web code, such as `<b>` or `<p>`. Also keep the subject to 150 characters and the intro to 1,000.

**The preview shows the wrong customer, amounts or products.**
Those are samples. Only your logo, store name, and the order number and items of the order-based previews come from your store. Real emails use the real customer's details.

**My logo isn't in the preview.**
Upload one under **Site Setup → Brand & Appearance**. Until then, the email shows your store name as text.

**Where do I turn off abandoned checkout emails?**
Open **Abandoned Checkout** in the **Templates** list and switch **Send this email** off.

**Is there a Welcome email?**
No. SimplePress doesn't send a welcome email when a customer creates an account. They get the **Verify Email** message to confirm their address.

**Can I see the sign-in emails exactly as customers get them?**
You can preview **Verify Email** and **Reset Password**. The real **Reset Password** email's subject reads "Reset your SimplePress password", because a customer's account works at every store that runs on SimplePress.
