## Overview

Product Reviews lets customers leave a star rating and a written review on any product page. Reviews from customers always wait for you: nothing a shopper writes goes live until you approve it. You can also add reviews yourself, which is handy for bringing over reviews from another website or for adding kind words a customer sent you by email.

Typical uses:

- Build trust on product pages with real feedback and an average star rating
- Move your existing reviews over when you switch to SimplePress
- Keep control of what appears on your store by approving, hiding, or deleting reviews

<Callout type="info" title="Product Reviews is off by default">
Turn it on yourself under **Settings → Features** (it is in the **Marketing** group). It needs **Products** and **Customer Accounts** to be on first. If either is off, the switch shows a **Requires** badge until you turn that one on. See [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags).
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Who can use it">
Owners and Managers can open **Reviews** and moderate reviews. Staff can't open it. See [Team and Roles](/docs/team-and-roles). Deleting several reviews at once is for Owners only (see [Deleting Reviews](#deleting-reviews)).
</Callout>

## Where to Find It

Once the feature is on, go to **Marketing → Reviews** in the admin sidebar. If a customer has sent a review that is waiting for you, a number appears next to **Reviews** in the sidebar.

The pending number counts every review that has not been approved, including a hidden review that was never approved. If the number looks higher than the **Pending** list you see, that is why.

If the feature is off, the page shows a note that Reviews is not enabled, with a button to **Settings → Features**.

## The Reviews List

The list shows every review for every product in one table, newest first. There are no tabs. Use the filters above the table instead.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Finds reviews by reviewer name, email, headline, review text, reviewer title, or product name |
| **Status** | **All reviews**, **Pending**, **Published**, or **Hidden** |
| **Source** | **All sources**, **Customer submitted**, or **Owner added** |
| **Sort** | **Newest first**, **Oldest first**, **Rating: high to low**, **Rating: low to high**, or **Most helpful** |

The table has these columns:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Review** | The star rating, a **Verified** badge when it applies, the headline, the first lines of the text, up to three photo thumbnails, and how many people found it helpful |
| **Reviewer** | Name, email, and reviewer title |
| **Product** | The product reviewed (click it to open the product) |
| **Source** | **Customer** or **Owner added** |
| **Status** | **Pending**, **Published**, or **Hidden** |
| **Date** | The review date |

The list shows 25 reviews per page. On a phone, the Reviewer, Product, Source, Status, and Date details appear in a single line under each review.

Click **Filters** to open the **Status**, **Source**, and **Sort** menus. A number on the button shows how many filters are on, and each active filter appears as a chip you can click to remove.

To work through new customer reviews, set **Status** to **Pending**.

![The Reviews list filtered to Pending, showing three customer reviews waiting for approval](/images/reviews/reviews-pending-filter.png)

### Review Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Waiting for you. Shoppers can't see it, and it does not count toward the product's average. This is where every customer review starts |
| **Published** | Approved and showing on the product page. It counts toward the average rating and review count |
| **Hidden** | Taken off your store but kept in your admin. It does not count toward the average |

A review that is hidden always shows as **Hidden**, even if you approved it before.

## Approving, Hiding, and Managing Reviews

### Approving a Customer Review

1. Open **Reviews** and set **Status** to **Pending**.
2. Read the review. Customer reviews can't be edited, so what you see is what the shopper wrote.
3. Click the **⋮** menu at the end of the row and choose **Approve**.

The review shows on the product page right away, and the product's average rating and review count update.

To take an approved review back to waiting, open its **⋮** menu and choose **Unapprove**.

![The row menu open on a pending review, showing Approve, Hide, and Delete](/images/reviews/reviews-row-menu.png)

### The ⋮ Menu

What you see depends on the review:

| Action | When it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Owner-added reviews only |
| **Approve** | Pending reviews |
| **Unapprove** | Published reviews |
| **Hide** or **Unhide** | Every review |
| **Delete** | Every review |

A hidden review shows neither **Approve** nor **Unapprove**. If you want to bring it back, choose **Unhide** first. It returns to **Published** if you had approved it, or to **Pending** if you had not.

### Hiding Reviews

**Hide** takes a review off your store but keeps it in your admin so you can bring it back with **Unhide**. A hidden review does not count toward the average rating or review count. Use this when you are not sure about a review and don't want to lose it.

### Deleting Reviews

1. Click **⋮** on the review and choose **Delete**.
2. In the **Delete review?** box, confirm. The box tells you this removes the review for good, updates the product's rating, and can't be undone.

Owners and Managers can delete one review at a time. Deleting is permanent, so if you only want it off your store, choose **Hide**.

### Working on Several Reviews at Once

Tick the box beside two or more reviews (or the box at the top of the table to select everything on the page). A bar appears with these actions:

- **Approve**
- **Unapprove**
- **Hide**
- **Unhide**
- **Delete** (Owners only; Managers don't see this button)

If your filters match more reviews than fit on one page, the bar offers to select all matching reviews. Approve, Unapprove, Hide, and Unhide work on up to 100 reviews at a time. **Delete** works on up to 25 at a time, and asks you to confirm first.

After Approve, Unapprove, Hide, or Unhide, a message appears with an **Undo** button. Delete has no undo.

![Three reviews ticked with the bulk action bar showing Approve, Unapprove, Hide, Unhide, and Delete](/images/reviews/reviews-bulk-bar.png)

## Adding a Review Yourself

Use **Add Review** (top right of the page) to add a review under any reviewer's name. It is meant for:

- Bringing over reviews from another site when you move to SimplePress
- Adding feedback a customer sent by email or in person
- Putting the first few reviews on a new product

1. Click **Add Review**.
2. Fill in **Reviewer Name**.
3. Optionally set **Review Date**. Leave it as today, or backdate it when importing.
4. Choose the **Product** the review is for.
5. Click a star to set the **Rating**. It starts at 5.
6. Type the **Review Text**.
7. Optionally open **Additional details** to add the reviewer's **Email (Optional)**, **Reviewer Title (Optional)**, and **Review Headline (Optional)**.
8. Switch **Verified Purchase** on if this person bought the product. Leave it off otherwise.
9. Leave **Publish Immediately** on to show the review on your store right away, or switch it off to save it as **Pending**.
10. Click **Add Review**.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reviewer Name** | Yes | Shown on the product page |
| **Review Date** | Yes (filled in for you) | Today by default. Backdate it for imports |
| **Product** | Yes | Chosen when you add the review. It can't be changed afterwards |
| **Rating** | Yes | 1 to 5 stars |
| **Review Text** | Yes | The review itself |
| **Email (Optional)** | No | For your records only. Shoppers never see it |
| **Reviewer Title (Optional)** | No | For example "Verified Buyer" or "Professional Chef". You see it in the list |
| **Review Headline (Optional)** | No | A short title shown above the text |
| **Verified Purchase** | No | Shows the **Verified Purchase** badge on your store |
| **Publish Immediately** | No | On by default |

You can't attach photos or video when you add a review.

![The Add Review dialog with Additional details open](/images/reviews/reviews-add-dialog.png)

<Callout type="info" title="Backdated reviews">
Setting an older **Review Date** changes where the review sorts (under **Most Recent**, it takes its place by that date). The small line of text next to the reviewer's name on the product page, such as "about 1 hour ago", counts from when you added the review to SimplePress, not from the date you chose.
</Callout>

### Editing an Owner-Added Review

Open the **⋮** menu on a review you added and choose **Edit**. You can change everything except the product. When you save, the product's rating updates.

Reviews written by customers can't be edited. You can only approve, hide, or delete them. This keeps customers' words their own.

## What Customers See

These are the details for the Default template. Other templates lay reviews out their own way, but they follow the same rules: only approved reviews show, and shoppers write reviews through the same steps.

### The Reviews Section

The reviews section sits near the bottom of each product page, under a small label and a heading.

**With no reviews yet**, shoppers see **No reviews yet** and "Be the first to share your thoughts on this product", with a **Write a Review** button.

**With reviews**, the section shows:

- **Average rating.** A large number such as 4.6, the stars, and "Based on 12 reviews"
- **Breakdown bars.** One bar each for 5, 4, 3, 2, and 1 stars, with a count. Shoppers can click a row to show only reviews with that rating, then click **Clear Filter** to see them all again
- **Sort menu.** **Most Recent**, **Most Helpful**, **Highest Rating**, or **Lowest Rating**
- **Write a Review** button
- **The reviews.** Ten at first, then a **Load more reviews** button that shows how many are left and adds ten more each time

Each review shows the stars, a **Verified Purchase** badge when it applies, the headline, the review text, the reviewer's name, how long ago it was added, and **Helpful?** thumbs-up and thumbs-down buttons with counts.

![The reviews section of a product page with the average rating, breakdown bars, sort menu, Write a Review button, and the first reviews](/images/reviews/reviews-product-page.png)

### Helpful Votes

Shoppers can mark a review as helpful or not helpful. They don't need to be signed in. Each person gets one vote per review, and they can change their vote. The counts feed the **Most Helpful** sort on the product page and in your admin list. Votes never hide or remove a review.

### Where Ratings Show

Star ratings and the review count show in the reviews section of the product page. They do not show on product cards in the shop grid or in related products. Your ratings are also passed to search engines, which may show stars next to your products in search results. Search engines decide whether they do.

### On a Phone

On a phone, the average rating and the breakdown bars stack above the sort menu and the reviews.

![The reviews section of a product page on a phone](/images/reviews/reviews-phone.png)

## How Customers Write a Review

1. On a product page, a shopper clicks **Write a Review**.
2. If they are not signed in, a **Sign in to write a review** box appears with **Cancel** and **Sign in** buttons. After signing in they come back to the product. They need a customer account, which is why Customer Accounts must be on (see [Customers](/docs/customers)).
3. A signed-in shopper sees the **Write a Review** form and fills it in.
4. They click **Submit Review**. A message says: "Thanks! Your review was submitted and will appear once approved."

![The Sign in to write a review box](/images/reviews/reviews-sign-in-prompt.png)

| Field | Required | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overall Rating** | Yes | 1 to 5 stars. Starts at 5 |
| **Review Title** | Yes | Up to 200 characters |
| **Your Review** | Yes | At least 10 and up to 2,000 characters |

The form also tells shoppers "Reviews are checked by the store before they appear publicly." The shopper's name comes from their account. They can't type a different one.

Shoppers can't attach photos or video to a review, and neither can you when you add one. Reviews show text and a rating only.

![The Write a Review form with a title and review filled in](/images/reviews/reviews-write-dialog.png)

### Verified Purchase

When a shopper's review goes in, SimplePress checks whether any order with that product was placed with the same email address as their account. If so, the review gets the **Verified Purchase** badge, and the form shows a green "Verified Purchase" line. Any order counts, whatever its status, so a cancelled or unpaid order still earns the badge.

### One Review Per Product

Each customer (by email address) can review a product once. If they try again, the box says "You have already reviewed this product." Shoppers can't edit or delete their review after sending it. If one asks you to remove theirs, you can delete it from your admin.

## Emails

You get one email for each review a customer submits.

| Email | Sent to | When | Subject |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New review | The store owner's email address | A customer submits a review | New review for [product name] |

It says who reviewed which product, shows the star rating, the headline, and the start of the review, and tells you the review is pending approval and won't show until you approve it. A **Review submission** button opens your Reviews page.

The email goes to the store owner's address only, not to Managers. Reviews you add yourself don't send an email. Customers do not get an email when you approve their review.

## Changing the Reviews Wording

On the Default template, two pieces of text around the reviews section are editable in the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor). Open the **Product** page and find the **Product page** group:

| Field | Default | What it controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reviews label** | Reviews | Small text above the heading. Leave it blank to hide it |
| **Reviews heading** | What customers are saying | The heading above the reviews. Leave it blank to hide it |

These apply to every product page. The wording inside the section itself (such as **Write a Review** and **Load more reviews**) can't be changed.

---

## Templates Without a Review Section

Most templates show reviews on their product pages, each in its own style. A few templates have no reviews section on the product page: **Animated Bamboo**, **Dark Trend**, and **Sledge**. On those, the Reviews page in your admin still works, but customers can't see or write reviews until you switch to a template that has the section. Templates that have no shop pages don't show reviews either.

If you are not sure about yours, open a product page on your live store after you turn Reviews on. If you want reviews and your template doesn't have them, contact [help@generativejustice.org](mailto:help@generativejustice.org).

## Reviews vs. Testimonials

They are two different features:

- **Reviews** are about one product. They appear on that product's page, carry a star rating, and feed the product's average. They only exist when [Products](/docs/products) is on.
- **Testimonials** are about your business as a whole. They appear on your homepage and a Testimonials page, with no star rating and no product. See [Testimonials](/docs/testimonials).

You can use either one or both.

## Tips

- **Check Pending now and then.** Customer reviews don't show until you approve them, and the sidebar number is your reminder.
- **Seed new products.** If you have real quotes from happy customers, add them with **Add Review** so a new product doesn't open with "No reviews yet".
- **Use Hide when unsure.** It is easy to undo. Delete is permanent.
- **Be honest with Verified Purchase.** Only switch it on for people who really bought the product.
- **Importing many reviews?** Add them with a backdated **Review Date** so they sort in a sensible order under **Most Recent**.

## Feature Flag

| Feature | Default | Where to toggle | Requires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Product Reviews** | Off | **Settings → Features** (Marketing) | **Products** and **Customer Accounts** |

Owners and Managers can switch it on or off. Turning it off hides the reviews section from your product pages and closes the Reviews page, but your reviews are kept for when you turn it back on. See [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags).

## What You Can and Cannot Do

**You can:**

- Turn Product Reviews on or off yourself
- Approve, unapprove, hide, unhide, and delete reviews, one at a time or in bulk
- Find reviews by search, status, source, and sort order
- Add reviews yourself, with a backdated date, a reviewer title, and the Verified Purchase badge
- Edit the reviews you added
- Choose whether an owner-added review is published or pending
- Change the Reviews label and heading on the Default product page

**You cannot:**

- Edit a review a customer wrote
- Reply to reviews
- Add photos or video to a review, or let shoppers attach them
- Let customers edit or delete their own review
- Let a customer review the same product twice
- Show star ratings on product cards in the shop
- Delete more than 25 reviews at once, or delete in bulk as a Manager
- Get emailed when you add a review yourself, or have customers emailed when you approve theirs
- Show reviews on templates that have no reviews section
