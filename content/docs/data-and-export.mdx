## Overview

Download Your Site gives you a complete copy of your store in one ZIP file. It includes your pages, blog posts, products, photos, customer and order spreadsheets, and ready-to-import files for other platforms. Use it when you want to move to a new website, keep a backup, or hand your content to a designer or developer.

> **Who can use it:** Store owners only. It is always available and has no feature flag. Managers see a note, "Downloading your site is Owner-only," and staff can't open the page. The file includes customer records, which is why it is limited to owners.

---

## What It Is

Downloading your site doesn't change or close your store. Your site stays live, and your customers can keep shopping, booking, and checking out while you download.

The download is a single ZIP file named after your store, for example **Maple Street Bakery - Site Export - 2026-10-09.zip**. Nothing is removed from your store when you download it.

The file name looks like this:

```text
Maple Street Bakery - Site Export - 2026-10-09.zip
```

---

## Downloading Your Site

1. Go to **Settings → Data & Export** in the admin sidebar.
2. Click **Download your site**.
3. Wait while the file is prepared. The page says "Preparing your download", and stores with many photos can take a minute or two. There's no progress bar. **Keep the page open** until the file saves.
4. When you see "Your download is ready.", find the ZIP in your Downloads folder. Unzip it. Your browser may unzip it for you.

![The Data & Export page with the Download your site button and the list of what's included](/images/data-and-export/data-export-page.png)

If the download fails, you'll see "Download failed" with a number. Wait a minute and click the button again.

Start with **START HERE.html** once the ZIP is unzipped. It opens in any web browser and explains what is inside.

## What's Inside

| Folder or file | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| **START HERE.html** | An overview of your export, a list of what's inside, and a "which option is right for me?" table. Open this first. |
| **Guides/** | Step-by-step guides for moving to WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, or Square Online. Also guides for hiring a designer or developer, keeping your domain name, and payments, customers and subscriptions. |
| **Site details.html** | Your business information, hours, social links, menus, logo, colors, SEO settings, and domain. |
| **Pages/, Blog/, Products/, [Services](/docs/services)/, Events/, Galleries/** | Readable copies of every page, post, product, and more. Open them in any browser, or copy and paste them into a new site. **Pages/** also holds your FAQs and testimonials. |
| **Photos/** | Every photo and video you uploaded, in folders by where it's used: Branding, Products (one folder per product), Collections, Galleries, Blog, Pages, Services, Events, Testimonials, Product reviews, Videos, and Site sections. Photos that couldn't be downloaded don't stop the export. They're listed in **START HERE.html**. People's profile photos are not included. |
| **Photos/Media Library/** | Files you uploaded but aren't used anywhere on your site. |
| **Photos/Template & stock images/** | Images shown on your site that you didn't upload. Check the rights before you reuse them. |
| **Spreadsheets/** | CSV files for products, collections, customers, addresses, orders, order items, discounts, reviews, testimonials, FAQs, invoices (with line items and payments), quotes, form entries, rewards members and points history, subscriptions, donations, inventory, email subscribers, [services](/docs/services) (with their offerings), events, and shipping rates. Open them in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets. A spreadsheet only appears if you have something in it. The exceptions are **products**, **customers** and **orders**, which are always there, even if they only have a header row. |
| **Import files/** | Ready-to-import product files for Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Square, and WordPress/WooCommerce. Also includes a WordPress file with your pages and blog posts. If you have no products, the product files are left out. |
| **For developers/** | Raw data files for a developer or designer who wants the full data. |
| **Licenses/** | Who owns what. Your content belongs to you. SimplePress, including its templates, is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license. |

## Exporting just your products

You don't have to download the whole site to get your product list. Owners can export products from the **Products** page too. It makes a WooCommerce-format CSV of the products you pick.

- It's **off by default**. Turn on **Products CSV export** under **Settings → Features**.
- Only the **Owner** can switch it on and use it. Managers can't.
- Steps are in [Exporting products (CSV)](/docs/products#exporting-products-csv).

Product **import** isn't a button you use yourself. The SimplePress team runs product imports for you. Email **help@generativejustice.org** and see [Importing from another store](/docs/products#importing-from-another-store).

## Choosing Where to Move Next

Open **START HERE.html** and use the "which option is right for me?" table to pick the platform that fits your store. Each platform has a matching guide in **Guides/** with step-by-step instructions.

If you plan to hire someone to help, share the whole ZIP file or the **Guides/** folder with them. The **Hiring a designer or developer** guide explains what to ask for.

Leaving SimplePress for good? Download your site **before** you ask us to close your store, since you can't download it yourself once a store is closed. See [If Your Store Is Paused or Closed](/docs/leaving-or-closing#leaving-or-closing-on-purpose) for the full checklist.

---

## Keeping Your Export Safe

The **Spreadsheets/** folder contains your customers' personal information, including names, email addresses, and addresses. Treat it like any other private customer record:

- Store the ZIP file somewhere only you can access.
- Don't email it or post it publicly.
- Share the spreadsheets only with people who need them to move your store.
- Delete your copies once your move is finished and you no longer need them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is it free?**
Yes. Downloading your site doesn't cost anything.

**Can I download more than once?**
Yes. Download again any time you need a fresh copy of your store. Keeping backups is your job, so we suggest downloading once a month. See [Back up regularly](/docs/leaving-or-closing#back-up-regularly).

**Does downloading close my site?**
No. Your site stays live, and customers can keep ordering, booking, and checking out. Downloading doesn't change anything in your store.

**Will my customers' cards move?**
No. Payments are processed through your own Stripe account, so the download doesn't move customers' cards. The **Payments, customers and subscriptions** guide in the download explains what to do next.

**What about my domain?**
The download doesn't change or transfer your domain name. Read the **Keeping your domain name** guide in the **Guides/** folder before you change where your domain points.

**Why does the download take a minute?**
Stores with many photos take longer because every photo and video is included. Keep the page open until the download starts.

## What you can and can't do

| Action | Available? |
| ------ | ---------- |
| Download a full copy of your site | Yes (Owners only) |
| Download as often as you like, for free | Yes |
| Keep selling while you download | Yes |
| Let a Manager or Staff member download it | No. It includes customer records |
| Pick which parts to include | No. You get everything |
| Export only your products | Yes, from the Products page (Owners only, with **Products CSV export** on) |
| Import products yourself | No. The SimplePress team does product imports |
| Have the export move your domain or customers' cards | No |
