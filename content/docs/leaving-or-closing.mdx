title: If Your Store Is Paused or Closed

---

## Overview

Every SimplePress store is in one of three states: **active**, **suspended** or **closed**. Almost every store is active, all the time. This page explains the other two, what you keep no matter what, and how to leave SimplePress on your own terms.

Only SimplePress can change a store's state. There's no switch for it in your admin. A store is usually suspended while a problem is fixed, for example a broken rule in the [Acceptable Use Policy](/docs/your-agreement#what-you-cant-sell-or-do), and closed when a business has shut down for good. SimplePress can do either **with or without notice**. See [Your Agreement with SimplePress](/docs/your-agreement).

<Callout type="info" title="Not the same as Maintenance Mode">
  [Maintenance Mode](/docs/maintenance-mode) is a switch **you** control. It shows visitors a "coming soon" or "under maintenance" page while you keep full use of your admin. A suspended or closed store is set by SimplePress, and you lose your admin too.
</Callout>

---

## The three store states

| | Active | Suspended | Closed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Visitors** | See and shop your store as normal. | Can't see your store. Every page, including the home page, shows a "This page isn't here" error. | Same as suspended. |
| **Customers** | Can buy, sign in and see their orders on your store. | Can't buy, donate or start a subscription, even from a cart they opened before the store was suspended. Can't open their account or orders on your store. | Same as suspended. |
| **Owners, Managers and Staff** | Use the admin as normal. | Can still sign in, but the admin shows a "page not found" error. Nothing in it can be opened or changed. | Can't sign in on your store's address at all. The admin is gone, as with suspended. |
| **Download your site** | Owners can download it any time. | Not available to you. SimplePress can download it for you. | Not available to you. SimplePress can download it for you. |
| **SimplePress** | Can open your admin for setup and support. | Can still open your admin to fix the problem, then make the store active again. | Can still download a copy of your store. |

A suspended store can be made active again at any time, and everything comes back as it was. Changing a store's state doesn't delete anything by itself. But SimplePress doesn't promise to keep your data, so don't count on it being there later. See [Back up regularly](#back-up-regularly).

<Callout type="warn" title="Subscriptions keep billing">
  Suspending or closing a store **doesn't cancel your customers' subscriptions**. They live in your Stripe account, so Stripe can keep charging customers until you cancel them. See [Subscriptions](/docs/subscriptions).
</Callout>

## What stays yours

Whatever happens to your store, these belong to you:

- **Your Stripe account.** Card payments go to your own Stripe account, not to SimplePress. You can sign in to it at [dashboard.stripe.com](https://dashboard.stripe.com) without SimplePress, and payouts to your bank carry on as Stripe normally pays them. See [Payments and Payouts](/docs/payments-and-payouts).
- **Your domain.** You bought it and you renew it. You can point it at a new website whenever you like. The **Keeping your domain name** guide in your site download explains how. See [Custom Domain](/docs/domain).
- **Your content.** Your words, photos, products and logo are yours. See [Your content is yours](/docs/your-agreement#your-content-is-yours).

## Getting your data out

**While you still have access**, use [Download Your Site](/docs/data-and-export). It's free, Owners can run it as often as they like, and it gives you your pages, products, photos, customer and order spreadsheets, and import files for other platforms in one ZIP. Your store stays live while it downloads.

**After you lose access**, SimplePress can still download a copy of a suspended or closed store. Email [help@generativejustice.org](mailto:help@generativejustice.org) and ask. Under the agreement this is done at SimplePress's discretion, so it's much safer to download your own copy first.

## Leaving or closing on purpose

You can't close your store yourself from the admin. To leave SimplePress, email [help@generativejustice.org](mailto:help@generativejustice.org) and tell us. Before you do, work through this list:

1. **Download your site.** Go to **Settings → Data & Export** and click **Download your site**. Keep the ZIP somewhere safe. See [Download Your Site](/docs/data-and-export).
2. **Tell your customers.** Let them know where to find you next. A [banner](/docs/banner-and-popup) or an email works well.
3. **Finish open orders.** Ship or refund anything still waiting. See [Orders](/docs/orders).
4. **Cancel subscriptions.** Cancel each active subscription from **Subscriptions**, or your customers can keep being charged. Turning the Subscriptions feature off is not enough. See [Subscriptions](/docs/subscriptions).
5. **Settle invoices.** Collect payment for, or cancel, any outstanding invoices. See [Invoices](/docs/invoices).
6. **Close your store to visitors (optional).** Turn on [Maintenance Mode](/docs/maintenance-mode) to stop new orders while you wrap up. You keep your admin while it's on.
7. **Move your domain.** When your new site is ready, point your domain at it.
8. **Check Stripe.** Your Stripe account stays open until you close it with Stripe. Wait for any last payouts and disputes to finish first.

Then email us, and we'll close the store.

## Back up regularly

Under the [Terms of Service](/docs/your-agreement#what-simplepress-doesnt-promise), keeping backups is **your job**. SimplePress doesn't guarantee uptime or that your data will be kept.

We suggest you download your site **once a month**, and again before any big change. Downloading is free and doesn't affect your store. Keep a few recent copies somewhere safe, because the spreadsheets hold your customers' personal information.

---

## Who at SimplePress can see your store

A few people on the SimplePress team are **platform admins**. A platform admin can open any store's admin, including orders, customers and settings, and can download any store's data. Downloading still works when a store is suspended or closed.

We use this to set up your store, to answer your support questions, and to fix problems. Platform admins don't show up in your [team list](/docs/team-and-roles), because they aren't members of your store.

---

## When all of SimplePress is down for maintenance

Sometimes SimplePress turns on maintenance for the **whole platform**, for example during a big update. This is different from your own Maintenance Mode, and it affects every store at once.

While it's on:

- **Your admin** shows a "We'll be back soon" maintenance page instead of the dashboard. This applies to everyone except SimplePress's platform admins, Owners included.
- **Your storefront** shows the maintenance page to every visitor. Unlike your own Maintenance Mode, you can't preview the real site while signed in.
- **Checkout** is blocked. Anyone who tries to pay sees "This store is temporarily unavailable. Please try again later."

When SimplePress turns it off, your store and admin come back as they were. You don't need to do anything.

---

## What you can and can't do

| You can | You can't |
| ------- | --------- |
| Download your whole site any time while your store is active. | Download your site yourself once the store is suspended or closed. |
| Ask SimplePress for a copy of a suspended or closed store. | Suspend, close or reopen a store yourself. |
| Keep your Stripe account, payouts, domain and content if you leave. | Rely on suspension or closing to cancel subscriptions. |
| Ask us to close your store by email. | Use your admin while your store is suspended or closed, or during platform-wide maintenance. |
