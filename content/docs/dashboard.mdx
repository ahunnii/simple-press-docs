## Overview

The Dashboard is your admin home screen. It answers three questions at a glance: how is the store doing, what needs my attention, and where do I go next?

It's mostly a window onto your store. It shows you what's happening and gives you shortcuts, and you make changes on the pages it links to. What you can change from here is the Dashboard itself: you choose which cards appear, how big they are, and what time period they cover (see [Customizing Your Dashboard](#customizing-your-dashboard)). You can also hide the Search readiness strip (see [Search Readiness](#search-readiness)).

To open it any time, click **Dashboard** at the top of the admin sidebar.

> **Where signing in takes you:** When you go to your admin, SimplePress sends you to the [Setup Guide](/docs/launch-checklist) until every **Required** step on it is done. Which steps those are depends on your kind of business. For a store that sells products, they include Stripe, a first product, and a custom domain. After that, you land on the Dashboard. The Setup Guide has a **Continue to dashboard** button if you want to skip ahead. See [Getting back to the Setup Guide](#getting-back-to-the-setup-guide).

> **Who can see it:** Owners and Managers. Each person has their own layout for each store. Staff don't have a Dashboard. When a Staff member signs in, they go straight to Orders, and they only see Orders, Customers and Inventory in the sidebar. See [Team & Roles](/docs/team-and-roles).

> **Time periods:** Each card has its own time range, which you can change in edit mode. Ranges follow your store's time zone, and there's no custom date range here. See [How time ranges work](#how-time-ranges-work). For deeper reports, see [Finances](/docs/finances).

## Payments-Paused Banner

If Stripe ever turns off charges on your account, a banner appears across the top of every admin page, not only the Dashboard:

**Payments are paused.** Stripe has disabled charges on your account, so checkout is failing for every shopper.

It has two links: **Fix in Stripe** opens your Stripe dashboard in a new tab, and **Details** opens **Settings → Integrations**. The banner can't be dismissed. It goes away on its own once Stripe turns charges back on. Staff members don't see it.

You won't see this banner in normal use. It only appears when Stripe has confirmed a problem with your account.

## Finish Setting Up Your Store

New stores see a **Finish setting up your store** card at the top. It shows a progress bar, "N of M steps complete", and a **Next:** line naming the next step to tackle. The card follows your [Setup Guide](/docs/getting-started#your-setup-guide), so the steps depend on your kind of business: selling products, services, informational, or donations. A store that sells products might see steps like these:

1. **Store created.** Already done when you arrive.
2. **Connect a custom domain.** Use your own web address instead of the temporary one.
3. **Connect payment processing.** Connect Stripe so you can take payments.
4. **Add your first product.**
5. **Set up sales tax** and **set up shipping or pickup.**
6. **Add your contact info**, **publish your store policies**, and **customize your storefront.** These are optional for a store that sells products.

Required steps come up as **Next:** before optional ones.

Click **Continue setup** to open the Setup Guide, which shows every step with buttons to complete each one, a link to preview your store, and links to this documentation and to support. The card disappears once every step is done.

![The Finish setting up your store card showing 4 of 5 steps complete, the next step, and the Continue setup button](/images/dashboard/finish-setup-card.png)

*This store has four of five steps done, and the next one is connecting a custom domain.*

Stores set up before the tailored Setup Guide keep their original five steps: store created, Stripe, custom domain, first product, and customize.

See [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started) and the [Launch checklist](/docs/launch-checklist) for a full walkthrough of the setup steps.

### Getting back to the Setup Guide

You can open the Setup Guide again whenever you like, even after you've finished setup:

- Click **Setup Guide** in the sidebar, just below **Settings**.
- While any **Required** step is still open, a small **Almost there!** box at the bottom of the sidebar says "Complete setup to start selling" and has a **Finish setup** button. It goes away once every Required step is done. Staff don't see this box; Owners and Managers do.
- Press the search shortcut described under [Finding Things Fast](#finding-things-fast) and choose **View setup guide**.

![The admin sidebar, ending with Settings and Setup Guide just above your account name](/images/dashboard/welcome-sidebar.png)

*The Setup Guide link sits below Settings at the bottom of the sidebar.*

## Search Readiness

Below the setup card you may see a **Search readiness** strip with a percentage. It estimates how well your store is prepared to show up in Google and other search tools, based on things like your site title and description, your social sharing image, whether your products have descriptions, and whether you have FAQs.

The strip shows:

- A progress bar and your current percentage.
- A **Next:** hint telling you the single most useful thing to do next.
- An **Improve search results** button, which opens **Site Setup → SEO & Meta**, where the full report lives.
- A small **Hide** button.

The strip disappears by itself once you reach 100%. To see the full report and what each item means, read [SEO & Search Readiness](/docs/seo).

![The Search readiness strip at 58 percent, with the Hide and Improve search results buttons](/images/dashboard/search-readiness.png)

*The Search readiness strip, with its next suggested step.*

### Hiding or showing the strip

Click **Hide** to remove the strip from your Dashboard. Hide switches off the **Search readiness on dashboard** feature for the whole store, so it disappears for everyone on your team, not just you. You'll see a message: "Search readiness hidden. Turn it back on under Settings → Features."

To bring it back, go to **Settings → Features**, find **Search readiness on dashboard**, and turn it on. This feature is on by default. See [Feature toggles](/docs/feature-flags).

Hiding the strip only hides it from the Dashboard. You can still open the full report any time under **Site Setup → SEO & Meta**.

## Needs Attention

When something is waiting on you, colored reminders appear in a strip below the setup and search cards. They stay pinned at the top and aren't part of the cards you arrange. Each reminder is clickable and takes you straight to the right list. Reminders only appear when there's something to do.

| Reminder                        | What it says                                                                  | Where it takes you                          |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **N orders to fulfill**         | "Paid but not yet shipped." Customers have paid, but the order hasn't shipped or been handed over yet. | The Orders list, filtered to **Open**, **Unfulfilled** and **Paid**. |
| **N orders awaiting payment**   | "Pending payment confirmation." Orders were started but payment hasn't been confirmed yet. | The Orders list, filtered to pending payment. |
| **No store policies published** | "Add a Terms of Service & Refund Policy." Your Terms of Service or Refund Policy is missing, empty, or not published. | **Site Setup → Policies**.                  |

Cancelled and refunded orders aren't counted in the first two reminders.

> **Partially shipped orders:** The "to fulfill" number counts only open, paid orders that haven't shipped at all. Partially shipped orders aren't counted, so the number always matches the list the card opens. It also matches the **Needs fulfillment** card on the Orders page and the **Orders to fulfill** card you can add to your Dashboard. To see partially shipped orders, use the **Fulfillment** filter on the Orders page and choose **Partially Fulfilled**.

![The Needs Attention card reading 20 orders to fulfill, Paid but not yet shipped](/images/dashboard/needs-attention.png)

*One Needs Attention card. When the other two apply, they appear beside it.*

See [Orders](/docs/orders) for how to fulfill orders and [Pages & Policies](/docs/pages-and-policies) for writing your policies.

### The "Cart is disabled" notice

If **Shopping Cart & Checkout** is turned off for your store, a **Cart is disabled** notice appears at the top of the Dashboard. Customers can't add items to a cart while it's off. Go to **Settings → Features** to turn it back on. See [Feature toggles](/docs/feature-flags).

## Customizing Your Dashboard

The cards below the pinned reminders are yours to arrange. You choose which numbers and lists appear, how big each one is, and what time period it covers.

Your layout belongs to you. Each Owner and Manager has their own Dashboard layout for each store, so changing yours doesn't change anyone else's. Staff don't see the Dashboard at all.

New people start with the standard layout:

| Card                              | Time range    |
| --------------------------------- | ------------- |
| **Net revenue**                   | Today         |
| **Gross revenue**                 | Last 7 days   |
| **Orders**                        | Last 7 days   |
| **Average order**                 | Last 30 days  |
| **Recent orders**                 | Latest orders |
| **Low stock alerts**              | Right now     |
| **Revenue chart**                 | Last 30 days  |
| **Top products**                  | Last 30 days  |

![The full Dashboard in its standard layout: the setup card and orders-to-fulfill reminder, four number cards, Recent orders, Low stock alerts, the Revenue chart and Top products, with the Edit dashboard button at the top right](/images/dashboard/dashboard-full-page.png)

*The standard layout on a store with a few orders. Your own page shows only the cards you chose.*

> **What stays put:** The setup checklist, the "orders to fulfill" reminder, and the oversold-items warning stay pinned at the top of the Dashboard. They aren't cards, so you can't move or remove them.

### Editing your layout

1. Click **Edit dashboard** at the top of the Dashboard.
2. An editing bar appears with **Add card**, **Reset to default**, **Cancel** and **Save**, plus a counter such as "8 / 24 cards". You can have up to 24 cards.
3. Make your changes (see below). Dashed outlines show the open spaces in the grid.
4. Click **Save** to keep them.

![The Dashboard in edit mode: the Editing dashboard bar with Add card, Reset to default, Cancel and Save, a size chip and handle on every card, and dashed open cells with an Add card tile below the last card](/images/dashboard/dashboard-edit-mode.png)

*Edit mode. Every card shows a drag handle, a settings button and its size. The dashed cells at the bottom are open space.*

**Your changes aren't kept until you press Save.** If you try to leave the page with unsaved changes, SimplePress asks first. **Cancel** throws away everything you changed since you started editing.

**Reset to default** puts back the standard set of cards shown above. It asks you to confirm first. As with any other change, you still need to press **Save** afterward.

### Adding a card

Click **Add card** and pick from the list. Cards are grouped by what they're about (see [The Cards You Can Add](#the-cards-you-can-add)). Once you've reached 24 cards, remove one before adding another.

You can add the same card more than once, with different settings. For example, you could show **Net revenue** for this month and again for all time.

![The Add a card window listing cards under Orders and revenue, each with a small size preview, a description and its default size and time range, and an On dashboard label on the ones you already have](/images/dashboard/add-card-dialog.png)

*The Add a card window. Cards you already have are marked "On dashboard", and you can still add them again.*

### Moving cards around

- **On a computer or tablet,** drag a card by its handle and drop it where you want it.
- **On a phone,** use the up and down arrows on each card.

The card menu also has **Move to start**, **Move earlier**, **Move later** and **Move to end**.

![The Dashboard in edit mode on a phone, with the Add, Reset, Cancel and Save buttons at the top and up and down arrows and a settings button on each card](/images/dashboard/edit-mode-phone.png)

*Edit mode on a phone. Each card has up and down arrows instead of a drag handle.*

### Changing a card

While you're editing, each card shows a sliders icon. Click it to open that card's settings:

- **Size.** Small (1×1), Wide (2×1), Large (2×2) or Full width (4×2). Each card offers only the sizes that make sense for it. A Wide number card also shows a small trend line.
- **Time range.** Today, This week (starts Monday), This month, This year, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days, or All time. Cards that show "right now" things, such as Low stock alerts, don't use a time range.
- **Compare to previous period.** On by default. The card shows a green ▲ or red ▼ with the change compared to the same stretch of the previous period, for example "vs last week". It isn't available for **All time**, or for cards that show a "right now" count.
- **Move** and **Remove.** Remove takes the card off your Dashboard. You can add it back any time.

![A card's settings menu open under the Gross revenue card, with Size (Small or Wide), Time range, Compare to previous period, move options and Remove card](/images/dashboard/card-settings-menu.png)

*A card's settings menu. The ranges and size choices differ a little from card to card.*

![The Gross revenue card set to Wide (2×1) and Last 30 days, with a small trend line beside the number](/images/dashboard/wide-card-trend.png)

*The same Gross revenue card after choosing Wide and Last 30 days. The trend line shows how the number moved across the period.*

![The row of four number cards: Net revenue (Today), Gross revenue (Last 7 days), Orders (Last 7 days) and Average order (Last 30 days), with a red down arrow and percentage on Average order](/images/dashboard/sales-cards.png)

### How time ranges work

Time ranges follow your store's time zone, which you set under [General Settings](/docs/general-settings). "To date" ranges count from the start of the period up to right now:

- **Today** is since midnight.
- **This week** starts on Monday.
- **This month** and **This year** start on the 1st.
- **Last 7 / 30 / 90 days** means that many calendar days, **including today**. "Last 7 days" is today plus the six days before it.
- **All time** is everything since your store began.

Because ranges are "to date," a comparison early in the week or month compares a short stretch to the same short stretch of the previous period, so it's like for like.

> **Numbers may look different from the old Dashboard.** Revenue now matches your [Finances](/docs/finances) page and leaves out cancelled orders. And "Last N days" now means N calendar days including today, in your store's time zone, instead of a rolling window. If a total moved a little after this change, that's why.

## The Cards You Can Add

Only cards for features that are turned on for your store appear in the **Add card** list. If you turn a feature off, its cards are hidden from your Dashboard, and they come back, with your settings, when you turn the feature back on. See [Feature toggles](/docs/feature-flags).

### Orders and revenue

| Card                   | What it shows                                                                                         |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gross revenue**      | Money from paid orders in the period, before refunds. Includes tax and shipping.                      |
| **Net revenue**        | Gross revenue minus refunds. This matches your [Finances](/docs/finances) page. Cancelled orders aren't counted. |
| **Refunds**            | How much you refunded in the period.                                                                  |
| **Orders**             | How many orders were placed in the period.                  |
| **Average order**      | Net revenue divided by the number of paid orders, so you see what a customer spends per order.       |
| **Orders to fulfill**  | Paid orders waiting to be shipped or handed over.                                                     |
| **Awaiting payment**   | Orders started but not yet paid.                                                                      |
| **Orders fulfilled**   | Of the orders placed in the period, how many have been fulfilled.                                     |
| **Orders cancelled**   | Of the orders placed in the period, how many were cancelled.                                          |
| **Recent orders**      | Your most recent orders, newest first, with order number, status, customer, how long ago, and total.  |
| **Revenue chart**      | A chart of revenue over the period, day by day.                                                       |
| **Top products**       | Your best sellers by revenue in the period, with photo, units sold and revenue.                       |

On **Recent orders**, click any row to open that order, or click the card's title to open the full Orders list. With no orders yet, it says "No orders yet."

On **Top products**, a store with no sales in the period sees "No sales data yet."

**Revenue chart.** Hover over a bar to see that day's total. Days with no sales show as zero, so a quiet store sees a mostly flat chart rather than an empty message. For the full picture of where the money went, see [Finances](/docs/finances).

![The Revenue chart (Last 30 days) bar chart above the Top products list](/images/dashboard/revenue-top-products.png)

*The Revenue chart and Top products cards.*

> **Refunds:** Net revenue shows what you sold after refunds. The up and down arrows compare your totals after refunds, so a big refund will pull the arrow down even if the gross number looks high.

### Inventory

| Card                    | What it shows                                                                |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Low stock**           | A count of products and items that are running low.                          |
| **Low stock alerts**    | The list of what's running low, lowest first, so you can restock before you sell out. |

### Low Stock Alerts

The **Low stock alerts** card lists items that are running low. Each row shows how many are "N left." The list shows up to five products or variants, lowest first, followed by any [inventory items](/docs/inventory-management) that are running low. The **Low stock** count card uses the same rules.

**How "low" is decided:**

- Each product uses its own **Low Inventory Threshold**, which you set on the product form. Variants use their product's threshold.
- Each inventory item uses its own **Low stock alert at** number, set on the item.
- If you haven't set a number, the cutoff is **10**.

What's included:

- Published products and variants that have **Track Inventory** turned on. Inventory items show "Inventory item" under their name.
- Inventory items you've set up as stock. Rental items aren't included, because a rental showing a low number may just mean the rest are out with customers.

Click an inventory item to open its details. Click the card's title to open your products and update quantities. A red warning icon on an inventory item means it's at zero or below.

When nothing is running low, the card shows "All stock levels are good!" and "No products are running low."

![The Low stock alerts card, with five product variants showing 0 left and a "+ 64 more low on stock" line at the bottom](/images/dashboard/low-stock.png)

*Low stock alerts. Inventory items that are running low follow the products and variants, marked "Inventory item."*

These cards need the [Inventory](/docs/inventory-management) features turned on.

The traffic cards need **Analytics** turned on under **Settings → Features**, and Umami analytics connected for your store under **Settings → Integrations**.

| Card                | What it shows                                              |

### Conversion Rate

The **Conversion rate** card shows the share of visitors in the period who placed a paid order, with a note such as "12 paid orders / 640 visitors." It stays hidden until your store has recorded visitors. If analytics can't connect for a moment, the card shows a dash with "Analytics can't connect right now" or "Analytics is temporarily unavailable" instead of a number.

See [Integrations](/docs/integrations) for connecting Umami, and [Analytics](/docs/analytics) for what the numbers mean.

## Oversold Items

If a customer bought more of something than you had in stock during the last 30 days, a red **Oversold items need attention** box appears at the top of the Dashboard. It lists up to ten affected items, how many were in stock at the time, and a link to each order so you can decide whether to restock, substitute, or contact the customer.

This box only appears when it happens, and it clears itself once the 30-day window passes. It stays pinned at the top and isn't one of your cards.

## Finding Things Fast

Press **Command + K** on a Mac or **Ctrl + K** on Windows, or click the "Search…" box at the top of any admin page, to open the **command palette**. It jumps to any admin page, starts common tasks such as **View setup guide**, and finds orders, customers and products by name, email, number or SKU. For everything it can do, plus the sidebar and other shortcuts, see [Finding Your Way Around the Admin](/docs/admin-basics#searching-and-jumping-around).

## Troubleshooting

**I see a "Cart is disabled" notice at the top.**
Shopping Cart & Checkout is turned off for your store, so customers can't add items to a cart. Go to **Settings → Features** and turn it on. See [Feature toggles](/docs/feature-flags).

**I see a "Payments are paused" banner.**
Stripe has disabled charges on your account, so shoppers can't check out. Click **Fix in Stripe** to see what Stripe needs from you, or **Details** to open **Settings → Integrations**. The banner clears once Stripe turns charges back on. See [Payments & Payouts](/docs/payments-and-payouts#when-stripe-pauses-payments).

**The numbers look different from my Stripe account.**
The Dashboard shows orders recorded in SimplePress, over the time range set on each card. Stripe reports on its own schedule, and payouts arrive after payments. See [Payments & Payouts](/docs/payments-and-payouts) and [Finances](/docs/finances).

**A product I'm low on isn't showing in Low stock alerts.**
The product must be published and have **Track Inventory** turned on. Products that aren't tracked are always treated as in stock. Also check its **Low Inventory Threshold**: the product only shows once its stock is at or below that number (or 10, if you haven't set one).

**I hid Search readiness and want it back.**
Go to **Settings → Features** and turn on **Search readiness on dashboard**.

**I don't see the Conversion rate or Visitors card.**
They need the **Analytics** feature turned on, Umami analytics connected under **Settings → Integrations**, and at least some visitors recorded in the period.

**A card I want isn't in the Add card list, or one I had disappeared.**
Cards only appear for features that are turned on. Check **Settings → Features**. If you turned a feature off, its cards are hidden, and they return, with your settings, when you turn it back on.

**I can't add another card.**
You can have up to 24 cards. The counter in the editing bar shows how many you have. Remove one, then add the new one.

**My changes disappeared.**
Changes are only kept when you press **Save**. **Cancel** and leaving the page without saving both throw them away.

**My Dashboard layout is different from another Owner's or Manager's.**
That's expected. Each person has their own layout for each store.

**My revenue numbers changed.**
Revenue now matches the Finances page and leaves out cancelled orders, and "Last N days" now counts N calendar days including today in your store's time zone. See [How time ranges work](#how-time-ranges-work).

**I signed in and ended up on the Setup Guide instead of the Dashboard.**
One or more **Required** steps on your Setup Guide are still open. Which steps those are depends on your kind of business. For a store that sells products, they include Stripe, a product, and a custom domain. Finish them, or click **Continue to dashboard** on the Setup Guide. See the [Launch checklist](/docs/launch-checklist).

**A team member can't see the Dashboard.**
Staff don't have access to it. Change their role to Manager if they need it. See [Team & Roles](/docs/team-and-roles).
