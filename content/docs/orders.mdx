## Overview

Every time a customer checks out on your storefront, a new order appears under **Orders** in your admin sidebar (`/admin/orders`). You can also create an order yourself for a phone order, a wholesale sale, or someone paying in person.

From an order you can:

- ship it and send the customer tracking, in one package or several;
- let a local pickup customer know their order is ready;
- issue a full or partial refund;
- cancel it, with or without returning the items to stock;
- print a packing slip or an invoice;
- keep a private note about it.

The order list and everything above it are covered below, in the order you'll usually need them.

<Callout type="info">
Orders is on by default and needs **Products** turned on. If Orders is switched off in **Settings → Features**, the Orders pages show a "Feature disabled" message instead. See [Feature flags](/docs/feature-flags).
</Callout>

## The Orders list

![The Orders page with Export CSV and Create Manual Order buttons, three queue cards (Open orders, Needs fulfillment, Awaiting payment), a search bar with a Filters button, and a table of orders](/images/orders/orders-list.png)

*The Orders list. The cards at the top are shortcuts to the orders that need you.*

### Queue cards

Three cards across the top count the orders that need attention across your whole store. Click a card to show only those orders:

| Card | Shows |
| ---- | ----- |
| **Open orders** | Every order with the status Open. |
| **Needs fulfillment** | Open orders that haven't shipped or been handed over yet. |
| **Awaiting payment** | Open orders where the payment hasn't come in yet. |

The card you're viewing gets a ring around it. The numbers always count your whole store, even while you're searching or filtering.

### Search

Type in the box that says "Search by customer, email, or order number…" and click **Search**. Each word you type is matched against the customer's name, their email and the order number, so `jordan 1042` or `#1042` both work.

### Filters and sorting

![The Filters popover open on the Orders page, with Status, Fulfillment, Payment and Sort dropdowns](/images/orders/orders-filters.png)

*Filters narrow the list by any of the three statuses and change the sort order.*

Click **Filters** to narrow the list:

| Filter | Options |
| ------ | ------- |
| **Status** | All Statuses, Open, Completed, Cancelled, Refunded |
| **Fulfillment** | All Fulfillment, Unfulfilled, Partially Fulfilled, Fulfilled |
| **Payment** | All Payments, Awaiting Payment, Paid, Failed, Refunded, Disputed |
| **Sort** | Newest (the default), Oldest, Highest total, Lowest total |

The list shows 25 orders per page. If nothing matches, you'll see "No orders match your filters" with a **Clear filters** button. A brand-new store shows "No orders yet" instead.

### Reading a row

Each row shows the **Order** number and the date it was placed, the **Customer**'s name and email, the number of **Items**, the **Total**, the **Status**, and a **View** button that opens the order.

Next to the status you may see extra badges:

| Badge | Means |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Awaiting payment** | Payment hasn't come in yet. |
| **Partially fulfilled** | Some items have shipped and some haven't. |
| **Pickup** | The customer chose in-store pickup. |
| **Subscription** | The order came from a product subscription. |
| **Oversold** | At least one item sold more units than you had in stock. |
| **Disputed** (red) | The customer has disputed the card payment with their bank. See [Disputes](/docs/payments-and-payouts#disputes). |

There are no bulk actions on the list. You fulfill, refund or cancel one order at a time.

### Exporting to a spreadsheet

![The Export CSV and Create Manual Order buttons](/images/orders/export-button.png)

*Export CSV downloads whatever the list is currently showing.*

Click **Export CSV** to download a spreadsheet of every order that matches your current search and filters (all pages, not just the one on screen). The file is named `orders-your-store-name-date.csv`, and a message confirms how many orders were exported.

The columns are: Order Number, Date, Customer Name, Customer Email, Status, Payment Status, Fulfillment Status, Delivery Method, Subtotal, Shipping, Tax, Discount, Total, Refund Amount, Discount Code, Item Count, Items, and Shipping Address.

To download everything in your store at once (products, customers and more), see [Download your site](/docs/data-and-export).

## Order statuses

Each order carries three separate statuses. The **status** is the big picture. **Payment** and **fulfillment** track the money and the shipping on their own.

### Order status

| Status | What it means |
| ------ | ------------- |
| **Open** | The order still needs something from you, usually shipping or a payment. |
| **Completed** | The order is paid **and** fully fulfilled. SimplePress sets this for you. You never pick it by hand. |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled the order. |
| **Refunded** | The full amount has been refunded. |

### Payment status

| Status | What it means |
| ------ | ------------- |
| **Awaiting Payment** (shown as Pending on the order page) | No payment has come in yet. |
| **Paid** | Payment received. |
| **Failed** | The payment didn't go through. Stripe sets this. |
| **Refunded** | The full amount has been refunded, or the customer won a dispute (even one for part of the order). |
| **Disputed** | The customer has disputed the card payment. Stripe sets this, and sets it back to Paid or Refunded when the dispute closes. While it's disputed, the refund and fulfillment buttons are hidden. See [Disputes](/docs/payments-and-payouts#disputes). |

### Fulfillment status

| Status | What it means |
| ------ | ------------- |
| **Unfulfilled** | Nothing has shipped or been handed over yet. |
| **Partially Fulfilled** | Some items have shipped and some haven't. |
| **Fulfilled** | Everything has shipped, or a pickup order is ready. |

## Inside an order

Click **View** on any row to open the order.

![An open order showing the Order Items card, the Mark as Fulfilled card, and cards on the right for Order Status, Customer Information, Shipping Address, Notes and Payment](/images/orders/order-detail.png)

*A paid order that's ready to ship.*

**Along the top:**

- The order date, plus a **Payment:** badge and a **Fulfillment:** badge. **Pickup**, **Oversold** and **Subscription** badges appear when they apply. The Subscription badge links to the subscription.
- Buttons for **Packing Slip**, **Invoice** and **More Options**.

<Callout type="info">
The order page's title shows the order number (for example "Order #1030"), the same number used in the Orders list, on the packing slip and invoice, and in your customer's emails.
</Callout>

**On the left:**

- **Order Items**: each product with its variant, SKU, quantity and price, then the subtotal, any discount (with the code used), shipping, tax and total. While an order is partially shipped, each item shows **Shipped**, **N of M shipped** or **Not shipped**.
- If something was oversold, a red **Oversold items in this order** alert lists each item and how many were in stock at the time of purchase, for example "(0 in stock at time of purchase)". You may want to contact the customer or restock. See [Inventory management](/docs/inventory-management).
- The card for getting the order to the customer: **Mark as Fulfilled** for shipping orders, or **Ready for Pickup** for pickup orders. It only appears once the order is paid.

**On the right:**

- **Order Status**: the status (Open, Completed and so on), a short hint about what's next, and the order's shipments once it has any.
- **Customer Information**: name, email and phone (if given).
- **Shipping Address**, or **Pickup Location** for pickup orders.
- **Notes**: your private note and the customer's note from checkout.
- **Payment**: the payment status, the **Method** (Card, Cash, Check or Manual), the refund reason if there is one, the Stripe **Payment ID** for card payments, and the refund button.

## Fulfilling and tracking

SimplePress doesn't buy shipping labels for you. You ship the order the way you normally would, then record it here so your customer gets an email with their tracking.

<video src="/videos/orders/fulfill-order.webm" poster="/videos/orders/fulfill-order-poster.png" controls muted playsInline className="rounded-lg border" />

*Adding a tracking number, clicking Mark as Fulfilled & Send Email, and the order switching to Completed with its shipment listed.*

1. Open the order. The **Mark as Fulfilled** card sits under the order items.
2. Leave **Include tracking information** ticked if you have a tracking number. Untick it if you're shipping without one; the customer gets a "your order has been fulfilled" email instead of a tracking email.
3. Type or paste the **Tracking Number**. The **Carrier** (USPS, UPS, FedEx or DHL) fills itself in from the number, and "Detected from tracking number" appears underneath. You can change it if it guessed wrong.
4. Click **Mark as Fulfilled & Send Email**.

![The Mark as Fulfilled card with tracking included, USPS selected, and a tracking number filled in](/images/orders/fulfill-card.png)

*The fulfillment card, ready to send. The blue note tells you exactly who will be emailed.*

You'll see a confirmation message that tells you which email went out, for example "Order fulfilled and email sent to customer". The order's fulfillment changes to **Fulfilled**, and if it's paid, its status changes to **Completed**. If the email fails to send, a warning says so, and you can resend it from **More Options**.

### Carriers and tracking links

| Carrier | Tracking link for the customer |
| ------- | ------------------------------ |
| USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL | Built for you from the tracking number. |
| Other | A **Tracking URL** field appears. Paste the carrier's tracking page link if you have one. It's optional. |

### Need a label?

When tracking is included, the card shows "Need a shipping label? Buy discounted postage on PirateShip." Click **Open PirateShip** to buy a discounted label in a new tab, then copy the tracking number back into SimplePress.

### More than one box

Click **Add Additional Tracking Information** to add another package to the same form. Each package gets its own carrier and tracking number, and the customer gets one shipping email per package.

### After it's fulfilled

Once the order is fulfilled, the **Order Status** card lists each package: "Package 1 — 2 hours ago", the carrier, the tracking number, a **Track Package →** link, and (for partial shipments) what was in the box, such as "Contains: 2× Crystal – Gold Chain".

- **Fix a typo:** click the pencil next to a package, correct the carrier or number, and click **Save Changes**. No email is sent for a correction.
- **Add another package later:** click **Add Additional Tracking**, fill it in, and click **Add & Send Email**. The customer is emailed only if you entered a tracking number.

## Partial shipments

If some items ship now and the rest later:

1. On the fulfillment card, untick **Ship all remaining items**.
2. Under **Items in this package**, set how many of each item are in this box. Each line shows how many are left to ship, for example "of 2".
3. Click **Fulfill Selected Items & Send Email** (or **Fulfill Selected Items** if you unticked tracking).

The order becomes **Partially Fulfilled**, and each item shows whether it has shipped. The card is renamed **Fulfill Remaining Items** so you can ship the rest when it's ready. If you're shipping several boxes at once, each package you add gets its own items list ("Package 2", "Package 3"…).

<Callout type="info">
A partial shipment **with** tracking emails the customer that tracking straight away. A partial shipment **without** tracking sends no email; the customer hears from you once the whole order is fulfilled. The button reads **Fulfill Selected Items** (without "& Send Email"), and the confirmation says "Partial shipment recorded. No email sent".
</Callout>

## Local pickup orders

If a customer chose in-store pickup, the order shows a **Pickup** badge, and you'll see a **Pickup Location** card where the shipping address would be. It shows your pickup location (or, if you left that blank, your business address from General Settings) and any pickup instructions. If it says "No pickup location configured.", add one in your shipping settings. See [Shipping](/docs/shipping).

![A pickup order with a Pickup badge, a Ready for Pickup card with a Mark Ready for Pickup button, and a Pickup Location card](/images/orders/pickup-order.png)

*Pickup orders get a Ready for Pickup card instead of the shipping form.*

When the order is packed and waiting:

1. Open the order.
2. In the **Ready for Pickup** card, click **Mark Ready for Pickup**.

The customer is emailed straight away that their order is ready, with your pickup location and instructions. The order is marked **Fulfilled**, and **Completed** if it's paid. The Order Status card then reads "Customer notified — ready for pickup."

## Refunds

The refund button lives at the bottom of the **Payment** card on the right. It appears only while the order's payment is **Paid**.

### Orders paid by card (through Stripe)

1. Click **Issue Refund**.
2. **Refund amount** starts at the most you can refund (shown as "max $X"). Lower it for a partial refund. You'll see "Partial refund — customer keeps the difference".
3. Optionally pick a **Reason**: Customer requested refund, Duplicate order, or Fraudulent order. It's shown on the order afterwards.
4. Tick **Return items to inventory** if the items are coming back and you want them counted as stock again. It's off by default.
5. **Notify customer by email** is on by default. Untick it if you don't want to send the refund email.
6. Click **Issue Full Refund** (or **Refund $X** for a partial amount).

![The Issue Refund dialog with a partial amount of $20.00 entered, a reason dropdown, Return items to inventory and Notify customer by email checkboxes, and a Refund $20.00 button](/images/orders/refund-dialog.png)

*A partial refund. The money goes back to the customer's card through Stripe, and this can't be undone.*

**What happens next:**

- **Full refund:** the order's status and payment both become **Refunded**.
- **Partial refund:** the order stays **Open** (or **Completed** if it was fully fulfilled), and payment stays **Paid**. On a cancelled order, a partial refund leaves it **Cancelled**. There's no separate "partially refunded" status. The refunded amount shows on the invoice and in the export's Refund Amount column.
- You can refund in several steps. Each time, the maximum is whatever hasn't been refunded yet.

### Orders paid another way

For cash, check or other manual payments, the button reads **Mark as Refunded**. It opens a **Mark as Refunded** dialog with the same reason, restock and email options.

<Callout type="warn">
**Mark as Refunded** only records the refund in SimplePress. No money moves, so pay the customer back yourself first. It always records a full refund.
</Callout>

On a cancelled order, neither refund dialog offers **Return items to inventory**, because cancelling already offered to restock.

### Rewards points

If you run a rewards program, refunding an order (fully or partly) takes back the points the customer earned on it. So does cancelling a paid order.

## Cancelling an order

1. Open the order and click **More Options → Cancel Order**.
2. In the **Cancel Order #N** dialog, choose:
   - **Return items to inventory** (ticked by default) puts the items back in stock. It doesn't appear if the order has already had a refund; use the refund's restock option instead. It also doesn't appear for an order that was never paid (Pending), because nothing was taken out of stock.
   - **Notify customer by email** (ticked by default) sends the cancellation email.
3. Click **Cancel Order**, or **Go back** to leave it alone.

![The Cancel Order dialog with Return items to inventory and Notify customer by email both ticked, a red warning, and Go back and Cancel Order buttons](/images/orders/cancel-dialog.png)

*Cancelling can't be undone, and it doesn't refund the customer.*

Things to know:

- **Cancelling doesn't refund.** If the customer paid, issue the refund first (see [Refunds](#refunds)), then cancel.
- **You can't cancel an order once any of it has shipped** (Fulfilled or Partially Fulfilled). **Cancel Order** disappears from the menu at that point.
- Cancelled orders can't be reopened.

## Changing a status by hand

Sometimes you need to correct a status, for example when a customer paid in cash after you created the order. Use **More Options → Override Status**.

![The More Options menu showing Resend Emails with Resend Order Confirmation, Override Status with Payment Status and Fulfillment Status, and Cancel Order](/images/orders/more-options.png)

*The More Options menu.*

| Override | Choices | Notes |
| -------- | ------- | ----- |
| **Payment Status** | Pending, Paid | **Paid** is one-way. Once an order is Paid, **Pending** is greyed out, and to back out of a paid sale you cancel the order instead. **Paid** is also blocked once an order has had any refund. To mark something refunded, use the refund button in the Payment card. On a **Disputed** order both choices are greyed out with the note "dispute open"; Stripe settles the status when the dispute closes. |
| **Fulfillment Status** | Unfulfilled, Partially Fulfilled, Fulfilled | **Unfulfilled** resets every item to "not shipped" and brings back the fulfillment form. A Completed order goes back to **Open**. |

- You can't set the order status (Open, Completed and so on) directly. It follows from payment and fulfillment.
- Overrides don't email the customer. The one exception to "status only" is switching a manual order from **Pending** to **Paid**: that takes the items out of stock, adds the order to the customer's order count and total spent, and awards rewards points, just as if you'd saved it as Paid. To send emails, use the fulfillment form, **Mark Ready for Pickup**, or the refund and cancel options instead.
- Overrides aren't available on cancelled orders.
- There's no "Mark as Unfulfilled" button. To undo a fulfillment, use **Override Status → Fulfillment Status → Unfulfilled**. The packages you recorded stay listed on the order.

### Resending emails

The top of **More Options** offers **Resend Emails** that fit the order's state:

| Option | Shown when |
| ------ | ---------- |
| **Resend Order Confirmation** | The order is paid. |
| **Resend Shipped Email** (one per package) | A package has a tracking number. |
| **Resend Fulfilled Email** | The order was fulfilled without tracking. |
| **Resend Refund Email** | The order is fully refunded. |

## Notes and addresses

### Notes

The **Notes** card holds two things:

- **Internal Note** "(not visible to customer)": a private note for you and your team. Click **Edit** (or the note itself), type, and click **Save**.
- **Customer Note**: anything the customer typed at checkout. You can read it but not edit it.

Manual orders start with "[Manual Order]" at the top of the internal note.

### Shipping address

- Click **Copy** to copy the whole address for a label site. You'll see "Address copied".
- Click the pencil to open **Edit Shipping Address** and fix a typo. If the order has no address, the dialog is **Add Shipping Address**. You can only add an address when the order is linked to a customer.

Editing the address only updates the order in SimplePress. It doesn't change a label you've already bought.

## Printing a packing slip or invoice

Click **Packing Slip** or **Invoice** at the top of an order. Each opens a printable page with **Back to Order** and **Print** buttons.

![A packing slip for order #1030 with the store's logo and address, a Ship To address, a Customer block, and an Item / SKU / Qty table](/images/orders/packing-slip.png)

*The packing slip leaves out prices, so it's safe to put in the box.*

| What it shows | Packing slip | Invoice |
| ------------- | :----------: | :-----: |
| Your logo, name, address and contact details | Yes | Yes |
| **Ship To** address (or **Pickup Order** for pickup) | Yes | Yes |
| Items with **SKU** and **Qty** | Yes | Yes |
| **Unit Price** and line **Total** | No | Yes |
| Payment status, and subtotal, discount, shipping, tax and total | No | Yes |
| **Refunded** and **Amount Due** lines | No | Yes, when there's been a refund |

The **Invoice** here is a printable receipt for this order. To send a bill a customer pays online, see [Invoices](/docs/invoices).

## Creating a manual order

Use a manual order for a phone order, a market stall sale, a wholesale order, or anything that didn't go through your checkout. No card payment is taken. You collect payment however you normally would.

1. On the Orders page, click **Create Manual Order**.
2. Fill in the **New Manual Order** page (cards described below).
3. Click **Save changes**. The badge next to the title reads **Unsaved Changes** until you save, and **Reset** clears the form.

After saving, you land on the new order.

![The New Manual Order page with Customer Information, Delivery, Shipping Address, Notes, Payment & Fulfillment, Additional Charges and Order Items cards](/images/orders/manual-order-form.png)

*The manual order form. The totals update as you add items and charges.*

| Card | What you fill in |
| ---- | ---------------- |
| **Customer Information** | **Name** and **Email** (both required), or click **Find existing customer** to search your customers. Tick **Send order confirmation email to customer** to email them a confirmation. |
| **Delivery** | **Method**: "Ship to customer" or "In-store pickup". **Order Date**: leave it blank for today, or pick an earlier date to backdate a sale. |
| **Shipping Address** | Optional, for shipping orders only. Click **Add address**. Recipient Name defaults to the customer's name. |
| **Notes** | **Internal Notes**, visible to admins only (up to 2,000 characters). |
| **Payment & Fulfillment** | **Payment**: Pending, Paid, Failed or Refunded. **Payment Method**: Card, Cash or Check, or leave it on "Manual (default)". **Fulfillment**: Unfulfilled, Fulfilled or Partially Fulfilled. The order status is worked out from these. |
| **Additional Charges** | Flat **Shipping**, **Tax** and **Discount** amounts in dollars. Tax isn't calculated for you on manual orders. |
| **Order Items** | Click **Add Item**, then search "Search by name, SKU, or variant…". Pick a variant if the product has them, and set the **Quantity**. Prices fill in from your catalog. Or click **Enter subtotal directly** to type one amount instead of listing items, and **Select items instead** to switch back. |

**Good to know:**

- **Stock, the customer's order history totals and rewards points change when the order is Paid.** That happens either when you save it as Paid, or later, when you switch it from Pending to Paid with **More Options → Override Status → Payment Status → Paid**.
- Saving creates a customer from the name and email, or updates the existing customer with that email. See [Customers](/docs/customers).
- Manual orders show up in the Orders list with everything else. The Payment card's **Method** tells you how it was paid.

## Emails your customers get

SimplePress sends these automatically, using your store's name and logo:

| Email | Sent when |
| ----- | --------- |
| **Order Confirmation** | A customer checks out, or you tick the confirmation box on a manual order. Pickup orders get a pickup version. |
| **Order Shipped** | You fulfill with a tracking number, once per package. |
| **Order Fulfilled** | You fulfill the whole order without tracking. |
| **Ready for Pickup** | You click **Mark Ready for Pickup**. |
| **Order Refunded** | You refund (or mark as refunded) with **Notify customer by email** ticked. |
| **Order Cancelled** | You cancel with **Notify customer by email** ticked. |

You get a **New Order** email yourself each time a customer checks out.

To see what each one looks like, open **Notification Emails** in the admin sidebar. See [Email previews](/docs/email-preview).

## Common questions

**Why isn't the fulfillment form showing?**
The order isn't paid yet. Once you've been paid, use **More Options → Override Status → Payment Status → Paid**, and the form appears.

**I marked an order fulfilled by mistake. How do I undo it?**
**More Options → Override Status → Fulfillment Status → Unfulfilled**. The fulfillment form comes back. The customer has already had their email, so let them know if anything changed.

**Can I edit the items or prices on an order after it's placed?**
No. Refund the difference for a price change. For a different item, cancel and create a new manual order.

**Can I move an order to a different customer, or merge two orders?**
No, neither is possible.

**Can I cancel an order after it has shipped?**
No. Once any part of an order is fulfilled, **Cancel Order** isn't offered. Refund it instead, ticking **Return items to inventory** if the items came back.

**Can SimplePress print shipping labels?**
No. Buy labels from PirateShip (there's a button on the fulfillment form) or your carrier, then paste the tracking number into the order.

**Can I fulfill or cancel lots of orders at once?**
Not yet. Each order is handled on its own page.
