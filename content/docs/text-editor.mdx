## Overview

The text editor is the writing box you use all over SimplePress. It's where you write your pages, blog posts, and policies, and it shows up in the Site Editor and on product pages too. It works much like a word processor: type your text, then use the toolbar across the top to style it.

Besides headings, lists, and colors, the editor lets you:

- Add **pictures**, size them, and describe them for screen readers
- Add **links** to your own pages or other websites, and turn a link into a **button**
- Lay content out in **rows and columns**, such as a photo beside a paragraph
- Tuck details into **collapsible sections** that visitors click to open
- Add **tables**, and extras like galleries, embeds, quote calculators, and forms

How your text looks on your live site depends on your template. SimplePress usually builds each business its own custom template, and **Default**, **Modern**, and **Elegant** are ready-made starting points. The editor itself works the same everywhere. The pictures on this page were taken on the Default template.

| **Maintenance message** | The **Custom message** box in [Maintenance Mode](/docs/maintenance-mode) | No | No | No | No |

Every editor has the full toolbar, including the **Insert elements** (**+**) menu with **Code block**, **Blockquote**, **Divider**, and **Table**. The table above only shows the extras that change from place to place. The one exception is the email campaign message.

A few things to know:

- **A product's Description is plain text**, not this editor. Use **Additional information** on the **Product page** tab for formatted text, pictures, and rows.
- If a row, collapsible section, or space already exists in a box that doesn't offer them, it still shows on your site.
- **The email campaign message uses an email-safe version.** Email programs can't show everything a web page can, so it has fewer tools: **Heading 1** to **Heading 3** only, no **Code** in **More formatting**, a set of email-safe text colors, and only **Blockquote** and **Divider** in **Insert elements** (no tables or code blocks). Links, buttons, pictures, lists, and text alignment all still work. See [Email Marketing](/docs/email-marketing).

## Formatting text

### Text styles and headings

Click in a line, open **Text styles**, and pick **Normal Text** or a heading from **Heading 1** to **Heading 6**. Headings break your text into sections and help visitors and search engines scan the page.

Most pages already show their title as the main heading, so start your own headings at **Heading 2**. On the Default template, a page with Heading 2 or Heading 3 headings also gets an automatic **On this page** contents list.

### Bold, italic, and more

Select some words and click **Bold**, **Italic**, or **Underline**. The **More formatting** menu has:

- **Strikethrough**, for crossed-out text like an old price
- **Code**, for short bits of code or text you want shown exactly
- **Clear formatting**, which takes all of these styles off the selected words

### Text color

Select some words, click **Text color**, and pick a swatch. There are three rows of seven colors: strong colors, softer colors, and very light ones. **Default** puts the words back to your template's normal text color.

Use color sparingly. Light colors can be hard to read on a light background.

### Lists

Open **Lists** and choose **Numbered list** or **Bullet list**. Press Enter for the next item. Press Enter on an empty item to end the list.

### Text alignment

Click in a paragraph or heading, open **Text alignment**, and pick **Align left**, **Align center**, or **Align right**. The menu's icon changes to show the current line's alignment.

![The Text alignment menu open in the toolbar, showing Align left, Align center and Align right with their keyboard shortcuts](/images/text-editor/alignment-menu.png)

Alignment works on paragraphs and headings. It doesn't move pictures. To move a picture, use its own toolbar (see [Sizing and placing a picture](#sizing-and-placing-a-picture)).

### Adding space

Press Enter on an empty line to add a blank line between paragraphs, pictures, or other blocks. Each blank line shows on your site as one line of space. Press Shift+Enter to start a new line inside the same paragraph, with no extra gap. Blank lines at the very start or end of the page don't show on your site.

For a set gap, open **Insert elements** (the **+** menu) and choose **Space**. It shows in the editor as a striped box. Click it to pick **Small**, **Medium**, or **Large**, or click the trash icon to remove it. On your site the box is invisible: only the space shows. **Large** gets a little shorter on phones. You can also add a space inside a row column or a collapsible section.

![A selected Space block in the editor, a striped box labelled Space between a paragraph and a row, with its toolbar above it showing Small, Medium (selected) and Large, and a red trash icon](/images/text-editor/spacer-bubble.png)

## Pictures

### Adding pictures

Click the **Image** button in the toolbar. The **Add images** box opens: "Upload from your device or pick photos you've already added." There are three ways to add a photo:

- **Drag photos onto the box**, or click it (or **Choose images**) to browse your device. You can pick several at once.
- **Choose from library** picks photos you've already uploaded to your [Media Library](/docs/media-library). This option appears only when the Media Library is turned on.
- **Paste an image link** if the photo is already on the web. Click it to open a box, paste the photo's address (it must start with `https://`), and click **Insert**.

![The Add images box with the drag-and-drop area, the Choose images button, the Choose from library button, and the Paste an image link option](/images/text-editor/add-images-dialog.png)

You can also drag a photo straight into the text, or copy and paste one in.

Photos can be up to 25MB. Big photos are shrunk automatically, so you don't need to resize them first. You can use JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, or HEIC files. If something goes wrong, a message tells you why:

- **"[name] isn't an image"** means the file isn't a picture. Choose a JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, or HEIC file.
- **"[name] is too big"** means the file is over 25MB.

New pictures go in at **full width, centered**. While a picture is uploading, the save button reads **Uploading…** and waits until it's done.

### Sizing and placing a picture

Click a picture to show its toolbar.

![A selected picture of a felted shawl with its toolbar above it: the S, M, L and Full size buttons, Align left, Align center and Align right, the Alt button, Replace image, and Remove image](/images/text-editor/picture-toolbar.png)

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **S** | Small: a quarter of the width (25%) |
| **M** | Medium: half the width (50%) |
| **L** | Large: three-quarters of the width (75%) |
| **Full** | Full width (100%) |
| **Align left**, **Align center**, **Align right** | Moves the picture within its space |
| **Alt** | Describes the picture for screen readers. See [Describing a picture](#describing-a-picture-alt-text). |
| **Replace image** | Swaps in a different photo. See [Replacing a picture](#replacing-a-picture). |
| **Remove image** | Takes the picture out of the text |

Sizes are a share of the space the picture sits in. In normal text that's the width of the text. Inside a row, it's the width of the picture's column (see [Rows and columns](#rows-and-columns)). Sizes adapt on phones, so pictures still fit the screen.

**You can also drag to resize.** When a picture is selected, pill-shaped handles appear on its left and right edges. Drag one and a label shows the size you're on, like "Medium · 50%". When you let go, the picture snaps to the nearest size. Press Escape while dragging to cancel.

![A picture being resized with its right-hand handle, shrunk inside its column, with the Medium · 50% label near the top](/images/text-editor/drag-resize.png)

<Callout type="info" title="Pictures added before these sizes">
  Pictures you added a while ago may have an exact width in pixels. They keep that width until you click one of the sizes (or drag a handle). After that, they use the new sizes.
</Callout>

Hovering over a picture also shows a small menu with **View image**, **Download image**, **Copy image to clipboard**, and **Copy image link**.

### Describing a picture (alt text)

Alt text is a short description of a picture. Screen readers read it aloud, and it shows if the picture can't load. Search engines use it too.

Click the picture, then click **Alt**. A **red dot** on the button means the picture doesn't have a description yet. Hover over the button to see the current description.

![The small alt text box open over the top of a picture, with the Describe this image field, the help text, and Cancel and Save buttons](/images/text-editor/alt-text-dialog.png)

Type a description in **Describe this image**, such as "Two hands shaping clay on a wheel", and click **Save**. If a picture is only there for decoration, leave the box empty. For tips on writing good descriptions, see [Accessibility](/docs/accessibility#pictures-in-your-text).

### Replacing a picture

Click the picture, then click **Replace image**. The **Replace image** box opens: "Choose the new picture. Its size and alignment stay the same." Upload a photo, choose one from your library, or paste a link and click **Use link**.

## Links

### Adding a link

Select the words you want to link, then click the **Link** button in the toolbar. The link box has:

| Field | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **URL** | Where the link goes. Type a page path like `/contact`, or a full web address like `https://example.com`. |
| **Link to a page on your site** | Opens a list of your pages to pick from. See [Linking to your own pages](#linking-to-your-own-pages). |
| **Display Text (optional)** | The words people click. Leave it as it is to keep the words you selected. |
| **Style** | **Link**, **Button**, or **Outline button**. See [Buttons](#buttons). |
| **Open in New Tab** | Opens the link in a new browser tab. It turns on by itself when you type an address on another website, and off for your own pages. You can always flip it. |

Click **Save**, or press Enter in the **URL** box.

![The link box with the URL field, the Link to a page on your site option, Display Text, the Link, Button and Outline button style choices, the Open in New Tab switch, and the Save button](/images/text-editor/link-dialog.png)

If the **URL** box is empty, you'll see "Enter a link." If the address can't be used, you'll see "Link must be a relative path or start with http://, https://, mailto:, tel:, or sms:". You can link to an email address with `mailto:` (for example `mailto:hello@yourstore.com`), a phone number with `tel:`, or a text message with `sms:` (for example `sms:+15555550100`).

### Linking to your own pages

The easiest way to link to one of your pages is to open **Link to a page on your site** and pick it. Type in **Search pages…** to narrow the list. The list has up to three groups:

- **Site pages**: built-in pages such as Shop, Collections, Blog, Events, Videos, FAQ, Contact, About, Donate, and Services. Each one is listed only when its feature is on.
- **Your pages**: your own custom pages. Pages that aren't published yet show **Draft**.
- **Blog posts**: your posts, when the blog is on. Unpublished posts show **Draft**.

![The Link to a page on your site list open in the link box, with the Search pages box and the Site pages, Your pages and Blog posts groups](/images/text-editor/link-page-picker.png)

Picking a page fills in the **URL** for you and turns **Open in New Tab** off.

You can also type a path yourself. The editor tidies up what you type:

| You type | It saves |
| --- | --- |
| `contact` | `/contact` |
| `/contact` or `#faq` | Unchanged |
| Your store's full address, like `https://yourstore.com/contact` | `/contact` |
| `example.com` | `https://example.com` |
| `mailto:`, `tel:`, or `sms:` links | Unchanged |

Links saved as a path, like `/contact`, keep working if you move your store to a custom domain. The editor shortens a full address only when it matches the address you're editing on (with or without `www.`). If you're working on your SimplePress address and type a link with your custom domain, it stays a full link. That still works. It just won't be shortened.

Links to your own pages open in the same tab. Turn on **Open in New Tab** if you want one to open in a new tab instead.

### Links to other websites

When you type an address on another website, **Open in New Tab** turns on by itself, so visitors don't lose your site. Turn it off if you'd rather the link open in the same tab. Older links to other websites, saved before this switch worked for them, still open in a new tab, and their switch shows on.

Email, phone, and text links (`mailto:`, `tel:`, `sms:`) start with the switch off, because they open another app rather than a web page.

### The link toolbar

Click a link in the editor to show a small toolbar:

- **Edit link** opens the link box again
- **Open link in a new tab** lets you check where it goes
- **Clear link** turns the link back into plain text
- **Copy** copies the address (it says "Copied!")

If the link is a button, the toolbar also shows a **Button** or **Outline button** label.

## Buttons

Any link can look like a button. That's handy for a clear next step, like "Book a class" or "Shop the collection".

1. Add a link, or click an existing one and choose **Edit link**.
2. Under **Style**, pick **Button** (filled) or **Outline button** (a border with no fill). **Link** turns it back into a normal link.
3. Click **Save**.

Buttons use your template's main color, so they match the rest of your site. Some templates have their own button styles. You can't pick a different color for one button.

![A storefront page showing a filled Button and an Outline button side by side under a paragraph about beginner classes](/images/text-editor/buttons-storefront.png)

## Rows and columns

Rows let you put things side by side, such as a photo next to a paragraph, or three short blocks of text across the page. Each row has columns, and each column can hold text, pictures, lists, links, and buttons.

<video src="/videos/text-editor/build-a-row.webm" poster="/videos/text-editor/build-a-row-poster.png" controls muted playsInline className="rounded-lg border" />

*A short clip: inserting a Columns row on the "Visit our studio" page, typing in each column, adding a picture from the library and setting it to M, switching the layout to Narrow + wide, turning a link into a Button, saving, and viewing the row on the live site.*

### Adding a row

1. Click where you want the row. An empty line works best: the row replaces it.
2. Open **Insert elements** (**+**) and choose **Columns**.
3. A row with two columns appears. Click in a column and start typing.

In the editor, rows and columns have dashed outlines so you can see them. The column you're typing in is highlighted.

You can't put a row inside another row. **Columns** is greyed out in the menu while you're in a row.

### Choosing a layout

While you're in a row, the columns toolbar floats above it.

![The columns toolbar above a row, with the 2 columns, 3 columns, 4 columns, Narrow + wide and Wide + narrow layout buttons and the red Remove row button](/images/text-editor/columns-bubble.png)

| Layout | What it looks like | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **2 columns** | Two equal columns | A photo beside text, or two short blocks of text |
| **3 columns** | Three equal columns | Three features, steps, or opening times |
| **4 columns** | Four equal columns | Small photos or short facts, like a row of icons |
| **Narrow + wide** | A narrow column on the left (one third) and a wide one on the right (two thirds) | A small photo or heading next to a longer paragraph |
| **Wide + narrow** | A wide column on the left and a narrow one on the right | A longer paragraph with a photo, price, or button beside it |

You can change the layout at any time. Nothing gets lost:

- **Fewer columns**: whatever was in the columns you dropped moves into the last remaining column.
- **More columns**: empty columns are added at the end.

<Callout type="warn" title="Remove row deletes everything in it">
  The red **Remove row** button takes out the whole row **and everything inside it**, including text and pictures. To keep the content, move it out of the row first. If you remove a row by mistake, press ⌘Z (Ctrl+Z on Windows) right away to undo.
</Callout>

### Pictures in a column

A picture in a column sizes against its column, not the whole page. **Full** fills the column, and **M** is half the column. So a **Full** picture in a narrow column is still small on the page.

### How rows look on phones

Rows switch to one column, stacked top to bottom, when there isn't room for them side by side. This depends on how wide the row itself is, not the screen. Roughly:

- **2 columns**, **Narrow + wide**, and **Wide + narrow** sit side by side once the row is about 512 pixels wide.
- **3 columns** sit side by side from about 576 pixels.
- **4 columns** go all the way across from about 768 pixels, show as two by two from about 448 pixels, and stack below that.

On a phone, nearly every row stacks. A row can also stack on a computer if your template puts it in a narrow space, such as a side column.

![A two-column row on a computer screen, with a photo of a felted shawl on the left and a heading, a paragraph and two buttons on the right, above a three-column row](/images/text-editor/row-desktop.png)

![The same row on a phone, with the photo stacked above the heading, the paragraph and the buttons](/images/text-editor/row-phone.png)

### Rows on wide pages

Rows have more room on a page set to **Wide**. Set this with **Page width** on the page's Content tab (see [Pages & Policies](/docs/pages-and-policies#content-tab)). On a Wide page, normal text keeps a comfortable reading width by default. You can change that for your whole site with **Text width on wide pages** in the Site Editor (see [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor#text-width-on-wide-pages)).

Good to know:

- **Page width** is only on your own custom pages, not blog posts or policies.
- **Page width** and **Text width** have no effect on the **Default**, **Modern**, **Sledge**, and **Builders** templates. Those always use the full width.
- Text inside a row is never narrowed by **Text width**. It always fills its column.

## Collapsible sections

A collapsible section is a title that visitors click to show more. It keeps long pages tidy and is good for details, care instructions, or questions and answers.

### Adding a collapsible section

1. Click in the paragraph you want to tuck away, or select several.
2. Open **Insert elements** (**+**) and choose **Collapsible section**.
3. Your text becomes the hidden part, and your cursor moves to an empty title line. Type a title, such as "Do I need any experience?"

Always give it a title. An empty title shows as a blank bar on your site.

In the editor, the section is a box with a small arrow (a chevron) next to the title. Click the arrow to open or close the hidden part so you can edit it.

![A collapsible section in the editor, open, with the chevron and the title Do I need any experience? on the first line and the answer below it, and the next collapsible section closed underneath](/images/text-editor/collapsible-editor.png)

### Typing in the title

- **Enter** in the title moves you into the hidden part if the section is open. If it's closed, Enter adds a normal paragraph below the section.
- **Backspace** at the start of the title undoes the section. The title becomes a normal paragraph again.

### On your site

Collapsible sections always load **closed**. Visitors click the bold title to open one, and the arrow turns. They can open several at once. They work even if a visitor's browser has JavaScript turned off.

Put several collapsible sections one after another and they share dividers, so they look like one neat list (sometimes called an accordion).

![Three collapsible sections on a storefront page, one after another with dividers between them, the second one open to show its answer](/images/text-editor/collapsibles-storefront.png)

<Callout type="info" title="Looking for an FAQ page?">
  SimplePress also has a separate FAQ feature with its own page and questions you manage in one place. See [FAQ](/docs/faq). Collapsible sections are handy for a few questions on a single page.
</Callout>

## Tables

To add a table, open **Insert elements** (**+**) and choose **Table**. You get three rows and three columns, with a header row at the top.

Click in the table to show its toolbar:

- **Add row above** and **Add row below**
- **Delete row** (the **Row** button)
- **Add column before** and **Add column after**
- **Delete column** (the **Col** button)
- **Delete table** (the **Table** button)

Tables suit things like price lists or class schedules. Keep them to a few columns so they still read well on a phone.

## Insert elements

The **Insert elements** menu is the **+** button at the end of the toolbar.

![The Insert elements menu open, listing Code block, Blockquote, Divider, Gallery, Embed, Quote calculator, Form, Table, Columns, Collapsible section and Space](/images/text-editor/insert-elements-menu.png)

These are always there:

- **Code block**: a box for code, shown in a fixed-width font
- **Blockquote**: sets a quote or testimonial apart from the text
- **Divider**: a line across the page between sections
- **Table**: see [Tables](#tables)

These appear only in editors that offer them (see [Where you'll find it](#where-youll-find-it)):

- **Columns**: see [Rows and columns](#rows-and-columns)
- **Collapsible section**: see [Collapsible sections](#collapsible-sections)
- **Space**: an empty gap you can set to Small, Medium, or Large; see [Adding space](#adding-space)

These appear only when the matching feature is switched on in **Settings → Features** (see [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags)). When a feature is on, it also gets its own button in the toolbar:

| Menu item | Toolbar button | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gallery** | **Insert gallery** | [Galleries](/docs/galleries) |
| **Embed** | **Insert embed** | [Embeds](/docs/embeds) |
| **Quote calculator** | **Insert quote calculator** | [Quote Calculators](/docs/quote-calculators) |
| **Form** | **Insert form** | [Forms](/docs/forms) |

If you don't see one of these, its feature is off, or you're in an editor that doesn't offer it (such as Policies, the banner and popup, or the maintenance message).

If you insert one of these but never finish choosing what it shows (for example, an Embed box you leave empty or a Form box with no form picked), it's left out when you save, so no empty box appears on your site.

## What You Can and Can't Do

**You can:**

- Use headings, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, colors, lists, and alignment
- Add pictures from your device, your Media Library, or a web link, and set them to S, M, L, or Full
- Add alt text to every picture
- Link to your own pages, blog posts, other websites, email addresses, phone numbers, and text messages
- Choose whether each link opens in the same tab or a new one
- Turn any link into a **Button** or an **Outline button**
- Lay out content in rows of 2, 3, or 4 columns, or narrow and wide pairs, and change the layout without losing anything
- Add collapsible sections, tables, quotes, dividers, and code blocks
- Add galleries, embeds, quote calculators, and forms when those features are on
- Undo and redo with ⌘Z and ⌘⇧Z

**You cannot:**

- Put a row inside another row
- Set a collapsible section to start open on your site
- Pick an exact pixel width for a picture. Use S, M, L, Full, or the drag handles.
- Move a picture with the **Text alignment** menu. Use the picture's own toolbar.
- Change a button's color or pick colors per button. Buttons follow your template.
- Add rows or collapsible sections to policies, the banner, the popup, or the maintenance message
- Use **Page width** on blog posts or policies. It's only on your own custom pages.
- Use this editor for a product's **Description**, which is plain text

Need something the editor doesn't do? Email [help@generativejustice.org](mailto:help@generativejustice.org).

## FAQ

**My columns are stacked on my phone. Is something wrong?**
No. Rows stack into one column when there isn't room for them side by side, which is almost always the case on a phone. It keeps the text readable. See [How rows look on phones](#how-rows-look-on-phones).

**My columns are stacked on my computer too.**
The row is probably in a narrow space, such as a side column in your template. Try a layout with fewer columns, or set the page's **Page width** to **Wide** (if your template uses it).

**Why doesn't Page width change anything on my site?**
**Page width** and **Text width** have no effect on the **Default**, **Modern**, **Sledge**, and **Builders** templates. Those always use the full width. On other templates, check you saved the page, and for **Text width**, that you published your Site Editor changes.

**My link opens in a new tab. How do I stop that?**
Click the link, choose **Edit link**, turn off **Open in New Tab**, and click **Save**. This works for links to your own pages and to other websites.

**Where did my pictures' old sizes go?**
They're still there. Pictures added before the S, M, L, and Full sizes keep their exact width until you click a size or drag a handle. After that they use the new sizes, which also fit phones better.

**Can I undo a mistake?**
Yes. Press ⌘Z (Ctrl+Z on Windows) to undo, and ⌘⇧Z to redo. This works while you're still in the editor, before you leave the page. In the page editor, **More Options → Reset** also throws away everything since your last save.

**I removed a row and lost what was in it.**
**Remove row** deletes everything in the row. Press ⌘Z right away to bring it back. If you've already saved, re-add the content by hand.

**Why can't I find Gallery or Form in the Insert elements menu?**
Their features are off, or you're in an editor that doesn't offer them. Turn them on in **Settings → Features** (see [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags)).
