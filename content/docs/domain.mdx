## Overview

Every store starts on a free SimplePress address, something like `yourstore.simplepress.co`. This is your **default subdomain**. It's set when your store is created and **can't be changed**.

The subdomain is perfect while you're building and testing. But **you need your own custom domain** (like `myshop.com`) before you start selling. It's the address customers remember, and the one your receipts, emails and search listings should use.

To get there, open **Settings → Domain**. Owners and Managers can open it. Staff can't. See [Team & roles](/docs/team-and-roles).

<Callout type="info" title="Where does the old address go?">
  Once your custom domain is **Active**, anyone who visits your store at the old SimplePress address is sent on to your custom domain automatically. Your admin still works at the old address too. See [Launch Checklist](/docs/launch-checklist#1-connect-your-custom-domain).
</Callout>

The page has two cards: **Default Subdomain** and **Custom Domain**. There's no Save button. Each action happens when you click its own button.

---

## Add your domain

Buy your domain from any registrar you like (Namecheap, GoDaddy, Cloudflare, Squarespace Domains and so on). SimplePress doesn't sell them.

![The Default Subdomain card with an Active badge, and the empty Custom Domain form](/images/domain/domain-empty.png)

1. Go to **Settings → Domain**.
2. In **Domain Name**, type your domain without `http://` or `www`. For example, `myshop.com`.
3. Click **Add Custom Domain**.

SimplePress changes everything you type to lowercase. It tells you if something's wrong:

![The error Enter a valid domain like shop.example.com under the Domain Name box](/images/domain/domain-field-error.png)

| Message | What it means |
| ------- | ------------- |
| Please enter a domain | The box is empty. |
| Enter a valid domain like shop.example.com | What you typed isn't a domain. Check for spaces or typos. |
| That domain is reserved by the platform and cannot be used as a custom domain | Addresses that belong to SimplePress itself (such as `simplepress.co` ones) can't be used. |
| This domain is already in use | Another store already has it. [Contact support](mailto:help@generativejustice.org) if you think it should be yours. |

When it works, the message says "Domain added! Configure DNS and verify below." Your domain is saved with a **Pending DNS** badge. The SimplePress team is notified and finishes the server setup on our side. You don't need to do anything for that.

<Callout type="warn" title="Using an address like shop.example.com?">
  If you want your store on a subdomain of a domain you own (for example `shop.example.com`), it will be accepted, but the DNS records shown on this page are written for a main domain like `example.com` and won't be right for it. Please email help@generativejustice.org and we'll give you the correct records.
</Callout>

## Add the DNS records

After you add your domain, the card shows **DNS Configuration Required** with two **A records**: one with the name `@` and one with the name `www`.

![The Pending DNS state with the DNS records to add and the Verify DNS and Remove Domain buttons, shown with an example domain](/images/domain/domain-pending.png)

*The Pending DNS state, shown with an example domain. Yours will show your own domain and address.*

| Type | Name | Value |
| ---- | ---- | ----- |
| A | `@` | The address shown in your admin |
| A | `www` | The same address |

Sign in to the company where you bought the domain and add both records. Copy each value carefully. The records on **Settings → Domain** are plain text, so select and copy them yourself. (The Setup Guide's domain step has copy buttons if you'd rather click.)

![The Setup Guide's domain step, which has a copy button beside each DNS value](/images/domain/setup-guide-domain.png)

*On the Setup Guide, each DNS value has a copy button. This shows an example domain.*

**DNS changes can take anywhere from a few minutes to about 48 hours to spread.** That depends on your domain company, not on SimplePress.

## Verify your domain

When you've added the records, come back to **Settings → Domain** and click **Verify DNS**.

If it works, you'll see "Domain verified successfully!" and the badge changes to **Active**.

![The Active state: Your custom domain is active and working, with a Remove Domain button, shown with an example domain](/images/domain/domain-active.png)

*An Active domain, shown with an example domain.*

If it doesn't work, you'll see one of these:

| Message | What to check |
| ------- | ------------- |
| DNS records not found. Please check your configuration. | The records aren't visible yet. Check they're saved at your registrar, then wait a while and try again. |
| Domain points to [addresses] but should point to [our address] | Your `@` record points somewhere else. Change it to the address shown in your admin. |

Some things to know about **Verify DNS**:

- **It only checks the main (`@`) record.** It doesn't check your `www` record. Add both anyway.
- **Active can show up a little before your site loads.** Verification only looks at DNS. After that the secure certificate (the padlock) still has to be set up, which takes a short while. If your domain shows an error when you visit it just after going Active, wait a bit and try again. If it's still not working after a few hours, email help@generativejustice.org.
- **Always visit your own domain** in a private or incognito window before you share it.

Adding your domain is also what ticks the domain step on your Setup Guide. That tick doesn't mean the domain works yet. Look for **Active** here.

## The badges

| Badge | Where | What it means |
| ----- | ----- | ------------- |
| **Active** (on Default Subdomain) | Default Subdomain card | Your free address is working. It's always there. |
| **Pending DNS** | Custom Domain card | Your domain is saved. It's waiting for DNS records and **Verify DNS**. |
| **Active** | Custom Domain card | Your domain is verified. |
| **Not Configured** | Custom Domain card | The domain exists but isn't set up. You normally won't see this. |

If you haven't added a custom domain, the Custom Domain card has no badge at all. It's just the empty form.

When your domain is **Active**, your store's emails, links and **View store** buttons use it.

## Remove a custom domain

You can remove your domain at any time, whether it's still pending or already active.

![The Remove Custom Domain dialog, shown with an example domain](/images/domain/domain-remove-dialog.png)

1. On the Custom Domain card, click **Remove Domain**.
2. Read the dialog. It says your domain "will stop working right away, and your store will be served from its default subdomain again."
3. Click **Remove Domain** to confirm, or **Cancel** to stay as you are.

You'll see "Custom domain removed. Your store is back on its subdomain." You can add a new domain at any time. To switch to a different one, remove the old one first, then add the new one.

<Callout type="warn" title="Don't remove it while you're selling">
  Removing your domain makes links, emails and bookmarks that use it stop working. Do this only when you mean to change addresses.
</Callout>

## What you can and can't do

| You can | You can't |
| ------- | --------- |
| Add one custom domain and verify it yourself. | Change your default subdomain. |
| Remove your domain and add a different one later. | Add more than one custom domain. |
| Use your subdomain while you build. | Use a domain that belongs to SimplePress, or one another store already uses. |
| Keep admin access at the old subdomain. | Skip the DNS step: the domain has to point to the right address. |

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to buy my domain through SimplePress?**
No. Buy it from any registrar. SimplePress only needs you to add the DNS records.

**Do I really need a custom domain?**
You can build and test on your free subdomain. To start selling, you need your own domain connected. See [Launch Checklist](/docs/launch-checklist).

**Will my old SimplePress address stop working?**
Once your domain is Active, visitors to your old address are sent to your new one. Your admin dashboard keeps working at the old address.

**How long does it take?**
The SimplePress team finishes the server side. The DNS spread time depends on your registrar. Most stores are live within a few hours to a day.

**Can I reuse a domain I removed?**
Yes. It goes through Pending DNS and Active again.
