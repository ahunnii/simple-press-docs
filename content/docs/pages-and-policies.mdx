## Overview

**Pages** are standalone content pages for your storefront, such as "Our Story", "Wholesale", or "Care Instructions". **Policies** are your four standard legal documents: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Returns & Refunds, and Shipping Policy.

You find them in two different places in the admin sidebar:

- **Pages**: **Content → Pages**
- **Policies**: **Site Setup → Policies**

Owners and Managers can use both. Staff can't open either one.

<Callout type="info" title="Pick a slug that isn't taken">
  The addresses of SimplePress's built-in pages, such as `/about`, `/contact`, `/faq`, `/blog`, `/shop`, `/services`, `/events`, `/cart`, `/collections`, `/donate`, and a few more, are reserved. So are the four policy addresses. The page editor won't let a custom page use one of them, so pick a different slug such as `our-story` or `meet-the-team`. For a Contact page or an FAQ page, use the built-in ones (see [Contact Form](/docs/contact-form)); you manage FAQ questions under **Site Setup → FAQ** (see [FAQ](/docs/faq)).
</Callout>

## Custom Pages

### The Pages List

Go to **Content → Pages**. Each row shows:

- **Page**: the cover image thumbnail, the title, a short excerpt, and the `/slug`
- **Updated**: when the page last changed
- **Status**: **Published** or **Draft**

![The Pages list with search, Filters, a row selected and the bulk action bar showing](/images/pages-and-policies/pages-list.png)

#### Finding and sorting

- **Search** matches the title, the slug, and the excerpt. Click **Filters** to open the **Status** and **Sort** options.
- **Status** filters between **All pages**, **Published**, and **Drafts**.
- **Sort** offers **Recently updated** (the default), **Least recently updated**, **Title A–Z**, and **Title Z–A**.

The list shows 25 pages at a time.

#### Row actions

Click a page's title to open it, or use the three-dot menu at the end of the row for **Edit**, **View on storefront** (published pages only, opens in a new tab), and **Delete**.

#### Doing several at once

Tick the checkboxes on the left. A bar appears with:

- **Publish** and **Unpublish**. A message appears afterwards with an **Undo** button.
- **Delete**: only the store **Owner** sees this button. Managers don't.

Deleting asks you to confirm ("Delete “Title”?") and is permanent.

### Creating a Page

Click **Create Page**. The editor has a toolbar across the top and two tabs: **Content** and **SEO**.

The toolbar has:

- A status label: **Unsaved Changes** or **Saved**
- The **Published** switch. **New pages start Published**, so switch it off before you save if you want to keep working privately.
- A **More Options** menu (see [More Options](#more-options))
- **Save & create another** (new pages only): saves this page and opens a blank editor so you can add the next one
- **Save changes**

#### Content Tab

The **Page Content** card has:

- **What is the page title?** _(required)_: the page's heading on your storefront.
- **Provide a short description of the page**: the excerpt. Shown under the title on the Default template, and used as the fallback meta description if you don't write one on the SEO tab.
- **Cover image**: "Shown as the page hero on supported templates". The Default template doesn't show it as a hero, but it's still used as the picture that appears when the page is shared on social media. Choose **Upload from device**, or **Choose from library** if the [Media Library](/docs/media-library) is turned on.
- **Write out your page content**: the full page body in the rich text editor (see [Rich-Text Tools](#rich-text-tools)).

Under the title you'll see the page's **Storefront URL**, like `/our-story`, with an **Edit in SEO** link. The URL itself isn't on this tab.

![The page editor Content tab with title, Storefront URL line, short description, cover image and rich text editor](/images/pages-and-policies/page-content-tab.png)

#### SEO Tab

The SEO tab has four cards:

- **Meta Tags**:
  - **URL Slug** _(required)_: the page's address, such as `our-story` for `yourstore.com/our-story`.
  - **Meta Title**: shown in browser tabs and search results. Your brand name is added after it (" | brand"), and the character counter includes that. Falls back to the page title if blank.
  - **Meta Description**: the snippet shown in search results. Falls back to the short description if blank.
- **Open Graph Image**: the picture used when the page is shared on social media.
- **Search Result Preview**: what the page may look like on Google.
- **Social Media Preview**: what it may look like when shared.

![The page editor SEO tab with Meta Tags, Open Graph Image and the two preview cards](/images/pages-and-policies/page-seo-tab.png)

#### How the URL slug works

- **Before the page has been published**, the slug follows the title as you type ("Our Story" becomes `our-story`).
- **After that**, changing the title doesn't change the URL. The Content tab shows "Title changed — the URL hasn't." with an **Update URL** button. Click it only if you really want the address to match the new title.
- If you change the slug of a saved page, the SEO tab shows "Heads up — this will change the page's URL."
- **Reserved addresses are blocked.** If the slug matches a built-in page or a policy address, an error appears right under the Storefront URL line and on the SEO tab, such as "/about" is already used by your site's built-in About page — choose a different URL. Saving is blocked until you pick another slug, such as `our-story` or `meet-the-team`. Pages that already use one of these slugs from before can still be edited; only changing the slug is checked.

<Callout type="warn" title="Changing a URL breaks old links">
  SimplePress does **not** create a redirect. Anyone with the old link, including bookmarks and search results, will land on a "not found" page. Navigation menu items store the address, not the page, so they keep pointing at the old URL. Update them under **Site Setup → Navigation**.
</Callout>

Slugs must be **unique across your pages, blog posts, and policies**. If you reuse one, you'll see "A page with this slug already exists". Built-in page addresses and policy addresses are blocked as well (see [How the URL slug works](#how-the-url-slug-works)). Keep slugs short, lowercase, and hyphen-separated.

### The Published Switch

- **Off (Draft)**: customers can't see the page, even with the direct link. Owners and Managers can still preview it.
- **On (Published)**: the page is live at `yourstore.com/[slug]`.

You can flip it at any time without losing content. There's no scheduling for pages; the switch takes effect when you save. (Blog posts can be scheduled. See [Blog](/docs/blog).)

---

### More Options

The **More Options** menu in the editor toolbar has:

- **View on storefront**: opens the live page in a new tab (published pages only)
- **Reset**: throws away your unsaved changes
- **Delete**: permanently removes the page after you confirm. Only on pages that are already saved.

![The More Options menu open in the page editor, showing View on storefront, Reset and Delete](/images/pages-and-policies/page-more-options.png)

### Rich-Text Tools

The content editor (used for pages, blog posts, and policies) includes:

- Headings, bold, italic, underline, and strikethrough
- Text color, links, and lists
- Block quotes, code, code blocks, and dividers
- Tables
- Images and video
- An **Insert elements** menu for adding richer blocks

Some **Insert elements** options only appear when that feature is switched on in **Settings → Features** (see [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags)):

- **Gallery**: drop in a photo gallery. See [Galleries](/docs/galleries).
- **Embed**: show an outside widget, such as a booking page or a map, on the page. See [Embeds](/docs/embeds).
- **Quote calculator**: add a quote calculator.
- **Form**: add one of your forms. See [Forms](/docs/forms).

If you don't see one of these in the menu, its feature is off. The policy editor doesn't have these inserts.

---

### Editing Pages in the Site Editor

You can also edit a page right on your storefront preview in the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor). Open the page there and you can change its **Title**, **Excerpt**, and **Content** as drafts, and use **Open page settings** to jump back to the full editor for the URL, SEO, and cover image.

---

### How Pages Appear on the Storefront

Custom pages live at `yourstore.com/[slug]` and use your template's page layout, with your header, footer, and branding. On the **Default** template that layout shows:

- The title, with the excerpt underneath
- "Last updated" and the date
- An automatic **On this page** contents list, when your page has H2 or H3 headings

Other templates lay pages out their own way. See [Templates](/docs/templates).

Pages are **not added to your navigation automatically**. To link one from your header or footer, go to **Site Setup → Navigation** and add it as a menu item.

## Policies

### Getting There

Go to **Site Setup → Policies**. The toolbar title is **Update Policies**, with **Reset** and **Save content** buttons. Four tabs sit below it, one per policy.

### The Four Policies

| Tab      | Card title        | Storefront URL                   |
| -------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Privacy  | Privacy Policy    | `yourstore.com/privacy-policy`   |
| Terms    | Terms of Service  | `yourstore.com/terms-of-service` |
| Refunds  | Returns & Refunds | `yourstore.com/refund-policy`    |
| Shipping | Shipping Policy   | `yourstore.com/shipping-policy`  |

Each policy has a fixed URL that you can't change, and no page or post can use these slugs.

![The Update Policies page on the Refunds tab with the Live on your site status, the Use Template and Remove policy buttons, the Show on your site switch, and the policy content editor](/images/pages-and-policies/policies-refunds.png)

### Editing and Saving

Click a tab, then write in the **Policy Content** editor. When you're done with any or all of the tabs, click **Save content** to save them together. **Reset** discards unsaved changes. The **Unsaved Changes** badge only appears when you've actually changed something, and **Reset** clears it.

If you haven't published Terms of Service or a Refund Policy yet, a banner reads **No published Terms of Service or Refund Policy**. Checkout tells buyers they're agreeing to your Terms, so publish at least the Terms and Refunds tabs. The same nudge appears on your dashboard. Hiding the Terms or Refunds policy brings that banner back.

### Using a Template

Each tab has a **Use Template** button. It loads a starter written from your current store settings: business name and contact details, where you ship and how you charge for it, pickup (if enabled), and which features are on. It's a snapshot. If you change shipping or features later, the saved policy doesn't update until you load the template again.

- If the tab already has text, a **Replace [policy name]?** box asks first. Choose **Keep current** or **Use Template**.
- After a template loads, a **Review before publishing** note appears. Anything shown like `[X] days` or `[your jurisdiction]` still needs your input, and the template is not legal advice. Read it through and edit it for your business.

![A policy tab after Use Template, with the Review before publishing note and placeholders shown in code style](/images/pages-and-policies/policy-use-template.png)

### Hiding or Removing a Policy

Each tab shows a status line under the policy name:

- **Live on your site.**
- **Hidden from your site**: its page, footer link, and checkout link are turned off.
- **Not created yet**: nothing shows on your site until you save it.

A policy you've never saved doesn't exist on your storefront (customers get a "not found" page).

**To hide a policy without losing its text**, turn off the **Show on your site** switch and click **Save content**. Its page stops working, and its footer, product-page, and checkout links disappear. To bring it back, turn the switch on and save.

**To remove a policy completely**, click **Remove policy** (it appears on a saved policy). SimplePress asks "Remove [policy name]?" and, once you confirm, deletes the policy and takes it off your site right away. You don't need to click Save.

If you clear all the text from a saved policy and click **Save content**, a **Remove a cleared policy?** box asks first. **Keep editing** saves nothing. **Remove and save** removes the cleared policy and saves your other tabs.

Empty tabs that were never saved are still skipped when you save.

### Where Customers See Your Policies

Links only appear for policies that are live: saved, shown on your site, and not blank. On the Default template:

- The footer's Help column links to your **Shipping** and **Returns** policies, but only the ones that are live.
- The footer's Privacy and Terms links point to the platform's own pages when your store doesn't have a live policy of its own.
- Product pages link to your shipping and refund policies when they're live.

Policy pages also show a small "Policy" label above the title.

### The Platform Notice

Your **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service** pages automatically end with a notice headed **Powered by SimplePress**, with links to the SimplePress Terms and Privacy Policy. It says your store is hosted on the SimplePress platform, so customers are covered by both your policies and the platform's.

**It can't be removed.** It doesn't appear on your refund or shipping pages.

![The bottom of a storefront Privacy Policy page with the Powered by SimplePress notice](/images/pages-and-policies/platform-notice.png)

## What You Can and Can't Do

**You can:**

- Create, edit, publish, unpublish, and delete custom pages any time
- Change a page's URL slug (just remember there's no redirect)
- Set SEO details per page
- Edit page text in the Site Editor
- Start all four policies from a template
- Hide or remove a policy

**You can't:**

- Use a slug that another page, blog post, or policy already has
- Give a custom page a built-in page's URL (`about`, `contact`, `faq`, and so on). The editor won't let you.
- Schedule a page for later
- Change a policy's URL or remove the platform notice (email [help@generativejustice.org](mailto:help@generativejustice.org))
- Add pages to your navigation automatically

---

## Feature Flag

Pages is **on by default** and can't be switched off. If you can't open **Content → Pages**, check your role under [Team & Roles](/docs/team-and-roles) or contact [help@generativejustice.org](mailto:help@generativejustice.org).
