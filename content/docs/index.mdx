Welcome to **SimplePress (SP)**! We at the **Center for Generative Justice / Artisanal Futures** are so happy you opted into using the platform. In case you didn’t know, SimplePress is our stripped down version of Shopify. We wanted to provide small business owners who don’t need all the bells and whistles to get set up online fast.

<Callout type="info" title="Super Important">

Please know that this free to use platform is still in development. We at the Center for Generative Justice / Artisanal Futures operate with a very small development team (pretty much just me :D). By using SimplePress, you acknowledge our terms and conditions ([read the plain-language summary](/docs/your-agreement)). Your feedback shapes the platform and can benefit other business owners using SP. We will do our absolute best to resolve any issues in a timely manner. Any feature requests, cosmetic adjustments, and low risk issues will be triaged based on our current work load with all of our clients and ongoing projects. Please contact us at [help@generativejustice.org](mailto:help@generativejustice.org) for any questions. I do my best to respond within 24 hours, but if I don’t, please send a follow up email. Thanks for your understanding and for your assistance in making SimplePress the greatest resource for small business owners!

</Callout>

## Admin vs storefront

- **Admin** (`/admin`): where you configure products, orders, customers, discounts, content, and store settings.
- **Storefront**: the public site shoppers visit—the template, branding, and content you configure in admin.

See [Finding Your Way Around the Admin](/docs/admin-basics) for a tour of the sidebar and shortcuts.

## How to use these docs

Topics follow a practical order: onboarding and the **Dashboard**, then **Storefront** and commerce features, then **Settings**, and finally **Your account & security**. Use the sidebar to move sequentially or jump to any page. Use search when you know the task (for example “tax” or “domain”) but not which section it lives in.
