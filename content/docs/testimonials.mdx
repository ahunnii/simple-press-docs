## Overview

Testimonials are short customer quotes about your business as a whole, such as "Best bakery in town" or "They fixed our sink in an hour." You collect them in two ways: you add one yourself (from an email, a text, or something said in person), or you invite a customer to write one on your site.

Testimonials are different from [Product Reviews](/docs/reviews). A testimonial is about your business. A product review is about one product on its product page. They have separate pages in your admin and separate switches under **Settings → Features**.

Customer-submitted testimonials wait for your approval by default, so nothing shows up on your site until you say so. You can turn on **Auto-approve** if you would rather they go live right away.

<Callout type="info" title="Testimonials is on by default">
  You can turn it off under **Settings → Features** (see [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags)). When it is off, **Testimonials** disappears from your admin sidebar and the `/testimonials` page on your site shows "not found". The testimonials you already collected are kept for when you turn it back on.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Who can use it">
  Owners and Managers can add, approve, hide, and delete testimonials and send invites. Staff can't open Testimonials (see [Team and Roles](/docs/team-and-roles)). Only the store **Owner** can delete several testimonials at once.
</Callout>

## Where to Find It

In your admin sidebar, go to **Marketing → Testimonials**. The page has three parts:

1. A card at the top with the **Auto-approve testimonials** switch.
2. Two buttons at the top right: **Add Testimonial** and **Send Invite**.
3. Two tabs: **Testimonials** and **Invites**. Each tab shows how many items it holds. If any testimonials are waiting for approval, a red number appears next to **Testimonials**.

## Approving Testimonials

### Auto-Approve

The **Auto-approve testimonials** card has one switch. Its description reads: "When on, customer-submitted testimonials are published immediately. When off (default), they land in a pending state below until approved."

- **Off (the default).** Every testimonial a customer submits is marked **Pending**. It stays hidden from your site until you approve it.
- **On.** Customer testimonials appear on your site the moment they are submitted.

The switch saves as soon as you click it, and a message confirms: "Testimonials will now be published immediately" or "Testimonials will now require approval before publishing."

A few things to know:

- The switch only affects **new** submissions. Testimonials that are already pending stay pending until you approve them.
- It does not apply to testimonials you add yourself. Those have their own **Approve Immediately** switch (see [Adding a Testimonial Yourself](#adding-a-testimonial-yourself)).
- You are not emailed when a customer submits a testimonial. Check the red number next to the **Testimonials** tab, or filter by **Pending**, to see what is waiting. See [Tips](#tips).

### Reviewing What Is Waiting

1. Open **Marketing → Testimonials**.
2. Set the **Status** filter to **Pending** (see below).
3. Read the quote. If you like it, open the **⋮** menu on its row and choose **Approve**. It becomes **Published** and appears on your site.
4. If you would rather not show it, choose **Hide** or **Delete** from the same menu.

![The Testimonials list filtered to Pending, with a row menu open on Approve, Hide, and Delete](/images/testimonials/testimonials-pending-filter.png)

## The Testimonials Tab

### Filters

Above the table you can search and filter. Search looks at the customer's name, email, quote, headline, job title, and company.

| Filter | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | **All testimonials**, **Pending**, **Published**, **Hidden** |
| **Source** | **All sources**, **Customer submitted**, **Owner added** |
| **Sort** | **Newest first**, **Oldest first**, **Name A–Z**, **Name Z–A** |

The table shows 25 testimonials per page. If a filter matches nothing, the page says "No testimonials match your filters" with a **Clear filters** button. If you have no testimonials at all, you see "No testimonials yet" with an **Add Testimonial** button.

### What the Table Shows

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The customer's name, their email (if you have it), and a line like "Owner at Acme" if you entered a job title or company |
| **Quote** | The headline (if any) in bold, then the first lines of the quote |
| **Source** | **Customer** (they wrote it) or **Owner added** (you entered it) |
| **Status** | **Pending** (gray), **Published** (green), or **Hidden** (red) |
| **Date** | The date of the testimonial |

On a phone, the source, status, and date appear in a line under the name instead of in their own columns.

### The Three Statuses

| Status | Meaning | On your site? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pending** | A customer submitted it and you have not approved it yet | No |
| **Published** | Approved and visible | Yes |
| **Hidden** | You pulled it from your site (it stays in your admin) | No |

**Hidden** wins over the others. A testimonial that is hidden never shows on your site, no matter whether it was approved.

### The ⋮ Menu on Each Row

| Action | When it appears | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Owner-added testimonials only | Opens the form so you can change the text, name, date, photos, and details |
| **Manage images** | Testimonials that have photos | Lets you remove individual photos (see [Managing Images](#managing-images)) |
| **Approve** | Pending testimonials | Publishes it |
| **Unapprove** | Published testimonials | Takes it off your site and puts it back to Pending |
| **Hide** / **Unhide** | Always | Hides it from your site, or shows it again |
| **Delete** | Always | Permanently deletes it after you confirm. This cannot be undone |

A hidden testimonial does not offer **Approve** or **Unapprove**. Choose **Unhide** first, and it goes back to whatever it was before (Pending or Published).

Customer-submitted testimonials can't be edited. Their words are the customer's own, so the **Edit** option only appears on testimonials you added.

### Bulk Actions

Tick the boxes at the left of several rows (or the box in the header to select the whole page) and a bar appears with these actions:

- **Approve**
- **Unapprove**
- **Hide**
- **Unhide**
- **Delete** (Owner only; Managers do not see this button)

After **Approve**, **Unapprove**, **Hide**, or **Unhide**, you get an **Undo** link for a few seconds. You can change up to 100 testimonials at once. Bulk delete is limited to 25 at a time and asks you to confirm first. Photos stay in your Media Library when a testimonial is deleted (see [Photos and the Media Library](#photos-and-the-media-library)).

## Adding a Testimonial Yourself

Use this when a customer told you something kind by email, text, or in person, or when you are moving quotes over from another site.

1. Click **Add Testimonial** at the top right.
2. Fill in **Customer Name** and **Testimonial Text**. These two are required.
3. Optionally change **Testimonial Date** ("Backdate if importing"). It starts as today.
4. Optionally upload up to 5 photos under **Photos (Optional, max 5)**.
5. Optionally open **Additional details** and fill in any of: **Email (Optional)**, **Customer Title (Optional)**, **Customer Company (Optional)**, and **Headline / Title (Optional)**.
6. Leave **Approve Immediately** on to publish it right away, or switch it off to save it as **Pending** for later.
7. Click **Add Testimonial**. You see "Testimonial added."

![The Add Testimonial dialog with a name, text, and the Additional details section filled in](/images/testimonials/testimonials-add-dialog.png)

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Customer Name** | Yes | Shown publicly. Up to 200 characters |
| **Testimonial Date** | No | Sets the date shown and the order on your site |
| **Testimonial Text** | Yes | The quote. Up to 5,000 characters |
| **Photos** | No | Up to 5 images |
| **Email** | No | For your records. Never shown on your storefront |
| **Customer Title** | No | A short line shown under the name, such as "Owner, Maple Street Bakery" |
| **Company** | No | For your records. Search finds it, and the admin table shows it |
| **Headline / Title** | No | A short bold line over the quote in your admin |
| **Approve Immediately** | — | On by default |

You can edit a testimonial you added at any time from its **⋮** menu. Customer-submitted testimonials can't be edited.

## Managing Images

When a testimonial has photos, its **⋮** menu has **Manage images**. This opens a window called **Manage testimonial images** with a small preview of each photo, labelled **Image 1**, **Image 2**, and so on.

1. Click the trash button next to any photo you no longer want.
2. Click **Save**. You see "Images updated", and the change shows on your site.

If you remove every photo, the window tells you there are none left. You can remove photos here, but you can't add new ones. To add a photo to a testimonial you wrote yourself, use **Edit** instead.

## Inviting a Customer to Write One

An invite sends a customer a personal link by email. They click it, write their testimonial, and it lands in your list. They do not need an account.

### Sending an Invite

1. Click **Send Invite** at the top right of the Testimonials page.
2. Optionally pick a customer from **Select Customer (Optional)**. This fills in their email. The list shows only your first page of customers, so for anyone else, type the email in yourself.
3. Check or type the **Email Address**. It is required.
4. Choose **Max photos (0–5)**: how many photos this customer can add. It starts at 3. Pick 0 if you do not want photos.
5. Click **Send Invite**. You see "Testimonial invite sent!"

![The Send Testimonial Invite dialog with a customer selected and Max photos set to 3](/images/testimonials/testimonials-send-invite-dialog.png)

If the email is not in your customer list, the customer is added to your [Customers](/docs/customers) list when they submit their testimonial.

You can have only one active invite per email address at a time. If you try again before the first one is used or expired, you see "An active invitation already exists for this email." Use **Resend** instead (see below).

### What the Customer Gets

The customer receives an email from your business name (replies go to your owner email) with the subject **Share your experience with [your store name]**. It says "We'd love your feedback!", has a **Share Your Experience** button, and notes that "This link is valid for 30 days and can only be used once."

The button opens a form on your site where they enter:

- **Your Name**
- Their testimonial (10 to 1,000 characters)
- Photos, up to the number you allowed. The form says "This invite allows up to N photo(s)" if they add too many.
- A quick automatic check that they are a real person (no puzzle to solve)

After they send it, they see **Thank You!**. If **Auto-approve** is on, the message says it is "now live on the site". If it is off, it says it "will appear once it's approved."

If the link has already been used or has expired, the customer sees **Invalid Invite** and "This testimonial invite link is invalid or has expired." Send them a new invite or use **Resend**.

<Callout type="warn" title="If the email can't be sent">
  Sometimes the invite is created but the email doesn't go out. You see "Invite created, but the email could not be sent." Open the **Invites** tab and click **Resend** to try again. There is no button to copy the link yourself.
</Callout>

### The Invites Tab

The **Invites** tab lists every invite you have sent, newest first. You can search by name or email and filter by **Status**: **All invites**, **Completed**, **Pending**, or **Expired**.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Recipient** | The customer's name (if known), their email, and "Up to N photos" |
| **Status** | **Completed** (they submitted; the date it was used is shown), **Pending** (sent, not used, not expired), or **Expired** |
| **Sent** | When you sent it |
| **Expires** | When the link stops working |

Each pending or expired invite has buttons at the right:

- **Resend** sends the email again and **restarts the 30 days**. It works on pending and expired invites. You see "Invite resent."
- **Cancel** is shown on pending invites. It asks "Cancel Invite?" and explains "This will expire the invite immediately. The recipient will no longer be able to use their link." Confirm with **Cancel invite**, or choose **Keep invite** to leave it alone.

A cancelled invite shows as **Expired**, so you can **Resend** it later if you change your mind. A completed invite has no buttons. Each invite link works once, so to ask the same customer again after they have submitted, send a new invite.

If you have not sent any yet, the tab says "No invites sent yet" with a **Send Invite** button.

![The Invites tab showing a Pending, a Completed, and an Expired invite](/images/testimonials/testimonials-invites-tab.png)

## Customers Who Write One Without an Invite

Customers do not need an invite. The Default template links to a form at `/testimonials/submit`, from the **Write a review** button on your testimonials page.

- The customer must be **signed in** to a customer account. If they are not, they are sent to sign in first.
- The testimonial uses the **name on their account** and they can add up to 5 photos.
- They write 10 to 1,000 characters.
- Each customer can submit **one** testimonial per store. If they try again they see "Already Submitted."

The form's button reads **Submit Testimonial**, and the small note under it follows your **Auto-approve** setting. With Auto-approve on, it says: "Your testimonial will appear on the site right away." With it off, it says: "Your testimonial will be reviewed before it appears on the site." After they submit, the thank-you message matches: it says "now live on the site" with Auto-approve on, or "will appear once it's approved" when it is off.

![The Share Your Experience form on a phone, signed in](/images/testimonials/testimonials-submit-form.png)

## What Customers See

What follows is the **Default** template. Other templates lay testimonials out in their own way, for example a small rotating selection of up to three, but they all show the same published testimonials.

### On the Homepage

The Default homepage shows **one** testimonial: the **latest published** one, by testimonial date. It appears as a large centered quote with the customer's name underneath, followed by a link to the full page.

If no testimonial is published, the section does not appear on the homepage at all. Photos are not shown here.

![The homepage Testimonial section with a large quote, the customer name, and the Read all reviews link](/images/testimonials/testimonials-homepage-section.png)

You can adjust this section in the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor) under **Homepage → Testimonial**:

- Show or hide the whole section.
- **Link text** (starts as "Read all reviews"). Leave it blank to hide the link.
- **Link** (starts as `/testimonials`).

Because the newest date wins, the quote on your homepage changes whenever you publish a testimonial with a newer date. If you want a specific testimonial there, give it the latest **Testimonial Date**, or hide the ones above it.

### On the Testimonials Page

The `/testimonials` page lists every published testimonial, newest first, in a grid. It has:

- A heading area with a small label ("From customers"), the heading "What people say.", and an intro line ("Real reviews from real orders.").
- A summary bar showing how many reviews there are.
- A card for each testimonial: five stars, the quote, small photo thumbnails, the customer's name and title, and the month and year.
- A banner at the bottom with the label "Recent purchase?", the heading "Tell us how it went.", a line of text, and a **Write a review** button that opens the form at `/testimonials/submit`.

If there are none, visitors see "No reviews yet — check back soon!"

![The testimonials page with the heading area, summary bar, grid of testimonial cards, and the Write a review banner](/images/testimonials/testimonials-page-desktop.png)

All of the words in the heading area, the empty message, and the banner can be changed in the Site Editor. Open the **Testimonials** page and edit **Testimonials** and **Share your experience**. Leave the banner's **Body text** or **Write a review button** blank to hide that piece. If you hide the button, customers can no longer reach the form from this page, except through an invite.

<Callout type="info" title="A few things you can't change on this page">
  Every card shows five stars and the summary reads "reviews from verified buyers". These are part of the design, and they are shown on owner-added testimonials too. Photos are small thumbnails and can't be opened larger. The customer's email, company, and headline are never shown to visitors.
</Callout>

### The Name "Reviews"

The Default template labels its menu link to this page **Reviews**, in the header and the footer. The page it leads to is your testimonials page, not your [Product Reviews](/docs/reviews). Keep that in mind if you also turn on product reviews so visitors do not confuse the two. You can rename or move the link in your menu (see [Navigation](/docs/navigation)).

## Photos and the Media Library

Photos that customers or you upload with a testimonial are saved in your [Media Library](/docs/media-library). They show up there when you pick the **Testimonials** filter. Deleting a testimonial does not delete its photos from the Media Library, so you can remove them there if you want to free space.

## Tips

- **Check for pending ones.** You are not emailed when a customer submits a testimonial, so look for the red number on the **Testimonials** tab from time to time. If you would rather not check, turn **Auto-approve** on, and use **Hide** to remove any you do not want.
- **Ask right after a good moment.** An invite sent a day or two after a delivery, a class, or a job well done gets the best responses.
- **Allow a photo or two.** A photo of the finished product or the happy customer makes a quote feel more real. Set **Max photos** to 0 if you just want words.
- **Backdate carefully.** The testimonial date decides which quote is "latest" on your homepage and where it sits on your testimonials page.
- **Hide instead of deleting.** **Hide** keeps the quote in your admin so you can bring it back. **Delete** is permanent.

## Feature Flag

| Feature | Default | Where to toggle | Requires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Testimonials** | On | Settings → Features (Marketing section) | Nothing |
| **Auto-approve testimonials** | Off | The card at the top of **Marketing → Testimonials** | Testimonials turned on |

Turning **Testimonials** off hides the admin page, the `/testimonials` page, the homepage testimonial, and the **Reviews** menu link on the Default template. Your saved testimonials and invites are kept. Invite links that were already sent stop working while the feature is off. See [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags).

## What You Can and Cannot Do

**You can:**

- Add testimonials yourself, with a date, photos, and optional details
- Invite customers by email with a personal link that works once and lasts 30 days
- Choose how many photos (0 to 5) each invited customer can add
- Resend or cancel invites
- Approve testimonials before they go live, or turn on Auto-approve
- Approve, unapprove, hide, unhide, and delete in bulk (bulk delete is Owner only)
- Remove individual photos from any testimonial
- Show, hide, and reword the testimonial sections on the Default homepage and testimonials page

**You cannot:**

- Edit the words of a testimonial a customer wrote
- Add photos to a customer's testimonial
- Choose which testimonials appear on your homepage (the latest published one is used)
- Be emailed when a new testimonial arrives
- Copy an invite link to send yourself
- Let a customer edit or delete their own testimonial
- Let one customer submit more than one testimonial
- Change the five stars or the "verified buyers" line on the Default testimonials page
