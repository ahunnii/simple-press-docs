## Overview

Most of your store runs itself. A few things still need a regular look from you, so nothing slips: an order that didn't ship, a review waiting for approval, a backup you meant to take.

This page is a checklist you can come back to. Each item links to the page that explains it. You won't need every line. If a feature is switched off for your store, skip its items.

<Callout type="info" title="We are not tax advisors or accountants">
  The tax items below point you to the right places. They aren't tax, legal or accounting advice. Please talk to a qualified professional about your own taxes.
</Callout>

## Every Day (or Whenever Orders Come In)

Start on your [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard). It shows what needs you.

- [ ] **Look at the Needs Attention cards.** They show orders to fulfill, orders awaiting payment, and missing store policies. Click a card to open the list. See [Needs Attention](/docs/dashboard#needs-attention).
- [ ] **Ship or hand over paid orders.** Mark each one fulfilled, with tracking if you have it. See [Fulfilling and tracking](/docs/orders#fulfilling-and-tracking) and [Local pickup orders](/docs/orders#local-pickup-orders).
- [ ] **Check for a red Oversold items box** on the Dashboard. Decide whether to restock, substitute, or contact the customer. See [Oversold Items](/docs/dashboard#oversold-items).
- [ ] **Watch for a "Payments are paused" banner** across the top of the admin. While it shows, nobody can check out. See [Payments-Paused Banner](/docs/dashboard#payments-paused-banner).
- [ ] **Answer dispute emails quickly.** A red **Disputed** badge on an order means a customer has gone to their bank. Stripe gives you a deadline to respond. See [Disputes](/docs/payments-and-payouts#disputes).
- [ ] **Reply to messages from your contact form.** They go to your Support Email. See [Contact Form](/docs/contact-form).
- [ ] **Approve or hide new product reviews.** A number next to **Reviews** in the sidebar means some are waiting. See [Approving a Customer Review](/docs/reviews#approving-a-customer-review).
- [ ] **Approve new testimonials.** You don't get an email for these. Look for the red number next to the **Testimonials** tab. See [Reviewing What Is Waiting](/docs/testimonials#reviewing-what-is-waiting).
- [ ] **Follow up on new quote requests.** In **Marketing → Quotes**, set the status filter to **New**. See [The Quotes Inbox](/docs/quote-calculators#the-quotes-inbox).
- [ ] **Read new form entries.** Open the form's entries and filter to **New**. See [Working With Entries](/docs/forms#working-with-entries).

> **Not getting the alert emails?** Most of them go to your Owner Email. See [Make sure these reach you](/docs/email-preview#make-sure-these-reach-you).

## Every Week

- [ ] **Restock what's running low.** Check the Dashboard's **Low Stock Alerts** card, and any low-stock emails. See [Low Stock Alerts](/docs/dashboard#low-stock-alerts) and [Email notifications](/docs/inventory-management#email-notifications).
- [ ] **Finish partly shipped orders.** On **Orders**, click **Filters** and set **Fulfillment** to **Partially Fulfilled**. These aren't counted on the Needs Attention card. See [Partial shipments](/docs/orders#partial-shipments).
- [ ] **Clear orders still awaiting payment.** If a customer paid you by cash or check for a manual order, mark it paid. See [Changing a status by hand](/docs/orders#changing-a-status-by-hand).
- [ ] **Chase overdue rentals.** In **Inventory → Check-outs**, open the **Overdue** tab. See [The check-out list](/docs/inventory-management#the-check-out-list).
- [ ] **Chase overdue invoices.** On **Invoices**, click the **Overdue** card. See [The Invoices list](/docs/invoices#the-invoices-list).
- [ ] **Keep your events current.** Past events archive themselves, so add the next ones before your Events page runs empty. See [Past Events and Archiving](/docs/events#past-events-and-archiving).
- [ ] **Check that payouts are arriving.** The **Recent Payouts** card on Finances shows each payout's status. A **Failed** payout usually means your bank details in Stripe need fixing. See [Recent Payouts](/docs/finances#recent-payouts).

## Every Month

- [ ] **Download your site as a backup.** Go to **Settings → Data & Export** and click **Download your site**. Keeping your own backups is your responsibility. The file holds customer details, so store it somewhere safe. See [Downloading Your Site](/docs/data-and-export#downloading-your-site), [Keeping Your Export Safe](/docs/data-and-export#keeping-your-export-safe), and [Leaving or Closing Your Store](/docs/leaving-or-closing).
- [ ] **Review your team.** Under **Settings → Team**, remove anyone who no longer helps, and revoke invitations nobody accepted. See [Removing a Team Member](/docs/team-and-roles#removing-a-team-member) and [Managing Pending and Expired Invitations](/docs/team-and-roles#managing-pending-and-expired-invitations).
- [ ] **Check where you're signed in.** Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, choose **Settings**, then click **Security**. Revoke any device you don't recognize. See [Active sessions](/docs/your-account-and-security#active-sessions).
- [ ] **Look over the month on Finances.** Set the range to **Last 30 days** to see what came in, what Stripe took, and what you've set aside for sales tax. See [Picking a time period](/docs/finances#picking-a-time-period).
- [ ] **Tally invoice payments** if you use invoices. Finances doesn't include them. In **Invoices > Activity**, set **Event** to **Payment recorded** and the dates to last month, then click **Export CSV**. See [Invoice activity](/docs/invoices#invoice-activity).
- [ ] **Glance at the INFORM Act badge** on Finances. If it says **Action Required**, finish the details Stripe asks for. See [INFORM Act compliance](/docs/finances#inform-act-compliance).
- [ ] **Raise your Search readiness score.** Do the **Next:** step on the Dashboard strip, or open **Site Setup → SEO & Meta** for the full list. See [The Score Tab](/docs/seo#the-score-tab).
- [ ] **Visit your own store** in a private or incognito window, on your phone and your computer. Click through a product and the cart, so you see what customers see. See [What Your Customers See](/docs/for-your-customers).

## Every Year (and at Tax Time)

### Before the year ends

- [ ] **Export your orders** for your records or your accountant. On **Orders**, click **Export CSV**. The spreadsheet has a Date column you can sort by year. See [Exporting to a spreadsheet](/docs/orders#exporting-to-a-spreadsheet).

### In the new year

- [ ] **Look at last year's totals.** On Finances, pick last year in the range selector (for example **Calendar year 2025**). Every card switches to last year except Recent Payouts, your Stripe balance, the INFORM Act card, and the year-to-date tax tile, which always shows this year. See [Picking a time period](/docs/finances#picking-a-time-period).
- [ ] **Get your tax forms from Stripe.** Your store takes card payments through your own Stripe account, which you sign in to directly. Any Form 1099-K comes from Stripe, not SimplePress, and only if your sales pass the IRS reporting threshold for that year. Look for it in your Stripe Dashboard early in the year. If you're not sure whether you should have one, ask Stripe's support or your tax professional.
- [ ] **Gather your sales tax figures.** The **Set aside (sales tax)** card on Finances shows what you've collected. Pick last year in the range selector to see last year's tax. For filing options, see [Set aside (sales tax)](/docs/finances#set-aside-sales-tax) and [Filing your returns](/docs/tax#filing-your-returns).

### Once a year, any time

- [ ] **Check your sales tax registrations.** If you're selling more into a new state, you may need to register there and add it in Stripe. See [Adding registrations as you grow](/docs/tax#adding-registrations-as-you-grow).
- [ ] **Re-read your store policies.** Make sure your Terms, Refund, Shipping and Privacy policies still match how you work. See [Policies](/docs/pages-and-policies#policies).
- [ ] **Renew your domain.** You bought your domain from a registrar, and the renewal happens there, not in SimplePress. If it lapses, your store's address stops working. Turn on auto-renew with your registrar if you can. See [Custom Domain](/docs/domain).
- [ ] **Check your contact details.** Make sure your Owner Email, Support Email, phone and address in **Settings → General** are current. See [General Settings](/docs/general-settings).
- [ ] **Review your shipping rates.** Carrier prices change. See [Shipping](/docs/shipping).
- [ ] **Re-read your agreement with SimplePress**, so you know what you've agreed to. See [Your Agreement](/docs/your-agreement).
