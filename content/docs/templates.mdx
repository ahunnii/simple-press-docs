## Overview

A template is the design of your storefront: the layout of your homepage, shop, product pages, and everything else customers see. Your products, orders, and settings stay the same no matter which template you use. The template decides how it all looks.

Most SimplePress stores don't pick a design off a shelf. **We usually build each business its own custom template**, made to look like your brand. Every store can also use a few ready-made templates, which are a good place to start while your own is being built.

---

## Your Own Custom Template

During the sandbox phase (see [Getting started](/docs/getting-started)), we'll ask you to share what you have, such as:

- Your current website, if you have one
- Your logo
- Photos of your products, your space, and your work
- Your brand colors and fonts
- Examples of other websites you like, and what you like about them

We use these to recreate your current site, or to design a new one, as a template made just for your store. **Only your store can use it.** It won't show up for anyone else.

Once it's ready, it appears in your template list and you can switch to it yourself (see [Switching Templates](#switching-templates) below). From then on, you edit its words, photos, and sections in the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor), just like any other template.

<Callout type="info" title="Want something changed in your design?">
  If your custom template needs a layout change or a new kind of section, send a note from the Site Editor or [contact SimplePress support](mailto:help@generativejustice.org). Words, photos, and anything else the Site Editor offers, you can change yourself anytime.
</Callout>

## Ready-Made Templates

These three templates are available to every store. They're a good way to get up and running, and to try out your products and content, while your custom template is in the works.

| Template | Best for |
| -------- | -------- |
| **Default** | A simple, flexible storefront that works for almost any kind of shop. A solid starting point you can make your own as you grow. |
| **Modern** | A clean, contemporary look that suits fashion, tech, and lifestyle brands. |
| **Elegant** | A soft, natural style made with beauty and wellness shops in mind, with a strip of short badges under the homepage banner to show off things like "organic". |

## Switching Templates

1. Go to **Site Setup → Brand & Appearance**.
2. In the **Site Visuals** card, open the **Select a template** dropdown and choose a template.
3. Click **Save changes** at the top of the page.

The new design goes live for customers as soon as you save. Picking a template from the dropdown doesn't change anything until you save, so you can look through the list without affecting your site.

You'll only see the templates your store is allowed to use: the three ready-made templates, plus your own custom template once it's ready.

![The Site Visuals card on Brand & Appearance, with the Select a template dropdown open](/images/templates/template-picker.png)

*Choose a template from the Site Visuals card, then save.*

## What Changes and What Stays

**Stays exactly the same:**

- Your products, collections, and inventory
- Your orders and customers
- Your pages, policies, and blog posts
- Your settings, including shipping, payments, domain, and features
- Your logo, checkout accent color, and navigation menu

**Changes:**

- The layout and look of every page on your storefront.
- **The words and photos you set in the Site Editor.** These are saved separately for each template. After you switch, your site shows the new template's starting text and pictures until you edit them. Your old template's content isn't deleted: switch back, and it all comes back just as you left it.
- **Style choices** (color palettes and font pairings in the Site Editor's Style panel, on templates that offer them) are made for one template, so they don't carry over.
- **Service page layouts.** Each [Services](/docs/services) page falls back to the new template's default layout, and any content you saved for the old layout won't show on it.

If a template doesn't have its own design for a particular page, that page uses the Default design instead, with your own content in it.

Your [product options](/docs/product-options) stay too. The picker on the product page is drawn in the template's own style, but the choices, prices and messages work the same on every template.

<Callout type="warn" title="Check before you switch">
  Switching takes effect the moment you save, for everyone visiting your store, and there's no way to preview a different template on your live site first. Look over the pictures above (or ask us if you'd like to see a template with your own content), then switch at a quiet time with little traffic. Right after switching, open the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor), go through each page, and fill in your own words and photos before you share the new look.
</Callout>
