## Overview

Connect outside services to your store from **Settings → Integrations**. This page covers three of them:

- **Stripe Connect** takes card payments at your checkout. Most of this page is about setting it up.
- **Umami Analytics** tracks visits to your storefront.
- **QuickBooks Online** sends deposit and final invoices for quotes. It only appears when QuickBooks Invoicing is turned on.

Owners and Managers can open this page. Staff can't.

<Callout type="info" title="Super Important">

Stripe is the only card processor SimplePress supports right now. You can still get paid other ways, such as cash, check, Venmo, Cash App and Zelle. These are covered in [Other ways to get paid](#other-ways-to-get-paid) below.

Also, we are NOT tax advisors or accountants, and we can't give tax advice. Please consult a qualified tax professional for guidance specific to your business, especially when it comes to registering for sales tax in your state. Stripe collects the tax, but you register with each state yourself and file your own returns.

</Callout>

## Stripe Connect

Stripe is the payment processor behind your storefront. Until you connect a Stripe account, customers can browse and fill their cart, but they can't check out. They see a "checkout unavailable" message instead. You also can't refund card payments, because the refund goes back through Stripe.

Your money goes straight to your own Stripe account. SimplePress never holds your funds, and never sees or stores customers' card numbers.

![The Stripe Connect card before an account is linked, with a Not Connected badge and the Connect with Stripe button](/images/integrations/stripe-not-connected.png)

Only Owners and Managers can connect or disconnect Stripe. Staff can't. See [Team & roles](/docs/team-and-roles).

### Connecting your Stripe account

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations**.
2. In the **Stripe Connect** card, click **Connect with Stripe**.
3. You'll be sent to Stripe to authorize the connection. Sign in to your existing Stripe account or create a new one.
4. After you authorize, Stripe sends you back to the page you started from, and a "Stripe connected" message pops up. The card now shows a **Connected** badge and your Stripe **Account ID**.

If something goes wrong, a message tells you what happened:

| Message | What it means |
| ------- | ------------- |
| Failed to initiate Stripe connection. Please try again. | SimplePress couldn't start the connection. Wait a moment and click **Connect with Stripe** again. |
| Stripe connection cancelled | You backed out on Stripe's page. Nothing was connected. Click **Connect with Stripe** when you're ready. |
| We couldn't finish connecting your Stripe account. Please try again. | Stripe approved the connection, but SimplePress couldn't finish it. Try again. |
| Stripe didn't send back an authorization, so your account wasn't connected. Please try again. | Stripe sent you back without approving. Try again. |
| Stripe connection failed. Please try again. | Something else went wrong on Stripe's side. Stripe's own explanation shows under the message. |

> Stripe handles the sign-in and permission step. SimplePress only keeps your Stripe Account ID, not your Stripe password.

Before you connect, it helps to have your bank account, business details and identity verification done in Stripe. See [Stripe Setup](/docs/stripe) for a walkthrough.

![The Stripe Connect card showing Connected, the Account ID, Open Dashboard and Disconnect buttons, and the Automatic Tax Collection switch](/images/integrations/stripe-connected.png)

### The badge on the card

The badge in the top right of the **Stripe Connect** card tells you where things stand:

| Badge | What it means |
| ----- | ------------- |
| **Not Connected** | No Stripe account is linked. Checkout is switched off and customers can't place orders. |
| **Connected** | Your Stripe account is linked and checkout is live. |
| **Charges paused** | Stripe has paused charges on your account, usually because it needs identity or business verification, more documents, or a reply on a dispute. Checkout fails for every shopper until you fix it in your Stripe Dashboard. |

If charges are paused, a red note on the card reads "Stripe has paused charges on this account", and a strip across the top of your admin says "Payments are paused." The strip has a **Fix in Stripe** link and a **Details** link. Once Stripe turns charges back on, the warning clears on its own within a few minutes. You don't need to reconnect.

### Once connected

- Your Stripe Account ID is shown on the card for reference.
- Card payments from customer orders are paid out to your Stripe account, then to your bank on Stripe's payout schedule.
- Click **Open Dashboard** to open your Stripe Dashboard in a new tab.

To see your balance, fees and recent payouts, open **Finances** in your admin sidebar. See [Finances](/docs/finances) and [Payments & Payouts](/docs/payments-and-payouts).

### Automatic Tax Collection

Once Stripe is connected, you'll see an **Automatic Tax Collection** switch in the same card. When it's on, Stripe Tax works out the sales tax for each customer's address and adds it at checkout. Stripe only charges tax in states where you've added a registration in Stripe.

**Before you turn it on**, set up Stripe Tax in your Stripe account: finish your Tax settings, mainly your head office address. If you haven't, the switch flips back off, nothing is saved, and a red message tells you what's missing, with an **Open Stripe Tax Dashboard →** link. Registrations aren't needed for the switch to turn on, but without one Stripe charges no tax.

When the switch is on, the card also shows a **Manage registrations →** link to your Stripe registrations.

A few things to know:

- The switch saves as soon as you click it. There's no Save button.
- Donations are never taxed, even with the switch on. A gift isn't a sale.
- Tax isn't calculated for you on orders you create by hand. See [Orders](/docs/orders).
- Stripe Tax collects the tax. Filing and paying it is up to you, unless you add Stripe's paid filing service. See [Tax](/docs/tax#filing-your-returns).

For a full guide to nexus and setting up Stripe Tax, see [Tax](/docs/tax), or click **Set up taxes →** next to the switch to open the Tax Guide in your admin.

### Disconnecting Stripe

Click **Disconnect** in the Stripe Connect card. A box titled "Disconnect Stripe account?" asks you to confirm: "You won't be able to accept payments until you reconnect a Stripe account." Click **Disconnect** again to go ahead, or **Cancel** to back out.

When you disconnect:

- Checkout turns off right away. Customers can't place new orders.
- SimplePress also removes its access to your account in Stripe. Your Stripe account itself isn't closed or changed.
- Existing orders and their history stay as they are.
- You can't refund card payments from SimplePress until you reconnect.
- You can click **Connect with Stripe** at any time to link an account again.

## Other ways to get paid

Stripe handles payments at your online checkout. You can take money other ways, and these don't need Stripe.

- **Manual orders.** For a phone order or an in-person sale, create the order yourself under **Orders** and set the **Payment Method** to Card, Cash or Check. No card is charged. See [Orders](/docs/orders).
- **Invoices.** Bill a customer directly and list how they can pay you: bank transfer, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, cash or check, or something else. You record the payment when it arrives. Add your methods in **Settings → Invoices**. See [Invoices](/docs/invoices).
- **Donations.** Your donate page can take card gifts through Stripe, and also show your Venmo username and Cash App cashtag. Venmo and Cash App work even if Stripe isn't connected. See [Donations](/docs/donations).

Customers can't pay with these methods at your normal checkout. Checkout goes through Stripe only. It shows cards plus any other payment methods you've turned on in your Stripe account.

---

## Fees

SimplePress doesn't take a cut of your sales. Stripe charges its own small fee on each card payment, and takes it before the money reaches you. Your Stripe Dashboard shows the exact rates for your account.

## Before you start selling

To sell, you need three things: Stripe connected, at least one product, and your own custom domain. Until all three are done, opening your admin takes you to the Setup Guide instead of the Dashboard. See [Launch checklist](/docs/launch-checklist) and [Custom Domain](/docs/domain).

---

**What you can do:**

- Connect a Stripe account from **Settings → Integrations**
- See your connected Stripe Account ID
- Open your Stripe Dashboard directly from the admin
- Turn Automatic Tax Collection on or off
- Disconnect your Stripe account
- Take cash, check, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle and similar payments through manual orders, invoices and your donate page

**What you cannot do:**

- Take card payments at checkout before Stripe is connected
- Use another card processor in place of Stripe
- Turn on Automatic Tax Collection before your Stripe Tax settings (head office address) are complete
- Change your payout schedule from SimplePress. You change it in your Stripe account.
- Refund card payments from SimplePress while Stripe is disconnected

## Umami Analytics

Umami is a privacy-focused analytics tool. In the **Umami Analytics** card, it tracks visits, page views, referrers and on-site events (product views, add-to-cart and embed engagement) on your storefront. It does this without third-party cookies or ad-network tracking.

The tracking script and event data are sent through your own storefront domain, so no third-party script loads in your visitors' browsers.

![The Umami Analytics card with the Website ID field, the Enable Analytics switch, the Enabled badge and the Save Changes button](/images/integrations/umami-card.png)

### Setting up Umami

SimplePress runs its own Umami instance, so you can't create this yourself. The card says so under **Website ID**: "SimplePress runs its own Umami analytics — you can't create a Website ID yourself. Email help@generativejustice.org with your storefront domain and we'll send you the Website ID to paste here." The Website ID isn't an Umami account ID or an API key.

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations**.
2. In the **Umami Analytics** card, paste your **Website ID** into the field.
3. Turn on **Enable Analytics**.
4. Click **Save Changes**. You'll see "Umami settings updated."

If you turn on **Enable Analytics** without a Website ID, you'll see "Website ID is required to enable analytics". If the ID isn't valid, you'll see "Enter a valid Umami website ID."

The badge in the top right of the card shows what is **saved**. It reads **Enabled** when a Website ID is saved and **Enable Analytics** was saved on. Otherwise it reads **Disabled**. If you change the field or the switch, the badge doesn't change yet. An **Unsaved changes** hint appears next to **Save Changes** until you save. Nothing is collected until you click **Save Changes** and see "Umami settings updated."

Once saved and enabled, the tracking script runs on your storefront pages and starts collecting visits right away.

### What isn't counted

Visits to these places are left out on purpose, so your own work doesn't skew your numbers:

- your admin, even if you click into it from your storefront
- sign-in, sign-up and password pages
- the Site Editor, and the previews it shows
- invoice pages your customers open from a link
- the "not permitted" page, and SimplePress's own back-office pages

So you can click around your admin as much as you like without adding to your visit counts. Real visits to your storefront pages are counted.

### Seeing your results

Your results appear on the [Analytics](/docs/analytics) dashboard in your admin. That dashboard is a separate feature, so it only shows up once you turn on **Analytics** under **Settings → Features**. See [Feature flags](/docs/feature-flags).

The two settings work independently:

- **Enable Analytics** in the card controls whether data is collected.
- The **Analytics** feature controls whether you can see the dashboard.

If you turn on collection but not the feature, data is collected but you can't view it in the admin yet. If you turn on the feature but not collection, the dashboard shows an **Analytics not connected** message with a link to **Settings → Integrations**.

---

**What you can do:**

- Enter your Umami Website ID
- Turn analytics collection on or off for your storefront

**What you cannot do:**

- Create your own Umami account or Website ID. The SimplePress team provides the Website ID.
- Count your own admin visits. They're always left out
- Use a different analytics provider through this card

## QuickBooks Online

QuickBooks Online lets you send invoices for quote leads through your own QuickBooks company: a deposit when the customer accepts, and the final invoice after the job. SimplePress creates the customer and invoice in QuickBooks, and QuickBooks emails it. Customers pay through QuickBooks' own invoice email, so money never passes through SimplePress.

**Where it is:** a **QuickBooks Online** card on **Settings → Integrations**. Owners and Managers can connect it. The card only appears when **QuickBooks Invoicing** is switched on under **Settings → Features**. See [Feature flags](/docs/feature-flags).

![The QuickBooks Online card showing a Sandbox badge, Connected, the company name and ID, Open QuickBooks and Disconnect buttons, and Deposit defaults](/images/integrations/quickbooks-card.png)

*A connected QuickBooks card. This screenshot is from a QuickBooks sandbox test company, which is why the card says Sandbox. A real company doesn't show that badge.*

### The badge on the card

| Badge | What it means | What you see |
| ----- | ------------- | ------------ |
| **Not configured** | QuickBooks isn't set up for your store yet. | A note to contact help@generativejustice.org, and a grayed-out **Connect QuickBooks** button. |
| **Not connected** | Ready to link. | A **Connect QuickBooks** button. |
| **Connected** | Linked and working. | **Company**, **Company ID**, "Connected since" (and "Last synced" once something has synced), **Open QuickBooks** and **Disconnect**. |
| **Needs reconnect** | QuickBooks no longer accepts the saved connection. | A **Reconnect QuickBooks** alert and a **Reconnect** button. You can't send invoices until you reconnect. |

QuickBooks connections expire after about 100 days, or sooner if access is removed inside QuickBooks. When that happens, click **Reconnect** and sign in again.

### Connecting

1. Click **Connect QuickBooks**.
2. Sign in with your Intuit account and choose the company you invoice from.
3. You come back to the page with a "QuickBooks connected" message.

If you back out you'll see "QuickBooks connection was cancelled". If something else goes wrong you'll see "QuickBooks connection failed". Try again.

If a red **Environment mismatch** note shows, the connection was made in a different mode (test or live) than your store now runs in. Reconnect to fix it. Email help@generativejustice.org if it doesn't clear.

### Deposit defaults

Under the connection is a **Deposit defaults** form. It prefills the deposit invoice on each quote, and you can change any invoice by hand.

| Setting | What it does |
| ------- | ------------ |
| **Deposit amount** | Choose **Percent of quote** or **Fixed amount**. |
| **Deposit percent** or **Deposit amount** | The percentage, or the dollar amount, depending on what you chose. |
| **Invoice due in (days)** | How many days customers have to pay, from 0 to 90. |

Click **Save**. You'll see "Deposit defaults saved."

### Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect**, then confirm in **Disconnect QuickBooks?** Your existing invoices stay in QuickBooks and in SimplePress. You just can't send new ones until you reconnect. Your deposit settings are kept.

For how deposit and final invoices work step by step, see [Invoices](/docs/invoices#quickbooks-invoicing).

---

**What you can do:**

- Connect, reconnect or disconnect your QuickBooks company
- Set the deposit type, amount and payment window that new quotes start with
- Open QuickBooks from the card

**What you cannot do:**

- See the card unless QuickBooks Invoicing is on, or connect it if the card says **Not configured**. Email help@generativejustice.org
- Send invoices while the card says **Needs reconnect**
- Take payments inside SimplePress. Customers pay through QuickBooks
