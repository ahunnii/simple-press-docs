## Overview

Connect outside services to your store from **Settings → Integrations**. The page is split into sections, in this order:

- **Payments** is a pointer to **Settings → Payments**, where **Stripe** takes card payments at your checkout. See [Stripe](#stripe) below.
- **Analytics & tracking** holds **Umami Analytics**, **Google Analytics** and **Meta Pixel**. See [Analytics & tracking](#analytics--tracking).
- **Customer chat** holds the **Chat assistant** card. See [Chat assistant](#chat-assistant).
- **Accounting** holds **QuickBooks Online**, which sends deposit and final invoices for quotes. See [QuickBooks Online](#quickbooks-online).
- **Developer** holds **API keys**. See [API keys](#api-keys).

Shippo shipping labels used to be here too. They're now connected in **Settings → Shipping**. See [Shippo shipping labels](#shippo-shipping-labels).

A section only appears when it has a card for your store, so you won't see an empty heading. **Customer chat** needs the team-managed **Chat assistant** feature. **Accounting** needs **QuickBooks Invoicing** switched on. **Developer** is for Owners only. See [Feature flags](/docs/feature-flags).

Owners and Managers can open this page. Staff can't.

![The top of Settings → Integrations, with the Payments moved to Settings → Payments pointer, the Analytics & tracking heading, a Privacy Policy notice and the Umami Analytics card](/images/integrations/integrations-overview.png)

<Callout type="info" title="Super Important">

Stripe is the only card processor SimplePress supports right now. You can still get paid other ways, such as cash, check, Venmo, Cash App and Zelle. These are covered in [Other ways to get paid](#other-ways-to-get-paid) below.

Also, we are NOT tax advisors or accountants, and we can't give tax advice. Please consult a qualified tax professional for guidance specific to your business, especially when it comes to registering for sales tax in your state. Stripe collects the tax, but you register with each state yourself and file your own returns.

</Callout>

## Stripe

Stripe is the payment processor behind your storefront. It has its own page now: **Settings → Payments**. Connect your Stripe account there, turn on Automatic Tax Collection, and see your payment providers.

Until Stripe is connected, customers can browse and fill their cart, but they can't check out. Only Owners can connect or disconnect Stripe, and Managers can see its status.

For the steps, the status badges, and what happens when you disconnect, see [Connecting Stripe](/docs/payments-and-payouts#connecting-stripe). For the tax switch, see [Tax](/docs/tax).

---

## Other ways to get paid

Stripe handles payments at your online checkout. You can take money other ways, and these don't need Stripe.

- **Manual orders.** For a phone order or an in-person sale, create the order yourself under **Orders** and set the **Payment method** to Card, Cash or Check. No card is charged. See [Orders](/docs/orders).
- **Invoices.** Bill a customer directly and list how they can pay you: bank transfer, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, cash or check, or something else. You record the payment when it arrives. Add your methods once, in **Settings → Payments** under **Other ways to pay**. See [Invoices](/docs/invoices).
- **Donations.** Your donate page can take card gifts through Stripe, and also show your Venmo and Cash App. They come from the same **Other ways to pay** list. Venmo and Cash App work even if Stripe isn't connected. See [Donations](/docs/donations).

Customers can't pay with these methods at your normal checkout. Checkout goes through Stripe only. It shows cards plus any other payment methods you've turned on in your Stripe account. Delayed methods such as bank debits are supported too. The order appears once the money clears; see [Bank debits and other delayed payment methods](/docs/payments-and-payouts#bank-debits-and-other-delayed-payment-methods).

---

## Fees

SimplePress doesn't take a cut of your sales. Stripe charges its own small fee on each card payment, and takes it before the money reaches you. Your Stripe Dashboard shows the exact rates for your account.

## Before you start selling

To sell products, you need Stripe connected, at least one product, and your own custom domain. If your store sells products, these are Required steps on your Setup Guide, and until every Required step is done, opening your admin takes you to the Setup Guide instead of the Dashboard. Some kinds of stores, such as services or informational sites, don't need Stripe. See [Launch checklist](/docs/launch-checklist) and [Custom Domain](/docs/domain).

---

**What you can do:**

- Connect a Stripe account from **Settings → Payments** (Owners only)
- See your connected Stripe Account ID
- Open your Stripe Dashboard directly from the admin
- Turn Automatic Tax Collection on or off
- Disconnect your Stripe account (Owners only)
- Take cash, check, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle and similar payments through manual orders, invoices and your donate page

**What you cannot do:**

- Take card payments at checkout before Stripe is connected
- Use another card processor in place of Stripe
- Turn on Automatic Tax Collection before your Stripe Tax settings (head office address) are complete
- Change your payout schedule from SimplePress. You change it in your Stripe account.
- Refund card payments from SimplePress while Stripe is disconnected

## Analytics & tracking

The **Analytics & tracking** section is for tools that show you who visits your store and where your sales come from. It holds three cards: **Umami Analytics**, **Google Analytics** and **Meta Pixel**. You can use any combination.

At the top of the section, a **Privacy Policy** notice appears when your policy doesn't match the tools you've turned on, for example "It doesn't mention Google Analytics yet." It has a button that takes you to **Content → Policies** (**Create a Privacy Policy**, **Open policies** or **Review privacy policy**). Nothing is changed for you. When any of these tools is switched on, a message also reminds you to update your Privacy Policy and offers an **Open policies** button. See [Privacy Policy warnings](/docs/pages-and-policies#privacy-policy-warnings).

Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel show your visitors a cookie banner. Umami doesn't need one. See [Cookie banner and your visitors](/docs/analytics#cookie-banner-and-your-visitors).

## Umami Analytics

Umami is a privacy-focused analytics tool run by SimplePress. In the **Umami Analytics** card, it counts visits, page views, where visitors came from, and shop actions like product views, add to cart, checkout and purchases on your storefront. It does this without cookies or ad-network tracking, so it doesn't need the cookie banner.

The tracking script loads from your own storefront domain, so no third-party script loads in your visitors' browsers. It skips you and your staff while you're signed in.

![The Umami Analytics card with the On badge and the Collect analytics switch turned on](/images/integrations/umami-card.png)

### Setting up Umami

Umami is self-serve now. There's no Website ID to paste and nothing to email us about.

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations**.
2. In the **Analytics & tracking** section, find the **Umami Analytics** card.
3. Switch on **Collect analytics**.

The switch **saves immediately**. While it works you'll see "Setting up analytics...", then the message **Analytics is on** with a reminder: "Update your Privacy Policy to mention Umami." and an **Open policies** button. Switching it off shows **Analytics is off**. If something goes wrong you'll see "Couldn't update analytics settings" and the switch goes back to where it was.

The badge in the top right of the card reads **On** or **Off**. Once it's on, the tracking script runs on your storefront pages and starts collecting visits right away.

### What isn't counted

Visits to these places are left out on purpose, so your own work doesn't skew your numbers:

- your admin, even if you click into it from your storefront
- sign-in, sign-up and password pages
- the Site Editor, and the previews it shows
- invoice pages your customers open from a link
- the "not permitted" page, and SimplePress's own back-office pages

You and your staff are also skipped while signed in. Real visits to your storefront pages are counted.

### Seeing your results

Your results appear on the [Analytics](/docs/analytics) dashboard in your admin. That dashboard is a separate feature, so it only shows up once you turn on **Analytics** under **Settings → Features**. The card says so too: "To see results in the admin, the Analytics feature must also be on." See [Feature flags](/docs/feature-flags).

The two settings work independently:

- **Collect analytics** in the card controls whether data is collected.
- The **Analytics** feature controls whether you can see the dashboard.

If you turn on collection but not the feature, data is collected but you can't view it in the admin yet. If you turn on the feature but not collection, the dashboard says **Analytics is off** with a link to **Settings → Integrations**.

---

**What you can do:**

- Turn analytics collection on or off for your storefront with one switch

**What you cannot do:**

- Count your own admin visits. They're always left out

## Google Analytics

The **Google Analytics** card counts visits and sales on your storefront in your own Google Analytics 4 property. You need a Google Analytics account. SimplePress only connects your store to it.

![The Google Analytics card with a Measurement ID typed in, the box for Google's EU user consent policy checked, the Events we send row, and Save changes with Unsaved changes beside it](/images/integrations/google-analytics-card.png)

*The badge still says Off here because the new ID hasn't been saved yet. It changes to On after you click Save changes.*

### Setting it up

1. In Google Analytics, open **Admin**, then **Data streams**, then your **Web** stream, and copy the **Measurement ID**. It looks like `G-ABC123XYZ9`. Keep **Enhanced measurement** on so page changes are counted.
2. In SimplePress, go to **Settings → Integrations** and find the **Google Analytics** card.
3. Paste it into **Measurement ID**.
4. Check **I'll follow Google's EU user consent policy**. This box appears whenever you're saving a new ID, and you can't save without it. There's a **Read the policy** link beside it. You're responsible for your Google Analytics account and for describing it accurately in your Privacy Policy.
5. Click **Save changes**. You'll see "Google Analytics settings updated" and a reminder to update your Privacy Policy.

If the ID isn't valid you'll see "Enter a valid Measurement ID, like G-ABC123XYZ9". To turn Google Analytics off, clear the field and save. The badge shows **On** or **Off** for what's saved. An **Unsaved changes** hint appears next to **Save changes** until you save.

### What visitors see

Visitors see a cookie banner with an Analytics choice. Google Analytics sets cookies only after a visitor allows **Analytics**, and Google's advertising features only after they allow **Advertising**. Before they choose, or if they reject, Google's Consent Mode still sends Google cookieless pings with their IP address, browser and device details, the page address and the time. Google Analytics never loads for you or your staff while you're signed in, or on admin, sign-in or account pages.

Purchases are reported with the Stripe checkout session ID (starting `cs_`) as the transaction ID. It matches the Stripe session shown on the order.

### Events we send

Open **Events we send** on the card for the list: `view_item`, `add_to_cart`, `remove_from_cart`, `view_cart`, `begin_checkout`, `purchase` (orders, donations and paid subscriptions), `generate_lead` (forms, contact and quote requests), `sign_up` (newsletter), `search` and `embed_engaged`. No names, emails or form answers are included.

To count the important ones as conversions, open Google Analytics, go to **Admin**, then **Events**, and turn on **Mark as key event** for `purchase`, `generate_lead` and `sign_up`. A new event can take a day to appear in that list.

### Server-side tracking (advanced)

Once a Measurement ID is saved, a **Server-side tracking (advanced)** section appears on the card. It's optional.

It also sends each paid order to Google Analytics from SimplePress's server, so sales still count when a shopper's browser blocks Google. It only does this for shoppers who allowed Analytics cookies before checking out, and it uses the same transaction ID as the browser so Google can match the two.

1. In Google Analytics, go to **Admin**, **Data streams**, your web stream, **Measurement Protocol API secrets**, then **Create**.
2. Paste the secret value into **Measurement Protocol API secret** and click **Save**. You'll see "API secret saved" and a reminder to mention server-side purchase tracking in your Privacy Policy.

A saved secret is never shown again. The section says **On**, with **Replace** and **Remove** buttons. **Remove** asks "Remove secret?" and stops server-side tracking until you save a new one. **Send a test event** checks the event format with Google's validation server. Nothing is recorded in your reports.

## Meta Pixel

The **Meta Pixel** card sends your storefront's visits and shopping actions to your own Meta (Facebook and Instagram) account, for measuring and optimizing ads.

![The Meta Pixel card with a Pixel ID typed in, the box for Meta's Business Tools Terms checked, the Events we send row, and Save changes with Unsaved changes beside it](/images/integrations/meta-pixel-card.png)

*The badge changes from Off to On once you save.*

### Setting it up

1. In Meta Events Manager, open **Data sources** and copy your **Pixel ID** (10 to 20 digits).
2. In SimplePress, go to **Settings → Integrations** and find the **Meta Pixel** card.
3. Paste it into **Pixel ID**.
4. Check **I accept Meta's Business Tools Terms**. It appears when you're saving a new ID, and you can't save without it. You're responsible for your Meta account, how you use the data, and describing the Pixel accurately in your Privacy Policy.
5. Click **Save changes**. You'll see "Meta Pixel settings updated" and a Privacy Policy reminder.

An invalid ID shows "Enter a valid Pixel ID: 10 to 20 digits". Clear the field and save to turn it off.

### What visitors see

Visitors see a cookie banner with an **Advertising** choice. The Pixel loads only after a visitor allows Advertising, and never when their browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal. While it's on, the link at the bottom of your store reads **Your privacy choices** so visitors can opt out at any time. It never loads for you or your staff while you're signed in, or on admin, sign-in or account pages.

If your store uses a SimplePress web address rather than your own domain, the card suggests connecting a custom domain for accurate ad attribution. See [Custom Domain](/docs/domain).

The events sent are `ViewContent`, `AddToCart`, `InitiateCheckout`, `Purchase`, `Donate`, `Subscribe`, `Lead`, `CompleteRegistration` (newsletter) and `Search` (without the search words). Open **Events we send** on the card to see how to check they arrive.

### Server-side tracking (advanced)

Once a Pixel ID is saved, an optional **Server-side tracking (advanced)** section appears. It also sends each paid order to Meta from SimplePress's server, so sales still count when a shopper's browser blocks the Pixel. It only does this for shoppers who allowed Advertising cookies before checking out, with Meta's cookie IDs, their IP address and browser details. No names or email addresses are sent. It uses the same event ID as the Pixel, so Meta counts the purchase once.

1. In Meta Events Manager, open your dataset (pixel), then **Settings**, then **Generate access token** under **Conversions API**.
2. Paste it into **Conversions API access token** and click **Save**. You'll see "Access token saved".

Once saved, you can **Replace** or **Remove** it, the same as with Google Analytics. To check it works, open your dataset's **Test Events** tab in Events Manager, copy the code under **Server** (like `TEST12345`) into **Test Events code**, and click **Send a test event**.

## Chat assistant

The **Chat assistant** card adds a chat button to your storefront. It works with GoHighLevel (LeadConnector) chat widgets. The **Customer chat** section only appears when the **Chat assistant** feature is on for your store. That feature is managed by the SimplePress team. See [Feature flags](/docs/feature-flags).

![The Chat assistant card before setup, with the Off badge, an empty Embed code box, and the two When should chat load? options](/images/integrations/chat-assistant-card.png)

### Setting it up

1. In GoHighLevel, copy your chat widget's embed code.
2. Go to **Settings → Integrations** and find the **Chat assistant** card in **Customer chat**.
3. Paste it into **Embed code**. You can paste the full snippet or just the widget ID. SimplePress shows "Recognized: ..., widget ..." and saves only the widget ID. The rest of the paste is ignored.
4. Choose **When should chat load?**:
   - **When a visitor taps Chat with us (recommended)**: pages are faster, and the chat tool can't track visitors who never open it. It doesn't need the cookie banner.
   - **On every page**: needed if GoHighLevel's SMS (A2P) compliance review says the chat widget isn't on your website. The chat tool then loads for every visitor, so it may set cookies and receive visitors' IP addresses before they open it. It isn't part of the cookie banner, so keep your Privacy Policy up to date.
5. Click **Save changes**. You'll see "Chat assistant saved" and a reminder to update your Privacy Policy.

To change only the loading choice, switch it and click **Save changes**. To take chat off, click **Remove**, then confirm **Remove chat assistant?**

The chat button is hidden while you're signed in, so check it in a private window. It never shows on admin, sign-in, account, checkout or order pages.

## API keys

The **API keys** card is in the **Developer** section, which only Owners see. It lets another app, such as ProfitMax, read your store's public business details and catalog: name, location, hours, products, prices, services and FAQs. Keys can't see orders, customers or payments.

![The API keys card with a Create key button and one active key, showing when it was Created, when it was Last used, and a Revoke button](/images/integrations/api-keys-card.png)

- **Create key** opens **Create an API key**. Give it a **Name**, then create it. The next screen, **Your new key**, shows the key and your **Site address** once, with copy buttons. "Copy this now — you won't be able to see it again."
- Each key in the list shows its name, when it was **Created**, and **Last used** (for example "Last used 2 days ago", or "Last used: Never").
- **Revoke** stops a key. You confirm first, and apps using it stop working right away. A revoked key stays in the list marked **Revoked** with its date.

## QuickBooks Online

QuickBooks Online lets you send invoices for quote leads through your own QuickBooks company: a deposit when the customer accepts, and the final invoice after the job. SimplePress creates the customer and invoice in QuickBooks, and QuickBooks emails it. Customers pay through QuickBooks' own invoice email, so money never passes through SimplePress.

**Where it is:** a **QuickBooks Online** card on **Settings → Integrations**. Owners and Managers can connect it. The card only appears when **QuickBooks Invoicing** is switched on under **Settings → Features**. See [Feature flags](/docs/feature-flags).

![The QuickBooks Online card showing a Sandbox badge, Connected, the company name and ID, Open QuickBooks and Disconnect buttons, and Deposit defaults](/images/integrations/quickbooks-card.png)

*A connected QuickBooks card. This screenshot is from a QuickBooks sandbox test company, which is why the card says Sandbox. A real company doesn't show that badge.*

### The badge on the card

| Badge | What it means | What you see |
| ----- | ------------- | ------------ |
| **Not configured** | QuickBooks isn't set up for your store yet. | A note to contact help@generativejustice.org, and a grayed-out **Connect QuickBooks** button. |
| **Not connected** | Ready to link. | A **Connect QuickBooks** button. |
| **Connected** | Linked and working. | **Company**, **Company ID**, "Connected since" (and "Last synced" once something has synced), **Open QuickBooks** and **Disconnect**. |
| **Needs reconnect** | QuickBooks no longer accepts the saved connection. | A **Reconnect QuickBooks** alert and a **Reconnect** button. You can't send invoices until you reconnect. |

QuickBooks connections expire after about 100 days, or sooner if access is removed inside QuickBooks. When that happens, click **Reconnect** and sign in again.

### Connecting

1. Click **Connect QuickBooks**.
2. Sign in with your Intuit account and choose the company you invoice from.
3. You come back to the page with a "QuickBooks connected" message.

If you back out you'll see "QuickBooks connection was cancelled". If something else goes wrong you'll see "QuickBooks connection failed". Try again.

If a red **Environment mismatch** note shows, the connection was made in a different mode (test or live) than your store now runs in. Reconnect to fix it. Email help@generativejustice.org if it doesn't clear.

### Deposit defaults

Under the connection is a **Deposit defaults** form. It prefills the deposit invoice on each quote, and you can change any invoice by hand.

| Setting | What it does |
| ------- | ------------ |
| **Deposit amount** | Choose **Percent of quote** or **Fixed amount**. |
| **Deposit percent** or **Deposit amount** | The percentage, or the dollar amount, depending on what you chose. |
| **Invoice due in (days)** | How many days customers have to pay, from 0 to 90. |

Click **Save**. You'll see "Deposit defaults saved."

### Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect**, then confirm in **Disconnect QuickBooks?** Your existing invoices stay in QuickBooks and in SimplePress. You just can't send new ones until you reconnect. Your deposit settings are kept.

For how deposit and final invoices work step by step, see [Invoices](/docs/invoices#quickbooks-invoicing).

---

**What you can do:**

- Connect, reconnect or disconnect your QuickBooks company
- Set the deposit type, amount and payment window that new quotes start with
- Open QuickBooks from the card

**What you cannot do:**

- See the card unless QuickBooks Invoicing is on, or connect it if the card says **Not configured**. Email help@generativejustice.org
- Send invoices while the card says **Needs reconnect**
- Take payments inside SimplePress. Customers pay through QuickBooks
