## Overview

SimplePress is a free-to-use platform. We're a very small team of developers doing our best to give you a great experience. We aren't a Fortune 500 company, so we can't offer the same level of support as a larger one.

## What you can expect

- We will be available to help you with any questions or issues you have. We'll do our best to respond within 24 hours, but if we don't, please send a follow-up email.
- We will continue to work with you to get your site up and running.
- We will be constantly updating the platform to improve the experience for you.

## What we can't promise

- We cannot promise that we'll be able to add every feature you need. SimplePress is meant to be a minimalistic platform. If you need more features, we can discuss other options with you.
- We cannot promise that issues, bugs, or features will be resolved quickly. We triage based on our current workload across all of our clients and ongoing projects.

## Getting help and requesting changes

There are a few ways to reach us, depending on what you need.

### Get Help

Click **Get Help** at the bottom of the admin sidebar to open this help guide. Everyone on your team sees it, including staff members.

### Contact Support

On the **Setup Guide** page, the **Need Help?** card has a **Contact Support** link that emails the SimplePress team.

### Notes in the Site Editor

For changes to how your site looks or reads, a note is the best way to ask. It goes straight to us with the page or section already attached, so there's no back-and-forth about where you mean.

1. Open the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor) and click **Notes** in the top bar.
2. Choose what the note is about: **Whole site**, **This page**, or **A section on this page**.
3. Describe the change you'd like.
4. Optionally click **Add photos** to attach up to 3 photos, such as a screenshot or an example you like.
5. Click **Send note**.

Each note you send shows as **Open** or **Resolved**, and our reply appears right under it. See [Editor Notes](/docs/site-editor#editor-notes) for the details.

### Feature requests

There's no separate request form. Send a note from the Site Editor or email us, and tell us what you're trying to do. We keep a running list of what we're working on in [Planned Features](/docs/planned-features).
