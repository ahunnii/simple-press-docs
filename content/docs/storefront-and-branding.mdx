## Overview

**Brand & Appearance** is where you set the parts of your store that make it look like yours: which template it uses, your logo, your favicon (the little icon in a browser tab), a tagline for the footer, and links to your social profiles. It lives inside **Site Setup**, the hub for the store-wide settings you configure once and rarely change.

Day-to-day wording, pictures, section order, colors, and fonts are edited in the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor), not here.

<Callout type="info" title="Owners and Managers only">
  Site Setup and the Site Editor are for Owners and Managers. Staff can't open them. See [Team and Roles](/docs/team-and-roles).
</Callout>

## Getting Here

In your admin sidebar, the **Content** group has two entries for your storefront:

- **Site Editor**: change what your site says and shows. See [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor).
- **Site Setup**: the one-time setup hub, described as "Brand, navigation, search listing, and policies."

Open **Site Setup** and you'll see a card for each area:

| Card | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Brand & Appearance** | Template, logo, favicon, footer tagline, social links. This page. |
| **Policies** | Privacy, Terms, Refunds, Shipping. See [Pages and Policies](/docs/pages-and-policies). |
| **Navigation** | Your header menu and footer links. See [Navigation](/docs/navigation). |
| **SEO & Meta** | How your store appears in search results and when shared. See [SEO & Search Readiness](/docs/seo). |
| **FAQ** | Add and manage the questions and answers shown on your storefront. |
| **Banner & Popup** | Your announcement banner and homepage popup. Shown only when the banners or popups feature is on. See [Banner & Popup](/docs/banner-and-popup). |

## Brand & Appearance

Click the **Brand & Appearance** card. The page has three cards: **Site Visuals**, **Business Branding**, and **Footer & social links**.

![The Brand & Appearance page with the Site Visuals, Business Branding and Footer & social links cards](/images/storefront-and-branding/brand-appearance.png)

### Site Visuals

This card is headed "How does your site look to people?" and has:

- **Select a template**: "A template changes the overall look and feel of your storefront." Pick one from the list. You'll only see templates your store has access to. For what each one does and what happens to your content when you switch, see [Templates](/docs/templates).
- **Checkout accent color**: one color, "Shown on checkout and cart buttons on supported templates." Click the swatch to pick a color. The same color also tints the install screen when a customer adds your store to an Android home screen.
- A link, **Edit sections, colors and fonts in the Site Editor**, that opens the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor).

<Callout type="info" title="Colors and fonts for the rest of your site live in the Site Editor">
  The checkout accent color is the only color set on this page. Your site's main colors and fonts are chosen in the Site Editor under **Theme**, on the templates that offer it. See [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor).
</Callout>

A template switch isn't applied until you save (see [Saving](#saving)). If your store doesn't have access to the template you picked, saving fails with "This template is not available for your store."

### Business Branding

#### Logo

Your logo appears in your storefront header and in the emails your store sends to customers. If you don't upload one, your store name is shown as text instead.

1. Click **Choose image**. (Once an image is set, the button reads **Replace image**.)
2. Pick **Upload from device**, or **Choose from library** to use a picture from your [Media Library](/docs/media-library). The library option appears only when the Media Library is turned on.
3. Click **Save changes**.

The preview shows "New image selected. Upload on submit." until you save, because the file is uploaded when you save, not when you pick it. Click the trash icon next to the image to remove it, then save.

- **Accepted formats:** JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, and SVG, up to 5MB.
- A transparent PNG at least 200 pixels wide works well in most headers.
- **Emails show your store name instead of an SVG logo.** Many email programs don't display SVG pictures, so if your logo is an SVG, customer emails use your store name as text. Upload a PNG if you want the logo itself in emails.

![The Logo field with the Replace image menu open, showing Upload from device and Choose from library](/images/storefront-and-branding/logo-menu.png)

#### Logo alt text

Describes your logo to screen readers, and to search engines when the picture can't load. It's usually just your business name.

#### Favicon

The favicon is the small icon in the browser tab and on a phone's home screen. Upload a square image, 512×512 or larger. PNG or SVG works best.

Under the field, a live preview shows how the icon will look:

- **Browser tab**: the icon next to your page title.
- **Home screen**: the icon on a white rounded tile, like a phone app icon.
- **Using your logo**: shown when you haven't set a favicon and your logo is being used instead.

The preview updates as soon as you pick a file, before you save. It can also show a warning:

- "This image isn't square. It'll be padded to fit."
- "This image is small (W×H). Icons may look blurry on phones." This appears for images smaller than 192 pixels.

![The Favicon field with the Browser tab and Home screen previews](/images/storefront-and-branding/favicon-preview.png)

*With no favicon set, the preview shows "Using your logo".*

A few things to know about favicons:

- You upload one image. SimplePress makes every size from it and serves them from your store's own address: the browser-tab icon (`/favicon.ico`, in 16, 32, and 48 pixel sizes), 48 and 192 pixel icons for Google Search, a 180 pixel Apple home-screen icon on a white background, and 192 and 512 pixel icons for Android home screens, including a maskable 512 version.
- Non-square images are padded to a square, never cropped.
- If you leave the favicon blank, your logo is used. If there's no logo either, the SimplePress icon is used.
- Browsers and Google cache favicons, so a new icon can take a while to appear everywhere.

### Footer & social links

- **Footer tagline**: shown in your storefront footer. Use it for a mission statement or a short blurb about your business. Up to 500 characters.
- **Social Links**: Instagram, Facebook, Twitter / X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, and YouTube. Fill in only the ones you use; only those appear in your footer. Each link must be a full address starting with `http://` or `https://`, for example `https://instagram.com/yourstore`.

The links in your footer's quick-links column are set separately. See [Navigation](/docs/navigation#footer-quick-links).

### Saving

All three cards save together with one button. The top bar shows an **Unsaved Changes** badge when you have edits, and **Saved** when everything is current.

- **Save changes** saves everything on the page, including a template switch.
- **Reset** throws away your unsaved edits and returns to the last saved state.
- **Cmd + Enter** (Mac) or **Ctrl + Enter** (Windows) saves from the keyboard.

## What You Cannot Change After Signup

- **Subdomain**: your subdomain (for example `yourstore.simplepress.co`) is set during onboarding and can't be changed afterward. To use your own address instead, see [Domain Settings](/docs/domain).
- **Template layout and code**: templates are built and managed by the platform. You choose one and customize its content, but you can't edit its layout.
- **Templates not assigned to your store**: some templates are made for specific accounts. You only see the ones available to you.

Need something changed that you can't do yourself? Email help@generativejustice.org.

## Can and Can't

**You can:**

- Switch templates and save the change from Brand & Appearance
- Upload a logo (including SVG), set its alt text, and remove or replace it
- Upload one favicon and let SimplePress make every size
- Set the footer tagline and up to seven social links
- Pick the checkout accent color
- Tune your search title, description, and share image in SEO & Meta (see [SEO & Search Readiness](/docs/seo))

**You cannot:**

- Set your main site colors or fonts here (use the Site Editor's Theme panel)
- Change your subdomain
- Add search keywords (there's no keywords field; see [SEO & Search Readiness](/docs/seo))
- Show an SVG logo inside customer emails (your store name is shown instead)
- Change a template's layout or code
