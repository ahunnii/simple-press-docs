## Overview

Collections let you group related products together under a shared page — perfect for seasonal drops, product categories, curated edits, or any grouping that makes sense for your store. Customers can browse all your published collections at `/collections` and click into any one to see its products.

---

## What Collections Are (and Aren't)

Collections are **organizational and display only**. Adding a product to a collection doesn't change its price, visibility, or inventory settings. A product can belong to multiple collections at the same time.

Collections have their own pages on your storefront, their own images, and their own SEO settings — they're browsable destinations, not just filters.

> **Requires:** Collections depend on the Products feature. If Products is turned off, Collections becomes unavailable too. You can switch the Collections feature on or off at **Settings → Features**.

---

## The Collections List

Go to **Collections** in your admin sidebar (under **Catalog**) to see all of your collections in a table. Each row shows:

- The collection's featured image (or a folder icon if you haven't uploaded one), its name, and its description
- **Products** — how many products are in it. If some of those products are drafts, a second line shows how many are "live" (published)
- **Status** — a **Published** or **Draft** badge

If a collection is published but has no published products in it, the row shows a warning: "Published, but shoppers see an empty page." Add a product or publish one of its products to fix it.

![The Collections list: a table of collections with image, name, description, product count and Published status, plus search and Filters](/images/collections/collections-list.png)

### Finding and sorting

- **Search collections…** matches the collection's name, its URL, and its description.
- **Status** filters between **All collections**, **Published**, and **Drafts**.
- **Sort** offers **Newest** (the default), **Name A–Z**, **Name Z–A**, **Oldest**, **Most products**, and **Fewest products**.

The list shows 25 collections per page.

### Row actions

Click a collection's name to open it, or use the three-dot menu at the end of the row:

- **Edit**
- **View on storefront** (only for published collections; opens in a new tab)
- **Duplicate** — makes a copy named "Copy of [name]" with the same image, products, product order, and SEO settings. The copy is saved as a **draft**, so nothing goes live until you publish it. Its URL slug is `<name>-copy` (then `-1`, `-2`, and so on, if that's taken).
- **Delete**

### Doing several at once

Tick the checkboxes on the left to select collections. A bar appears with:

- **Publish** and **Unpublish** — up to 100 collections at a time. A message appears afterwards with an **Undo** button.
- **Delete** — up to 25 collections at a time. Only the store **Owner** sees this button.

When every collection on the page is ticked and more collections match your search or filters, the bar also offers **Select all N collections**. That selects every match, not just the ones on this page.

## Creating or Editing a Collection

Click **Create Collection** or click a collection's name to open the collection editor. The editor has two tabs, **Basics** and **SEO**, and a toolbar across the top with:

- A status label: **Draft** (new and unsaved), **Unsaved Changes**, or **Saved**
- The **Published** switch
- A **More Options** menu: **View on storefront** (only for a saved, published collection), **Duplicate** and **Delete** (saved collections only), and **Reset** (throws away your unsaved changes; greyed out until you have some)
- **Save & create another** (new collections only) and **Save changes**

![The collection editor on the Basics tab, with the name, storefront URL, description and image fields, and the Published switch, More Options and Save changes in the toolbar](/images/collections/collection-editor.png)

### Basic Information

On the **Basics** tab:

- **What is the name of this collection?** _(required)_ — the collection's display name, shown on the storefront and in the browser tab. Up to 120 characters.
- **Describe this collection in a sentence or two** — shown on the collection page. Up to 1,000 characters.
- **Upload an image that represents this collection** — the featured image customers see for this collection.

Under the name, the editor shows the collection's **Storefront URL**, with an **Edit in SEO** link.

### Published Toggle

The **Published** switch in the top toolbar controls whether the collection is visible on your storefront. **New collections start with Published switched on**, so switch it off before you save if you want to keep working on it privately.

Unpublished collections return a "not found" page to customers — even if they have the direct URL. You can keep saving changes to a collection while it's unpublished without it going live.

### Products

The **Products** card is where you choose what goes in the collection:

1. Type in **Search products to add…** and click a result to add it. (Press Enter to add the first result.) Each result shows its price, and products already in the collection carry an **Added** tag. Results are capped at the first 10 matches, with **Showing first 10 — refine your search** shown beneath them. Products that are still drafts are labeled **Draft**.
2. Your picks appear under **In this collection**. Drag them into the order you want shoppers to see. Shoppers see this order by default, under the **Featured** sort. If the collection is empty, the list reads **No products yet — search above to add some.**
3. Click the **×** on a product to take it out of the collection.

The card shows how many products are in the collection. A collection can hold up to 500. Your changes are saved when you click **Save changes**.

If every product in a published collection is a draft (or there are no products at all), the editor warns you that the storefront page will render empty. Draft products stay out of the collection page until you publish them.

![The Products card in the collection editor: a "Search products to add…" box and the products in the collection, each with a drag handle and a remove button](/images/collections/collection-products.png)

You can also add a product to collections from the product side. Open the product and go to its **Collections** tab (it appears when Collections is turned on; see [Products](/docs/products)). Use the **Search collections...** box to find a collection and tick it. Collections the product already belongs to show as removable badges.

### Slug (URL)

On the **SEO** tab, **URL Slug** controls the collection's address. For example, the slug `summer-drop` gives you `/collections/summer-drop`. Slugs can use letters, numbers, dots, dashes, tildes, and underscores, and each one must be unique in your store.

While you're creating a collection (or editing one that's still a draft), the slug follows the name automatically as you type. You can also type your own slug at any time. For a collection that's already published, the slug stays put when you rename it. The editor then shows a note, **Name changed — the URL hasn't.**, with an **Update URL** button if you'd like to change the URL to match the new name.

> **Note:** Changing a collection's URL breaks any links pointing to the old one, including bookmarks and search results. SimplePress does not create a redirect. When you change a saved slug, the editor shows an inline notice, **Heads up — this will change the collection's URL.**, before you save. If the collection has been live and shared publicly, be cautious.

### SEO

The **SEO** tab also has:

- **Meta Title** — the page title shown in search results and browser tabs. The counter reads `N/60 characters including " | Your Store"`, using your SEO brand name (or your business name if you haven't set one). Aim for 50–60 characters; the field allows up to 70. Leave blank to use the collection name.
- **Meta Description** — the description shown in search results. Aim for 150–160 characters; the field allows up to 200. Leave blank to use the collection description.
- **Open Graph Image** — the picture used when the page is shared on social media. If you don't upload one, the collection's featured image is used.
- **Search Result Preview** and **Social Media Preview** — live previews of how the page might look in Google and when shared.

If you leave the meta fields blank and the collection has no description either, the storefront uses "Shop [collection name] at [your business name]" as the description. Published collections are also included in your site's sitemap for search engines.

## Deleting a Collection

You can delete a collection from the collection editor (**More Options → Delete**) or from the collections list (the three-dot menu on its row → **Delete**). A confirmation dialog appears before anything is removed, and it can't be undone.

**Deleting a collection removes the collection and its page from your storefront, but does not delete the products in it.** Products remain in your catalog untouched.

## How Collections Look on Your Storefront

- **Collections index** — `/collections` lists all published collections, in the order they were created. Each one links to its page, and most templates show its image and how many products it has. The display style depends on your template.
- **Collection page** — `/collections/[slug]` shows the collection's image, name, description, and its published products in the order you set in the editor. Many templates also show your other collections at the bottom of the page, so customers can keep browsing.

**On the Default template,** the collections pages use text you can edit in the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor):

- **Collections index:** the **Label above the heading** (default "Shop by collection"), the **Heading** (default "Collections"), and the **Empty state message** shown when there are no collections yet ("No collections available at this time.").
- **Collection page:** a breadcrumb trail, the collection name, a product count (such as "12 products"), a wide banner that appears only when the collection has an image, up to 3 other collections under the **Related collections heading** (default "More collections") with an **All collections →** link, and the **Empty state message** ("No products in this collection yet.") with a **View all products →** link when the collection has no products.

The Default template also has its own layout for these pages. Other templates may lay them out differently and may not offer every one of these fields.

![A collection page on the Default template: breadcrumb, collection name and product count, banner image, and the product grid](/images/collections/storefront-collection.png)

Only published products show up on the storefront, and product counts only include published products.

Unpublished collections are completely invisible — they return a not-found page rather than a hidden/preview view.

Some templates also let you feature one of your collections on another page, such as the homepage. In the [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor), pick it from a **Collection** field; if you leave it on "None (use featured products)", the template shows your featured products instead.

## Who Can Manage Collections

Owners and Managers can create, edit, duplicate, publish, and delete collections. Only Owners can delete several at once. Staff can't open Collections. See [Team and Roles](/docs/team-and-roles) for the full list.

---

## Can and Can't

**You can:**

- Create as many collections as you need
- Add a product to more than one collection
- Save a collection as a draft (unpublished) while you work on it
- Duplicate a collection to use as a starting point
- Drag the products in a collection into the order you want
- Delete a collection without affecting any products
- Set per-collection SEO titles and descriptions
- Upload a featured image and a separate share image for each collection
- Edit a collection's URL slug (with the caveats above)

**You cannot:**

- Use collections if the Products feature is disabled
- Apply pricing, discounts, or inventory changes through a collection (collections are display only)
- Reorder the collections themselves — they appear in the order they were created
- Set an automatic redirect from an old collection URL to a new one

---

## Feature Flag

**Collections** is off by default. Owners and Managers can switch it on in **Settings → Features** (it's in the **E-Commerce** group). While Collections is off, the admin section is hidden and `/collections` shows a not-found page.
