## Overview

Your store has two sides:

- **The storefront** is the public website your customers visit. It shows your template, your pages, and your products.
- **The admin** is your private workspace. It's where you add products, handle orders, edit your pages, and change settings. Only people on your team can open it.

Everything you change in the admin shows up on the storefront. This page is a tour of the admin itself: where to find it, how the sidebar is laid out, and the shortcuts that save you clicks.

---

## Where Your Admin Lives

Your admin is at your store's own web address with `/admin` on the end. For example, if your store is `yourstore.com`, your admin is `yourstore.com/admin`.

- If you're not signed in, you'll go to the sign-in page first, then straight back to the admin.
- Your admin also keeps working at your free SimplePress subdomain after you connect your own domain. See [Custom Domain](/docs/domain#frequently-asked-questions).
- Where you land depends on your store and your role. Owners and Managers land on the [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard), or on the [Setup Guide](/docs/launch-checklist) until every Required setup step is done. Staff land on Orders.

<Callout type="info" title="One admin per store">
  Each store has its own admin at its own address. There's no menu for switching between stores. If you help run more than one store, use the same SimplePress account, and go to each store's own address with `/admin` on the end. Bookmark each one. You may need to sign in at each store separately.
</Callout>

### Getting to your storefront

- Click **View Store** at the top of the [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard#the-buttons-at-the-top). Your storefront opens in a new tab, on your own domain if you have one.
- Click the **simple_press** name at the very top of the sidebar, where your store's name is shown underneath. It opens your storefront's home page in the same tab.

To see what your customers see, step by step, read [What Your Customers See](/docs/for-your-customers).

## The Sidebar

The sidebar down the left side of every admin page is how you get around. At the top is the **simple_press** name with your store's name under it. Below that, pages are grouped under five headings: **Sell**, **Catalog**, **Marketing**, **Content** and **Insights**. At the bottom are **Settings**, **Setup Guide**, **Get Help**, and your account menu.

![The admin sidebar, ending with Settings and Setup Guide just above your account name](/images/dashboard/welcome-sidebar.png)

*The sidebar on a store with several features turned on. Yours shows only the pages your store uses.*

The page you're on is highlighted. If customers have sent product reviews that are waiting for you, a number appears next to **Reviews**.

### Why a page might be missing

The sidebar only shows what you can use. A page is hidden when:

- **Its feature is switched off.** Most pages belong to a feature in **Settings → Features**. Switch the feature off and its page leaves the sidebar for everyone, along with its search results and shortcuts. Many features start off, so a new store won't see Invoices, Events, Forms and others until you turn them on. See [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags).
- **Your role can't use it.** Staff only see **Orders**, **Customers**, **Inventory** and **Get Help**. Managers see everything an Owner sees. See [Team & Roles](/docs/team-and-roles#what-each-role-can-do).

If a group has nothing left in it, its heading disappears too.

### Hiding the sidebar

Click the sidebar button at the top left of any admin page, just left of the page trail, to hide the sidebar and give the page more room. Click it again to bring it back. You can also press **Command + B** on a Mac or **Ctrl + B** on Windows.

### The page trail

Across the top of each page is a trail showing where you are, such as **Dashboard › Settings › Team**. Click any part of it to go back up a level.

## Settings and Site Setup

Two sidebar items open a page of cards instead of a single screen:

- **Settings** holds how your store runs.
- **Site Setup** holds how your site presents itself.

Click a card to open that section. Once you're inside, a row of tabs across the top lets you jump between sections without going back. On a narrow screen, swipe the tabs sideways to see the rest.

## Searching and Jumping Around

You don't have to click through the sidebar every time. A search box at the top right of every admin page opens the **command palette**, which can jump to any page and look up records. On a wide screen the box reads "Search…" with ⌘K beside it. On a phone it's a magnifying glass icon.

To open it, press **Command + K** on a Mac or **Ctrl + K** on Windows, or click the search box. Press the same keys again, or click outside the box, to close it.

![The command palette open with a few search results](/images/dashboard/command-palette.png)

*The command palette after typing "clay," showing matching pages and a product.*

The palette's search field says "Search orders, customers, products, or jump to a page…". Start typing and the results narrow as you go. The list is grouped like this:

- **Actions** start common tasks: **Edit site**, **Add product**, **Create discount**, **Create manual order**, **Add event**, **Add video**, **Invite team member**, **Send email marketing**, **New quote calculator**, **New form**, **New invoice**, **New QuickBooks invoice**, **View setup guide**, **Business settings**, **Rewards settings**, and **Tax Guide**. You only see the ones whose feature is on.
- **Go to** jumps to any page in the sidebar, such as Orders, Products or Finances.
- **Settings** and **Content** jump to the tabs inside Settings and Site Setup, such as **Brand & Appearance**, **SEO & Meta**, **Policies** or **Team**.
- **Orders**, **Customers** and **Products** show up once you've typed at least two characters, with up to five matches in each group. Click one to open it.
  - **Orders** read "#1042 — Customer Name", with the total and status on the right. They match the order number or the customer's name or email. Typing `1042` or `#1042` finds that order.
  - **Customers** match on name or email.
  - **Products** match on the product name, its web address (slug), or the SKU of the product or any of its variants.

It understands everyday words too. Typing "coupon" finds **Discounts**, and typing "newsletter" finds **Email Marketing**.

The palette follows the same rules as the sidebar. Switched-off features and pages your role can't use don't appear. Staff see only Orders, Customers and Inventory, with no Actions, Settings or Content groups and no product results.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Mac | Windows | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Search | **Command + K** | **Ctrl + K** | Opens or closes the [command palette](#searching-and-jumping-around). |
| Sidebar | **Command + B** | **Ctrl + B** | Hides or shows the sidebar. |
| Save | **Command + Enter** | **Ctrl + Enter** | Saves the form you're on, as if you'd clicked its save button. |

**Command + Enter** (or **Ctrl + Enter**) works on these pages:

- Products, Collections, Services and Discounts, when adding or editing one
- Pages and Blog posts, when adding or editing one
- New manual order
- **Site Setup → Brand & Appearance** and **SEO & Meta**
- **Settings → General**, **Shipping**, **Donations**, **Invoices** and **Rewards**

It does nothing while your cursor is inside a rich-text box, such as the main text of a page or post, or while a pop-up window is open. Click outside it first.

## Leaving a Page With Unsaved Changes

On most forms, if you've changed something and try to leave before saving, a box titled **Discard unsaved changes?** asks first. Click **Stay** to go back and save, or **Discard changes** to leave without saving.

## Your Account Menu

Your name sits at the very bottom of the sidebar. Click it to open a menu with your name and email, then **Settings** and **Sign Out**.

- **Settings** opens your own account: your name, photo, password and signed-in devices. These belong to you, not the store. See [Your Account & Security](/docs/your-account-and-security).
- **Sign Out** signs you out on this device.

Store settings are under **Settings** in the sidebar, not in this menu.

---

## Using the Admin on a Phone

The admin works on a phone or small tablet. On a narrow screen:

- **The sidebar is tucked away.** Tap the sidebar button at the top left of the page, and it slides out from the left. Tap a page, and the panel closes by itself.
- **The search box becomes a magnifying glass.** Tap it to open the command palette.
- **Settings and Site Setup tabs scroll sideways.** Swipe them to find the one you want.

![The admin on a phone with the sidebar slid out from the left over the dimmed Dashboard, showing the Sell, Catalog and Marketing groups and the account menu at the bottom](/images/admin-basics/phone-sidebar.png)

For editing your pages from a phone, see [Site Editor](/docs/site-editor#editing-from-your-phone-or-tablet).

## What You Can and Cannot Do

| Action | Available |
| --- | --- |
| Open your admin from any computer or phone | Yes, at your store's address with `/admin` on the end |
| Search for orders, customers and products from any admin page | Yes, with the command palette |
| Hide the sidebar to get more room | Yes |
| Save most forms with a keyboard shortcut | Yes, **Command + Enter** or **Ctrl + Enter** |
| Rearrange or rename sidebar items | No |
| Add your own shortcuts or bookmarks inside the admin | No. Use your browser's bookmarks |
| Switch between stores from inside the admin | No. Go to each store's own address |
| Show a hidden page to Staff | No. Change their role to Manager. See [Team & Roles](/docs/team-and-roles) |
| Bring back a page that's missing from the sidebar | Yes, if its feature is off. Turn it on in **Settings → Features** |
