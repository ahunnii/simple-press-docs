## Overview

**General Settings** is where you keep the basic facts about your business: its name, your time zone, the email addresses customers and SimplePress use to reach you, your phone number and your address.

To open it, go to **Settings → General** in the admin. Owners and Managers can open it. Staff can't. See [Team & roles](/docs/team-and-roles).

The page has three cards:

| Card | What's in it |
| ---- | ------------ |
| **Business Information** | Business Name, Store Slug and Time Zone. |
| **Contact Details** | Owner Email, Support Email and Phone Number. |
| **Business address** | Street, city, state and ZIP, plus an optional map pin. |

---

## Business Information

![The Business Information card with Business Name, Store Slug and Time Zone](/images/general-settings/general-business-info.png)

### Business Name

The name of your business. It's required. It appears in your storefront header and footer, and in the emails your customers get from you, such as order confirmations, shipping notices and refunds. A change takes effect as soon as you save.

### Store Slug

Your slug is your store's internal ID. SimplePress sets it when your store is created, based on your business name, and it's **greyed out** here because it can't be changed.

<Callout type="info" title="The slug is not your web address">
  Your slug and your web address are separate things. Your address (your free subdomain, or your own domain once you connect one) is managed under **Settings → Domain**. The two can look alike, but changing one never changes the other. See [Custom Domain](/docs/domain).
</Callout>

### Time Zone

Pick the time zone your business actually operates in. The list has 16 common zones, and it starts on **Eastern Time — Detroit** until you change it.

![The Time Zone menu open, showing the list of zones](/images/general-settings/time-zone-menu.png)

SimplePress uses your time zone to show times the way you and your shoppers expect them:

- Event dates and times on your storefront and in your admin.
- The day that counts as "today" on your Dashboard.
- Invoice due dates.
- The launch date and time in [Maintenance Mode & Coming Soon](/docs/maintenance-mode).

Your [Business Hours](/docs/business-hours) are the exception. They are shown exactly as you type them and are never converted to another time zone.

## Contact Details

![The Contact Details card with Owner Email, Support Email and Phone Number](/images/general-settings/contact-details.png)

### Owner Email

Your **Owner Email** is the business's main contact address. The help text calls it "not a public facing email", and it isn't printed in your storefront footer. But it isn't entirely private either, so use an address you're comfortable with customers occasionally seeing.

- **Alerts come here.** Most store alerts go to your Owner Email, such as new orders, low stock and new quote requests. Disputes and "payments paused" alerts go to the Owner Email **and** to every Owner and Manager on your team. See [Emails You Receive](/docs/email-preview#emails-you-receive).
- **Customers can reply to it.** The emails your customers get (order confirmations, shipping notices, refunds, "ready for pickup" and others) use your Owner Email as their **Reply-To** address. When a customer hits Reply, it lands here.
- **It can show in some emails.** For example, a quote confirmation says "or contact" followed by this address, and the footers of testimonial and team invitation emails include it.

<Callout type="info" title="This is not your sign-in email">
  Your Owner Email is a contact address for the business. It isn't the email you sign in with, and changing it doesn't change your login.
</Callout>

### Support Email

Your **Support Email** is the public address customers use to reach you. It's required, so you can't save the page without one. It's shown on your storefront (the help text says so), and contact form messages are sent to it. See [Contact Form](/docs/contact-form).

On the Default template, it appears in the footer as an envelope icon next to your social icons, and on your Contact page. Use a dedicated support address you check often.

### Phone Number

Your public business phone number. It's optional. Where it shows depends on the template, but on the Default template it appears under the **Help** column in the footer and on your Contact page. Leave it blank if you don't want a number on your site.

![The Default storefront footer, with the support email as an envelope icon beside the social icons, and the phone number and address under Help](/images/general-settings/storefront-footer-contact.png)

*The Default footer. Your Support Email is the envelope icon. Your phone and address sit under the Help column. A custom template may place them differently.*

## Business address

The **Business address** card holds your address in four boxes: **Street Address**, **City**, **State** and **ZIP**. They're split out so search engines can understand where you are. Your storefront shows the address as one line.

Underneath, a line reads **Shown on your site as:** followed by how your address will look to visitors. Check it before you save.

![The Business address card with the four address boxes, the Shown on your site as line, and the Map pin section](/images/general-settings/business-address.png)

If your store was set up before the address was split into four boxes, your old one-line address is in the **Street Address** box. Move the city, state and ZIP into their own boxes when you get a chance.

Leave the address blank if you don't want one shown.

### Map pin (optional)

The **Map pin (optional)** section takes a **Latitude** and a **Longitude**. Fill in both or leave both blank. If you fill in only one, SimplePress asks for the other before it will save.

The easiest way to get the numbers: open [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com), right-click your exact spot, and click the coordinates at the top of the menu to copy them. Paste into either box and both fill in. Once both are valid, a **Check the pin on Google Maps ↗** link appears so you can confirm the spot.

What the pin does depends on your site design:

- **Some templates show a map**, usually on the homepage or the Contact page, and use your pin for it. The **Default template shows no map**, so the pin won't add one there.
- **Search engines can use it.** The pin helps them place your business when **Local presence** is set to **Storefront** on the **Search engines** tab of **Site Setup → SEO & Meta**. See [SEO](/docs/seo).

If you're not sure whether your template shows a map, leave the pin blank. It's safe to skip.

## Saving your changes

Click **Save changes** in the top toolbar, or press **Cmd+Enter** (**Ctrl+Enter** on Windows). On a phone the button just says **Save**.

![The toolbar with the Unsaved Changes badge, Reset and Save changes buttons](/images/general-settings/unsaved-toolbar.png)

- The badge beside the page title reads **Saved** or **Unsaved Changes**.
- **Reset** throws away everything you haven't saved and goes back to the last saved version. It's hidden on narrow screens.
- If you try to leave with unsaved edits, your browser asks you to confirm.

If a box isn't valid, SimplePress highlights it and tells you what to fix. For example, a ZIP over 16 characters, or a latitude outside -90 to 90.

## What you can and can't do

| You can | You can't |
| ------- | --------- |
| Rename your business any time. | Change your store slug. |
| Pick the time zone your business runs in. | Save without a Support Email. |
| Set separate Owner and Support emails. | Change your sign-in email here (the Owner Email is only a contact address). |
| Add a phone number and a structured address. | Make your Owner Email completely hidden: it's the Reply-To on customer emails. |
| Add a map pin. | Make your hours follow your time zone: hours show as typed. |
