## Overview

The Site Editor is where you change what your storefront says and shows. You see your real site in the middle of the screen. Click any part of it, such as a headline, a photo, or a row of cards, and an editing panel opens beside it. As you type, the preview updates so you can see exactly how the change will look.

Nothing you do in the Site Editor reaches your customers until you click **Publish**. Until then your changes are kept as a private draft, so you can experiment, leave, and come back later without anyone seeing a half-finished page.

<Callout type="info" title="Replaces the old Template Fields screen">
  The Site Editor is how you edit your template's content now. The fields described on the [Template Fields](/docs/template-fields) page are the same ones you edit here, but you edit them on top of a live preview instead of in a long form.
</Callout>

---

## Opening the Site Editor

There are three ways in:

- Click **Site Editor** in the **Content** part of the admin sidebar.
- Open the command palette (the search box at the top of any admin page, or `Cmd + K` / `Ctrl + K`) and choose **Edit site**.
- On the **Brand & Appearance** page, click **Edit sections, colors and fonts in the Site Editor**.

The editor opens full screen, without the admin sidebar. To leave, click **Exit** (the back arrow) in the top-left corner to go back to your admin.

![The Content group in the admin sidebar, with Site Editor highlighted](/images/site-editor/sidebar-content.png)

## The Editor Layout

On a computer, the editor has a bar across the top and three columns underneath.

![The Site Editor on a computer: top bar, Sections list on the left, live preview in the middle, and the Hero editing panel on the right](/images/site-editor/editor-desktop.png)

| Area                 | What it does                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Top bar**          | Exit button, your store name and template, the page picker, the Desktop / Tablet / Mobile preview buttons, **Notes**, your save status, **Discard** (only shown when you have unpublished changes), and **Publish**. |
| **Sections list**    | Left column. Lists every editable section on the page you're viewing, then the **Site-wide** sections (header, footer, and so on), shortcuts to other admin pages, and **Style** under **Design**. |
| **Live preview**     | Middle. Your actual storefront page, showing your draft changes. Hover over any part of it to see an **Edit** label, and click to edit that section.                                       |
| **Editing panel**    | Right column. Opens when you pick a section, Style, Notes, or one of your own pages. Only one panel is open at a time. Close it with the X.                                            |

If a page has nothing to edit, the Sections list says "No editable sections on this page." A section with nothing to change says "This section has no editable fields."

Above the preview there are two small buttons: **Refresh preview** reloads the page, and **Open storefront in new tab** opens the page in its own browser tab so you can see it full size.

### Two ways to pick something to edit

- **Click it in the preview.** If you click a specific headline, photo, or list item, the editing panel opens with that exact field ready to type in.
- **Pick it from the Sections list.** The preview scrolls to that section and briefly highlights it.

## Choosing a Page

Use the page picker in the middle of the top bar to switch pages. It has up to three groups:

- **Site pages:** the pages your template builds for you, such as Homepage, About, Contact, Shop, Collections, and others, depending on your template. The shop listing is called **Shop** or **Products** depending on your template, and your FAQ page shows up as **FAQ**. If your template has a services page, it appears here once you turn on [Services](/docs/services).
- **Your pages:** custom pages you've created under **Pages**. Ones that aren't published yet are marked "(unpublished)".
- **Blog posts:** your blog posts, if you have the Blog feature turned on.

A few pages behave slightly differently:

- **Product:** this previews one of your products, your newest published one. A note above the preview reminds you that changes here apply to **every** product page, not just the one you're looking at. To change a single product's name, photos, or description, edit the product itself in [Products](/docs/products).
- **Authentication:** on templates that have one, this previews the sign-in screen. It's always last in the list. Changes appear on every sign-in and sign-up screen. The side image only shows on larger screens, so you won't see it in the tablet or mobile preview.
- Pages for features you've turned off (for example, Blog or Events) don't appear in the picker, because they don't exist on your live site.

![The page picker open, showing the Site pages, Your pages, and Blog posts groups](/images/site-editor/page-picker.png)

## Editing a Section

When a section's panel opens, you'll see its name and a short description at the top, then the fields you can change. Some sections are split into smaller groups with their own headings.

Any field you've changed since your last publish shows a **Modified** tag next to its name, so you can see at a glance what's about to go live.

Text, paragraph, link, number, color, and on/off fields you haven't touched show what your site is currently displaying. A headline box, for example, shows the template's built-in wording rather than an empty box. **Image fields are different:** an untouched image field looks empty even when your template shows a built-in picture there. Upload one to replace it. See [Images and Videos](#images-and-videos).

![The Hero editing panel with a small Modified tag beside the Small label field](/images/site-editor/hero-panel-modified.png)

### Field types

Depending on your template and the section, you may see these kinds of fields:

| Field               | What it's for                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Text**            | A single line, such as a headline, button label, or tagline.                                                                                         |
| **Paragraph**       | A few lines of plain text, such as an intro or description.                                                                                          |
| **Rich text**       | Formatted writing with bold, italics, headings, lists, links, and images, plus rows and columns and collapsible sections. An **Insert elements** menu can add a Gallery, Embed, Quote calculator, or Form, but each one only appears when that feature is switched on in **Settings → Features**. (The banner, popup, and policy editors leave out rows, collapsible sections, and these extras.) See [Text Editor](/docs/text-editor). |
| **Link**            | A web address for a button or link. Use a page on your site (like `/shop`) or a full address (like `https://example.com`).                         |
| **Image**           | One photo. Upload one or choose from your Media Library. See [Images and Videos](#images-and-videos).                                               |
| **Video**           | One video file. Upload one or choose from your Media Library.                                                                                       |
| **Color**           | A color picker. **Clear** removes the color entirely (it shows as "None").                                                                          |
| **Number / slider** | A number, sometimes shown as a slider with a live readout (for example, how strongly a photo is darkened). Sliders have a **Reset** button.         |
| **On/off switch**   | Turns something on or off, such as showing an extra line or badge.                                                                                  |
| **Gallery**         | Picks one of your saved [Galleries](/docs/galleries) to show in that spot. Choose **None** to show no gallery.                                      |
| **Collection**      | Picks one of your [Collections](/docs/collections) to feature in that spot. **None (use featured products)** shows your featured products instead.  |
| **FAQ picker**      | Picks which of your published FAQ questions to show, and in what order. Click **Add question** to add one, and use the trash icon to remove one (an **Undo** button appears). If you pick none, the section shows your first published questions, and a hint says how many. If the section still has older hand-typed questions, a notice offers **Replace with picked questions**. |
| **Embed**           | Paste a link or embed code (**URL or embed code**) for outside content, such as a booking widget or video. You can also set an **Aspect ratio** (or **Width** and **Height (px)**), whether it shows **Inline** or opens in a dialog (**Display**) with a **Button label**, and a **Title**. Requires the **Embeds** feature; without it the field says "Embeds are disabled for this business" and points you to **Settings → Features** (see [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags)). Booking widgets for a service page are set on that service instead (see [Services](/docs/services)). |
| **List**            | A set of repeating rows, such as feature cards, steps, or team members. See [Lists](#lists).                                                        |

For what each field type accepts and how to fill it in well, see [Template Fields](/docs/template-fields).

Some fields only appear once a related setting is on. For example, a "darken photo" slider might only show up after you add a background photo. That's normal: the editor hides controls that wouldn't do anything yet.

### Related content

Some sections show things that live elsewhere in your admin, such as a row of products, testimonials, or blog posts. The Site Editor lets you change those sections' headings and wording, but not the items themselves. At the bottom of the panel, a **Related content** box links to the admin page where you manage them. These links open in a new tab, so your place in the editor isn't lost.

### The Contact page's FAQ section

On many templates, the Contact page ends with a **FAQ** section. In the Site Editor you can change its small label, its heading, and which questions it shows (up to 6). If you pick none, it shows your first 6 published questions. The questions themselves are written under **Site Setup → FAQ** in your admin, and you can hide the whole section with the eye icon.

### Showing and hiding sections

Some sections can be hidden. In the Sections list, hover over one (or look to the right of it on a touch screen) and click the eye icon.

- A hidden section stays in the list, dimmed, with a crossed-out eye. You can still edit it while it's hidden.
- Hiding and showing are part of your draft, like any other change. Visitors see the difference after you publish.
- If a section has no eye icon, the template doesn't allow hiding it.

![The Sections list with the Testimonial section hidden: dimmed, with a crossed-out eye icon](/images/site-editor/hidden-section.png)

## Lists

Many sections use lists: a row of feature cards, a set of steps, a team, a set of logos, and so on. Each row starts collapsed, showing a small preview and a one-line summary. Click a row to open it and edit its parts.

| To do this          | Do this                                                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Add a row**       | Click **Add** (for example, **Add card** or **Add step**) below the list. The new row opens ready to fill in. |
| **Reorder rows**    | Drag the handle (the dotted grip) on the left of a row, or open the **⋯** menu and choose **Move up** or **Move down**. |
| **Remove a row**    | Click the trash icon. An **Undo** button appears for a few seconds in case you didn't mean it.             |

Some rows have a small icon picker for choosing an icon, and some sub-fields are marked **(optional)** after their label, so you can leave them blank.

Some lists have a maximum number of rows. When you reach it, the add button is replaced by a note such as "Maximum of 6 cards". Some lists also require a minimum, so the trash icon is greyed out when you're at that number.

![A list field with one row expanded, showing the drag handle, the ⋯ menu, and the trash icon](/images/site-editor/list-row-expanded.png)

### Built-in rows

Many templates come with starter rows already filled in, so a new site never looks empty. When a list is still showing those, you'll see the note "Showing the built-in … — edit any of them to make them your own."

- These rows behave like normal rows. As soon as you edit, add, remove, or reorder any of them, the whole list becomes yours.
- If you delete every row, your site goes back to showing the built-in rows, and a message says "All cards removed — your site shows the built-in ones. Use the section's visibility toggle to hide them." To get rid of the section completely, hide it with the eye icon in the Sections list instead.
- Some lists have built-in rows you can't edit one by one. Those show "No cards yet." and "Your site is showing the built-in cards. Add one to replace them." Your first added row replaces them all.

## Images and Videos

Image and video fields show the picture (or video) you've uploaded, with options to replace or remove it. Remember that an image field you haven't touched looks empty, even if your template shows a built-in picture there.

- **Upload from device:** drag a file onto the box, or click to choose one. On a phone, tap to pick from your photos.
- **Choose from library:** pick something you've already uploaded to your [Media Library](/docs/media-library). This option appears when the Media Library feature is on, which it is by default.
- **Remove:** clears your image from that spot. Depending on the section, the template then shows nothing there or falls back to its own built-in picture. Either way, the field itself goes back to looking empty.

| Limit      | Images                                                                                     | Videos   |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------- |
| File size  | Up to 5MB. Large phone photos are resized automatically, and iPhone (HEIC) photos are converted. | Up to 50MB |

Pictures you add inside a rich-text field work differently. Use the **Image** button in its toolbar, drag a photo into the text, or paste one in. Those can be up to 25MB, and big photos are shrunk automatically. See [Adding pictures](/docs/text-editor#adding-pictures).

Everything you upload in the Site Editor also shows up in your Media Library, so you can reuse it elsewhere.

![An image field with the Replace image menu open, offering Upload from device and Choose from library](/images/site-editor/replace-image-menu.png)

## Style

The **Style** panel sits under **Design** at the bottom of the Sections list. It holds the settings that affect your whole site. You'll find it on every template, but what's inside depends on the template.

### Color palettes and fonts

Some templates come with color palettes and font pairings that restyle your whole site in a couple of clicks:

- **Color palette:** choose from a few color schemes designed for your template. Each one shows three color dots.
- **Fonts:** choose from a few font pairings designed for your template.
- **Original** (at the top of each list) returns that part to the template's standard design.

The choices are limited on purpose. Each one has been checked to look good with your template. Style changes are part of your draft, so you can try them all in the preview before publishing.

If your template doesn't offer palettes or font pairings, those lists won't appear in the Style panel. Your logo and checkout accent color are set on the [Brand & Appearance](/docs/storefront-and-branding) page instead.

### Text width on wide pages

Pages set to **Wide** (see [Page width](/docs/pages-and-policies#content-tab)) run their pictures, rows, and columns across the full content width. The text on those pages keeps a comfortable reading width by default. To change how wide that text runs across your whole site, open **Text width on wide pages** in the Style panel and pick one:

- **Readable** (the default) keeps lines at a comfortable reading width.
- **Roomy** lets the text run a little wider.
- **Full** lines the text up with your rows and columns.

It only affects custom pages set to **Wide**, and never the text inside rows and columns. It has no effect on the Default, Modern, Sledge and Builders templates, which always use the full width. Like other Style changes, it's part of your draft until you publish.

On templates without color palettes or font pairings, the Style panel holds only **Text width on wide pages**.

![The Style panel on the Default template, with Color palette choices (Original, Slate, Forest, Burgundy and Ochre), Fonts (Original, All Poppins and All Inter), and Text width on wide pages set to Readable](/images/site-editor/style-panel.png)

## Previewing on Different Screens

Most of your visitors will probably be on their phones, so check your changes at more than one size.

On a computer, use the three buttons in the top bar to switch the preview between **Desktop**, **Tablet**, and **Mobile** widths. The preview is still fully clickable at every size.

### Editing from your phone or tablet

You can use the Site Editor on a phone or a tablet held upright. The layout changes to fit the smaller screen:

- Your site fills the screen. **Tap anything on your site to edit it.**
- A **Sections** button at the bottom opens the list of sections (it's called **Content** when you're on one of your own pages).
- The editing panel slides up from the bottom and covers about half the screen, so the section you're editing stays visible above it. Drag the handle at the top to make it taller. It moves up on its own when you start typing. Swipe it down or tap the X to close it.
- A hint at the top says "Tap anything on your site to edit it". Tap **Expand editor** to make the panel taller, and **Shrink editor** to bring it back.
- The **⋯** menu in the top bar holds **Notes**, **Refresh preview**, **Open site in new tab**, and **Discard changes** (only when you have unpublished changes).
- Your save status shows as a small icon next to **Publish** (see the table below).

![The Site Editor on a phone, with the editing sheet open over the lower half of the screen](/images/site-editor/phone-sheet.png)

## Saving and Publishing

### Your changes save automatically

There is no Save button. About a second after you stop typing, your change is saved to your **draft**. The draft is kept on SimplePress's servers, not just in your browser, so you can close the editor, come back tomorrow, or open it on another device and pick up where you left off.

The status in the top bar tells you where things stand:

| Status                       | On a phone      | What it means                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Saving…**                  | Spinning circle | Your latest change is being saved to your draft.                                                                   |
| **Unpublished changes**      | Amber dot       | Your draft is saved but differs from your live site. Customers don't see these changes yet.                        |
| **Published**                | Check mark      | Your live site matches what's in the editor.                                                                       |
| **Couldn't save**            | Red alert icon  | The editor tried several times and couldn't save your latest change. Check your internet connection. Making another edit tries again. |

If saving fails, the editor retries on its own a few times before showing **Couldn't save**.

### Publishing

When you're happy with what you see, click **Publish**. Every draft change goes live at once: field edits, hidden or shown sections, theme choices, and edits to your own pages and blog posts. A "Your changes are live" message confirms it.

<video src="/videos/site-editor/edit-and-publish.webm" poster="/videos/site-editor/edit-and-publish-poster.png" controls muted playsInline className="rounded-lg border" />

*A short clip: editing the hero's intro line in the Site Editor, then clicking Publish and seeing the "Your changes are live" message.*

**Publish** is greyed out when there's nothing new to publish. If you click it while a last change is still saving, you'll see "Finishing your last action — one moment…" and it publishes right after. While a picture you added to page or post text is still uploading, the button reads **Uploading…** and can't be clicked until the upload finishes.

Publishing is blocked if one of your own pages or posts has no title. You'll see a message that the page needs a title before you can publish. Add one in that page's panel and try again.

### Discarding

To throw away your draft and go back to what's currently live, click **Discard** (on a phone, **Discard changes** in the **⋯** menu). It only appears when you have unpublished changes. A box asks "Discard unpublished changes?" Choose **Discard** to confirm, or **Keep editing** to cancel. A "Draft discarded" message follows. This undoes **every** unpublished change across all pages, including edits to your own pages and blog posts. It can't be undone.

### Leaving the editor

Once the status says **Unpublished changes** or **Published**, your work is safe and you can leave anytime. If you click **Exit** while a change is still saving, the editor asks "Leave the editor?" and you can choose **Stay** or **Leave anyway**. Your browser also warns you if you try to close the tab at that moment.

<Callout type="warn" title="One draft per store">
  Your store has a single draft that everyone shares. If two people on your team edit in the Site Editor at the same time, one person's changes can overwrite the other's. Take turns, and publish (or discard) before handing over.
</Callout>

### Changes made outside the editor

The Sections list has an **In your admin** group with shortcuts to **Navigation menu** and **Logo & brand**. These open in a new tab. Changes you save on those pages go live straight away. They aren't part of the editor's draft, and publishing from the editor won't undo them.

## Resetting to Defaults

There is no single "reset everything" button. Here's how to undo different kinds of changes:

| What you want to undo                                 | How                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Everything you've changed since your last publish     | Click **Discard**.                                                                                                                    |
| A color palette or font choice                        | Open **Style** and choose **Original**.                                                                                               |
| A slider                                              | Click its **Reset** button.                                                                                                          |
| A list you've customized                              | Delete all of its rows. Your site goes back to the template's built-in rows (if it has them).                                        |
| A hidden section                                      | Click the eye icon again to show it.                                                                                                 |
| A deleted list row                                    | Click **Undo** in the message that appears right after you delete it.                                                                |

If you clear a text box completely and publish, that text is removed from the page. For some links and buttons, the template uses its own built-in value instead. There's no button that brings back a template's original wording once you've published over it. If you need it back, [contact SimplePress support](mailto:help@generativejustice.org).

## Editing Your Own Pages and Blog Posts

Pages you've created under **Pages**, and blog posts if you use the [Blog](/docs/blog), also appear in the page picker. When you open one, the panel shows:

- **Title** (required: changes can't be saved while it's empty)
- **Excerpt**, the short summary
- **Content**, the full page body, with the same [text editor](/docs/text-editor) you use elsewhere in your admin. The Content editor here has no **Video** button.

![The Site Editor with a custom page open, and its Title, Excerpt, and Content panel on the right](/images/site-editor/custom-page-panel.png)

**Page width** (Standard or Wide) isn't here. You set it on the page's own admin screen under **Pages**.

These edits use the same draft as the rest of the editor. They show in the preview right away and go live when you click **Publish**.

A few things are still managed on the page's own admin screen, linked as **Site Admin** at the bottom of the panel: the page address (slug), SEO settings, whether the page is published, and, for blog posts, the hero image. Publishing from the Site Editor updates a page's content but does **not** publish a page that's set to unpublished. When you're editing a page visitors can't see yet, a banner says "This page is unpublished — visitors can't see it. You're viewing it in preview."

On these pages the left list shows **Page content** (or **Post content** for a blog post) and a link to **Open page settings** (or **Open post settings**).

On a blog post, the Sections list also shows any **Article sections** your template adds to the end of every post (such as a sign-up or call-to-action box), plus your site-wide sections.

To create a new page or delete one, use [Pages and Policies](/docs/pages-and-policies).

## Editor Notes

Sometimes you'd rather ask for a change than make it yourself: a layout tweak, something the editor doesn't offer, or a question about a section. **Notes** lets you send that request to the SimplePress team without leaving the editor.

![The Notes panel with Scope set to a section, a written note, two attached photos, and the Send note button](/images/site-editor/notes-panel.png)

### Sending a note

1. Click **Notes** in the top bar (on a phone, it's in the **⋯** menu).
2. Choose what the note is about under **Scope**: **Whole site**, **This page: [page name]**, or **A section on this page**. **This page** is the default.
3. If you choose a section, pick it from the list (site-wide sections like the header and footer are in a **Site-wide** group), or just click that section in the preview. While Notes is open, clicking the preview picks a section for your note (the label says **Pick for note** instead of **Edit**).
4. Write your note (up to 2,000 characters).
5. Optionally click **Add photos** to attach up to **3 photos**, such as a screenshot or an example you like.
6. Click **Send note**.

### Following up

Below the form you'll see every note sent from your store, with what it was about, any photos, and when it was sent. Each note is marked **Open** or **Resolved**. When the team responds, the reply appears under the note as **Reply from your site team**. A small number next to **Notes** shows how many notes are still open.

Good to know:

- Every owner and manager on your store can see all of the store's notes.
- Notes are separate from your draft. Publishing or discarding doesn't affect them.
- Photos attached to notes don't appear in your Media Library.
- To prevent accidental floods, a store can send up to 10 notes an hour.

## Switching Templates

You change your template on the [Brand & Appearance](/docs/storefront-and-branding) page, not in the Site Editor. A template switch goes live as soon as you save it there.

What happens to your Site Editor work:

- **Each template keeps its own content.** The words, photos, and lists you set up belong to the template you set them up in. After switching, the editor shows the new template's sections and built-in content. You'll need to fill those in again.
- **Your old content isn't deleted.** If you switch back, it comes back.
- **Style choices are designed for one template.** A palette or font pairing from your old template usually won't apply to the new one, which starts with its original design (if it offers color or font choices at all). The exception is a preset with the same name in both templates (some templates share presets such as Forest or Terracotta), which can carry over.
- **Hidden sections can carry over.** Whether a section is shown or hidden is remembered by the section's name, not by template. If you hid the homepage hero in your old template and the new one has a section by the same name, it starts out hidden.
- **Everything outside the editor stays the same**, including products, pages, blog posts, galleries, your logo, navigation, and settings.

After switching, open the Site Editor and go through each page, including which sections are shown or hidden, before you tell customers about the new look.

## What You Can and Cannot Do

**You can:**

- Edit any text, image, video, link, color, and list your template offers, on every page and in the site-wide header and footer
- See every change in a live preview at desktop, tablet, and mobile sizes before it goes live
- Hide and show sections that your template allows to be hidden
- Choose a curated color palette and font pairing (on templates that offer them), and set the text width on wide pages, in the Style panel
- Edit the title, summary, and content of your own pages and blog posts
- Save a draft, come back later from any device, and publish when you're ready
- Send notes, with photos, to the SimplePress team

**You cannot:**

- Move sections into a different order, or add new kinds of sections. Each template's layout is fixed.
- Change a section's design beyond the fields and theme options it offers
- Edit products, testimonials, FAQ questions (those are under **Site Setup → FAQ**), or other items from the Site Editor. Use the **Related content** links to reach the pages where you manage them.
- Create or delete pages, or change a page's address, SEO, or published status. Do that in [Pages and Policies](/docs/pages-and-policies).
- Change your template, logo, or navigation menu here. Use the **In your admin** shortcuts.
- Edit the wording on your cart and checkout pages. If your template has text there you'd like changed, [contact SimplePress support](mailto:help@generativejustice.org).
- Schedule a publish for later, or roll back to an older published version

If there's something on your site you'd like to change but can't find, send a note from the editor or [contact SimplePress support](mailto:help@generativejustice.org).
